# Source discovery

Read the [shared discovery rules](../README.md) first. This lane discovers changes from exact decompiled sources without wiki hints or concrete implementations. Receive an exact release pair, source/mapping identities, bounded owned slices and the permitted source-only progress projection.

## Plan and compare

Before many pair assignments, make one rough movement-area map to estimate size and dependencies. Assign whole pairs or recurring areas according to size while preserving exclusive ownership and full-pair closure. Check compatible naming families (Yarn/Legacy Yarn or Mojmap/published unobfuscated names), especially the transition around 1.14; equal symbols do not establish equal behavior.

1. Map the complete reachable player tick: input, pre-travel state/decisions, travel branches, post-travel, position/velocity/box writers and consumers, collision/shape providers and registrations, callbacks, fluids, pose/dimensions, modifiers/equipment/effects and externally triggered direct player responses. Follow callers, inherited methods, resources/tags and neighboring-block dependencies. A method-name match or unchanged caller does not close its dependencies.

2. Compare bounded paired slices with enclosing gates and call order. Preserve historical numeric procedures exactly. Explain a concrete reachable precondition, the changed behavior and its player movement meaning; distinguish source facts from predicted trajectory effects. Addition/removal needs evidence of the replacement or checked absence on the other side.

3. Write one independent difference using the shared finding path and format. Report checked slices with no difference as coverage, not as artificial findings. Keep excluded candidates as dispositions for the master.
4. Send exact inputs, ordered tick/inventory coverage, compared slices, dependencies, finding paths and the next bounded action to the master. Include examined, partial and unexamined areas explicitly; a generic partial flag cannot replace the inventory. The master updates the pair's coverage record with the backlog tool.

## Independent review and freeze

A different blind reviewer retraces the complete inventory and tick graph, including terminal slices without findings and dependency closure. Full-pair freeze requires complete inventories, paired evidence for every terminal slice, closed dependencies and that independent coverage audit. A worker completion message, self-review or an accepted individual finding does not close the pair.

Bind each accepted finding to its immutable revision and exact evidence identity. Independently accepted findings with closed dependencies and established applicability may enter separately authorized implementation before pair freeze; source isolation continues unchanged. Unknown intermediate patch boundaries remain open.

After freeze, the master reconciles both independent wiki inventories with the frozen source inventory and assigns bounded follow-up verification. Record new evidence separately from the historical frozen verdict and renew affected reviews when claims change.
