## Synchronized and live inputs

- The server configuration path sends dynamic registry data and serialized tags (`server/network/ServerConfigurationPacketListenerImpl.java:71–72`, both releases). Client registry data is received at `client/multiplayer/ClientConfigurationPacketListenerImpl.java:53` in 1.20.2 and `:54` in 1.20.4. Tag payloads pass through `network/protocol/common/ClientboundUpdateTagsPacket.java` and `ClientCommonPacketListenerImpl.handleUpdateTags` (`:193–198` in 1.20.2; `:151–161` in 1.20.4).
- `ClientPacketListener.handlePlayerAbilities` installs walking/flying speeds from `network/protocol/game/ClientboundPlayerAbilitiesPacket.java` (handler 1.20.2 `:1734–1742`; 1.20.4 `:1783–1791`). `handleUpdateAttributes` applies snapshots from `network/protocol/game/ClientboundUpdateAttributesPacket.java` (handler 1.20.2 `:1982` onward; 1.20.4 `:2049` onward). These are live player movement inputs; packet contents and runtime values are not pinned here.
- Source code identifies the tag keys, registry constants and consumer paths. No tag JSON, datapack resource contents, per-resource hashes, synchronized tag memberships, dynamic-registry values, or world-state payloads were inspected. Their runtime contents remain open.

## Exclusions and remaining edges

The `LocalPlayer` water-tag references at `:749–753, 1037` affect water-vision presentation, not movement. `BlockTags.FIRE` in `Entity` concerns fire-state handling; `BlockTags.INSIDE_STEP_SOUND_BLOCKS` and `COMBINATION_STEP_SOUND_BLOCKS` affect sound/statistics. Damage-only tags and consumers are outside this movement-input slice. Vehicle physics and passenger rotation are excluded.
