# Cauldron floor contact: bounded local-player displacement witness

## Scope and source identity

This is a source-only conditional witness for the Cauldron floor geometry delta, bound to the accepted-input specialized-provider draft `build/source-drafts/1.12.2--1.13.2-specialized-collision-providers.md` at SHA-256 `A3C6874B7897EF8D717F35B088215C5F50AB9ED2F11FB9A50E54791130A556C3`. It uses only exact local 1.12.2 and 1.13.2 Ornithe Feather sources, each ready with Feather Gen2 build 2; source manifest hashes are 1.12.2 `b8a37ccfccd2aac5f40f5e34fec85873dbdfa93a266043c103e592e4a8c949da` and 1.13.2 `2c8cfb646bf622fb26ac0ca0cb5e02a354fb512e29f8b1010f1aaacd509e1211`. Cited source hashes below match those manifests.

Claim is limited to the ordinary airborne player `move` call in an empty, loaded, in-border scene. The Cauldron is dry (LEVEL=0). No other collision shapes or entities interfere; the player is not on fire. The witness does not cover stepped movement, an already-overlapping Cauldron, callback side effects, water-filled cauldrons, or query arithmetic beyond the specific collection and Y clipping needed here.

## Exact setup

Put one Cauldron at block position `(0,0,0)`, with no other solid blocks intersecting the movement sweep. Use the ordinary player dimensions `0.6F × 1.8F`, set the player center to `(0.5, 0.5, 0.5)`, and call the ordinary movement path with `dx=0`, `dy=-0.30`, `dz=0` while airborne. Keep horizontal input neutral (or auto-jump disabled) so the local-player post-move auto-jump hook does not add another movement.

Both player classes set dimensions to `0.6F, 1.8F` at 1.12.2 `PlayerEntity.java:399` and 1.13.2 `PlayerEntity.java:450`. `Entity.setPosition` uses `f = width / 2.0F` and builds `[x-f, x+f] × [y,y+height] × [z-f,z+f]` (1.12.2 `Entity.java:298-306`; 1.13.2 `Entity.java:309-317`). Thus, for center x=z=0.5, `f=0.3F` (0.300000011920928955...), giving x/z bounds approximately `[0.19999998807907104, 0.800000011920929]`; feet start exactly at y=0.5.

These x/z bounds are strictly inside the Cauldron wall opening, 2/16..14/16 (`0.125..0.875`). At y=0.5, the player AABB has no positive-volume overlap with the base: old base ends at 5/16 (`0.3125`); new floor ends at 4/16 (`0.25`). Although the player body extends above the rim, its horizontal footprint remains in the hollow center and misses all four walls.

The local-player route is direct. Both local player classes inherit `ClientPlayerEntity` and their tick delegates to `super.tick()` (1.12.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:81,165-167`; 1.13.2 `:89,181-183`). Their `move` override delegates to `super.move` before the auto-jump follow-up (1.12.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:846-851`; 1.13.2 `:871-876`). For `dx=dz=0`, neutral input prevents the follow-up from supplying another move. Both sides invoke `Entity.move` from living movement (`LivingEntity.java:1514` at 1.12.2 and `:1575` at 1.13.2). At 1.12.2, ordinary dry falling subtracts `0.08` and multiplies vertical velocity by `0.98F` after movement (`LivingEntity.java:1524-1536`). At 1.13.2, the ordinary non-fluid branch subtracts `d` and multiplies by `0.98F` after movement (`LivingEntity.java:1580-1597`); `d` is `0.08` except for Slow Falling (`0.01`) (`:1480-1483`). With loaded chunks, gravity enabled, `abilities.flying=false`, `noClip=false`, and a dry player that is neither fall-flying nor climbing and has no Levitation or Slow Falling, a descending velocity below -0.25 is reachable in free fall. Player flight overrides `moveRelative` and restores vertical velocity as `oldY * 0.6` (1.12.2 `PlayerEntity.java:1386,1390-1395`; 1.13.2 `:1442,1454-1459`); `Entity.move` bypasses collision clipping when `noClip` is true (1.12.2 `Entity.java:462-466`; 1.13.2 `:473-477`). The precise vertical placement can be chosen continuously by the preceding fall; no block grid quantizes entity Y.

## Paired provider and query trace

1.12.2 Cauldron provider constants are `BASE_SHAPE` y=0..5/16 and walls at x/z widths 2/16 (`CauldronBlock.java:31-38`). `addCollisions` sends the base and each wall through `addCollision` (`:47-53`). The movement collector is called with the entity's swept expanded box (`Entity.java:567`) and invokes each block's `addCollisions` with `forceShape=false` (`World.java:1001`). `Box.intersectY` requires strict horizontal overlap and caps a negative move at `obstacle.maxY - player.minY` (`Box.java:205-221`). Here the walls have no x/z overlap; the floor does. Thus the downward request is clipped by the old 5/16 floor to `0.3125 - 0.5 = -0.1875`, leaving player feet at y=0.3125.

1.13.2 Cauldron collision shape is `VoxelShapes.join(VoxelShapes.block(), INSIDE_SHAPE, ONLY_FIRST)`, with the removed interior starting at 2/16 in x/z and 4/16 in y (`CauldronBlock.java:34-45`). Local movement supplies the current player box and motion to `WorldView.getCollisions` (`Entity.java:580`); it constructs a swept query and passes the current box as `excludeBlock` (`WorldView.java:158-163`). `getBlockCollisions` excludes a block only when its collision shape overlaps that initial box (`WorldView.java:112-150`). The player box lies wholly within the hollow x/z interior and above the floor, so this Cauldron is retained; the swept box descends below y=0.25, so its floor is also in the returned collision candidates. Y clipping then calls `VoxelShapes.calculateMaxOffset` before moving the entity (`Entity.java:581-583`; `VoxelShapes.java:194-203`; `VoxelShape.java:184-245`). The new 4/16 floor clips the requested descent to `0.25 - 0.5 = -0.25`, leaving feet at y=0.25.

**Conditional witness result:** for the setup above, old post-move feet y is 0.3125 and new post-move feet y is 0.25, a 1/16-block downward player displacement difference. Both providers are independent of Cauldron LEVEL; LEVEL=0 is chosen to exclude contained fluid and extinguishing behavior.

Local-player source hashes: 1.12.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java` `01a58e94d8c6ff98a8e3794227cdc76a5fcbdbad795c70c9cf28854aff9823cc`; 1.13.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java` `2583495f3a02b4791aa036e6a8d354d7596c4984761969a0f29b29d8d9bf42bf`.

## Guards and falsifier

- Require center x=z=0.5 and ordinary 0.6F width. If the footprint leaves the 2/16..14/16 opening, a wall may overlap the current box or clip the query; the new collector's `excludeBlock` test can then remove the entire Cauldron candidate. That falsifies this exact witness precondition.
- Require feet y=0.5, airborne, `dy < -0.25`, `dx=dz=0`, and no auto-jump follow-up (neutral horizontal input or auto-jump disabled). With `dy >= -0.25`, the new floor is not reached; with other movement or grounded/step conditions, this proof does not cover the caller branch.
- Require dry Cauldron and no other colliders, border sentinel, unloaded query area, or entity collision. A filled Cauldron introduces separate fluid movement; neighboring solids can change clipping order/results.
- For the falling-velocity collision route, require `abilities.flying=false` and `noClip=false`, plus no Slow Falling, Levitation, no-gravity state, fall-flying, climbing, or unloaded client movement chunk. These select different vertical velocity updates or bypass/constrain collision; this note does not cover them.
- Recheck that 1.13.2 `getBlockCollisions` retains this block for the current player box. If `VoxelShapes.overlap(excludeBlock, cauldronShape, AND)` is true in the stated geometry, the new candidate is filtered and the computed new floor clip does not occur; that would falsify the witness.
- This proves the stipulated geometry and source route, not a full tick-parity result or release cutover boundary between 1.12.2 and 1.13.2.
