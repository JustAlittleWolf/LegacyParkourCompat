# B01 Brewing Stand AutoJump step-control refinement

## Result: COMPLETED

The original bottom-slab 1.375 no-arm witness remains physically reachable in both exact controls, 1.11.2 and 1.12.2. `Entity.move` does run its 0.6-block step retry after horizontal clipping, but the retry cannot make horizontal progress onto the Brewing Stand plate from feet Y=0.5: reaching its top requires 0.625. The retry's attempted 0.6 rise leaves the player 0.025 below the plate top, so the plate still clips horizontal motion. The candidate has no greater horizontal displacement and the source restores the flat collision result. The post-move horizontal displacement remains zero; AutoJump uses its input/yaw fallback, sees the stand stem first at world maxY 1.875, computes rise 1.375, and returns above the 1.2 bound.

The full-block-floor scenario is also reachable, but the earlier control's post-move state and rise were wrong. From feet Y=1, the plate top is only 0.125 above the floor, so the 0.6 step retry moves across the plate and lands on it. After the retry, the player has feet Y=1.125 and horizontal displacement +0.13 for the stated sprint input. LocalClientPlayerEntity passes that actual displacement to AutoJump after `super.move`. Its +X rays then hit the stem at world maxY 1.875; the selected rise is 0.75, which is within the timer bounds and sets the timer to 1. This gives a legal positive full-floor witness, but it is a post-step witness, not a no-step query-entry at feet Y=1.0 with rise 0.875. A direct AutoJump/query-entry control at feet Y=1.0 remains useful for isolating the AutoJump selection logic, but it does not model the ordinary move call's result.

## Paired movement trace

- **Step height and retry:** In both exact releases, `LivingEntity` initializes `stepHeight` to `0.6F` (1.11.2 `LivingEntity.java:172`; 1.12.2 `:175`). `Entity.move` first clips the ordinary move, then enters its step candidate when the entity is grounded and horizontal movement was clipped (1.11.2 `Entity.java:600-612`; 1.12.2 `:603-614`). It evaluates the up/across/down candidate and keeps it only when horizontal squared displacement improves; otherwise it restores the saved ordinary result (1.11.2 `:662-688`; 1.12.2 `:665-691`). `Box.intersectY` only limits positive Y motion when the moving box is vertically below the collision box (`Box.java:205-218` in both versions).
- **Bottom-slab fixture:** At feet `0.5`, the stand plate spans world Y `1..1.125`. The upward step candidate is at most `0.6`, reaching feet `1.1`, still 0.025 below the plate top. When its raised shape attempts +X, it still vertically overlaps the plate and `intersectX` clips the move; its horizontal squared distance stays zero. The equal-distance check discards the candidate and restores the original box. With the ordinary grounded downward collision, `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move` then calls `autoJump` using actual horizontal displacement zero (1.11.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:824-828`; 1.12.2 `:846-850`). The zero-displacement fallback reconstructs +X from input/yaw. Stand boxes remain stem then plate; the first ray intersects the stem, so rise is `1.875-0.5=1.375`, above the 1.2 timer maximum. This confirms both retry behavior and the reachability of the no-arm witness.
- **Full-block-floor control:** At feet `1.0`, the plate top is `1.125`, only `0.125` up. The step candidate can clear the plate, cross it by +0.13, and descend until the plate top clips the downward move, yielding feet `1.125`. The path has greater horizontal squared displacement than the clipped flat path, so `Entity.move` retains it. On an ordinary grounded tick with the original negative vertical request, the downward clip leaves `onGround` true; `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move` then calls AutoJump with actual +X displacement. AutoJump's ray start is at the post-move feet and its endpoint extends the actual direction by its normal reach; the first collision is the stem. The rise is `1.875-1.125=0.75`, so the timer arms. `LocalClientPlayerEntity` calls AutoJump after `super.move` in both versions (same cited ranges above).
- **Exact scope of positive witness:** The full-block floor remains a legal positive control once its post-move step result is included. The old `feet Y=1.0`, unchanged-box, `rise=0.875` result is only a query-entry calculation and should not be represented as the result of the stated move. Smaller positive horizontal input still enters the same step path if it clips; there is no need to alter friction for the corrected witness.
