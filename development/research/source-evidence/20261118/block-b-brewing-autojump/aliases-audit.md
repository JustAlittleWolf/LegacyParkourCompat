## Independent derivation

- **Legal support and slab floor:** 1.11.2 SlabBlock defines its bottom collision shape to Y=0.5 (`net/minecraft/block/SlabBlock.java:20, 39-43`); the corresponding .12 implementation has the same shape and 1.13 uses the 0..8/16 bottom VoxelShape (`:27, 51-60`). Thus feet Y=0.5 is a supported standing height. BrewingStandBlock adds no support predicate: the old Block placement check is only replaceable material (`1.11.2/net/minecraft/block/Block.java:500-505`, same default in 1.12); in 1.13 Block.canBePlaced returns true and BlockState.canSurvive delegates to it (`Block.java:578-579`, `block/state/BlockState.java:301-302`). Placing the stand in the air cell above the slab is valid.
- **Initial player box and AutoJump query box:** At `(-0.3, 0.5, 0.8)`, with width 0.6 and height 1.8, the initial player box is X `[-0.6,0]`, Y `[0.5,2.3]`, Z `[0.5,1.1]`. Slabs at x=-1 support it; it only touches the stand's plate at x=0 and does not overlap the stand collision shapes. In the old aliases the plate prevents positive-X movement from this face, so actual dx/dz are zero. The AutoJump broad query from the feet to the extended endpoint plus player height, grown by width horizontally, includes the stand; the support slabs end at the query's Y minimum and do not intersect either raised ray. With one stand as obstacle, no unrelated collision candidate can precede it.
- **Fallback ray and collision admission order:** All three aliases gate AutoJump on enabled, timer `<=0`, grounded, not sneaking/riding, and nonzero input. The clipped zero displacement has squared length `<=0.001F`, so source rebuilds the horizontal probe from speed, input and yaw. Forward input at yaw -90 degrees gives +X; the sprint speed input described in the bound witness gives reach 0.91. The two lateral rays are raised by `0.51F`, at Z 0.5 and 1.1 for the stated player position. Old BrewingStandBlock shapes are a central stem AABB (X/Z .4375..5625, Y 0..875) and full-footprint plate AABB (Y 0..125); `addCollisions` appends the stem before the plate (`1.11.2/BrewingStandBlock.java:36-37, 70-72`; same bodies in 1.12 and 1.12.1). The first probe at Z=.5 intersects both boxes; the second clears them. AutoJump accepts the first returned collision AABB intersecting either ray and breaks (`1.11.2/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:888-925`; identical order/logic in .12 and .12.1 at `:905-942`). Therefore the selected obstacle is the stem, not the lower plate.
- **Slab-floor result and timer:** With stand at (0,1,0), the stem's world max Y is 1.875; its center floors to (0,1,0), and the next checked block at (0,2,0) is empty. Rise from feet Y=.5 is 1.375. The timer is only armed when rise is `>0.5F && <=1.2F`, so this alias case leaves it unarmed. The test loop's integer t with `t < 1.2F` reaches t=1 only; the Y=2 clearance cell is the one examined for this candidate.
- **Full-block floor control:** At feet Y=1 with full blocks under the same positions and stand at (0,1,0), the probe is Y=1.51. It clears the plate and intersects the stem; rise is 1.875-1=0.875. This falls inside the timer interval, so all three aliases set `ticksToNextAutojump = 1`. The control distinguishes the specific slab-height gate and agrees with the comparison witness.
