## Direct local passenger movement with the bounded horizontal inputs

In 1.8.9, `Entity.rideTick()` zeros rider velocity, runs the passenger tick, then calls `vehicle.updateRiderPositon()` (Feather `Entity.java:1275-1285`). The mounted local player's `LivingEntity.mobTick()` can call `jump()` when `jumping && onGround && jumpingCooldown == 0` and then calls `moveRelative(...)` (`LivingEntity.java:1431-1450`). `moveRelative` calls `move(velocityX, velocityY, velocityZ)` (`LivingEntity.java:1119-1121,1165`). The prior audit establishes that the own boat is offered as a collision shape in this old call, but under the fixed 0.35 carrier bound its box remains overlapping the stale player box on both horizontal axes. Therefore this candidate cannot clip either x or z in the old direct player move. This statement is only about the own-boat collision contribution; it says nothing about unrelated colliders or the general movement result.

In 26.2, `Entity.rideTick()` also zeros rider delta movement, ticks the rider, and positions the passenger afterward (`Entity.java:2381-2398`). A mounted `Player.travel(input)` routes to `super.travel(input)` (`Player.java:1402-1405`); the direct air movement path calls `move(MoverType.SELF, ...)` (`LivingEntity.java:2468-2479,2676-2679`). `Entity.move` turns the movement vector into a swept bounding box and invokes collision resolution (`Entity.java:711-755,1141-1148`). But `EntityGetter.getEntityCollisions` filters candidate entities through `source::canCollideWith` (prior audit: `EntityGetter.java:54-71`), and `Entity.canCollideWith` excludes same-root passengers (`Entity.java:2373-2378,3576-3578`; `AbstractBoat.canVehicleCollide`: `AbstractBoat.java:109-121`). Thus the local passenger's own boat cannot affect the direct move's clipped vector, velocity response, or position, even if its seat position is stale. For the fixed horizontal bound, the old own-boat collision path also has no horizontal clip, so no own-boat-induced horizontal move/velocity/position difference is established between these two sources. This is not a claim that their complete native movement algorithms match.

## Vertical stale interval

The old rider tick can request upward movement: `mobTick()` invokes `jump()` on the stated jumping/on-ground/cooldown gate, and `moveRelative()` passes the resulting `velocityY` to `Entity.move` before the post-tick seat update. A vertical own-boat clip would require a separated box and a sweep reaching the boat. With the prior seated intervals (player y `[by-0.65, by+1.15]`; boat y `[by, by+0.6]`), a boat displaced upward more than `1.15` before the passenger query would put it above the stale player box, where an upward request could sweep into it. A downward separated case requires the boat displaced below the stale player's bottom (more than `0.65` downward) together with a downward request. The previous audit did not establish either carrier displacement as an ordinary reachable local boat state. This review does not supply that missing vertical reachability witness, and therefore does not claim an old vertical clip or a vertical trajectory difference.

In 26.2, same-root filtering removes the boat before shape collision resolution irrespective of vertical separation, so the corresponding own-boat vertical clip cannot affect direct player movement there. The vertical question that remains open is specifically whether the old ordinary local mounted tick can reach one of the separated states above while issuing the matching vertical request; answering it requires a bounded source witness for boat vertical displacement/state and the rider input/ground gate. Vehicle physics and any trajectory derivation remain outside this result.

## Result

Under the accepted ordinary horizontal bound, the 1.8.9 stale own-boat candidate produces no x/z clip; 26.2 omits that same-root candidate. No horizontal direct-response difference is established. Modern source directly confirms own-boat collision exclusion from the player's move and post-tick passenger repositioning. A vertical difference remains unproved because the concrete old reachable separated-state-plus-sweep prerequisite is not established. No product code, tests, game launch, decompilation, wiki material, or vehicle-physics claim was used.
