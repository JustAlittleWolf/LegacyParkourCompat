# Frozen blind source audit: ordinary Nether portal, 26.1–26.1.1

## Boundary and verdict

This blind audit covers ordinary, non-riding player contact with a Nether portal: block contact and local portal-process admission, cooldown and transition-delay gates, server-side transition eligibility, and the source-defined server/client position-correction boundary. It excludes riding, portal geometry and exit search, fluids, End/gateway paths, packet timing and runtime inference.

**Verdict:** bounded source equivalence. No difference was found in the examined operation chain between 26.1 and 26.1.1. The compared source files below are byte-identical across the pair, and their raw SHA-256 values match their respective source-manifest entries. This does not establish runtime parity or inspect intermediate patches.
