## Ordered provider and consumer trace

1. `LocalPlayer.tick` calls `super.tick`; `LivingEntity.tick` calls `super.tick`; `Entity.tick` calls `baseTick` (`LocalPlayer.java:195-200`, `LivingEntity.java:2542-2545`, `Entity.java:475-500` on both sides). `baseTick` calls water body detection/current pushing, then eye-fluid sampling, then swimming update, in that order.
2. `Entity.updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing` clears `fluidHeight`, invokes the water-current path, then handles lava. For a non-riding local player, the ordinary water path calls `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing(FluidTags.WATER, 0.014)`. Its water gates, boat exclusion, splash/fall-distance side effects, and `wasTouchingWater` assignment match exactly (`Entity.java:1478-1499` in 1.21.9; `1483-1504` in 1.21.10). `isInWater()` returns `wasTouchingWater` on both sides (`1428-1430`; `1433-1435`); `isUnderWater()` combines the prior eye-water bit and body-water predicate on both sides (`1445-1447`; `1450-1452`).
3. The body sampler uses the same `getBoundingBox().deflate(0.001)` and floor/ceil-exclusive X/Y/Z block loops. A matching fluid contributes when its surface `blockY + getHeight(...)` reaches the deflated box bottom. The recorded body height is the maximum surface overlap. If pushed by fluid, each accepted cell contributes `getFlow`; contributions are depth-scaled while the maximum overlap is below `0.4`, summed and averaged by accepted-cell count, left unnormalized for `Player`, scaled by `0.014`, subject to the same minimum-push condition (`0.0045000000000000005` when horizontal velocity components are both below `0.003`), then added to delta movement. The method body matches (`Entity.java:3480-3545` in 1.21.9; `3485-3550` in 1.21.10). Player fluid-push eligibility is `!abilities.flying` on both sides (`Player.java:1604-1606`); flight behavior was not audited.
4. `updateFluidOnEyes` first snapshots the previously recorded eye-water state, clears eye tags, samples `getEyeY()` in the containing block, computes block Y plus fluid `getHeight`, and adds the sampled fluid tags only when that surface is strictly above the eye. The ordinary non-riding path bypasses the boat branch. This body is textually equivalent (`Entity.java:1501-1513` in 1.21.9; `1506-1518` in 1.21.10). `FluidState.getHeight` delegates to its type; `FlowingFluid.getHeight` returns `1.0F` for same fluid above or `getOwnHeight`, whose amount divisor is `9.0F` (`FluidState.java:53-55`; `FlowingFluid.java:438-445` on both sides).
5. Water-flow sampling delegates from `FluidState.getFlow` to `FlowingFluid.getFlow` (`FluidState.java:95-97`; `FlowingFluid.java:55-95`). The four-horizontal-neighbor gradient sampling, below-neighbor fallback, falling-column adjustment, and returned vector are identical. The provider and its `Level`/`BlockGetter`, block-state, position, bounding-box, rounding, and vector helpers have identical consumed source hashes above.
6. `LocalPlayer.aiStep` reaches `super.aiStep`; for the ordinary non-riding and movable local-player path, `LivingEntity.aiStep` builds movement input and calls `travel`. `travel` dispatches to `travelInFluid` when in water, affected by fluids, and not standing on the current fluid (`LocalPlayer.java:687-699, 834`; `LivingEntity.java:2751-2755, 2833-2849, 2275-2284`). `LivingEntity.java` is byte-for-byte identical by source hash across the pair. For the ordinary water branch, both sides use sprint slowdown `0.9F` or `getWaterSlowDown()` `0.8F`, base relative acceleration `0.02F`, the same water-efficiency and Dolphins Grace gates, `moveRelative`, movement, optional climbable-collision vertical `0.2`, then X/Z slowdown and Y factor `0.8F` before `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement`. The latter's gravity/16 subtraction, near-terminal `-0.003` case, and sprint bypass are identical (`LivingEntity.java:2331-2360, 2488-2501`). A reachable bounded witness is a non-sprinting local player in ordinary water, non-flying and non-riding, no water-efficiency attribute or Dolphins Grace, open water with no horizontal collision, and a downward Y velocity away from the helper's near-terminal threshold: the same movement input and pre-travel velocity produce the same move request and post-travel drag/vertical response in both source versions.
