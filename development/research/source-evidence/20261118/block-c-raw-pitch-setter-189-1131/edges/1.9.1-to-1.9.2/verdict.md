## Literal evidence labels

**Ordinary local pitch input — no difference.** In both releases, `client/render/GameRenderer.java:831–854` calls `minecraft.player.updateLocalPlayerCamera(h, i * j)` from the focused mouse-look path (smooth and nonsmooth branches). `entity/Entity.java:97–100` stores `pitch` and `lastPitch` as `float`. In both versions, `Entity.java:283–291` saves old current pitch in float `f`, computes `(float)(this.pitch - pitch * 0.15)` (the unsuffixed `0.15` makes the multiply/subtract double precision before the float cast), clamps the resulting current pitch to `[-90.0F, 90.0F]`, then updates previous pitch by `lastPitch + (clamped pitch - old current pitch)`. The previous pitch update is after the clamp. `LivingEntity.java:299–305` and `Entity.java:314–319` also snapshot `lastPitch = pitch` during their tick paths; `LivingEntity.java:1586–1592` wraps `lastPitch` by ±360 until `pitch - lastPitch` is in `[-180, 180)`. All these source bodies are identical in the pair.

**Finite ordering witness — literal values.** Starting with current pitch `89.0F`, previous pitch `88.0F`, and camera pitch delta `-10.0F`: the double multiply gives `-1.5`, the current candidate is `90.5`, and the clamp writes current pitch `90.0F`. Then the source expression writes previous pitch `88.0F + (90.0F - 89.0F) = 89.0F`. Updating previous pitch before the clamp would instead produce `88.0F + (90.5F - 89.0F) = 89.5F`. This finite witness confirms that clamp-before-previous-update is observable; the source uses the first ordering in both versions.

**Direct position/angle setter — no difference.** In both versions, `Entity.java:985–1003` clamps the incoming pitch to `[-90, 90]`, assigns that value to both `lastPitch` and `pitch`, then calls `setRotation`; `setRotation` at 269–272 stores `pitch % 360.0F`. `Entity.java:1009–1015`'s separate `setPositionAndAngles` path directly assigns current `pitch` and does not assign `lastPitch` or clamp. These exact operations and order match across the pair.

**First dry, active, nonpassenger local Elytra pitch use — no difference.** A local player reaches the shared living travel step through the normal tick path: `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:141–155` delegates `tick()` to its superclass. `LivingEntity.java:1697–1703` calls `flyingTick()` before `moveRelative(...)`. The flight flag is retained only while airborne, not riding, and wearing an Elytra for which `ElytraItem.canFly` is true (`LivingEntity.java:1709–1727`; local execution does not write the synchronized flag there). The `PlayerEntity.moveRelative` override (`PlayerEntity.java:1366–1384`) falls through to `super.moveRelative` for the ordinary non-creative-flying path. In `LivingEntity.moveRelative` (`1293–1308`), the locally controlled path is admitted, water and lava are excluded for a dry non-flying player, and `isFallFlying()` selects the Elytra branch. Its first pitch read is `getLookVector()` at 1302: `LivingEntity.getLookVector()` at 1807–1810 requests `getRotationVector(1.0F)`, and 1813–1820 selects the current `pitch` (not `lastPitch`). The next explicit current-pitch read is `float i = this.pitch * (float)(Math.PI / 180.0)` at 1303. The angle `i` feeds `cos(i)` and the downward-pitch `i < 0.0F` / `sin(i)` term at 1307–1321, changing lift/descent and velocity. The gates, input width, expression order, and movement formula match in 1.9.1 and 1.9.2; `LivingEntity.java` has the same verified per-source hash in both.
