# 1.19.4 → 1.20.0 Elytra glide route and cap

## Scope and result

Blind exact-source comparison of already-active, ordinary chest-slot Elytra gliding on the logical client, with dry movement and either climbable or ordinary feet. Elytra start decisions, final `fallFlyTicks` accumulation, collision/contact outcomes, fluid branches, and unrelated glide effects are outside the core comparison. The source pair is exact 1.19.4 → 1.20.0 Mojmap; 1.20.1 was not used.

For the ordinary state, there is no behavioral change in the active glide route, its pre-move arithmetic, or its relationship to climbable cleanup. A separate effect-conditioned difference is recorded below: when Slow Falling is active and vertical velocity is non-positive, 1.19.4 resets fall distance in the travel setup and 1.20.0 does not.

## Active flag, route, and climbable handling: no difference

- **1.19.4** `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`: `aiStep` calls `updateFallFlying()` at 2543, before travel at 2550; `updateFallFlying()` is 2588–2613. If shared fall-flying flag 7 is set, its candidate remains true only while not on ground, not a passenger, not levitating, and still wearing an enabled Elytra (2590–2608). It writes that candidate shared flag only when `!level.isClientSide` (2610–2612).
- **1.20.0** same class: `aiStep` calls `updateFallFlying()` at 2562, before travel at 2572; `updateFallFlying()` is 2610–2635. The same gates and write guard remain in the same order. The on-ground access changed from `this.onGround` to `this.onGround()`; 1.20.0 `Entity.onGround()` at 589–591 simply returns `this.onGround`.
- In both releases, `travel` selects `else if (this.isFallFlying())` before the ordinary friction route: 1.19.4 `LivingEntity.java` 2048–2088; 1.20.0 2068–2108. There is no `onClimbable()` gate in the active glide route. Its first action is glide-specific slow-fall-distance handling, followed by the glide math and `move`.
- Climbable velocity cleanup remains inside `handleOnClimbable`: 1.19.4 `LivingEntity.java` 2180–2194 and 1.20.0 2199–2213. It resets fall distance, clamps X/Z to ±0.15F, floors Y at −0.15, then applies the ladder-sneak player rule. The ordinary friction movement calls this helper (1.19.4 `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement` at 2154–2164; 1.20.0 at 2171–2181). Since the active glide branch precedes that route and does not call the helper, an already-active glider at a climbable bypasses this cleanup in both releases. Ordinary feet also take the same glide branch while the flag remains active.
- On-ground cancellation after glide movement is also unchanged in meaning: 1.19.4 lines 2085–2087 and 1.20.0 lines 2105–2107 clear shared flag 7 only on the non-client side. The client-side `updateFallFlying` write guard likewise remains. This is a flag-write/gate comparison only; it does not infer collision or contact outcomes.

## Pre-move glide arithmetic: no cap difference

- 1.19.4 `LivingEntity.java` 2048–2074; 1.20.0 2068–2094 contain the same active glide equations and order. Both take current delta movement, look vector, float pitch in radians, horizontal look magnitude, horizontal speed, and look length; apply gravity plus pitch lift; add the downward-vector correction; add the negative-pitch lift; align horizontal velocity; multiply by `0.99F, 0.98F, 0.99F`; then call `move`.
- No velocity clamp/cap is applied before that `move` in either glide branch. `$$21 * 3.2` is the negative-pitch lift contribution to Y, not a cap. It is a `double` expression; `$$15` is a `float`. The final attenuation uses the same three `float` literals in both versions, passed to `Vec3.multiply` before the move.
- Post-move horizontal-collision damage and grounded flag clearing are outside the requested collision outcome, except for the flag-write comparison above.

## Effect-conditioned adjacent difference

In the travel setup, 1.19.4 `LivingEntity.java` 1983–1991 sets gravity to `0.01` when `getDeltaMovement().y <= 0.0` and Slow Falling is present, and also calls `resetFallDistance()`. 1.20.0 lines 2004–2010 retains the same gravity selection and gate but removes `resetFallDistance()`. Both glide branches then call `checkSlowFallDistance()` (`LivingEntity.java` 2049 and 2069 respectively); the unchanged helper in `Entity.java` (1.19.4 2167–2171; 1.20.0 2274–2278) only reduces fall distance to 1.0F when current Y velocity is greater than −0.5 and fall distance exceeds 1.0F. Thus with Slow Falling, non-positive starting Y, and preexisting fall distance above zero, 1.19.4 resets the value before that check, while 1.20.0 preserves it or caps it to 1.0F under that helper's separate gate. For ordinary state without Slow Falling this branch does not apply. This is the only material difference found in the requested route; the exact pair establishes the endpoint change, not applicability to other effects or paths.
