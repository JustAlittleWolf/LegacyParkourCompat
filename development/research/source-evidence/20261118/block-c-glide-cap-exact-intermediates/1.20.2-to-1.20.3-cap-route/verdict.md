# Independent source audit: 1.20.2 → 1.20.3 active Elytra route and climbable cap

## Verdict

**Accepted for the bounded claim: no movement-behavior difference is present in the audited slice.** The frozen report’s route and pre-move-cap descriptions are supported by the exact sources. The conclusion is limited to the stated dry, already-active client glide and the adjacent ordinary/climbable routing; it is not full-pair coverage or runtime parity evidence.

## Independent checks

- In both `LivingEntity.java` files, `aiStep` enters the profiler’s `travel` phase, calls `updateFallFlying()`, then calls `travel(...)` (1.20.2 lines 2587–2601; 1.20.3 lines 2589–2603). In both, `updateFallFlying()` starts with shared flag 7, checks airborne/not-passenger/not-levitating and a chest-slot Elytra with `ElytraItem.isFlyEnabled`, and only writes the computed flag under `!level().isClientSide` (1.20.2 lines 2639–2664; 1.20.3 lines 2641–2666). `isFallFlying()` reads shared flag 7 (1.20.2 lines 3084–3086; 1.20.3 lines 3086–3088). Thus the client-side eligibility calculation itself does not clear an already-set flag; the route predicate continues to read the synchronized flag. The conditions and write gate match.
- `Player.travel()` reaches `super.travel()` on its ordinary non-ability-flight path in both releases. The separate `abilities.flying && !isPassenger()` arm clears flag 7 after `super.travel()` in both (1.20.2 lines 1465–1474; 1.20.3 lines 1463–1472). The 1.20.2 post-branch `checkMovementStatistics(...)` has no counterpart in 1.20.3; it is outside the audited movement route and executes after `super.travel()`.
- `LivingEntity.travel()` checks water, lava, then `isFallFlying()`, and only afterward reaches ordinary friction movement in both (1.20.2 lines 2038–2166; 1.20.3 lines 2035–2163). On the stated dry and already-active path, the glide arm wins before ordinary movement; it performs the same velocity math, multiplies by `(0.99F, 0.98F, 0.99F)`, then calls `move`. It does not query `onClimbable()` or apply the ordinary climbable cap. This remains true for ladder/vine feet because feet state is not consulted in that selected glide arm.
- `onClimbable()` has the same spectator, feet-state `BlockTags.CLIMBABLE`, and trapdoor checks (1.20.2 lines 1511–1525; 1.20.3 lines 1508–1522). For the ordinary branch, `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement()` calls `moveRelative`, applies `handleOnClimbable` to delta movement, and then calls `move` (1.20.2 lines 2205–2209; 1.20.3 lines 2202–2206). The helper is identical: x/z clamp to `[-0.15F, 0.15F]`, y floor at `-0.15F`, then the negative-y suppression condition for non-scaffolding feet, ladder-slide suppression, and `Player` (1.20.2 lines 2233–2247; 1.20.3 lines 2230–2244). The later post-move collision/jump y response is also unchanged.
- The source file hashes validate the inspected files and match their source identity records. `LocalPlayer.java` is byte-identical between releases. No candidate claim in the bounded slice failed review.
