## Exact-touch movement and 0.6 step retry

`1.10.2 Entity.java:514–538, 542–624, 630–633` clips vertical motion before X then Z, sets the grounded/downward-collision flag, and retries from the original box when step height is positive and horizontal motion clipped. The retry sets `dy = this.stepHeight`, queries collisions, clips the upward trial and horizontal axes, compares the two horizontal outcomes and retains/reverts by the squared-distance comparison. `LivingEntity.java:165` supplies player step height `0.6F`.

In 1.11 / 1.11.1 / 1.11.2 the corresponding `Entity.move` route has the newer `MoverType` signature and explicit zero-axis guards. For this fixture, requested X and downward Y are nonzero while Z is zero, so those guards do not alter the Y-before-X collision order; applying a zero Z clip in the older method is a no-op. All three anchors retain the same `onGround || downwardYWasClipped` eligibility, positive-`stepHeight` plus horizontally-clipped retry gate, original-box retry with `dy = this.stepHeight`, Y-then-X-then-Z collision clipping, horizontal-distance choice and rollback comparison. Their living-player constructors also set `stepHeight = 0.6F` (`LivingEntity.java:172`). This matches the supplied exact-touch route: downward motion clips on the bottom slab, and ordinary +X motion is clipped at exact touch by the full-footprint base plate beginning at x=0. The 0.6 retry remains blocked by the plate: raised feet at 1.1 overlap the plate top at 1.125 by 0.025 while the retry sweeps into +X. After movement, the AutoJump side-ray collision list has the stem first because `addCollisions` submits stem before plate; the stem supplies the `s = 1.875` height candidate. The single player AutoJump callback then sees the fully resolved result.

The geometry is also source-equal for this route. In 1.10.2 `BrewingStandBlock.java:36–37, 70–72` defines center stem `(0.4375, 0, 0.4375)–(0.5625, 0.875, 0.5625)` and base plate `(0, 0, 0)–(1, 0.125, 1)`, submitting stem before plate. All three 1.11.x versions use the same two boxes and submit stem before plate. Their `addCollisions` signatures differ at this patch boundary: 1.11 omits `forceShape`, while 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 add the boolean parameter; the two box additions remain unconditional and identical. The bottom slab remains `(0,0,0)–(1,0.5,1)` in `SlabBlock.java`, selected whenever the single slab is not top-half (`1.10.2:19–20, 34–40`; 1.11.2:20–21, 39–45). The shape constants and ordered box additions match between target and all anchors, with only the noted 1.11 `addCollisions` signature difference.

## Height result and 1.10 aliases

The shared AutoJump method compares `u = (float)(s - this.getShape().minY)` and only writes `ticksToNextAutojump = 1` when `u > 0.5F` and `u <= o`, with `o` initialized to `1.2F` absent Jump Boost. For the stem ray, `s = 1 + 0.875 = 1.875`; from feet `0.5`, `u = 1.375`. Since `1.375 > 1.2`, the final predicate fails: the stem-only exact-touch fixture does **not** arm the timer. For the full-floor control, post-step feet are `1.125`, so the same stem gives `u = 1.875 - 1.125 = 0.75`; `0.5 < 0.75 <= 1.2`, so it **does** arm the one-tick timer. This corrects the prior draft's reversed interpretation.
