Difference: Across every inspected stable version from 1.10 through 1.17.1, the inherited entity water-contact update has a Boat-passenger gate that writes the false body-water flag before the fluid scan. The operation evolves from `checkWaterCollisions` / `checkWaterState` and `inWater` / `insideWater` / `wasInWater` to the fluid-pushing method and `wasTouchingWater`; its relevant unconditional Boat gate remains equivalent. This agrees with the accepted 1.9.4 and 1.18–1.19.2 scopes. The first inspected scoped change after this run is 1.19.3, which conditions the Boat clear on the Boat being underwater.

Movement meaning: The local client player inherits through the player/living-entity hierarchy from `Entity`; the entity tick invokes the water-contact update. While the local player is a passenger of a Boat, the inherited body-water predicate is therefore cleared each tick irrespective of the player's own water overlap through 1.17.1 and through 1.19.2. In 1.19.3 the clear is conditional, allowing the ordinary water-contact scan to affect the player in the stated non-underwater-Boat case. This finding covers only the player's inherited water-contact flag/predicate and its direct scan gate, not Boat movement or vehicle physics.
