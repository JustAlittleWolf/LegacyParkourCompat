Extension flips zero to `1.0F`; the overload 162–193 subtracts 1 from the full Stone box's EAST-facing `maxX`, yielding `[0,0] × [0,1] × [0,1]`. Stone inherits the base full-unit collision shape (`Block` default bounds; `StoneBlock` has no collision-shape override). `Entity.move` 439 gathers `World.getCollisions` for the displacement-expanded box before clipping axes.
