# Cobblestone wall beside TNT and Oak Stairs

The independent source audit accepted the tuple of a dry cobblestone wall with one TNT neighbor, one dry Oak Stair whose resolved shape is straight and whose facing is toward the wall, and air on both remaining sides. Air above also fixes the opposite-pair post result. The accepted source draft has SHA-256 `749bf5f2bb6ba52f35a3936f4938d42a3d1efe69c253aa7ba3a97d6efa8c6145`. The independent audit has SHA-256 `5cb4909d55939d3d8f6bd52865873ed49e963ae78670509d60e7741d6be32661`.

- **1.11.2, Ornithe Feather build.2.** `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java:148–157, 188–195` (`shouldConnectTo`, `resolveVirtualProperties`) rejects TNT through `TntBlock.java:26–29` and `Material.java:26, 103–105`, and rejects the stair through `StairsBlock.java:145–147` and `StateDefinition.java:273–275`. Both bits are clear. `WallBlock.getShapeIndex` and its collision array select index zero, the centered box `(0.25, 0, 0.25)–(0.75, 1.5, 0.75)`. The checked files match the source manifest SHA-256 `d908c2af76598ad26999563bfef0089f1d3c8c32483eee6b108f0defa2773df0`.
- **1.12, Ornithe Feather build.2.** `WallBlock.java:146–159, 190–196` uses directional face shape. TNT inherits the `SOLID` result in `Block.java:362–364`. `StairsBlock.java:140–162, 328–359` recomputes the stair shape from adjacent states and returns `SOLID` for a straight stair facing the queried wall. Recomputed mask indices are 12 for NE, 6 for NW, 9 for SE, 3 for SW, 5 for NS and 10 for EW. The post flag does not enter `getShapeIndex`. The checked files match the sources in `.decompile-provenance.json` SHA-256 `27515e5f4c71e7db2f035a81716507128d77466c75380ad1ef7d140c06401467`.

In both releases, `World.getCollisions` calls `WallBlock.addCollisions` with `forceShape=false` before `Entity.move` clips movement against those boxes. An intersecting player path can therefore see the side-arm difference. The verdict covers the exact source comparison and supplies no measured movement result.
