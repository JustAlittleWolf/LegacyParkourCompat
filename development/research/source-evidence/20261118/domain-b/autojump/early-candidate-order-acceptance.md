# Early AutoJump collider order and provider admission

All cited releases use Ornithe Feather Gen2 build.2. The independent ordering review accepted the loaded, interior, entity-free fence and bottom-slab collision route for exact 1.10 through 1.12.2. Author SHA-256 `206d3c383cce03b8ff5e275c19a7af1759ab22e7b53a3585429e19ed94c114a7`, independent review `65ec50d179d37d5ad741a8b78ef74e865a72c6d12a0ba27865b7ba05f4ef688f`.

- `World.getCollisions` uses the same floor/ceil bounds and X/Z/Y order. A cell on two horizontal edges is skipped. On a single edge, 1.10, 1.10.1, 1.10.2 and 1.11 skip the first and last Y rows. Exact 1.11.1 changes the block callback to `addCollisions(..., false)` and skips only the last Y row. That rule persists in 1.11.2 and the three exact 1.12 releases.
- The grounded producer is independently accepted. A dry bottom slab can support the standing player before a forward sprint with Speed III toward an isolated tall fence post. Neither the ordinary movement nor its `0.6F` step retry can advance through that post. `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move` invokes `autoJump` after `super.move` with the actual X/Z deltas cast to float. The detector consumes the first candidate box intersecting either side segment. `FenceBlock.addCollisions` supplies the centered post and `SlabBlock.getShape` the bottom-half box. Grounded author SHA-256 `dad721ecb0e02631c5b1667d1bbaca5bbefb3fca8cf5c07f0352a2e04b555c36`, independent review `46df691ca907c090fab78720b55266b2091d605f26fdfb15f894a32667f0f839`.
- A separate accepted interior fixture in 1.11, 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 uses a single dry Brewing Stand beside a bottom-slab floor. `BrewingStandBlock.addCollisions` checks the translated centered stem before the full-footprint plate. The initial review was conditional on the side segment striking the stem. The renewal independently checked the corrected lateral position, yaw, input and reach and confirmed that condition. Corrected author SHA-256 `e4bab20dab665b60f0dfa9adf41a2dbd97cf07392869a379b0351f98d5d42faa`, initial review `47beb4b17fa536418a104ec3bd9bbd502a70b566bdc72cb2136014ec98b8c0d9`, renewal `eb055b2df86406e0e6d69cb317f6399185f3fe9b17b2f55f61290169a190951b`. The early Brewing Stand projection applies from 1.11. Its 1.10.x reachability gate remains open.

The verdicts cover the cited candidate order, provider geometry and bounded producing paths. Runtime parity remains unverified.
