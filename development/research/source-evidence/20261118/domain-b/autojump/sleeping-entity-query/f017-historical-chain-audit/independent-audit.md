## Coexistence and sequence audit

1. **Sleep and inventory:** Bed activation does not predict local sleep; the server's tracked pose/sleep-position update reaches the local player through the tracker self-send route. When inventory is already current, `MinecraftClient.tick` does not replace it with `SleepingChatScreen` because that branch requires `currentScreen == null`. `InventoryScreen` passes events and does not pause remote-world ticks. This route exists in both versions.
2. **Mouse raw input:** In a narrow inventory with recipe book open, an unused click outside the recipe controls is unhandled. `Mouse`'s pass-events route sets the mouse-bound `key.forward`; `KeyboardInput.tick` reads it as positive `movementForward`. Sleeping clears entity movement-speed fields, not the separate `ClientPlayerEntity.input` vector AutoJump reads. This establishes raw input, not movement while asleep.
3. **Relocation, support and headroom:** Permission-level-2 `/spreadplayers` accepts entity targets and teleports without `/teleport`'s explicit wake step. On flat full-cube support it places the player centered at the support's top Y. The sleeping `0.2F` box has its first `floor(maxY)` cell and the next cell empty. The next local downward move resolves against support and sets `onGround` before `ClientPlayerEntity.move` calls the detector after `super.move`. Keep the tracked bed intact through timer scheduling.
4. **Boat placement:** Relative to the measured pile center `(x+0.5, floorY, z+0.5)`, the boat center `(x+1.5, floorY+(double)0.2F+4e-8, z+0.5)` leaves a positive vertical gap about `4.0e-8` above the sleeper box and a horizontal gap about `0.2125` beyond its X edge. Thus it neither overlaps nor contacts the sleeping player. The boat's `1.375F × 0.5625F` box intersects raised side probes, its top gives a rise about `0.7625F` within the detector's `>0.5F && <=1.2F`, and the block above its center can be air. Vanilla `/summon` accepts double coordinates/NBT; `NoGravity` plus zero velocity and no contact/fluid keeps this pose under ordinary ticking. This is source-reachable, conditional on command permission/execution.
5. **Paired detector result:** In both releases the relevant `ClientPlayerEntity` gates/order are the same: cached AutoJump, timer, ground, sneak/vehicle, positive input, two empty head cells, fallback when realized X/Z is zero, ray probes, then height threshold. For the setup, the candidate envelope finds the boat in both versions. 1.14.2 filters against the original query with strict intersection; 1.14.3 expands the final query by `1.0E-7` (the nontrivial query passes its average-dimension guard). Only the new query bridges the positive gap, so the same boat box produces timer 1 only in 1.14.3. Its center-above block is air; floor support is below the raised rays.
6. **Wake and jump:** Destroying the original bed after scheduling makes server bed validation wake the sleeper. With no bed at the stored position, `LivingEntity.wakeUp` skips its bed-side reposition and clears sleep metadata while retaining the relocated coordinates. `ServerPlayerEntity.wakeUp` sends animation then self position-look; the tracked player receives the animation. Client wake plus the subsequent position/look sync can restore standing pose and destination before the next local tick, preserving the grounded position. The timer writes `input.jumping` after raw input sampling. If wake precedes the base jump gate, `cannotMove()` no longer clears jumping and the grounded, zero-cooldown, unobstructed path calls ordinary `jump()`. If still asleep at that gate, source instead clears effective jumping. Network handling is main-thread scheduled; source supplies no guarantee of the required packet timing in any individual run.

The comparable setup is otherwise source-permitted on both sides; the operation-level difference is the entity-query epsilon. This is not runtime evidence, modern representability, product applicability, an intermediate-patch boundary, or full tick-parity evidence.
