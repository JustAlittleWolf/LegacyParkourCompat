The independent source review accepted the bounded end-to-end witness in `zero-thickness-stone-source-r2.md`, SHA-256 `37c5225bc05255e27e09def81d778092eb932db91510c3ab9b70882a87a4dbf`. In 1.8.9 Ornithe Feather Gen2 build.2, a loaded, airborne standing local player can acquire a small negative Y request from the preceding dry travel tick and positive X with zero Z from sideways input. `MovingBlock.getCollisionShape` in `net/minecraft/block/MovingBlock.java:118–130, 162–197` samples `MovingBlockEntity.getProgress(0.0F)`. During the `(progress, lastProgress) = (0.5F, 0.0F)` entity phase, outward extension trims the East-moving Stone's box to its west face. `World.getCollisions` (`World.java:891–928`) visits that cell and `Block.addCollisions` (`Block.java:339–344`) admits its zero-width box when the swept query crosses the face. The rechecked `WorldChunk.java:710–735` uses strict entity-box intersection, consistent with the empty entity envelope required by the witness. The checked WorldChunk file SHA-256 is `a658562b5df4a209e802d8bf25033c8399599a4d4652ddb012e32937e6cd4ca2`.

`Entity.move` applies Y before X. `Box.intersectY` (`Box.java:115–130`) rejects exact transverse contact on X, leaving Y unchanged. `Box.intersectX` (`:93–108`) then sees Y and Z overlap and clips the positive X request at the touching face, yielding zero X. The independent alias review traced this order and shape path in 1.8 and 1.8.1 and confirmed equality of the relevant 1.8.2 through 1.8.9 files. In exact 1.9 Feather Gen2 build.2, `MovingBlock.getCollisionShape` delegates to `MovingBlockEntity.getShape`, which unions current and previous positive-volume boxes. The accepted cutover is 1.9.

The verdict covers this single collision candidate in ordinary dry movement. Runtime parity remains unverified.
