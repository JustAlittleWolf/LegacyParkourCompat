# Raw mouse-turn pitch batching: 1.20.4 → 1.20.5

1.20.4, Mojmap (official Mojang client mappings): `net/minecraft/client/MouseHandler.java`, `onMove` lines 187–221 and `turnPlayer` lines 224–266; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `turn` lines 398–410 and `setXRot` lines 3309–3315; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, fall-flying branch in `travel` lines 2099–2137.

1.20.5, Mojmap (official Mojang client mappings): `net/minecraft/client/MouseHandler.java`, `onMove` lines 187–203, `handleAccumulatedMovement` lines 205–233 and `turnPlayer` lines 235–267; `net/minecraft/client/Minecraft.java`, render call at line 1190; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `turn` lines 400–412, `setXRot` lines 3324–3330 and `getLookAngle` lines 2027–2028; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, fall-flying branch in `travel` lines 2168–2207.

Difference: In 1.20.4, each accepted mouse-move callback adds its coordinate delta only while the mouse is grabbed and the window active, immediately calls `turnPlayer`, and then updates the prior cursor coordinates. `turnPlayer` computes sensitivity and dispatches `player.turn` for that callback before zeroing the accumulated deltas. In 1.20.5, `onMove` adds deltas while the window is active (without a grabbed check); `Minecraft.render` calls `handleAccumulatedMovement`, which turns the player once with the combined deltas and clears them. The turn call is gated by active window, grabbed mouse and non-null local player. Thus a set of mouse callbacks between handling passes reaches `Entity.turn` separately in 1.20.4 and as one net delta in 1.20.5. The sensitivity expression and branch math are unchanged: `s = sensitivity * 0.6F + 0.2F`, `s3 = s * s * s`, `s8 = s3 * 8.0`; smooth-camera mode feeds `s8`-scaled deltas through `SmoothDouble`; first-person scoping without smooth camera uses `s3`; otherwise the deltas use `s8`. Y inversion multiplies the vertical result by −1 when enabled.

The pitch writer is otherwise unchanged. `Entity.turn` casts the vertical `double` delta to `float`, multiplies by `0.15F`, adds that to current pitch and calls `setXRot`; it updates yaw next, then clamps the resulting pitch to inclusive `[-90.0F, 90.0F]`. It separately adds the unclamped pitch delta to `xRotO`, then clamps `xRotO` to the same bounds. `setXRot` rejects non-finite values (with `Util.logAndPauseIfInIde`) and otherwise assigns the raw float. It performs no modulo/wrap. No setter or clamp ordering change was found between these releases.

Reachable consequence: with mouse grabbed, active window, local player present, smooth camera off, no scoped first-person path, sensitivity 1.0, starting pitch 0, and two vertical callback deltas of +200 then −200 pixels before the 1.20.5 handling pass, 1.20.4 applies two turns. Each vertical turn is approximately 122.88 degrees before clamping: pitch becomes +90, then −32.88. 1.20.5 sums the callbacks to zero and leaves pitch at 0. These finite deltas are accepted by the mouse path; the difference follows from the source math and clamp placement, without requiring a mouse delta outside the floating-point or entity-pitch bounds. This is a source-derived witness, not runtime verification.

The pitch is read by reachable active fall-flying travel: `LivingEntity.travel` obtains `getLookAngle()` and also computes `pitchRadians = getXRot() * (float)(Math.PI / 180.0)`; its cosine squared contributes to vertical acceleration and its negative-pitch test contributes a dive lift. `Entity.getLookAngle()` derives the vector from current pitch and yaw. Thus a pitch difference surviving until local-player fall-flying travel changes the polar input to movement.
