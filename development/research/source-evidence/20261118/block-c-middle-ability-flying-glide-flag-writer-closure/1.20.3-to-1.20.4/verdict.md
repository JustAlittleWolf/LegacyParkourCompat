# Independent source audit verdict: 1.20.3 to 1.20.4

## Verdict

**Accepted: no source difference in the assigned movement slice.** The author report’s conclusion is correct for ordinary dry airborne local Elytra glide and lawful local creative ability flight under the supplied preconditions. The ordered travel operations and the invoked movement implementation are byte-identical across the pair. This is an operation-level result, not complete player-tick coverage or proof of an intermediate patch boundary.

The report needs a citation correction and a trace supplement: `Entity.setSharedFlag(int, boolean)` begins at line 2233, not 2229, and the `move(MoverType, Vec3)` invoked by `LivingEntity.travel` is inherited from `Entity`; it is declared at `Entity.java:596` and runs through line 725. `LivingEntity` does not declare this `move` method. Both cited methods and their enclosing source files are identical across the pair, so these report issues do not change its no-difference result.
