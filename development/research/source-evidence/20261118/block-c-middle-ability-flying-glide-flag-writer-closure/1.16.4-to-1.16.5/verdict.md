## Frozen trace in both exact releases

The following method bodies are byte-identical across the two releases (same SHA-256s above); the cited operations and gates are thus unchanged in the paired source.

1. `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep`, around lines 2451–2460: the tick applies input updates, calls `updateFallFlying()`, then dynamically invokes `this.travel(new Vec3(this.xxa, this.yya, this.zza))`. This closes the local player tick-to-travel edge for this trace.
2. `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lines 1387–1416: after optional swimming adjustment, it tests `this.abilities.flying && !this.isPassenger()`. On the true branch it saves vertical velocity and `flyingSpeed`, sets speed to `abilities.getFlyingSpeed() * (isSprinting() ? 2 : 1)`, invokes `super.travel(vec3)`, restores horizontal / scaled vertical delta movement and saved speed, then writes `fallDistance = 0.0F`, then `setSharedFlag(7, false)`, in that order. The false branch invokes `super.travel(vec3)` without those two writes. Movement statistics follow either branch.
3. `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lines 1914–2021: when dry, the fluid branches do not take precedence; the `isFallFlying()` branch computes glide motion, scales it by `(0.99F, 0.98F, 0.99F)`, and performs `move(MoverType.SELF, this.getDeltaMovement())` before returning to `Player.travel`. The branch may clear bit 7 on ground only under `onGround && !level.isClientSide`; that gate is false for the frozen airborne client case. In Creative flight, the outer `Player.travel` ability-flight branch still takes this `super.travel` path when the player is simultaneously fall-flying and not a passenger, then performs its own separate two writes after the glide move.

**Pair delta at freeze:** none in the traced source slice. Both versioned Player and LivingEntity byte hashes match exactly, with the relevant call, movement, gates, and post-move writes present identically. A behavioral difference claim for this bounded operation is therefore not supported by these exact sources.


## Independent review of author evidence and verdict

The author report was opened only after the frozen `Player.travel` / `LivingEntity.travel` trace above. Its immutable SHA-256 is `8ff7ffa4ce5cafcee7bf40a560f9d5e96472763e8c9d964f75b4a1a056c7a138`. The later addendum SHA-256 is `ac6ab2fccd893c4661c44599b2a4e5cb9ff48f2dba178923f0e6e703bb828b6f`; its stated bound original report hash matches the verified report SHA above.

**Verdict: ACCEPT the bounded source no-difference claim.** The report's four relied 1.16.4 source hashes are confirmed by the 1.16.4 mapping-root provenance source map. The 1.16.5 side is correctly established by the adjacent legacy recognized version-root evidence: ready marker binds the source manifest and artifact manifest by hashes; the exact Player.java and LivingEntity.java entries in that source manifest match the actual bytes, and those bytes match the 1.16.4 provenance hashes. Therefore the original report's claim that no 1.16.5 source-manifest identity existed is superseded by its addendum and is not a remaining evidence gap. The same validation also succeeds for the report's LocalPlayer.java and Entity.java hashes: LocalPlayer is `6011569e766bb1568609147be9aa14e9c08c51948e3d3a60fd066e848f6a8c2b`; Entity is `f9a9a073fe3105a0aa53d0f21ec72e59084e8d21a14c1cd3be75703865ee2666` on both sides. Their inputs, local movement dispatch and actual bounding-box move code are byte-identical too.

The original report's path details are supported: LocalPlayer's non-spectator `mayfly` double-jump path and Elytra start checks permit activating the Elytra state before the subsequent ability-flight branch; Player.travel then enters only for flying and not passenger; LivingEntity's dry `isFallFlying` branch issues the glide `move`; on the airborne client its on-ground/server flag clear is gated off; Player.travel's separate fall-distance reset and bit-7 clear follow the move in that order. The two state writes remain independently observable operations even though this pair has no change between them.

The report/addendum's own scope limit stands: this accepts only the source-pinned, byte-identical bounded trace; it does not certify full-pair tick/inventory coverage or runtime parity. The 1.16.5 provenance sidecar has `mappingArtifacts: null`; this is disclosed as a provenance limitation, while the precise relied source and artifact hashes are bound through the ready marker and manifests as required for this legacy publication. Raw 1.16.5 jars are absent locally, but no direct raw-file verification is claimed or needed to validate these exact source bytes against the bound manifest.
