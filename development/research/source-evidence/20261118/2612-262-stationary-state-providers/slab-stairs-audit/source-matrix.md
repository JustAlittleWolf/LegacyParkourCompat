## Independent likely verdict before claim review

No stationary dry slab or stair state→collision-shape difference is indicated between 26.1.2 and 26.2. The complete legal TYPE × HALF × SHAPE × FACING selection for this bounded family is covered by unchanged provider logic and unchanged stair-state topology. SlabBlock's whole-file hash changes, but getShape is textually identical at the compared endpoints; no collision-provider delta is established from that unrelated file change. Registered weathering copper subclasses inherit their parent provider in both versions.

This is an endpoint comparison only. It does not prove when any change entered during an intermediate release, and it is not a full-pair source inventory, audit, or freeze. Exact author claims remain unreviewed until this independent source roster and matrix are frozen.
