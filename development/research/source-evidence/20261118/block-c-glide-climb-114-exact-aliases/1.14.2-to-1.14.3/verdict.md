## Bounded review

- **Climb predicate and feet block:** `LivingEntity.isClimbing()` is at 1.14.2 `LivingEntity.java:1222–1235` and 1.14.3 `LivingEntity.java:1233–1246`. The ladder/vine case here means one of those states in the player's block position at feet level.
- **Tick update and flag authority:** `LivingEntity.tickMovement()` invokes `initAi()` immediately before `travel()` at 1.14.2 `LivingEntity.java:2204–2241` and 1.14.3 `LivingEntity.java:2214–2251`. For an already-set flag 7, airborne/non-riding player and usable chest Elytra, `initAi()` retains `bl = true`; the final `setFlag(7, bl)` is guarded by `!world.isClient`. On the client this does not clear the active tracked flag. The predicates and order match; mappings rename the chest-slot/Elytra symbols.
- **Initiation and server authority context:** the client jump-edge request is gated by not already gliding, airborne, descending, and not ability-flying at `ClientPlayerEntity.java:696–703` in both releases (mapped names differ); that request path does not re-initiate in this already-active case. If sent, `ServerPlayNetworkHandler` validates airborne, descending, not already gliding, not in water, and a usable chest Elytra before calling `ServerPlayerEntity.method_14243()` to set flag 7: 1.14.2 `ServerPlayNetworkHandler.java:1094–1103`, `ServerPlayerEntity.java:1271–1272`; 1.14.3 `ServerPlayNetworkHandler.java:1096–1105`, with the same setter body. These server checks provide acceptance authority; they do not run historical client physics.
- **Player-to-living dispatch:** with default `abilities.flying == false` and no vehicle, `PlayerEntity.travel()` falls through to `super.travel(arg)` at 1.14.2 `PlayerEntity.java:1362–1389` and 1.14.3 `PlayerEntity.java:1364–1391`. The ability-flight branch that clears fall distance and flag 7 is not taken.
- **Glide precedence and the threshold:** `LivingEntity.travel()` checks `isFallFlying()` before its ordinary movement `else`: 1.14.2 `LivingEntity.java:1769–1829`, 1.14.3 `LivingEntity.java:1779–1839`. The glide branch has no `isClimbing()` guard. It tests `lv5.y > -0.5` (strict comparison of the double velocity component against the double literal) and assigns `fallDistance = 1.0F` before glide-vector math and before `move()`. Equality at `-0.5` does not assign. The matched operations are at 1.14.2 lines 1781–1809 and 1.14.3 lines 1791–1819.
- **Climb cleanup and later boundaries inside this operation:** only the ordinary movement branch calls `method_18801`, whose `isClimbing()` case clears fall distance and clamps velocity (1.14.2 `LivingEntity.java:1823–1829`, helper at `1927`; 1.14.3 `LivingEntity.java:1833–1839`, helper at `1937`). The glide branch's later landing flag clear is guarded by `onGround && !world.isClient` (1.14.2 lines 1820–1822; 1.14.3 lines 1830–1832); wall damage is likewise server-only. Neither is a client-side ladder/vine climb reset. The comparison closes these branch/authority boundaries only for this pair.
