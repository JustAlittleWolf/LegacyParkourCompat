My source-only assessment was based on `PlayerEntity.java:1464–1480`, `Entity.java:946–958`, and `Entity.java:1041–1049`: `PlayerEntity.updateSwimming()` clears swimming when flying; otherwise it delegates if the player is not swimming, or if the exact clearance conjunction is true. For an already-swimming player, the delegate is skipped when the current block is non-solid but the block one block above is solid: `doesNotSuffocate(pos)` becomes false because it requires both `!pos.isSolid()` and `!pos.up().isSolid()`. A source-water cell at the player's feet and full stone at `pos.up()` therefore preserve the swim flag regardless of sprint. This is physically reachable after the player first acquires swim pose in open, deeper water: `PlayerEntity.java:333–357` sets the 0.6 × 0.6 dimensions, and `PlayerEntity.java:174–254` calls this pose update after the superclass tick. 1.13's `updatePlayerPose()` sets dimensions to `0.6 × 0.6` when swimming, and only applies the resize when `world.hasNoCollisions(null, box)` succeeds. With the swim box wholly in a water cell beneath a stone roof, the floor and roof have vertical clearances and water has no collision. The source-defined eye height while swimming is `0.4`, so a player at `y=64.2` samples water at `y=64`; source-water height `8/9` plus the submerged check's `0.11111111F` tolerance admits that eye point.
