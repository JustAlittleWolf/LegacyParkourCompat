## Paired trace

**Player.travel** — 1.21.8 `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java:1438–1461`; 1.21.9 same file `:1295–1318`. Both first branch on `isPassenger()`: passenger travel delegates immediately to `super.travel` and skips this player-local correction. For nonpassengers, the correction is gated by `isSwimming()`. It samples `double lookY = this.getLookAngle().y`, then chooses `double strength = lookY < -0.2 ? 0.085 : 0.06`. It conditionally applies when `lookY <= 0.0 || this.jumping || !fluidState(BlockPos.containing(x, y + 1.0 - 0.1, z)).isEmpty()`. Due to `||` order, the above-player fluid-state lookup is skipped when either earlier condition is true. The sample uses current X/Z and current Y plus `0.9`, converted through `BlockPos.containing`; it is not an eye position or a swept volume. If admitted, it reads current delta movement and writes `oldDelta.add(0.0, (lookY - oldDelta.y) * strength, 0.0)`. This preserves X/Z, eases vertical velocity toward the look-vector Y component, and executes before the flying check and before `super.travel`. The flying subbranch saves the then-current Y, runs inherited travel, and afterward replaces Y with saved-Y times `0.6`; the ordinary branch invokes inherited travel directly. The compared control flow and expressions match exactly.

**Current pitch and float lookup** — 1.21.8 `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java:1746–1754,2356–2358` and `Mth.java:28–36,48–50`; 1.21.9 `Entity.java:1801–1809,2424–2426` and `Mth.java:28–36,48–50`. `getLookAngle()` calls `calculateViewVector(this.getXRot(), this.getYRot())`, so its pitch input is current `getXRot()`, not interpolated `xRotO`. `calculateViewVector` forms float-radian pitch and negative yaw (`angle * (float)(Math.PI / 180.0)`), obtains float sine/cosine from `Mth`, and returns `new Vec3(sinYaw * cosPitch, -sinPitch, cosYaw * cosPitch)`. The Y term is calculated as float `-sinPitch`, then widened by `Vec3`'s double constructor; Player's `double lookY` therefore carries the widened float lookup result, not a double-precision trigonometric result. Both releases use the same `float[65536]` sine LUT, initialized for indices `0..length-1` as `(float)Math.sin(index * Math.PI * 2.0 / 65536.0)`. `sin(float)` returns `SIN[(int)(angle * 10430.378F) & 65535]`: float multiplication, Java narrowing to int (truncation toward zero), 16-bit mask, and float result. No table width, population, index, sign or widening change is present.

**Inherited movement path** — 1.21.8 `LivingEntity.java:2245–2254,2301–2330`; 1.21.9 `LivingEntity.java:2275–2284,2331–2360`. After Player's direct write, its nonpassenger `super.travel` selects `travelInFluid` when the entity is in water or lava, affected by fluids, and not standing on the sampled fluid. In water the inherited method calls `moveRelative`, then `this.move(MoverType.SELF, this.getDeltaMovement())`, then applies horizontal-collision/climb handling and multiplies velocity by water drag before fluid-fall adjustment. `Entity.move` is the inherited collision/position method (`Entity.java:663` in 1.21.8; `:670` in 1.21.9). The route and relevant order are present on both sides; the local source trace does not mistake the direct velocity mutation for the actual movement operation.
