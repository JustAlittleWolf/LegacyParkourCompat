## Compared source and behavior

- 1.21.1 Mojmap `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`: `move` entry gate lines 598–618; `collide` lines 875–904; `collectCandidateStepUpHeights` lines 906–925; `collideBoundingBox` / `collectColliders` lines 927–946; `collideWithShapes` lines 948–983.
- 1.21.2 Mojmap `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`: `move` entry gate lines 619–640; `collide` lines 908–937; `collectCandidateStepUpHeights` lines 939–958; `collideBoundingBox` / `collectColliders` lines 960–979; `collideWithShapes` lines 981–1016.
- Both `move` entry paths bypass collision for `noPhysics`; the ordinary path applies edge backoff, then calls `collide`. The existing early return for a zero limited piston displacement also precedes collision. In `collide`, the initial box is expanded along the attempted motion and queried through `Level.getEntityCollisions`; the zero-length input bypasses `collideBoundingBox`. Step search requires positive `maxUpStep`, downward clipping or grounded state, and horizontal clipping. A downward clipped attempt moves the search box by the clipped Y amount; a non-downward attempt extends the candidate query box down by `1.0E-5F`. Candidate shapes are collected over that query box.
- Candidate heights are derived by walking each collected shape’s Y coordinates and converting each boundary to `float` after subtracting the search box `minY`. Negative candidates and candidates exactly equal to the initial clipped Y displacement are skipped. The ascending per-shape scan breaks once a boundary exceeds `maxUpStep`; accepted candidates enter a `FloatArraySet`, then its array is unstable-sorted ascending. For each candidate in that order, collision is recomputed with the original requested X/Z and candidate Y. A candidate is accepted only when its horizontal-distance squared is strictly greater than the initial clipped result’s. The first such candidate returns immediately, with vertical settlement subtracting the difference between the original box `minY` and the search box `minY`; if none qualifies, the original clipped result is returned.
- Ordinary collision collection preserves input entity-collision shapes, conditionally adds the world-border collision shape only when the entity is non-null and `isInsideCloseToBorder` is true, then appends block collisions. Under the assigned border-clear/entity-free precondition, the added entity and border constraints are absent and block collision shapes feed the shared axis collision procedure. Axis order and tie branch are identical: Y first; then Z before X only when `abs(dx) < abs(dz)`; otherwise X before Z.
- Shape controls agree in both releases. `SlabBlock.getShape` maps `DOUBLE` to `Shapes.block()`, `TOP` to `TOP_AABB`, and default/bottom to `BOTTOM_AABB`. `BOTTOM_AABB` is `Block.box(0,0,0,16,8,16)`, and `Block.box` divides coordinates by 16, giving the lower-slab top boundary 0.5. The full-cube shape control is `Shapes.block()`. Thus candidate acquisition sees the corresponding top boundary at 1.0 for a full cube and 0.5 for a lower slab in both versions, subject to the same box-relative subtraction and candidate gates.
