### Bounded witness for 1.21.4 → 1.21.5

Use the same flat full-cube support floor at y=0, clear movement space, and one WebBlock at `(0, 1, 1)`. The `COBWEB` registration uses `.noCollission()` in all three versions (`Blocks.java` lines 686–693 in 1.21.4, 697–704 in 1.21.5/1.21.6), so the witness does not clip the movement. A non-riding, non-flying player has width 0.6, feet at y=1, and center `(x,z)=(0.5,0.5)`. One ordinary SELF move accepts horizontal displacement `(1,0,1)`, ending at `(1.5,1,1.5)`; no collision clips the requested movement, and support/effects are otherwise equal.

In 1.21.4, the direct swept diagonal intersects the WebBlock’s default entity-inside full-cube shape: during the segment, the player’s X range overlaps block x=0 while its Z range overlaps block z=1. `checkInsideBlocks` invokes WebBlock and leaves its multiplier for the next ordinary move. In 1.21.5, equal X/Z uses YXZ (Y is zero, then X then Z): on the X leg the player’s Z extent ends at 0.8; on the Z leg its X extent begins at 1.2. Neither leg touches `(0,1,1)`, so no Web multiplier is written for the next move. This is an ordinary SELF path, no piston/riding/flying/fluid/damage involvement. 1.21.6 follows the same axis legs as 1.21.5 for this movement.
