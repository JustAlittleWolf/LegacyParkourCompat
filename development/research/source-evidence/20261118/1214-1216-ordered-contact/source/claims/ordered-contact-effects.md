# Ordered block contact effects

1.21.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` and `applyEffectsFromBlocks` (`627–660`, `747–763`); `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `boxTraverseBlocks` (`172–189`); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/HoneyBlock.java`, `entityInside` / `doSlideMovement` (`62–70`, `105–115`); `WebBlock.java`, `entityInside` (`25–33`); `SweetBerryBushBlock.java`, `entityInside` (`78–93`).
1.21.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move`, `applyEffectsFromBlocks`, and `checkInsideBlocks` (`667–680`, `768–802`, `1079–1122`); `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `forEachBlockIntersectedBetween` (`172–190`); `HoneyBlock.java`, `entityInside` / `doSlideMovement` (`63–71`, `106–116`); `WebBlock.java`, `entityInside` (`26–34`); `SweetBerryBushBlock.java`, `entityInside` (`80–95`).
Difference: For an ordinary successful local movement, 1.21.4 checks inside-block effects along one old-position-to-final-position segment. 1.21.5 records the collision-resolved displacement as sequential axis-ordered segments and checks each segment in order. Honey, Web, and Sweet Berry Bush perform their assigned movement writes directly as each eligible contact is visited. When those two routes intersect different eligible target shapes or order, the target callbacks can consequently differ: Honey can rewrite current velocity and reset fall distance; Web or Sweet Berry Bush can set a different next-move stuck multiplier and reset fall distance. Exact patch introduction within 1.21.4–1.21.5 is unverified.
Movement meaning: On the next ordinary dry SELF move, a stored stuck multiplier gates on squared length greater than `1.0E-7`, multiplies requested movement, is cleared, and current delta movement is zeroed before edge backoff and collision. The route effect is conditional on a diagonal local move for which target contact differs; with the same ordered eligible contact sequence, these block callback results are unchanged. Fall damage and the server-only Sweet Berry damage predicate are outside this movement claim.
