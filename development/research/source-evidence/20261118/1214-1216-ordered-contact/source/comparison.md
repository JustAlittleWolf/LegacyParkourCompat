## Coverage and bounds

Compared the ordinary dry, non-riding, non-flying local player path through `LivingEntity.handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement` → `move(MoverType.SELF, ...)`, end-of-travel block-contact dispatch, Honey/Web/Sweet Berry direct callbacks, and the first following ordinary SELF movement that consumes a stored stuck multiplier. Contact-shape geometry and collision-resolved displacement are held equal; the finding is conditional on the dispatcher route changing which eligible inside-shapes are intersected or their order. Piston-only handling, damage, fluids, portals, Frost/powder, other locomotion branches, full-pair inventory, runtime parity, and pair freeze are not covered.

## 1.21.4 → 1.21.5: eligible ordered-contact path difference

`Entity.move` in 1.21.4 does not record its ordinary local successful displacement as axis legs. After `LivingEntity` travel, `Entity.applyEffectsFromBlocks()` adds the one `oldPosition()` → final `position` segment and visits the resulting endpoint box with `BlockGetter.boxTraverseBlocks`; `checkInsideBlocks` invokes `BlockState.entityInside` immediately for eligible blocks (1.21.4 `Entity.java:627–660, 747–763, 1050–1084`; `BlockGetter.java:172–189`).

In 1.21.5, each successful `Entity.move` decomposes the collision-resolved displacement into `axisStepOrder` orthogonal segments and records them in order (1.21.5 `Entity.java:667–680`). End-of-travel dispatch flattens those recorded segments and visits them in sequence through `checkInsideBlocks` / `forEachBlockIntersectedBetween` (1.21.5 `Entity.java:768–802, 1079–1122`; `BlockGetter.java:172–190`). Thus, for a diagonal displacement whose orthogonal-leg route intersects a different target inside-shape set/order than the direct old-to-final route, a target contact callback can be added, omitted, or reordered. The interval establishes only that this route change is present in 1.21.5 and absent in 1.21.4; the exact introducing patch inside that interval is unknown.

The assigned callbacks write movement state directly while contacts are visited; they do not submit these writes to the new `InsideBlockEffectApplier` collector:

- Honey `entityInside` calls `doSlideMovement` before its superclass callback. `doSlideMovement` reads current velocity, applies its existing `getOldDeltaY` threshold and `getNewDeltaY(-0.05)` expression, then resets fall distance (1.21.4 `HoneyBlock.java:62–70, 72–78, 105–115`; 1.21.5 `HoneyBlock.java:63–71, 73–79, 106–116`). A different eligible Honey contact can therefore trigger or skip this velocity rewrite; later direct Honey contacts read the velocity left by earlier ones.
- Web contact calls `makeStuckInBlock` with `(0.25, 0.05F, 0.25)`; Sweet Berry Bush contact calls it with `(0.8F, 0.75, 0.8)` for LivingEntity other than Fox or Bee (1.21.4 `WebBlock.java:25–33`, `SweetBerryBushBlock.java:78–93`; 1.21.5 `WebBlock.java:26–34`, `SweetBerryBushBlock.java:80–95`). Each call resets fall distance and stores its multiplier; where different eligible sticky block effects are encountered, the last direct write determines the stored multiplier.
- `Entity.move` on the next ordinary SELF call consumes a nonzero stored multiplier before edge backoff and collision: it multiplies the requested movement, clears the stored multiplier, and zeros current delta movement (1.21.4 `Entity.java:627–648`; 1.21.5 `Entity.java:643–656`). The ordinary dry `LivingEntity` path calls this move from `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement` in both versions (1.21.4 `LivingEntity.java:2362–2366`; 1.21.5 `LivingEntity.java:2394–2398`).

The Honey `fallOn` parameter changes from float to double between these sources, but its body only dispatches fall damage/sounds and does not alter the movement result; fall-damage behavior is excluded. The Sweet Berry server damage-condition edits likewise do not enter this client movement claim.

## 1.21.5 → 1.21.6: bounded equivalence for assigned effects

The Honey, Web, Sweet Berry Bush, and `InsideBlockEffectApplier` source bodies are byte-identical between 1.21.5 and 1.21.6. The direct Honey velocity/fall-distance writes and Web/Berry multiplier/fall-distance writes are consequently unchanged. For an ordinary one-call SELF displacement (well below the 1.21.6 100-record cap), the recorded route is also equivalent: 1.21.5 stores each axis-ordered leg in sequence; 1.21.6 stores the displacement with `axisIndependant=true` and `checkInsideBlocks` expands it using the same `axisStepOrder` before dispatch (1.21.5 `Entity.java:667–680, 1079–1122`; 1.21.6 `Entity.java:694–697, 798–807, 1113–1135`). Their `BlockGetter` intersection DDA performs the same per-leg ordered visits for this bound (1.21.5 `BlockGetter.java:172–190`; 1.21.6 `BlockGetter.java:172–199`). The first subsequent ordinary SELF stuck-multiplier consumption has the same gate, multiplication, clear, and delta-zero order (1.21.5 `Entity.java:649–653`; 1.21.6 `Entity.java:676–680`). No assigned Honey/Web/Sweet Berry movement difference was found on this edge.
