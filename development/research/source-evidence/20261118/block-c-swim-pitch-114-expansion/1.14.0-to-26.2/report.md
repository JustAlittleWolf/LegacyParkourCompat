## Look-Y table-index witness

The vector expressions agree structurally for Y: `-sin(pitchRadians)`. Their table-index arithmetic does not agree for all legal finite held pitches.

- 1.14 `Entity.getLookVector()` calls `getRotationVector(pitch, yaw)` (`Entity.java:1181-1189`, SHA-256 `15e9d4cde45d857ca3117ccfe2d5d9071c08e7ee4a67b4053f7b006fe7a4bfa9`). Pitch radians are a `float`: `pitch * (float)(Math.PI / 180.0)`. `MathHelper.sin(float)` indexes with `(int)(radians * 10430.378F) & 65535` (`MathHelper.java:11-29`, SHA-256 `e39d5dfc69c17a9032be086d7d4d14cc63525962a0ff83615b1befdcca599843`). The product rounds to float before truncation toward zero.
- 26.2 `Entity.getLookAngle()` calls `calculateViewVector(getXRot(), getYRot())` (`Entity.java:2579-2581, 1966-1974`, SHA-256 `7afb9c1294893ffe73e3b1acffcad41c648f15de8378bff3dffaff869bb811d5`). `realXRot` is likewise assigned to a `float` after the degree conversion, then passed as a float promoted to double. `Mth.sin(double)` indexes with `(int)((long)(radians * 10430.378350470453) & 65535L)` (`Mth.java:31-55`, SHA-256 `30455c684f2401c79290847822bca82e77162c4a2bcf8618e85cac9898a2dc17`). The product remains double until truncation toward zero via the long cast.

For legal pitch `84.375F` (within the local player's `[-90F, 90F]` pitch range; 1.14 clamps pitch in `Entity.java:317-319`, and 26.2 clamps view pitch during `Entity.turn` at `Entity.java:489-497`; the latter source SHA-256 is `7afb9c1294893ffe73e3b1acffcad41c648f15de8378bff3dffaff869bb811d5`), source-form arithmetic gives radians `1.4726215600967407F`. The old scale product is `15359.9990234375F`, so its table index is `15359`; the new double product is `15360.000038869068`, so its table index is `15360`. The table generators also differ: 1.14 stores `(float)Math.sin(i * Math.PI * 2.0 / 65536.0)`; 26.2 stores `(float)Math.sin(i / 10430.378350470453)`. Thus the witnessed look-Y values are approximately `-0.995175302028656F` (old) and `-0.9951847195625305F` (new), before promotion into the double adjustment expression. This pitch selects multiplier `0.085` at both endpoints. The difference establishes a concrete legal pitch witness; it does not claim that every pitch produces a different index or output.
