## Frozen technical trace and provisional result

The player `isSwimming()` predicate also excludes flying and spectator players (1.14.2 lines 1739–1741; 1.14.3 lines 1741–1743). In this branch, `g = getRotationVector().y`, and `h` is `0.085` when `g < -0.2`, otherwise `0.06`. If `g <= 0.0 || jumping || the fluid at BlockPos(x, y + 1.0 - 0.1, z) is nonempty`, it reads current velocity and writes `lv.add(0.0, (g - lv.y) * h, 0.0)`. This adds only vertical velocity and preserves x/z; no clamp or cast is present. The pitch/yaw vector helper in `Entity` converts pitch to radians with `pitch * (float)(PI / 180.0)`, then returns y as `-MathHelper.sin(pitchRadians)` (1.14.2 lines 1182–1190; 1.14.3 lines 1226–1234). Thus downward look (positive pitch, negative `g`) always enters the inner gate and pulls vertical velocity toward the negative look vector with branch coefficient 0.085 below -0.2, else 0.06; upward look (`g > 0`) only writes when jumping or fluid occupies the sampled block above.

Immediately afterward, `PlayerEntity.travel` runs `super.travel(arg)` (except its separate creative-flying branch, impossible while `isSwimming()` is true). The water branch of `LivingEntity.travel` performs velocity update, `move(SELF, velocity)`, optional climb collision y override, scales velocity by `(f, 0.8F, f)`, then, only if gravity applies and the player is not sprinting, replaces y using its `bl`/near-0.005 special case or `lv2.y - d / 16.0`; a horizontal collision escape may finally set y to `0.3F`. The writer therefore affects this tick's water movement and downstream y velocity subject to those later operations. Entry to `LivingEntity.travel` is gated by `canMoveVoluntarily() || isLogicalSideForUpdatingMovement()` in both releases.

Independent comparison of the bounded writer, its rotation-vector helper, and the immediately consuming water travel branch finds identical gates, operands, constants, branch boundaries, arithmetic, type promotion, write order and fallthrough in 1.14.2 and 1.14.3.
