# Ordinary water travel changes vertical gravity and sprint friction

1.12.2, ornithe-feather: `1.12.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative(float, float, float)`, water and lava branches, lines 1425-1584.
1.13, ornithe-feather: `1.13/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative(float, float, float)`, water and lava branches, lines 1459-1635.
1.13.2, ornithe-feather: `1.13.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative(float, float, float)`, corresponding water and lava branches, lines 1478-1654.

Difference: In 1.12.2 the water branch begins with `f = getBaseMovementSpeedMultiplier()` (`0.8F`), adjusts `f` for Depth Strider, moves, applies `velocityX/Z *= f`, `velocityY *= 0.8F`, then subtracts `0.02` from vertical velocity unless gravity is disabled. From 1.13 the base horizontal factor is `isSprinting() ? 0.9F : getBaseMovementSpeedMultiplier()`, before the Depth Strider adjustment; after the same movement and vertical `0.8F` damping, ordinary nonsprinting water travel applies gravity `d / 16.0` (normally `0.005`, with a near-zero terminal-velocity correction) instead of `0.02`. The sprint multiplier is `0.9F` before Depth Strider. The later Dolphins' Grace override is excluded as a newly introduced effect. In the ordinary lava branch, all three velocity axes are still multiplied by `0.5`; with ordinary gravity `d == 0.08`, `d / 4.0` equals the old `0.02`. Thus this comparison establishes no default lava drag change. Slow Falling and sprint-specific gravity suppression are separate gates and are not part of the default witness.

Movement meaning: For a default nonflying player in water, with no Depth Strider or status effect, not sprinting, gravity enabled, and pre-travel vertical velocity zero, the old post-damping vertical velocity becomes `-0.02`; the new branch becomes `-0.005`. For an otherwise ordinary sprinting player with nonzero horizontal velocity and no Depth Strider in shallow water below eye level, the old water factor is `0.8`, while the new factor is `0.9`; this witness does not require the new submerged swimming state. These are direct reachable player velocity differences. The change first appears in inspected 1.13 source and persists in 1.13.2; exact intermediate patch introduction is not established.
