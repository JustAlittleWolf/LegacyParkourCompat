The six rows are `Explosion.java`, `ExplosionDamageCalculator.java`, `EntityBasedExplosionDamageCalculator.java`, `DamageSource.java`, `EntityDamageSource.java`, and `IndirectEntityDamageSource.java`. The paired resistance and damage-source selection methods show only parameter/local-name changes. One real producer delta is present: 1.18.1 `Explosion.getSeenPercent` increments its three sample coordinates as `float`; 1.18.2 uses `double`. This can change sampled exposure and the server-computed knockback vector. The producer is excluded here; the local client's separate packet response is the distinct receiver described above. No bounded closure for these producer methods was identified in the pool reports. The adjacent packet route is not part of these 356 rows; the routing table marks `ClientboundExplodePacket.java` as still open at its receiver/application method, so this audit does not close it.
