## Still open: attached firework position feeds its follow-up hit query

`FireworkRocketEntity.tick` is a reachable direct-player movement response: while the attached entity is fall-flying, the client-side method adds

```java
look.x * 0.1 + (look.x * 1.5 - velocity.x) * 0.5
look.y * 0.1 + (look.y * 1.5 - velocity.y) * 0.5
look.z * 0.1 + (look.z * 1.5 - velocity.z) * 0.5
```

to the attached player’s velocity. That velocity formula is unchanged in all four releases (`FireworkRocketEntity.java:111–125` in 1.17.1; `:111–123` in 1.18/1.18.1; `:111–124` in 1.18.2). However, 1.18.2 adds `Entity.getHandHoldingItemAngle(Items.FIREWORK_ROCKET)` and uses its vector to place the attached rocket at the player’s hand (`Entity.java:1820–1827`; `FireworkRocketEntity.java:112–130`). The earlier versions place the attached rocket at the player origin (`FireworkRocketEntity.java:111–128`).

The changed position is consumed later in the same `tick` by `ProjectileUtil.getHitResult` (`FireworkRocketEntity.java:141–145` in 1.17.1; `:143–147` in 1.18.2). A hit then enters `onHitEntity`/`onHitBlock`; on the server the entity-hit branch explodes and discards, while the block-hit branch does so when the rocket has an explosion (`FireworkRocketEntity.java:171–195` in 1.17.1; `:173–197` in 1.18.2). Since an attached rocket supplies further player velocity on later ticks, the changed hand anchor can change the query’s hit input and whether that later boost source remains. This is a reachable movement dependency, not a claim that a particular block hit or trajectory occurs.
