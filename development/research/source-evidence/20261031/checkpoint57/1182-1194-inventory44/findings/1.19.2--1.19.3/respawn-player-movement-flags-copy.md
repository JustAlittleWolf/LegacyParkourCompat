# Respawn replacement drops prior player movement flags when entity data is not kept

1.19, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, 989–992, unconditionally assigns `$$3.getEntityData().getAll()` to the replacement player. `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `DATA_SHARED_FLAGS_ID` / `isSprinting`, 192–196 and 1984–1989; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, 2052–2082 and `isFallFlying`, 3045–3047.

1.19.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, 998–1001, retains the unconditional entity-data copy.

1.19.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, 998–1001, retains the unconditional entity-data copy. `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundRespawnPacket.java`, `shouldKeepAllPlayerData`, 106–108, gates attribute copying separately.

1.19.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, 1051–1059, copies only nondefault entity data when `shouldKeep((byte)2)` is true, while the attributes copy separately checks bit 1. `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundRespawnPacket.java`, `KEEP_ATTRIBUTES`, `KEEP_ENTITY_DATA`, and `shouldKeep`, 15–17 and 109–110. `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `DATA_SHARED_FLAGS_ID`, sprinting bit and accessor, 192–196 and 1993–1998; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, water travel and fall-flying access, 2062–2092 and 3063–3065.

Difference: For a respawn packet with the entity-data keep bit clear, 1.19, 1.19.1 and 1.19.2 transfer the old local player's full synchronized entity data to the replacement; 1.19.3 leaves the replacement's defaults. The shared entity-data byte contains movement-facing flags, including sprinting (bit 3), swimming (bit 4) and fall flying (bit 7). This claim is limited to those player movement flags and their direct travel reads, not health or visual entity data. At the stable releases inspected, the copy changes from unconditional to bit-gated between 1.19.2 and 1.19.3; the earlier introduction boundary remains unknown.

Movement meaning: With the same respawn packet (entity-data keep bit clear) and an old local player whose shared sprint flag is set, 1.19.2 retains that flag while 1.19.3's new player starts with it clear. On an ensuing underwater forward-input tick with adequate food and no sprint key pressed, the old sprint state is retained; the new player only starts its auto-sprint delay. `LivingEntity.travel` then selects water horizontal slowdown `0.9F` for the old state and `getWaterSlowDown()` (`0.8F`) for the new state, yielding different horizontal velocity and subsequent position. A retained fall-flying flag also selects the fall-flying travel branch in the older path, while the default flag selects the ordinary branch in the newer path. The packet's server-side keep-bit production and the frequency of these logical inputs are not asserted.
