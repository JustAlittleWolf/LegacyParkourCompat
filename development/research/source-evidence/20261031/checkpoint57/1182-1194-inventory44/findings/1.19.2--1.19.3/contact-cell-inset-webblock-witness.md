# Additional WebBlock witness for the contact-cell inset finding`r`n`r`nThis is a supplementary callback witness for the same `Entity.checkInsideBlocks` operation; it is not a separate finding.

1.19.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move`, 560–564 applies and clears the stored stuck multiplier; `tryCheckInsideBlocks` calls `checkInsideBlocks`, 682–688; `checkInsideBlocks`, 886–901, insets the AABB by `0.001` before scanning inclusive block coordinates and dispatching `BlockState.entityInside`; `makeStuckInBlock`, 2173–2176, stores the multiplier. `net/minecraft/world/level/block/WebBlock.java`, `entityInside`, 11–20, installs multiplier `(0.25, 0.05F, 0.25)`. `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, 348–350, registers cobweb as `WebBlock` with no collision.

1.19.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move`, 560–564; `tryCheckInsideBlocks` and `checkInsideBlocks`, 689–691 and 894–909, use an inset of `1.0E-7` before the same scan and callback dispatch; `makeStuckInBlock`, 2188–2191. `net/minecraft/world/level/block/WebBlock.java`, `entityInside`, 11–20, is unchanged. `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, 377–379, registers the same no-collision cobweb provider.

Difference: Between 1.19.2 and 1.19.3, `Entity.checkInsideBlocks` reduces the AABB scan inset from `0.001` to `1.0E-7`. The smaller inset can include a neighboring cell when the player's box overlaps that cell by more than `1.0E-7` and no more than `0.001`. Existing cobweb is non-colliding, so the player box can occupy this overlap band while moving. When scanned, `WebBlock.entityInside` calls `Entity.makeStuckInBlock`, which stores `(0.25, 0.05F, 0.25)`; the next `Entity.move` scales the requested displacement by that value and clears it. This is a direct local-player movement response. No collision-shape contact is required. The condition is the stated narrow overlap and an ensuing movement call; no claim is made about server-side datapack or custom-block producers.
