# Mounted sprint survives an underwater dismount

1.19.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep`, 771–795; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `baseTick` and `updateSwimming`, 403–430 and 1051–1059; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel`, 1464–1480; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, 2042–2060.

1.19.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` and `hasEnoughFoodToStartSprinting`, 693–727 and 1047–1049; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleSetEntityPassengersPacket`, 922–945; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `baseTick`, `updateSwimming`, and `stopRiding`, 403–430, 1056–1064, and 1784–1800; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel`, 1448–1461; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, 2052–2090.

Difference: with food at or below 6, a non-flying local player can start sprinting while an underwater passenger in 1.19.3 because the food gate accepts passengers; 1.19.2 does not. Under the same incoming dismount packet, the client clears the passenger relation without clearing sprint. On the next tick, `Entity.updateSwimming` sees the unmounted sprinting player underwater and enables swimming before `LocalPlayer.aiStep`. With the dismount shift input still down and underwater state maintained, the swimming sprint-maintenance branch leaves sprint active despite the food gate. 1.19.2 remains unsprinting and non-swimming. Movement meaning: the new state reaches on-foot `Player.travel` and water `LivingEntity.travel`, changing the swimming pitch adjustment and selecting the `0.9F` sprint damping in place of `getWaterSlowDown()`.

Applicability is conditional on the same incoming packet trace not overwriting shared sprint/swimming flags before the on-foot tick. The client source confirms the relevant direct state path; this finding does not infer server mirroring or server reply production. See `mounted-sprint-dismount-applicability.md` for detailed route, packet boundary, hashes and unresolved limit.
