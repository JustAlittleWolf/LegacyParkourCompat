# Jump-strength attribute and execution gate
1.20.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `getJumpPower`/`jumpFromGround`, lines 2000–2017 and `createLivingAttributes`, lines 280–288; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `createAttributes`, lines 204–210 and `jumpFromGround`, lines 1440–1448; `net/minecraft/world/entity/ai/attributes/Attributes.java`, `JUMP_STRENGTH`, lines 24–26.
1.20.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `getJumpPower`/`jumpFromGround`, lines 2057–2081 and `createLivingAttributes`, lines 292–305; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `createAttributes`, lines 221–230 and `jumpFromGround`, lines 1462–1470; `net/minecraft/world/entity/ai/attributes/Attributes.java`, `JUMP_STRENGTH`, lines 45–47.
Difference: 1.20.4 computes `0.42F * getBlockJumpFactor() + getJumpBoostPower()` and unconditionally writes that vertical power, then adds sprint impulse with a `0.2F` product and sets `hasImpulse`. Its `Player.createAttributes()` inherits a living-attribute builder without jump strength; its `JUMP_STRENGTH` is the horse-specific attribute (default `0.7`). In 1.20.5, `Player.createAttributes()` inherits a living-attribute builder that now includes the new generic `JUMP_STRENGTH` (default `0.42F`); `getJumpPower()` delegates to `(float)getAttributeValue(Attributes.JUMP_STRENGTH) * scale * getBlockJumpFactor() + getJumpBoostPower()`. The jump executor performs no velocity write, sprint impulse or impulse flag when power is `<= 1.0E-5F`; otherwise it writes y and adds sprint impulse using a double `0.2` product. The jump-effect addition remains `0.1F * (amplifier + 1.0F)`.
Movement meaning: A player reaching `Player.jumpFromGround` can now have block-scaled jump strength modified through the new attribute; near-zero or negative computed power no longer overwrites vertical velocity or marks an impulse. When jump power passes the executor gate, a sprinting jump also forms its horizontal impulse with different product precision. The new LocalPlayer grounded flight-enable call in `grounded-flight-toggle-jump.md` reaches this executor.
