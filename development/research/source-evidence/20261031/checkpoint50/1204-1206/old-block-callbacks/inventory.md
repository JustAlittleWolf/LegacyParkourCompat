## Reachability and dispatch checked

- `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`: `move` calls `checkInsideBlocks` after movement/collision resolution (1.20.4 line 758; 1.20.5 line 759). `checkInsideBlocks` enumerates block cells overlapped by the entity box and dispatches `BlockState.entityInside`, then `onInsideBlock` (1.20.4 lines 968–1002; 1.20.5 lines 969–1003). That is an ordinary local-player path, including client prediction. The callback wrapper remains `BlockBehaviour.BlockStateBase.entityInside` forwarding unchanged to its block (BlockBehaviour 1.20.4 lines 740–741; 1.20.5 lines 714–715).
- `Entity.move` invokes `checkFallDamage` after applying movement (1.20.4 line 646; 1.20.5 line 647). `LivingEntity.checkFallDamage` handles landing and delegates to the entity base. The shared block member bodies `stepOn`, `fallOn`, and `updateEntityAfterFallOn` in `Block.java` retain their movement/damage operations; `fallOn` damage itself is outside this assignment's accepted movement scope. Step/fall callback forwarding through `BlockBehaviour.BlockStateBase` is structurally unchanged.
- Neighbor updates / `updateShape` can change the vanilla block state and thereby future collision shape. This pass compared the changed old-block callback overrides below and the state wrapper. Placement/neighbor producers are world updates, not run every movement tick; clients receive/update vanilla states and use them for collision. The changed old-era callback gates in this slice do not add a client player movement delta.
- Piston event path: `PistonMovingBlockEntity.tick` calls `moveCollidedEntities` then `moveStuckEntities` while extending/retracting; those methods directly move intersecting entities. Compared these methods and the relevant piston block callbacks in 1.20.4 vs 1.20.5. No behavior change found; 1.20.5 and 1.20.6 files are byte-identical.

## New/modern-only callbacks and changes

The inventory has added 1.20.5 block types (including Heavy Core / Vault paths) and additional 1.21-era systems. Their newly introduced block state/callback implementations do not exist in 1.20.4 and are not old-era block behavior. They remain modern-only under the assigned fixed 1.20.4 profile and were not accepted as movement findings. The comparison covered hash-changed files under `net/minecraft/world/level/block/` and corresponding block entities, checked callback bodies for `entityInside`, `stepOn`, `fallOn`, `updateEntityAfterFallOn`, `updateShape`, `neighborChanged`, `onPlace` and `getCollisionShape`, and checked the piston response methods. The enchantment-table path rename is dispositioned above. Remaining block-file changes outside those members were not audited as a general movement-area inventory.
