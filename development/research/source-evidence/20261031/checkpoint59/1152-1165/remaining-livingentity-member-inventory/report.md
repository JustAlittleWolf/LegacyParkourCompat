- `setOnGround`/last-climbable state is read by combat tracking only, as above.
- `calculateEntityAnimation` extracts the former inline animation-speed calculation; render/animation state only. `updatingUsingItem()` now refreshes the used-stack reference before the unchanged use-tick path; it does not change the local `usingItem` movement-slowdown predicate or its tick order. - `isSensitiveToWater` and the added tail of `aiStep` route to server-side drowning damage only.
- `isDeadOrDying` and the corresponding `baseTick`/`hurt` substitutions affect death/health processing, not movement math.
- `getBoundingBoxForCulling` is culling-only. `getRelativePortalPosition` / `resetForwardDirectionOfRelativePortalPosition` are portal destination-position helpers with no ordinary local tick movement consumer.
