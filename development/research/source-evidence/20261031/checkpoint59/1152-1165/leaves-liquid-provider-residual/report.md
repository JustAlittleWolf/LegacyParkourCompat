With equal effective tag membership, an ordinary tagged leaf is rejected at both endpoints. If the queried state is not in the old endpoint's effective `BlockTags.LEAVES` membership (and, under the equal-membership premise, is also not a member at the new endpoint), its old full collision face may support while the new empty support shape cannot. A tag removal only at 1.16.5 does not establish this delta because the old tag gate already rejected the state. The exact state-to-tag assumption is material.
- **Client movement consumer check:** the direct consumer that can matter to the named player fluid-current path is `FlowingFluid.isSolidFace` (`FlowingFluid.java:103–113` in 1.16.5; `:105–115` in 1.15.2), which asks the ordinary FULL `BlockState.isFaceSturdy` predicate for non-ice, non-up faces. CENTER/RIGID support helpers and other support predicates route to block placement, state maintenance, light/render or world-generation consumers in the checked sources; no other changed fixed-state local-player movement read was found. ## LiquidBlock disposition

- **`entityInside` removal: covered movement delta, no duplicate finding.** 1.15.2 `LiquidBlock.entityInside` sets Entity's lava flag whenever the held fluid matches `FluidTags.LAVA` (`LiquidBlock.java:170–175`). It is absent in 1.16.5; `Entity` now derives lava occupancy from fluid-height scanning. The existing pool33 lava-occupancy / fluid-height closures trace the collision-pass callback and replacement scanner through `Entity.isInLava` and `LivingEntity.travel`, including the partial-height interval and applicable local-player gates. This is the known fixed-state/tag-conditional client movement delta.
- **`getCollisionShape`: new conditional shape, but local player cannot satisfy its fluid-stand predicate.** 1.16.5 adds `STABLE_SHAPE` and a collision provider returning the half-height `STABLE_SHAPE` only when collision context is above it, LEVEL is 0, and the context can stand on the fluid immediately above (`LiquidBlock.java:34, 51–57`). 1.15.2 has no such override. On the local-player route, `EntityCollisionContext.canStandOnFluid` forwards to the entity predicate; `LivingEntity.canStandOnFluid` returns false, and Player/LocalPlayer do not override it (`EntityCollisionContext.java:49–51`; `LivingEntity.java:1910–1912`). Therefore the new provider returns empty for local player collision queries even if the geometric and LEVEL gates pass. It can affect non-player entities (e.g. Strider) and is excluded. Entity.move's ordinary block-collision consumer does request the state collision shape, but the local-player gate above makes this callback no-delta for the player.
- **Other changed members:** `isRandomlyTicking`, `randomTick`, removal of `getTickDelay`, and `shouldSpreadLiquid` changes concern fluid tick scheduling, random/server fluid evolution, and lava-water/soul-soil reactions; they do not alter the response to a fixed fluid/block state during local-player movement. `getFluidState` retains the same cached level-state mapping to the held fluid. No changed friction, support, `stepOn`/`fallOn`, or other player contact callback remains. The changed fluid-flow/current vector consumer and depth/height formulas are already closed by the named pool33 fluid-flow, height, current, and occupancy reports; this residual adds only the new LiquidBlock collision gate and removed contact callback dispositions above.
