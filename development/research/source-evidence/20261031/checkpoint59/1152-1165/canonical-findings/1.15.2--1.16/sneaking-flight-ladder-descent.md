# Flying shift input does not suppress ladder descent

1.15.2 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `isSuppressingSlidingDownLadder` (inherited; lines 2686–2688), `onLadder` (1226–1248), and `handleOnClimbable` (1989–2003), called before the land `SELF` move (1890–1891); `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, flying shift input (735–748); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel` (1448–1470).

1.16 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, new `isSuppressingSlidingDownLadder` override (347–349) and flying shift input (770–782); `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `onClimbable` (1361–1393) and `handleOnClimbable` (2087–2103), called before the land `SELF` move (2062–2063); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel` (1380–1402).

Difference: In 1.15.2 the local player inherits `LivingEntity.isSuppressingSlidingDownLadder()`, which returns the shift-key state. In 1.16, LocalPlayer returns `!abilities.flying && super.isSuppressingSlidingDownLadder()`. Both climb helpers first clamp vertical motion with `max(y, -0.15)` and zero a negative result only when the feet block is not Scaffolding, the suppression predicate is true, and the entity is a Player; each helper applies that result before its `MoverType.SELF` movement. The climbability predicates differ, but both explicitly accept an open trapdoor over a ladder when their facing directions match, so this witness does not rely on tag membership. The boundary is present in the exact 1.16 source (version ID `1.16`, not `1.16.0`) and remains in 1.16.5.

Movement meaning: For a nonspectator LocalPlayer with abilities.flying, shift held, negative vertical motion, and feet at that open trapdoor-over-ladder state, the old predicate suppresses sliding and sets the downward component to zero. The new predicate does not suppress it, leaving a downward component clamped no lower than -0.15 for the move. In an unclipped ordinary dry-land travel tick (not fall-flying or passenger travel), this changes the local player's downward position movement. Player.travel later restores the flying vertical velocity from its saved pre-super Y velocity times 0.6; that velocity write occurs after the move and does not undo the position change.
