# Matching block-break acknowledgment skips the newer player correction

1.15.2 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleBlockBreakAck` forwards the same-thread response to `MultiPlayerGameMode.handleBlockBreakAck` (2354–2365); `MultiPlayerGameMode.sendBlockAction` saves a `PosAndRot` under `(BlockPos, Action)`, and `handleBlockBreakAck` (424–434) reconciles on `!allGood` and restores the saved player position and yaw/pitch with `LocalPlayer.absMoveTo` when an entry is present.
1.16 (Mojmap): same receiver forwards the packet under its same-thread guard (`ClientPacketListener.java:2403–2414`); `MultiPlayerGameMode.handleBlockBreakAck` (435–445) adds the final `currentState != acknowledgedState` conjunct to its correction predicate. This predicate form persists through 1.16.5. Boundary: 1.15.2 → 1.16.

Difference: For a present saved `(position, action)` entry, `allGood == false`, and a current client block state reference equal to the acknowledged state reference, 1.15.2 enters correction and calls `absMoveTo` with the saved position and rotation. In 1.16 the final state-difference conjunct is false, so it skips state reconciliation and the player write. Choosing a saved position different from current is sufficient to witness a position change; to witness rotation too, choose saved angles whose normalized results differ from current angles.

Movement meaning: The old callback changes the LocalPlayer position/bounding box (and, for the stated angular condition, rotation) before subsequent movement. This is a direct client receiver result. The claim does not depend on how often the server sends acknowledgments, packet production, or producer timing; it is conditional on the matching saved entry and equal state references.
