# Bed wake selects a different stand-up position

1.15.2 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BedBlock.java`, `findStandUpPosition` / `getStandingLocationAtOrBelow` (237–297), called by `LivingEntity.stopSleeping` (2815–2828). The search scans offsets in X/Z order, searches down for a support shape, adds `2.0E-7` to the support Y, and accepts the first candidate within two blocks that passes `noCollision`.
1.16.5 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BedBlock.java`, `findStandUpPosition` and non-bunk candidate order (233–244, 276–289, 338–359), called by `LivingEntity.stopSleeping` (3032–3051); `net/minecraft/world/entity/vehicle/DismountHelper.java`, `findSafeDismountLocation` (82–98); `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `getBlockFloorHeight(VoxelShape,Supplier)` (75–82). The new path orders non-bunk candidates by bed/player yaw and derives supported floor height through the helper.

Difference: For a north-facing non-bunk bed at `(0,64,0)` over a level stone floor with air in the adjacent candidate cells, the old X/Z search first accepts `(-0.5,64.0000002,-0.5)`. The new yaw-ordered search first accepts either the west center `(-0.5,64,0.5)` or east center `(1.5,64,0.5)`, depending on player yaw. Both endpoints' player boxes clear the bed and stone support. The result is a different horizontal stand-up cell and a `2.0E-7` Y difference. Attribute this to the paired bed candidate algorithms and surrounding gates, not to the new helper alone.

Movement meaning: The existing client bed-wake response sends action 2, which reaches `Player.stopSleepInBed(false,false)` and `LivingEntity.stopSleeping`; the local player commits the selected position with `setPos`. Bed exit placement therefore changes the local player's position directly. Bed-entry eligibility is outside this finding. The exact intermediate release introducing the paired change was not checked.
