# Open Shulker Boxes trigger the old local-player escape probe

1.15.2 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` probe order (648–654) and `super.aiStep()` (778), `checkInBlock` (385–429) and `blocked` (431–443); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, standing dimensions (111–114) and `freeAt` (1489–1490); `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `setPos` (315–320); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/state/BlockState.java`, `isSuffocating` dispatch (268–270); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/ShulkerBoxBlock.java`, `isSuffocating` (62–64), `getShape` (223–225); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/entity/ShulkerBoxBlockEntity.java`, `updateAnimation` (61–86), `getProgress` (271–273) and `getBoundingBox` (93–100).

1.16.5 (Mojmap): `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, four `aiStep` probes (656–661) and `super.aiStep()` (788), `moveTowardsClosestSpace` (414–441) and `suffocatesAt` (443–447); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, standing dimensions (112–115); `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `setPos` (331–334); `net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityDimensions.java`, `makeBoundingBox` (21–24); `net/minecraft/world/level/CollisionGetter.java`, `noBlockCollision` and `getBlockCollisions` (62–68); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, Shulker suffocation predicate (3224–3236); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/ShulkerBoxBlock.java`, `getShape` (218–220); `net/minecraft/world/level/block/entity/ShulkerBoxBlockEntity.java`, `updateAnimation` (61–86), `getProgress` (271–273), `getBoundingBox` (93–100) and `isClosed` (290–292); `net/minecraft/world/level/CollisionSpliterator.java`, predicate before collision-shape check (71–86).

Difference: A 1.15.2 ShulkerBox reports itself suffocating even while its block entity is open. In 1.16.5 the registered predicate accepts only a missing or closed block entity; the collision spliterator applies this predicate before checking the box shape. With an EAST-facing ShulkerBox at `(0,0,0)` whose matching block entity is stably `OPENED` at progress `1.0F`, both versions use the same extended collision box, but only the old escape probe treats the queried cell as suffocating.

Movement meaning: With a standing LocalPlayer at `(0.5,0.25,0.5)`, no initial velocity and `noPhysics=false`, all four old `aiStep` probes floor to the Shulker cell. With surrounding queried cells air, the sequential nearest-side checks write X velocity `-0.1, -0.1, +0.1, +0.1`, leaving `+0.1` before `super.aiStep`. The 1.16.5 suffocation predicate rejects the open Shulker before its intersecting shape is tested, so the four calls leave the initial X velocity unchanged. The changed local-player velocity response occurs on the pre-travel client tick; this source witness does not assert later position or trajectory parity.
