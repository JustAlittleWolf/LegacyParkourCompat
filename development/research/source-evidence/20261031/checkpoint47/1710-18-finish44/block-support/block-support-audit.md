## Finding decision

No independent movement difference is proven in this block-only support/displacement query branch. The removed base-helper downward probe is dead, and the base helper is not the local-player override. The local-player suffocation implementation is equivalent for ordinary players. The scan/predicate algorithms of `getBlockCollisions`, `isFullCube`, and `hasSolidTop` are equivalent; changes in collision shapes supplied by individual providers remain separate provider findings/dependencies. Therefore this lane has no standalone finding to draft.
