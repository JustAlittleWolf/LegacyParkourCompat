# Step solver adds a horizontal-sweep clearance candidate
1.7.10, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 494–557, raises the pre-move box by `stepHeight`, tests one candidate by clipping Y, then X and Z, descends by `-stepHeight`, and restores ordinary clipped movement when its horizontal squared distance is greater than or equal to that candidate.
1.8, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 459–538, tests two candidates against the same raised-sweep collision list. Candidate A tests upward clearance against the start box expanded by the horizontal movement, then clips X and Z. Candidate B tests upward clearance against the unexpanded start box, then clips X and Z. It selects A only when A's horizontal squared distance is strictly greater; ties select B. After descending, it restores ordinary clipped movement on a tie or loss.
Difference: 1.8's first candidate can have its lift limited by overhead collision over the horizontal sweep, then move at the resulting lower height. 1.7.10 has no such candidate. The separate step-eligibility gate and the default `stepHeight` change are not part of this finding.
Movement meaning: The ordinary local-player route reaches `Entity.move` through `LivingEntity.mobTick` → `moveRelative` → movement. For a player with width `0.6F`, feet at Y=1 and center `(x,z)=(0.69,0.5)`, put a two-layer snow block at `(1,1,0)`, a full floor at Y=0, and a full overhead block at `(1,3,0)`. Request `(dx,dy,dz)=(0.10,-0.08,0)` while grounded and not sneaking. Ordinary clipping leaves `dx=0.01` on both sides. The old single candidate is raised 0.5; the overhead block then limits its horizontal movement to 0.01, tying ordinary movement, so the final center X remains 0.70. In 1.8 candidate A is lifted only 0.2 by the horizontally swept overhead collision, reaches the full 0.10 X movement with its top exactly touching the overhead block, and descends onto the snow top at Y=1.125. Candidate B is limited to 0.01 X by the overhead block and loses to A. The resulting center X is 0.79. This source-derived witness isolates the solver candidate: a 0.5F lift still permits candidate A's 0.2F rise, while the old single-candidate solver remains blocked by overhead clearance.
