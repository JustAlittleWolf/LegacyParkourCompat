# Ordinary living-player step height increases from 0.5F to 0.6F
1.7.10, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, constructor, line 127, assigns `stepHeight = 0.5F`.
1.8, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, constructor, line 131, assigns `stepHeight = 0.6F`. The ordinary local player inherits this value; player constructors delegate to `LivingEntity`, and neither player class overwrites `stepHeight`.
Difference: The ordinary player's raised step candidate is 0.1 blocks higher in 1.8. `Entity.move` uses this field for both the step gate and the upward/downward candidate distance (old lines 494–509, 552–554; new lines 459–532).
Movement meaning: Put a bed collision box with top at Y=1.5625 in block cell `(1,1,0)`, with a full floor at Y=0. Start the ordinary local player grounded and not sneaking, feet at Y=1 and center X=0.69 (width `0.6F`); request `(dx,dy,dz)=(0.20,-0.08,0)`. The bed edge clips ordinary horizontal movement to 0.01. The old `0.5F` lift ends at Y=1.5 and remains 1/16 inside the bed, so its step candidate stays at X movement 0.01 and ties ordinary movement. The new `0.6F` lift clears the bed; it moves the full 0.20 and descends to bed top Y=1.5625. This is a grounded-walk source witness, not a runtime result, and does not rely on a jump or elevated start. The separate step-solver and sneak-offset eligibility changes are not attributed to the numeric step-height delta.
