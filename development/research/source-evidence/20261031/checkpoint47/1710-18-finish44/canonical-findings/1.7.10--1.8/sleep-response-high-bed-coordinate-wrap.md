# Sleep response wraps bed heights above 127 in the old packet
1.7.10, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/server/entity/living/player/ServerPlayerEntity.java`, `trySleep`, lines 444–453, constructs the packet after `super.trySleep` returns `OK`, sends it to tracked entities, then sends the player's position correction and the sleep packet to its own connection in that order. The correction reaches `net/minecraft/client/network/handler/ClientPlayNetworkHandler.java`, `handlePlayerMove`, lines 480–499, and clears `eyeHeightSneakOffset`. `net/minecraft/network/packet/s2c/play/PlayerSleepS2CPacket.java`, `write`, line 37, serializes Y with `writeByte`; `read`, line 29, reads it with signed `readByte`. `ClientPlayNetworkHandler.handlePlayerSleep`, lines 620–621, passes that decoded Y to `PlayerEntity.trySleep`. In `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, `isChunkLoaded(x,y,z)`, lines 236–238, rejects Y below zero. The unloaded-chunk branch in `PlayerEntity.trySleep`, lines 1068–1069, positions at `(x+0.5, y+0.9375F, z+0.5)`.
1.8, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/server/entity/living/player/ServerPlayerEntity.java`, `trySleep`, lines 495–504, sends a `PlayerSleepS2CPacket` with the successful `BlockPos`. `net/minecraft/network/packet/s2c/play/PlayerSleepS2CPacket.java`, `write`/`read`, lines 22–32, serializes/deserializes that position through `PacketByteBuf.writeBlockPos/readBlockPos`; the latter use the packed long in `PacketByteBuf.java:57–62` and `BlockPos.toLong/fromLong`, `BlockPos.java:120–128`, preserving Y=128. `ClientPlayNetworkHandler.handlePlayerSleep`, lines 684–686, passes that position to `PlayerEntity.trySleep`. `World.isChunkLoaded(BlockPos)`, lines 168–173, requires the position within Y `[0,256)` and the containing chunk loaded. For a loaded position at Y=128 it takes the bed-positioned branch at `PlayerEntity.java:1125–1127`.
Difference: A valid old-server bed at Y=128 is serialized by 1.7.10 as byte `0x80`, then decoded as `-128`. The 1.7.10 client therefore fails its Y-range chunk gate and takes its center fallback at requested Y `-128 + 0.9375F`, instead of at the real bed. 1.8's BlockPos packet preserves Y=128 and uses the loaded-bed branch. Both server methods emit the sleep packet only after successful sleep, and the client-side trySleep eligibility checks are bypassed in their respective multiplayer/client worlds. This packet-coordinate difference is separate from the ordinary low-height sleep-placement difference.
Movement meaning: With a loaded bed at `(x,128,z)` in a valid natural dimension and a successful server sleep response, the old client positions the sleeping player's box minY at `-127.26250000298023`, while the 1.8 client positions its box minY at `128.6875`. The preceding old position correction clears the sneak offset, then the sleep handler applies the wrapped Y placement. This is a source-derived reachable response path; no runtime trajectory was run.
