# Sneaking airborne landing step eligibility
1.7.10, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 470–494 (gate at 494); `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/InputClientPlayerEntity.java`, lines 151–153.
1.8, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 438–459 (gate at 459).
Difference: both require positive `stepHeight`, pre-step support (`onGround` or downward Y clipped), and horizontal clipping. The old additional condition `(var20 || eyeHeightSneakOffset < 0.05F)` is removed in 1.8. In 1.7.10 the local input sets the offset to `0.2F` while sneaking; `Entity.move` scales it by `0.4F` before the gate, so a sneaking landing move reads `0.08F`.
Movement meaning: if an ordinary local player is airborne, sneaking, descending onto support and moving horizontally into an obstacle, the shared support and horizontal-clipping conditions can be true while `var20` is false. The old gate then rejects the step because `0.08F < 0.05F` is false; 1.8 attempts step resolution. Ordinary non-sneaking movement and crouch-release ticks do not witness this gate delta. The scenario is reachable by the source gates; no trajectory was run.
