# Product reconciliation: remaining 1.16.5 → 1.17 operations

Read-only reconciliation of the seven accepted operations assigned from `.native-source/20261031/1165-1171-finish45/final-blind-audit/audit.md` and its controlling errata against both `src/main/java` and `src/client/java`. The accepted source boundary is 1.16.5 → 1.17; the audit reports the listed differences unchanged through 1.17.1. This review changes no product code and does not claim runtime parity.

## Accepted residuals and required contracts

### Short-pose, three-axis swept query: uncovered

The accepted witness is `.native-source/20261031/1165-1171-finish45/collision-query-review/20261031-short-pose-addendum.md`. Its Mojmap route is `Entity.collide` → `Entity.collideBoundingBoxHeuristically` → `CollisionGetter.getBlockCollisions` / `CollisionSpliterator.<init>` → `Shapes.create(AABB)` → Boolean-AND admission. It requires `Pose.SWIMMING`, all three SELF displacement components nonzero, and the equal conditional `MOVING_PISTON` state (`STONE`, retracting WEST, progress `0.12500004470348358F`). The old query admits the provider and clips X to `-0.1499998503953552`; the 1.17 query does not and leaves `-0.15`.

`src/main/java/me/wolfii/legacyparkourcompat/mechanic/hook/block/BlockCollisionCollectionHook.java` explicitly defines a block-stream operation and excludes entity and border shapes. `src/client/java/me/wolfii/legacyparkourcompat/mixin/collision/EntityMovementCollisionsMixin.java` redirects the block-stream calls inside `Entity.collectCollidersIgnoringWorldBorder`; `MovementRuntime.collectHistoricalMovementBlocks` operates on that stream. Those seams do not change the query shape constructed by `CollisionSpliterator`, and the `V1_13` / `V1_13_1` collectors are not this encoding boundary. No product Change for the 1.16.5 → 1.17 query-encoding response was found in either source set.

Needed neutral seam: for the LocalPlayer movement query only, expose the historical encoding of the expanded query AABB (or an equivalent historical AND-admission decision) at the `CollisionSpliterator` construction/shape-admission boundary, preserving the exact 1.16.5 `findBits`/merger behavior for this route. Keep the native collision stream, entity candidates, border handling, and all unrelated `Shapes.create` callsites intact. Gate on a movement-scoped LocalPlayer query with three nonzero movement components; do not generalize the witness to every short-pose query or piston progress.

### Static WorldBorder inner clamp: uncovered

The accepted equal-input response is `.native-source/20261031/1165-1171-finish45/worldborder-review/review.md`. Mojmap names are `WorldBorder.StaticBorderExtent.updateBox` / `getCollisionShape`, then `Entity.collide`. With the reviewed raw center `(59,999,984, 0)`, size `59,999,968`, and default absolute maximum, 1.16.5 clamps the candidate X endpoints in reverse order and `Shapes.box` sorts them into an outside strip. In 1.17+, both endpoints clamp to `29,999,984`, `Shapes.create` collapses the extent, and the collision shape becomes `INFINITY`. The admitted old shape clips the reviewed eastward SELF request; the new shape is rejected by the unchanged overlap gate. This is conditional on equal raw border/player inputs, not on ordinary server border production.

Product has `BorderCollisionAdmissionHook` and `BorderCollisionAdmission` (`src/client/.../change/v1_17_1/BorderCollisionAdmission.java`), dispatched through `EntityBorderCollisionMixin`; that changes admission based on the current box, not static shape construction. `WorldBorderCollisionHook`, `ElapsedWorldBorderCollision`, `ClientBorderLerpState`, and the same mixin's shape capture concern moving-border clock/shape samples at 1.21.10. Neither restores static reversed-endpoint geometry. The existing collector explicitly excludes world-border shapes.

Needed neutral seam: a separately resolved static-border geometry operation that can return the historical collision shape from the current raw extent/center/size values at `StaticBorderExtent.updateBox` or `getCollisionShape`, scoped to client LocalPlayer collision and the 1.16.5 endpoint-ordering behavior. Preserve native geometry for the ordinary case and native moving-border operation; do not route this through block collision collection or broaden it to moving borders.

### Tiny residual ShulkerBox lid: uncovered; direct one-axis scan

The controlling source route is `final-blind-audit/audit.erratum-1.md`, with the accepted geometry and equal-progress calculation in `shulkerbox-progress-independent-review/verdict.md` and the response correction in `shulkerbox-dynamic-collision-pair/report.erratum-1.md`. The one-component SELF request bypasses `CollisionSpliterator` and its Boolean-AND block stream.

The reachable equal progress `1.4901161193847656e-8F` leaves a tiny outward lid strip for WEST/DOWN/NORTH in 1.16.5; 1.17 and 1.17.1 encode the provider as `Shapes.block()`. The accepted standing LocalPlayer witness uses WEST, progress above, and X-only SELF displacement `0.049999999`, producing old clipped `0.0499999925494194` versus new unchanged displacement. No ShulkerBox shape Change, direct provider override, or relevant block-shape hook was found in `src/main/java` or `src/client/java`. The broad block-collision collector cannot cover this one-axis path.

Needed neutral seam: movement-scoped historical provider shape for this LocalPlayer block-collision lookup, keyed by current ShulkerBox state/facing/progress and collision query; for the accepted case restore the exact old AABB-backed outward strip before axis clipping. Gate to the eligible client movement shape read and the reachable tiny residual/negative-facing conditions. Do not alter block state, rendering, or unrelated ShulkerBox queries.

### Shulker entity opening push: uncovered

The accepted review is `.native-source/20261031/1165-1171-finish45/shulker-response-review/review.md`; the direct-writer audit is `core-writer-residual/closure.md`. Mojmap aliases are 1.16.5 `Shulker.tick` and 1.17/1.17.1 `Shulker.updatePeekAmount`, `getPhysicalPeek`, and `onPeekAmountChange`. On the first normal opening update from raw target 100, the LocalPlayer is on foot, non-spectating, not `noPhysics`, and in the overlap shared by both queries. The old path computes the physical peek difference in double; the new path subtracts float physical-peek values. The resulting `MoverType.SHULKER` vector passed to `Entity.move` differs before the separate `Entity.move` writer. Both source effects are accepted: the query membership changes as well as the arithmetic, although the numeric witness isolates the displacement arithmetic.

`ShulkerPeekPushQueryHook`, `ShulkerMixin`, and `ShulkerPeekPushQuery` only restore a later origin-corner candidate-bounds operation. The Change is annotated `V1_21_2`; the mixin falls back to native bounds for selected profiles older than `V1_20_5`. It does not change the accepted 1.16.5 physical-peek arithmetic or the old full-body candidate query. No compatible 1.16.5 → 1.17 displacement Change was found.

Needed neutral seam(s): resolve the old push magnitude/vector immediately before the LocalPlayer `Entity.move(MoverType.SHULKER, ...)`, using the same synchronized peek and attachment data and preserving float/double operation order; separately preserve the old candidate query/selector membership when implementing the full accepted response. Scope both to the client-side Shulker callback and LocalPlayer candidate. Keep the newer `V1_21_2` query-bounds behavior independently resolved, and do not merge this source operation with the downstream Entity position writer.

## Covered accepted operation

### `Entity.push(Entity)` square-root precision: covered

`core-writer-residual/closure.md` accepts the LocalPlayer impulse difference for an eligible nearby pushable entity, with `noPhysics` / same-vehicle checks passed and `absMax(dx,dz) >= 0.01`. Product coverage is direct: `src/client/java/me/wolfii/legacyparkourcompat/change/v1_16_2/PlayerPushNormalization.java` is annotated `V1_16_2`, calculates `Mth.absMax` and rounds `Math.sqrt` through `float` before widening. `src/client/java/me/wolfii/legacyparkourcompat/mixin/EntityMixin.java` wraps both recipient `push(DDD)` arguments in `Entity.push(Entity)` and invokes the hook only when that recipient is a `Player`; native outer predicates remain in the original call. `PlayerPushNormalizationHook` defines the independent recipient-vector operation.

This correctly covers 1.16.5 through the `V1_16_2` release group and preserves the 1.17 native double behavior for later selected profiles. No new candidate is needed for this operation. This is an implementation-coverage disposition only, not a runtime-parity claim.

## Local-ID yaw interpolation: uncovered at 1.16.5

The accepted conditional receiver finding is `.native-source/20261031/1165-1171-finish45/local-id-interpolation-review/review.md`. Mojmap receiver aliases are `ClientPacketListener.handleMoveEntity` / `handleTeleportEntity` → inherited `LivingEntity.lerpTo` → `LivingEntity.aiStep` interpolation → `Entity.getInputVector` / `Mth.sin`. Under the accepted identical inbound relative rotation packet and equal on-foot LocalPlayer state, 1.16.5 divides the wrapped yaw difference in double and casts after addition; 1.17/1.17.1 cast the difference to float before division/addition. The one-ULP sample can change the sine-table index and horizontal input addition. The reviewed normal tracking sender excludes its own `ServerPlayer`; this finding does not claim ordinary self-tracking traffic.

There is packet queue infrastructure: `ClientReceivedInterpolationHook`, `ClientPacketListenerMovementMixin`, `LivingEntityPacketInterpolationMixin`, and `QueuedEntityPacketInterpolation.advance()` reproduce the 1.16 double form. But `QueuedEntityPacketInterpolation` is `@MovementChange(V1_16)`, while `ParkourVersion.V1_16_2` contains 1.16.2–1.16.5. Per the resolver's closest-change-from-selected-or-later rule, that earlier Change is not selected for the accepted 1.16.5 profile. The next applicable interpolation Change is `LocalPlayerPacketInterpolationBarrier` at `V1_18_2`; it reports `handlesLegacyPackets=false`, `queue=false`, and `acceptsServerMovement=false`, leaving current packet interpolation in place. The 1.19.3 Change likewise addresses receiver authority and does not restore the 1.16.5-vs-1.17 interpolation arithmetic. Thus nearby infrastructure is not coverage of this operation at the accepted profile.

Needed neutral seam: a separately resolved local-player interpolation-sample operation at the actual inherited living interpolation step, restoring the 1.16.5 wrapped-difference double divide and final cast for the 1.16.5 profile. Keep the 1.17 float operation native. Gate to the accepted on-foot local receiver and identical accepted packet route; do not change packet production or claim that vanilla tracking sends this packet to the local player's own connection.
