# ClientPacketListener local-player response audit

Scope: exact Mojmap `ClientPacketListener` handler roster across 1.16.5 → 1.17 → 1.17.1, with direct client-side delegates and local-player tick consumers. This report closes the bounded handler census, not the overall pair's full source freeze. Hash binding: `source-hashes.tsv` verifies the three listener sources against the authoritative publication manifests/sidecars; `dependency-hashes.tsv` binds exact dependent source files to those manifests. Each ledger has a `.sha256` sidecar. Handler membership and body comparisons are in `clientpacket-handler-roster.txt` and `handler-body-diff-index.tsv`, each with its own `.sha256`.

## Paired no-difference direct response routes

The following local-player-directed handler/delegate paths were screened in the frozen roster and have no movement-relevant response difference on either boundary under equal raw logical inputs: `handleSetEntityMotion` → `Entity.lerpMotion`/`setDeltaMovement`; `handleMovePlayer` position correction and on-foot relative resolution (vehicle dismount difference is vehicle-only); `handleExplosion` knockback addition; `handlePlayerAbilities` (getter/API substitutions only); `handleUpdateAttributes`; `handleSetEquipment`; `handleUpdateMobEffect` and `handleRemoveMobEffect` (new effect-source argument in 1.17, client attribute mutation remains server-gated); `handleSetHealth`/FoodData raw values; `handleGameEvent` current game-mode ability state; `handleAnimate` wake path; login/respawn reset for built-in 1.17 dimensions. Direct body and dependency classifications are recorded in `handler-body-diff-index.tsv` and `dependency-hashes.tsv`.

## Excluded routes and limits

Handlers that only write world/block/chunk/tag state or nonplayer entities are excluded under equal raw world/state inputs; vehicle movement and damage feedback are outside this lane. `handleSetEntityData` is retained above only for its direct local-player pose callback. The body index contains every declared handler (89 in 1.16.5; 97 in 1.17 and 1.17.1), but this report is limited to client packet response consumers. It does not claim that the broader source pair or provider/callback inventory is complete. No builds, tests, game launches, or runtime trajectory checks were run.
