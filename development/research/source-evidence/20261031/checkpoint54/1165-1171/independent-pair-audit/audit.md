1. Client input snapshot and movement intent: `Input`, `KeyboardInput`, `LocalPlayer` input sampling and key state.
2. Per-tick player entry and pre-travel gates/state: `LocalPlayer.tick`, `aiStep`, `LivingEntity` tick/ai step, abilities, sprint/sneak/spectator/sleeping/riding/swimming/pose state, effect/equipment-derived movement attributes.
3. Jump decision and impulse: local jump input, `LivingEntity` jump methods/power/boosts, auto-jump and fluid/horse-specific jump paths; check 1.17 rideable jump boundary.

5. Movement realization: relative acceleration, collision clipping and step-up, piston/entity collision response, position and bounding-box writes, velocity clipping/reset, fall distance/on-ground/horizontal collision flags, block effects.
6. Post-travel and next-tick state: landing/fall response gates (movement state only), sprint/swim/pose refresh, friction/speed-factor reads, fluid-height state, block callbacks, input/key synchronization.
7. Client received-response paths: direct player position/velocity/pose/state effects from client packets, including teleport/move/motion/entity-event/block updates and their player-targeted branches.
8. Reachable external responses: piston movement/pushes and other direct entity/player responses, world-border/collision queries, block collision shapes/callbacks, fluids, climbables, powder snow, bubble columns, honey/slime/web/soul sand/ice and other movement-affecting existing blocks; distinguish producer behavior from direct player response.
