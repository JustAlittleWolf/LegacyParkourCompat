## Jump input and ordinary local-player route

`KeyboardInput.tick(boolean)` reads `options.keyJump.isDown()` into `jumping` in all three versions (1.16.5 `KeyboardInput.java:13–26`; 1.17 and 1.17.1 `:14–27`). Its method body hash matches in all three releases. `Input.java` is byte-identical. The local player's option copy and jump snapshot remain in the tick path: `LocalPlayer.tick()` copies `options.autoJump` to `autoJumpEnabled` (1.16.5 `:269`; 1.17/1.17.1 `:277`), and `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` snapshots `input.jumping` (1.16.5 `:634`; 1.17 `:658`; 1.17.1 `:656`). The ordinary player jump dispatch then delegates through `LivingEntity.aiStep`; its grounded/fluid gates and jump write are owned by the ordinary-tick source inventory, not repeated as a separate candidate here.

`LocalPlayer.aiStep()` has file-level differences, but the compared ordinary jump/input and rideable charge sequence keeps its branch order and operations. The source-level refactors in this body are direct `Abilities` field accesses to `getAbilities()` and the Elytra item identity check from `getItem() == Items.ELYTRA` to `ItemStack.is(Items.ELYTRA)`. The 1.17 and 1.17.1 helper is `return this.getItem() == item` (`ItemStack.java:194–196` / `:192–194`), so these are equivalent reads/checks, not changed jump gates. No jump-control difference is established at either adjacent boundary.

## Auto-jump

The direct LocalPlayer movement hook remains `move(MoverType, Vec3)`: save X/Z, call `super.move`, then pass the actual resulting X/Z displacement to `updateAutoJump` (1.16.5 `LocalPlayer.java:863–868`; 1.17 `:895–900`; 1.17.1 `:893–898`). The auto-jump option, timer, grounded/non-passenger/moving/jump-factor gates remain the same (`canAutoJump`: 1.16.5 `:974–982`; 1.17 `:1006–1014`; 1.17.1 `:1004–1012`). The geometry/query and thresholds in `updateAutoJump` are unchanged. Its only operation-level source edits at 1.17 are `this.yRot` to `this.getYRot()` in the fallback input-vector sine/cosine calculations (old `:885–886`; 1.17 `:917–918`; 1.17.1 `:915–916`). The 1.17 and 1.17.1 `Entity.getYRot()` is exactly `return this.yRot` (`Entity.java:3005–3007` and `:3007–3009`); no Player or LocalPlayer override was found. The direction value and float operation order are therefore unchanged. When a qualifying obstacle sets `autoJumpTime = 1`, the next LocalPlayer AI step raises `input.jumping` as before. This closes the auto-jump candidate as a paired no-difference in behavior.

## Rideable jump boundary and disposition

`LocalPlayer.isRidingJumpable()` uses the same `isPassenger() && vehicle instanceof PlayerRideableJumping && canJump()` gate in all three versions (1.16.5 `:542–545`; 1.17 `:549–552`; 1.17.1 `:547–550`). `getJumpRidingScale()`, `sendRidingJump()`, and the LocalPlayer rideable charge/release sequence in `aiStep()` have matching method-body hashes / operation sequence (`isRidingJumpable`: 1.16.5 `:542–545`, 1.17 `:549–552`, 1.17.1 `:547–550`; charge block: 1.16.5 `:760–785`, 1.17 `:784–810`, 1.17.1 `:782–808`). On release, LocalPlayer calls the vehicle's `onPlayerJump(floor(scale * 100))` and sends the riding-jump command. The sequence does not write the player's position or velocity.

At the 1.17 boundary, `PlayerRideableJumping` adds `extends PlayerRideable`; the new `PlayerRideable` is an empty marker interface. Its methods remain `onPlayerJump`, `canJump`, `handleStartJump`, and `handleStopJump`. In the vanilla source tree, `AbstractHorse` is the implementing family. The compared `onPlayerJump`, `canJump`, `handleStartJump`, and `handleStopJump` method-body hashes match across all three releases. These callbacks are vehicle jump state/physics, outside the assigned player-only movement scope. The LocalPlayer callback/input protocol is unchanged and produces no accepted local-player movement delta.
