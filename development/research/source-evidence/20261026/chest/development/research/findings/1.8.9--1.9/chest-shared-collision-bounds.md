# Chest collision consumes shared stale bounds
1.8.9, Ornithe Feather gen2 build.2: `net/minecraft/block/ChestBlock.java`, constructor and updateShape, lines 32–68; `block/Block.java`, setShape and collision methods, 292–299 and 339–350; `world/World.java`, getCollisions, 891–925.
1.9, Ornithe Feather gen2 build.2: `net/minecraft/block/ChestBlock.java`, constants and getShape, 31–73; `block/Block.java`, addCollisions/getCollisionShape, 313–327; `block/state/StateDefinition.java`, 348–359; `world/World.java`, getCollisions, 893–927.
Difference: 1.8.9 stores the chest's six shape coordinates on its singleton Block instance. updateShape chooses north, south, west, east, or single in that order, but ordinary collision queries inherit Block.addCollisions/getCollisionShape, which read those fields without refreshing them for the queried position. In 1.9 the same coordinates and neighbor priority are returned for the queried position through getShape on every collision query. Normal and trapped chest have separate singleton instances.
Movement meaning: the 1.8.9 client tick's pick can leave the geometry of one chest active while ordinary player movement collides with another chest of the same type. For example, looking at a north-connected double chest writes minZ=0; an isolated same-type chest subsequently has a collision north boundary of 0 instead of 1/16. This changes positive-Z displacement when the player's swept box reaches that strip. The replacement remains present in exact 1.9.4. The introduction is bounded by the inspected endpoints; intermediate development releases were not inspected.
