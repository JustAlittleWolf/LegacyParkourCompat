Difference: The 1.15.2 pose-fit query uses the dimension-derived candidate AABB directly; 1.16 shrinks all six faces by 1.0E-7 first. This is present in each verified exact source 1.16, 1.16.1, 1.16.2 and 1.16.5. The unchanged player pose-selection sequence first checks swimming fit, then the requested pose, then crouching, then swimming fallback. Default player dimensions come directly from POSES: width 0.6F, standing height 1.8F, crouching height 1.5F, swimming height 0.6F; no scale multiplication occurs in the override.
Movement meaning: Ordinary crouching can fit under a top slab whose lower face is y=1.5. At its horizontal edge, a standing candidate can overlap the slab by more than 1.0E-7 but less than 2.0E-7. The old shape merger retains this overlap; the inset reduces it below the merger tolerance and lets the new player stand on shift release. This is not a generic strict-AABB shell argument: 1.15.2 uses Shapes.joinIsNotEmpty for full cubes too, and its IndirectMerger coalesces coordinates within 1.0E-7. Consequently the previous candidate minX=0.9999999480790711 against a full cube is not evidence of an endpoint difference.
A source-arithmetic witness starts horizontally stationary, grounded and crouched at x=z=0.5, y=0 on a continuous ordinary 0.6F-friction floor. Put top slabs in block layer y=1, x=0, covering the traversed z interval; leave x>=1 open. Keep default movement speed 0.1F, no effects, no fluids, no flight, no entities, no jumping; hold forward at yaw -21.4453125F for 35 ticks. Hold shift for ticks 1–34 and release it on tick 35. Both clients determine slowdown before refreshing keyboard input, so tick 35 still uses the crouch-scaled input. Keyboard input is float(0.3), LivingEntity multiplies by 0.98F, yielding forward impulse 0.2940000295639038. Ground acceleration speed is 0.10000000149011612; Mth's sine table index is 61633, sine -0.3655237555503845. Therefore x acceleration is 0.01074639965394605, and friction is float(0.6F*0.91F)=0.546000063419342. Repeated AABB translation and midpoint reconstruction give x=1.2999998924227154 and the standing candidate minX=0.9999998805017865 at tick 35. Its slab overlap is 1.194982135466205E-7. After deflation the overlap is approximately 1.94982136E-8; IndirectMerger merges that boundary. Swimming and crouching fit in both versions; old requested standing fails and falls back to crouching, while the inset query accepts standing. The crouching movement box does not collide with the slab: its top is exactly y=1.5 and horizontal collision slices inset orthogonal axes by 1.0E-7.
