# Received effect amplifier sign

1.20.4, official Mojang client mappings: `1.20.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundUpdateMobEffectPacket.java`, constructor/write/getEffectAmplifier, lines 23–26, 45–60, 76–78; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleUpdateMobEffect`, lines 1501–1517; `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, constructor, lines 60–71; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `forceAddEffect`, lines 975–983, and `travel`, lines 2035–2042, 2139–2160.

1.20.5 and 1.20.6, official Mojang client mappings: the corresponding files under each release's `mojmap/` are byte-identical between these releases. `ClientboundUpdateMobEffectPacket.java`, constructor/read/write/getEffectAmplifier, lines 28–31, 52–66, 85–87; `ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleUpdateMobEffect`, lines 1534–1546; `MobEffectInstance.java`, constructor, lines 78–86; `LivingEntity.java`, `forceAddEffect`, lines 999–1008, and `travel`, lines 2104–2111, 2208–2227.

Difference: 1.20.4 writes the amplifier as a byte, reads it with `readByte()`, returns that signed byte, and passes it directly to a constructor that stores the integer unchanged. Amplifiers 128–255 therefore become -128–-1 in the client's active effect. In 1.20.5 and 1.20.6, the packet stores and transports a VarInt, and the receiving instance constructor clamps to 0–255. `forceAddEffect` stores that instance in both versions. This is an exact 1.20.5 cutover; 1.20.4 and 1.20.5 are adjacent stable releases.

Movement meaning: an existing levitation effect with server amplifier 128 is a reachable vanilla state: 1.20.4 `server/commands/EffectCommands.java`, lines 88–107, permits amplifiers 0–255, and `server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `onEffectAdded`, lines 1274–1282, sends the effect to that player's client (1.20.5/1.20.6 lines 1348–1356). For the locally controlled player outside fluids and fall-flying, travel reads the received amplifier in the unchanged expression `vy += (0.05 * (amplifier + 1) - vy) * 0.2`, then applies the float constant `0.98F` to vertical velocity. With post-move `vy = 0`, amplifier 128 produces `(-6.35 * 0.2) * (double)0.98F` in 1.20.4 and `(6.45 * 0.2) * (double)0.98F` in 1.20.5/1.20.6: downward versus upward velocity for the following movement tick. The effect-production rules are supporting reachability evidence; the movement difference is how the client reads existing effect state.
