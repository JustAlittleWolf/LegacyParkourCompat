The older height is `state.getLevel() / 9.0F`. Exact 1.14 adds world/position context: height is `1.0F` when the selected fluid accepts the above-cell fluid, otherwise the old division. Water accepts source and flowing water. The new guarded helper also checks the eye cell's chunk when requested by the player cache updater. This gate passes for the loaded ordinary local player: its enclosing tick already checks the same X/Z chunk (`LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, 1.13.2 lines 181–183; 1.14 lines 178–180).

Movement meaning: take a received loaded vanilla chunk containing `WATER[LEVEL=1]` at the eye cell, water directly above, and water through the player's body below. Both releases map block `LEVEL=1` to nonfalling flowing-fluid level 7 (`LiquidBlock.java`, 1.13.2 lines 39–49, 68–70; 1.14 lines 35–46, 65–67). Let the ordinary unmounted player's eye fraction be 0.9. The old predicate is false because `7 / 9.0F + 0.11111111F` is about 0.8889; exact 1.14's contextual predicate is true because `1.0F + 0.11111111F` is about 1.1111. The comparison stays strict and the addition remains float arithmetic before addition to the integer block Y.
