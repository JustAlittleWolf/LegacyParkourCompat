# Submerged sprint activation accepts reduced forward input

1.14, Ornithe Feather `1.14+build.2`: `1.14/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick`, lines 625-679; `KeyboardInput.java`, `tick`, lines 14-27.
1.14.4, Ornithe Feather `1.14.4+build.2`: `1.14.4/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick`, lines 625-677, and `m_03985577`, lines 961-964; `Input.java`, `m_49149051`, lines 22-24.

Difference: The previous-input snapshot and both sprint-start gates use `movementForward >= 0.8F` in 1.14. In 1.14.4 they use a helper: when submerged in water, forward input must exceed `1.0E-5F`; otherwise the float input is compared against double `0.8`. The snapshot stays before `input.tick`, and the two start decisions stay after keyboard refresh and item-use reduction. The sprint-key path still requires sufficient food or flight permission, no item use, no blindness, and either no water contact or submersion.

Movement meaning: A non-flying, non-spectator player submerged in water, holding forward and sneak, produces `(float)(1.0F * 0.3)` forward input in the unchanged keyboard producer. With the sprint key held, adequate food, no item use or blindness, this is below the old start threshold and above the new submerged threshold: the new client sets sprinting while the old client does not. This is a state change with traced movement consumers: `LivingEntity.setSprinting` updates the movement-speed modifier, and water travel selects `0.9F` drag while sprinting rather than its base multiplier (`LivingEntity.java`, lines 1640-1649 and 1882-1926 in 1.14.4). `Entity.updateSwimming` also consumes sprinting on its subsequent base tick (lines 938-953). No trajectory is asserted from the gate alone.

The first stable release with the new operation is 1.14.2. Exact intermediate sources show 1.14.1 Yarn `1.14.1+build.10`, `net/minecraft/client/network/ClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tickMovement`, lines 613-677, retaining the old comparisons; 1.14.2 Yarn `1.14.2+build.7`, the same file/member, lines 613-676 and `method_20623`, lines 949-952, has the new helper and calls. 1.14.3 Yarn `1.14.3+build.13` retains them. Yarn `Entity.isInWater` in these sources means the Feather submerged predicate: it returns the eye-submersion field AND water contact (`1.14.2/yarn/net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 887-901), not mere contact.
