# Sprint retention accepts reduced positive forward input

1.14, Ornithe Feather `1.14+build.2`: `1.14/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick`, lines 625-689.
1.14.4, Ornithe Feather `1.14.4+build.2`: `1.14.4/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick`, lines 625-688; `Input.java`, `m_49149051`, lines 22-24; `KeyboardInput.java`, `tick`, lines 14-27.

Difference: After keyboard refresh and item-use reduction, the old sprint-stop decision treats `movementForward < 0.8F || !foodEligible` as insufficient. The new decision uses `!(movementForward > 1.0E-5F) || !foodEligible`. The enclosing swimming versus non-swimming branches, horizontal-collision test, water-surface test and stop-call order remain identical. The sprint-start gates still reject item use.

Movement meaning: Start with an already sprinting ordinary player on dry ground, holding forward, eligible food, no horizontal collision, no riding, and beginning a sustained item use such as using a shield. The unchanged producer supplies forward `1.0F`, then the unchanged item gate multiplies it by `0.2F` before sprint retention. The old client calls `setSprinting(false)`; the new client retains sprinting because `0.2F > 1.0E-5F`. This changes a concrete movement consumer: `LivingEntity.setSprinting` removes/adds the `0.3F` multiply-total movement-speed modifier (`LivingEntity.java`, lines 113-116 and 1640-1649 in 1.14.4), which the player reads into speed after inherited movement (`PlayerEntity.java`, lines 509-520). The refreshed input is copied into forward speed by `LocalClientPlayerEntity.serverTickAi`, then scaled and passed to travel in `LivingEntity.mobTick` (lines 600-606 and 2197-2227 respectively). Thus the retained state reaches movement; this finding does not claim a measured trajectory or parity result.

The first stable release with the new retention condition is 1.14.2. Exact 1.14.1 Yarn `1.14.1+build.10`, `net/minecraft/client/network/ClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tickMovement`, lines 667-677, has the old comparison. Exact 1.14.2 Yarn `1.14.2+build.7`, the same file/member, lines 667-676, and `net/minecraft/client/input/Input.java`, lines 22-24, has the new positive-forward predicate; 1.14.3 Yarn `1.14.3+build.13` retains it.
