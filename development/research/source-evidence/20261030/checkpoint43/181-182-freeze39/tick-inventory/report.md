## Ordered inventory and paired evidence

1. **Client tick dispatch.** `net/minecraft/client/Minecraft.java`: 1.8.1 `tick()` calls `world.tickEntities()` at 1599 and `world.tick()` at 1615; 1.8.2 calls them at 1634 and 1650. Both calls are in the unpaused-world path. `net/minecraft/client/world/ClientWorld.java:60–81` in both versions runs `super.tick()`, then its chunk-cache tick and `tickChunks()`; `tickChunks()` delegates to `super.tickChunks()` at 94–95.

2. **Entity tick terminal.** `net/minecraft/world/World.java`: 1.8.1 `tickEntities()` starts at 1141, iterates global entities (`Entity.tick()` at 1150), then regular entities through `tickEntity(entity)` at 1201, then ticking block entities through `Tickable.tick()` at 1236. 1.8.2 starts at 1159, calls global `Entity.tick()` at 1168, regular `tickEntity(entity)` at 1219, and ticking block-entity `Tickable.tick()` at 1254. The paired `tickEntity(Entity, boolean)` slices are 1312–1396 and 1330–1395: under `requireLoaded && entity.inChunk`, they increment ticks and select `rideTick()` when mounted or `tick()` otherwise. The local-player wrapper is `client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:111–120` (1.8.1) and `:105–114` (1.8.2); it gates `super.tick()` on the player chunk being loaded, then sends movement/input state. Full player and living movement bodies were not re-audited here.

3. **World/chunk terminal.** `net/minecraft/world/World.java`: `tick()` delegates to weather (`1748–49` / `1768–69`); `tickChunks()` delegates to `purgeTickingChunks()` (`1881–82` / `1901–02`). The paired `purgeTickingChunks()` bodies (`1819–52` / `1839–72`) rebuild the ticking-chunk set, decrement ambience cooldown and run a player-nearby light check. `tickAmbienceAndLight()` only chooses the ambient-cave-sound path when `!isClient` and checks border light (`1856–79` / `1876–99`). `ClientWorld.tickChunks()` adds client chunk-cache ticking/ambience setup (`ClientWorld.java:94–116`, both versions). This is not a client scheduled/random-block-tick terminal. Server-side scheduled block ticks were not audited as player movement.

4. **Direct block collision callbacks.** `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`: after collision resolution, `move()` invokes the grounded `onSteppedOn` path (1.8.1 `:579–589`; 1.8.2 `:575–585`) and then `checkBlockCollisions()` (1.8.1 `:611–618`, callback dispatch at `:652–670`; 1.8.2 `:607–614`, dispatch at `:648–666`). The default provider methods are in `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`: `onSteppedOn` (`:542` / `:582`), `onEntityCollision` (`:607` / `:647`), and `beforeCollision` (`:690` / `:734`). The direct velocity-changing provider sinks inspected were `SlimeBlock.beforeCollision` (`:33`, both), `SlimeBlock.onSteppedOn` (`:42`, both), and `SoulSandBlock.onEntityCollision` (`:25`, both); their checked operations match, with only local-variable renaming in the slime step callback and soul-sand shape local. `CobwebBlock.java` is byte-identical across the pair. `MovingBlockEntity.moveEntities()` at `:80` and `tick()` at `:128` in both versions are a separate direct player-response terminal: its checked excerpt (`:87–108`) selects intersecting entities, writes the facing-axis velocity for extending slime blocks, or calls `entity.move(amount * facing offsets)`. The displayed pair diff changes generic type annotations and local names, not these operations.

5. **Piston movement event path.** Client network handler `handleBlockEvent()` is at `client/network/handler/ClientPlayNetworkHandler.java:993` / `:992`, and dispatches `world.doEvent(...)` at `:1091` / `:1093`; `World` resolves the block and invokes `block.doEvent(...)` at `:2460` / `:2488`. `MovingBlockEntity.tick()` above is the direct entity-push endpoint. A separate source difference was observed in `block/PistonBaseBlock.java:83–103`: `shouldExtend()` tests `world.hasSignal(pos, Direction.NORTH)` in 1.8.1 (`:90`) and `Direction.DOWN` in 1.8.2 (`:90`). `checkExtended()` is gated by `!world.isClient` at `:60` in both; the difference therefore changes the server-side piston trigger predicate and does not, by itself, establish a client player-movement delta. **Disposition/ownership remains open; do not treat this as an accepted movement finding from this audit.** The later client block event and moving-block callback remain the relevant direct-response path.

6. **Out-of-tick client packet inputs.** `client/network/handler/ClientPlayNetworkHandler.java` was paired at these operation sinks: `handleEntityVelocity` (`:412` / `:402`) resolves the entity and lerps each component divided by `8000.0`; `handlePlayerMove` (`:525` / `:517`) adds relative axes/angles, zeroes absolute-axis velocity, updates player position/angles, sends a correction and initializes first-update fields; `handleExplosion` (`:824` / `:821`) damages blocks before adding packet velocity to player XYZ; `handlePlayerAbilities` (`:1267` / `:1271`) sets flying/creative/invulnerable/can-fly and fly/walk speeds. The checked operation order/gates match; mapped argument/nested-class names and the player local type differ. Entry points for `handleBlocksUpdate`, `handleBlockUpdate`, `handleBlockEvent`, status-effect add/remove, and entity attributes are present at `:571/:568`, `:602/:599`, `:993/:992`, `:1131/:1133`, `:1219/:1223`, and `:1495/:1581`; these were inventoried but not fully compared through packet payload and state-application dependencies.
