- **Status add, upgrade, remove, and initial state.** `ServerPlayerEntity.onStatusEffectApplied`, `onStatusEffectUpgraded`, and `onStatusEffectRemoved` send the corresponding add/remove packets to that player's connection after `super`; both releases preserve this order and packet arguments. `PlayerManager` sends the player's current effects on login and respawn. `TrackedEntity.updatePlayer` sends current effects after an entity is added to that observer's tracked set. The client sink's resolved-entity/main-thread gate and its `addStatusEffect`/`removeEffect` writes were paired in the allowed sink report. The packet constructor retains effect/amplifier byte masks, duration saturation at 32767, and particle flag; read/write field order and widths match: entity VarInt, effect byte, amplifier byte, duration VarInt, flags byte. Remove packet remains entity VarInt plus effect byte. No producer, gate, payload, or codec delta found in these routes.
- **Tracked attributes and equipment.** `TrackedEntity.tick` sends dirty tracked attributes from `sendDataAndAttributes` and clears the tracked set after sending; when a viewer begins tracking a living entity it sends nonempty trackable attributes. Its `syncVelocity` gate and packet ordering remain as in 1.8.1. `LivingEntity`'s equipment-change loop sends a slot/item packet when the copied prior equipment differs by `ItemStack.matches`; `TrackedEntity.updatePlayer` sends non-null equipment slots 0–4 after spawn/attributes/velocity and before status effects. Paired packet constructors/codecs preserve base double, modifier UUID/amount/operation, entity ID, slot short and item-stack encoding/order. These producer checks and ordering are semantically unchanged in the checked bodies. Client-side attribute/equipment entity lookup and state writes were covered by the permitted sink report.
- **Abilities.** `PlayerManager` sends `PlayerAbilitiesS2CPacket` during player setup; `ServerPlayerEntity.sendAbilitiesUpdate` sends it on the established update path. Packet construction still copies invulnerability, flying, allow-flying, creative-mode, fly speed and walk speed; the flag byte bit assignments and two trailing floats are unchanged. - **Velocity.** `TrackedEntity.tick` sends velocity under the same `syncVelocity` branch, using the same squared-difference threshold (`0.02²`) or nonzero-to-zero transition condition and then updates its cached components. The named producer gates/order, conversion and codec show no delta.

## Adjacent source delta; scope disposition

`TrackedEntity.tick`'s non-player entity movement packet selection differs in 1.8.2. The 1.8.1 branch falls back to teleport if any relative coordinate delta is outside `[-128,128)`, the no-vehicle timer exceeds 400, riding is set, or on-ground changed; otherwise it independently chooses position+angles, position, angles, or no packet from the threshold booleans. The 1.8.2 code expresses the fallback as the positive in-range/conjunction gate and, inside it, emits position+angles when both thresholds are met **or when the tracked entity is an `ArrowEntity`**; otherwise it chooses position or angles. Thus on an in-range arrow update with neither threshold met, the newer producer emits a position+angles packet instead of no movement packet. This concerns tracked non-player entity movement synchronization, not one of the assigned player-state producer routes above; no claim is made here about downstream player parity or whether an arrow's client-side movement/collision can change a player's response. It is a bounded adjacent delta for the master to route if remote-entity collision responses are within the remaining source inventory.
