# Local auto-jump queues a jump after a detected step

1.9.4, Ornithe Feather Gen2: `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick` lines 603–771; no local-player `move` override, auto-jump detector/countdown, or synthetic-jump check exists. `net/minecraft/client/options/GameOptions.java` and `net/minecraft/client/gui/screen/menu/options/ControlsOptionsScreen.java` have no auto-jump option. `Input.java` has no movement-vector accessor.

1.10, 1.10.1, and 1.10.2, Ornithe Feather Gen2: `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, cached setting copy at line 228, countdown consumption at lines 672–677, flight double-tap guard at lines 713–727, and new `move`/`autoJump` methods at lines 812–923; `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/Input.java`, new `getMovement` lines 18–20; `net/minecraft/client/options/GameOptions.java`, enabled-by-default option at line 108 and setting/load/save logic at lines 396–398, 469–470, 830–831, and 948; `net/minecraft/client/gui/screen/menu/options/ControlsOptionsScreen.java`, auto-jump option registration.

Difference: In all three inspected 1.10 releases, `sendMovementToServer` copies the option to the local player's cache only under its `isCamera()` gate. After `super.move`, the local-player override passes actual horizontal displacement to the detector. The detector requires enabled, no queued countdown, grounded, not sneaking, not riding, and nonzero movement input. It falls back to yaw-rotated input if actual displacement squared is at most `0.001F`, normalizes the resulting direction, rejects backward-facing dot products below `-0.15F`, and requires both initial block collision shapes above the player to be null. The swept collision scan queues `ticksToNextAutojump = 1` only for a rise strictly above `0.5F` and no higher than the current ceiling: `1.2F`, raised by `(jump-boost amplifier + 1) * 0.75F` while Jump Boost is active. On the next local tick, the countdown sets `input.jumping = true`; its `bl4` guard prevents that injected press from toggling flight. The detector code, input accessor, controls option registration and these gates are unchanged across 1.10, 1.10.1 and 1.10.2.

Movement meaning: grounded motion toward a collision obstacle with an eligible rise may enqueue a synthetic jump input for the next local tick, changing jump and subsequent movement state. The earliest inspected source-present release is 1.10; this bounded finding does not establish a commit date or inspect other 1.9.x releases. Collision-shape and world-collision dependencies remain in the collision lane.
