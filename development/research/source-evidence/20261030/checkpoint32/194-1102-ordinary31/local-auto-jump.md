# Local auto-jump queues a jump after a detected step
1.9.4, Ornithe Feather Gen2: `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `mobTick` line 604; no local-player `move` override or auto-jump check exists.
1.10.2, Ornithe Feather Gen2: same file, `ticksToNextAutojump` line 106, `mobTick` lines 620–778 and new `move`/auto-jump methods lines 812–920; `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/Input.java`, new `getMovement` lines 18–20.
Difference: 1.10.2 synchronizes the auto-jump option and, after local movement, checks enabled, grounded, non-sneaking, non-riding movement with nonzero input and no already queued jump. It predicts a forward sweep from displacement or yaw-rotated input, accounts for jump-boost height, and tests collision boxes for an obstacle rise greater than `0.5F` and at most `1.2F`. A match queues `ticksToNextAutojump = 1`; the next local tick sets `input.jumping = true`. The local tick adds a gate preventing that synthetic jump press from toggling flight.
Movement meaning: grounded forward motion into an eligible step may queue an automatic jump for the next player tick. This changes jump and subsequent movement state. Its collision-shape, world-collision and `Entity.move` dependencies remain open in the assigned collision lane. The introduction boundary is unknown within the compared releases.
