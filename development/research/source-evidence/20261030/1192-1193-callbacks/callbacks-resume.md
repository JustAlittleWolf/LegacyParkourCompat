## Exact sampling and movement route

`LocalPlayer.tick()` calls `super.tick()` on both sides (`LocalPlayer.java:200–218` / `188–207`). Its inherited `LivingEntity.travel()` calls `move(MoverType.SELF, getDeltaMovement())` (1.19.2 `LivingEntity.java:2042–2197`; 1.19.3 `2052–2204`). `Entity.move()` resolves collision movement, calls `tryCheckInsideBlocks()` after movement (`Entity.java:657` in both), and then applies later movement bookkeeping. `tryCheckInsideBlocks()` wraps `checkInsideBlocks()` without a side gate (`Entity.java:685–694`, identical method-body fingerprint).

The only paired change in `Entity.checkInsideBlocks()` is the six inset literals: old min coordinates add `0.001` and max coordinates subtract `0.001` (`Entity.java:886–912`); new min coordinates add `1.0E-7` and max coordinates subtract `1.0E-7` (`Entity.java:894–920`). Replacing only those six old literals with the new literal makes the extracted method bodies byte-for-byte equal. Both versions then use `level.hasChunksAt(minPos,maxPos)`, scan each axis inclusively in x/y/z order, fetch the state, call `BlockState.entityInside(level,pos,this)`, then `onInsideBlock(state)`, with the same crash wrapping. `BlockStateBase.entityInside()` delegates directly to `getBlock().entityInside(asState(), level, pos, entity)` (`BlockBehaviour.java:636–638` / `653–655`); the wrapper bodies are identical. `BlockPos(double...) -> Vec3i(double...) -> Mth.floor(double)` gives floor semantics on both sides (`BlockPos.java:46–48`, `Vec3i.java:39–41`, `Mth.java:65–68` old / `70–73` new).

For an integer plane `N`, away from exact floating-point endpoints, a lower AABB face at `N - δ` changes its sampled cell from `N` to `N-1` for `1.0E-7 < δ < 0.001`. An upper face at `N + δ` changes from `N-1` to `N` for the same interior interval. Equality at either threshold depends on the exact IEEE-754 addition/subtraction and floor result; the concrete `δ≈0.0005` witnesses avoid those endpoints. Other coordinates do not change the per-axis index; each changed bound can add one sampled layer, and their cross product can add corner/edge cells. The `hasChunksAt` guard remains a gate on the entire callback scan.

Concrete ordinary-movement endpoint: the standing player dimensions are 0.6 wide by 1.8 high (`Player.java:126`; `EntityDimensions.makeBoundingBox()` uses `x ± width/2`, `z ± width/2`, and feet-y through feet-y+height at lines 17–24). With feet `y=64.25` and center `z=10.5`, the inclusive scan includes the cell `(9,64,10)` only in 1.19.3. A prior position `x=10.3015` moved by a collision-free SELF displacement of `-0.002` gives that endpoint; `Blocks.COBWEB` is registered with `noCollission()` in both versions (`Blocks.java:348–350` / `377–379`), so that block does not clip the movement. `Entity.move()` and `setPos()` retain double coordinates and rebuild the box without grid rounding (`Entity.java:547–679`, `376–383`).
