## Verdict

**Pair not frozen.** One collision response remains supported as a candidate: a non-sneaking local player clipped downward by an existing bed receives `-velocityY * 0.66F` in 1.12.2, while 1.11.2 zeroes vertical velocity. The four-release bed alias check finds that behavior first in 1.12 and unchanged through 1.12.2.

The ordinary `getLookVector` claim is not closed by the normal entity insertion order. A same-dimension respawn retains nonremoved world entities, appends the replacement local player after them, preserves its network ID, and copies synced player data. A firework earlier in the list can resolve that preserved ID to the replacement player if its cached attachment is still null. Its tick then has a direct velocity write gated by `isFallFlying()`. The source establishes this structural ordering and lookup path, but I did not establish that an attached firework can be in the uncached state across the respawn/receive sequence with the needed yaw/headYaw mismatch at its first tick. Retain as an **open reachable-state dependency**, not an accepted finding and not a negative conclusion.

Full tick and terminal-state closure is also incomplete: the prior ordinary audit explicitly left input/modifier/effect/dimension/provider sections partial, and the terminal inventory leaves item/effect/equipment response writers open. The matrix audit proves source inventory integrity, but does not by itself establish movement relevance for every changed file. Therefore no full-pair coverage freeze or parity claim is made.
