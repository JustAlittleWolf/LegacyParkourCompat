## Claim audit

- **Fire:** Old `BaseFireBlock.entityInside` performs the fire-immune gate, negative-tick increment or `ServerPlayer` random increment, conditional eight-second ignition, unconditional in-fire `hurt`, then `super.entityInside` synchronously (`1.21.2/mojmap/.../BaseFireBlock.java:130–145`; same file hash in 1.21.3). The 26.2 body registers `CLEAR_FREEZE`, `FIRE_IGNITE`, and one after-consumer for each callback (`26.2/unobfuscated/.../BaseFireBlock.java:131–142`). `StepBasedCollector` coalesces the typed ignition per step but preserves after-consumers and flushes by enum order (`InsideBlockEffectApplier.java:32–80`). The closure's synchronous-versus-deferred characterization is accurate.
- **Lava fluid:** Old `LavaFluid` has no `entityInside` override, and the cited old sweep invokes block callbacks rather than fluid callbacks. Its separate `Entity.baseTick` lava sample still calls `lavaHurt` and halves fall distance (`1.21.2/mojmap/.../Entity.java:476–479`). 26.2 `LavaFluid.entityInside` registers `CLEAR_FREEZE`, `LAVA_IGNITE`, and a per-callback `lavaHurt` after-consumer (`26.2/unobfuscated/.../LavaFluid.java:118–123`). The closure correctly keeps base-tick effects distinct from swept-fluid dispatch.
- **Lava cauldron:** Old `entityInside` gates `lavaHurt` through `isEntityInsideContent`; the helper is exactly `entity.getY() < pos.y + getContentHeight(state) && entity.boundingBox.maxY > pos.y + 0.25` (`1.21.2/mojmap/.../LavaCauldronBlock.java:34–37`, `AbstractCauldronBlock.java:52–54`). Modern callback has no such content check and registers the lava effects (`26.2/unobfuscated/.../LavaCauldronBlock.java:47–58`). Its `FILLED_SHAPE` alone does not prove equivalence to both old inequalities, so the stated eligibility caveat is appropriate.
- **Layered cauldron:** Old callback requires `ServerLevel`, on-fire, and old content eligibility; it clears fire before `mayInteract`, then immediately lowers the supplied state and emits `BLOCK_CHANGE` (`1.21.2/mojmap/.../LayeredCauldronBlock.java:63–85`). Snow conversion and lower-level state selection match the closure. Modern callback registers a `runBefore(EXTINGUISH)` only on `ServerLevel`, whose deferred consumer checks on-fire and `mayInteract` but not old content eligibility, then applies `EXTINGUISH` unconditionally (`26.2/unobfuscated/.../LayeredCauldronBlock.java:84–118`). Collector source confirms the before-consumer precedes the type effect at flush. Thus the timing, gate and non-server conclusions are supported.
- **Phase order:** Old `applyEffectsFromBlocks(Vec3,Vec3)` invokes on-ground `stepOn` before adding/scanning movement, synchronously scans, summarizes collected FIRE/LAVA states, clears the collections, and runs the stated fire-tick/sound follow-up (`1.21.2/mojmap/.../Entity.java:740–765`). The old sweep adds a state to the summary only after `entityInside` and `onInsideBlock` return (`:1052–1071`). Modern `stepOn` likewise precedes scan; `applyAndClear` follows scan and precedes rain extinguishing, snapshot-based sound and final fire-tick update (`26.2/unobfuscated/.../Entity.java:949–974`). These checks support the supplement's bounded ordering claims; the DDA-specific traversal itself was not re-audited.

## Correction

The closure's sentence “`Entity.clearFire` sets remaining fire ticks to zero in both endpoints” is not universally correct. Old 1.21.2/1.21.3 assigns zero (`Entity.java:562–564`); 26.2 assigns `Math.min(0, remainingFireTicks)` (`Entity.java:650–652`), retaining a negative fire timer. The reviewed correction note states this distinction accurately. Its path-specific conclusion is also accurate: old `LayeredCauldronBlock` invokes `clearFire` only behind `ServerLevel`, `isOnFire()`, and content gates, so the eligible old callback has positive remaining ticks and clears to zero; modern `EXTINGUISH` routes to the clamping helper at flush. The note correctly retains the requirement to preserve the old gate and immediate write. This correction does not change the layered-cauldron callback's eligible-response conclusion.
