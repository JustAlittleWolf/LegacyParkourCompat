## Review result

The diagonal geometry and correction are valid. This witness closes the previously open ordinary-player applicability gate for the 1.8 → 1.8.1 step-descent candidate. This finding-level result does not itself declare broader pair freeze, implementation status or runtime parity.

The reported second candidate order was wrong in the original witness and is correctly fixed by the supplement: both versions run candidate one and candidate two as Up → X → Z. In 1.8.1 the route score is `var75² + var76²` versus `var82² + var83²`, and equality selects candidate two (`else`); the chosen rise is therefore `var81`. In this geometry both candidates rise by `.20`, move X by `.25`, then Z by `-.03`, and end at the same box `[.45,1.05] × [1.20,3.00] × [-.62,-.02]`.

The normal sweep clips positive X to `.20` at the carpet's west face; after that clip the player box only touches the carpet in X, so Z reaches `-.03`. Its horizontal score is `.20² + .03² = .0409`. The raised candidate clears the carpet vertically during its X/Z clips and scores `.25² + .03² = .0634`; the source fallback therefore retains the raised route. Both ascent boxes intersect the ceiling horizontally. The ceiling underside at Y=3 limits the `.60` rise to `.20`. The unchanged CarpetBlock source in both releases sets collision height to `1/16`; its only 1.8→1.8.1 change here is map-color reporting.

At descent, the raised endpoint has maxZ `-.02`, so it is strictly outside the carpet's Z interval. The ceiling cannot clip a downward move from below it. The raised sweep starts at Y=1 and extends upward; the full floor's top is exactly Y=1. `Box.intersects` uses strict inequalities, so the floor does not enter the reused raised collision list. Thus neither provider clips 1.8's `-.60` descent or 1.8.1's selected `-.20` descent. The resulting feet Y values are `.60` and `1.00`. This agrees with the source-order supplement and the provider closure in the assigned collision report.

## Source-derived travel reachability

The witness's five-tick velocity derivation is valid. Although `PlayerEntity.speed` is initialized to `.1F`, ordinary player travel does not use that cached field: in both releases `LivingEntity.moveRelative` calls virtual `this.getSpeed()`, and the `PlayerEntity.getSpeed()` override at lines 1296–1298 reads the live `MOVEMENT_SPEED` attribute. `LocalClientPlayerEntity.mobTick` sets sprinting before calling `super.mobTick`; the sprint modifier is installed immediately by `LivingEntity.setSprinting`. The operation-2 `.3F` modifier multiplies the player's `.1F` base to `.13F`, so the first grounded sprint travel already gets `.13F` (subject only to the near-1 ground acceleration factor).

With normalized full-forward input, `LivingEntity.mobTick` multiplies by `.98F` before `moveRelative`, so the sprint acceleration is approximately `.1274`. Ground friction on the default full-cube floor is `.6F × .91F = .546F`. Starting from rest, the post-friction horizontal speed after five same-heading sprint moves is `f × a × (1-f⁵)/(1-f) ≈ .145782`, matching the witness. On the witness tick the target displacement magnitude is `sqrt(.25² + .03²) ≈ .251794`; combining `.145782` and `.1274` vectors can yield magnitudes in approximately `[.018382,.273182]`. The target lies strictly inside that interval, requiring a turn of about `45.756°` between prior and current headings. The exact source's float yaw/trig operations only perturb this construction; normal X clipping has `.05` excess and the final Z edge clears carpet by `.02`.

Orienting the prior heading for that resultant gives a positive-X approach direction. The approach origin can be translated back along the five grounded moves so the witness begins centered at `(.50,-.29)`; every preceding player box remains west of the carpet (`maxX ≤ .80`) and below the ceiling. On the witness tick, ordinary grounded travel supplies a downward request (steady-floor gravity leaves about `-.0784`); the initial pass clips it to zero on the floor, while the prior `onGround` state independently satisfies the step gate. Hence the geometry does not depend on injecting motion, teleporting or running non-player physics. The exact probe `(dx,dy,dz)=(.25,0,-.03)` and its grounded travel version share the same horizontal step result.
