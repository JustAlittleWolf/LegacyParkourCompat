## Verdict

## Reachability and remaining gates

The source route is local-client reachable: piston retraction creates the source moving block entity in the changed base cell; client `World.tickEntities` ticks loaded block entities without a logical-side exclusion; player/entity collision collection runs on the client. The permitted piston-response and block-entity audits establish ordinary player eligibility and entity-before-block-entity world ordering. `MovingBlockEntity.tick` uses the stored half-step sequence `0 → 0.5 → 1`; fractional renderer interpolation is not a substitute for a stored collision progress value.

The precise unresolved work is not whether the helpers are called in source; their call gates are explicit above. It is a paired response trace through (a) a retracting source piston where the primary movement first succeeds and leaves an eligible local player in the base-cell overlap satisfying the clearance-equality gate, and (b) the terminal-progress ordering where the player's ordinary tick/collision solve precedes the old-only final `moveEntities()` call. The existing accepted witnesses use different operations or phases and cannot be transferred to these conditions.
