## Required corrections

1. **Implement the accepted 1.12 stair arm.** The `v1_11_2/FenceCollisionState` maps every recognized stair to `DISCONNECTED`, and has no version-dependent branch. Since its registration is the latest registration for this hook in the candidate, it is also selected for 1.12 profiles and later. That contradicts the accepted finding: the exact 1.12.2 `FenceBlock.shouldConnectTo` / `resolveVirtualProperties` path queries the adjacent stair’s directional face, and the straight north-facing stair south of the fence returns `SOLID`, adding the south collision arm. Introduce the needed distinct 1.12 resolution behavior (with the correct resolver cut), evaluating the neighbor face and stair state for each direction, or use an equivalent operation that preserves that directional result. Do not implement the 1.12 behavior as `true` for all stair IDs: upside-down and differently oriented stair states are state-dependent. The 1.11.2 registration must retain the no-stair-arm behavior.
2. **Constrain the reconstructed mask to source-closed inputs or preserve unsupported arms correctly.** The candidate returns empty for any neighbor outside air, six listed wood fences, and the listed stairs. Empty invokes modern stored-state geometry, even for a recognized legacy wood fence whose other neighbor is an ordinary solid, a fence gate, or another source-supported connection candidate. That is not a source-faithful fallback for the accepted query: historical 1.11.2 recomputes virtual properties using its material/full-cube rule; 1.12 uses directional face shape plus its own same-fence/gate predicates. A partial neighbor whitelist must not silently retain modern stored bits for the unsupported directions while claiming a complete legacy mask. Either close the relevant raw-neighbor domain and implement those predicates with exact mapped block/state keys, or limit this change to the independently accepted stair-arm witness and compose it with already-resolved/native directional bits without replacing other directions.
3. **Close the lower applicability claim.** The lower `v1_10_1` no-op is selected for 1.10.1 and earlier, but the accepted independent audit explicitly leaves behavior before 1.10.2 uninspected. The comment asserting this as a lower resolved boundary cannot establish that native fence behavior is correct for those profiles. Remove that unsupported implication or provide exact source/boundary evidence before using it as a native resolver fence. The audited old behavior is proven at 1.10.2 and 1.11–1.11.2; the new directional-face behavior is proven at 1.12–1.12.2.

At minimum prove that the new query-state geometry cannot bypass or overwrite those resolved transformations.

This differs structurally from historical `addCollisions` emitting ordered boxes, so it supports only the accepted geometry witness, not collision component/order parity as a whole.
