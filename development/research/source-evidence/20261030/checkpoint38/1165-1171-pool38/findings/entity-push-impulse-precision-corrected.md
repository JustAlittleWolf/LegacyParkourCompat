Difference: after the existing `absMax(dx,dz) >= 0.01F` gate, 1.16.5 assigns the float result of `Mth.sqrt(f)` into the double divisor; 1.17.1 uses `Math.sqrt(f)` as a double. With local-player-minus-nearby-Mob offsets `dx=0.03,dz=0`, both pass the threshold. Given `pushthrough=0`, the local-player X term from `d/sqrt(max(|d|,|e|))*min(1,1/sqrt(...))*0.05F` is `-0.008660254322328477` in 1.16.5 and `-0.008660254166892228` in 1.17.1 (difference `+1.5543624894143182e-10`). This is a conditional arithmetic witness, not a recorded trajectory.
Movement meaning: on the logical client, a nearby non-player Mob's `tick()` enters the inherited `LivingEntity.tick()` and virtual `Mob.aiStep()` calls `super.aiStep()`. `LivingEntity.aiStep()` reaches `pushEntities()` after its own travel. In that query, `EntitySelector.pushableBy(mob)` admits a pushable local-player candidate on the client. `doPush(localPlayer)` calls `localPlayer.push(mob)`; under the stated movement, vehicle, sleep and physics gates, the local player receives the changed impulse in delta movement. The local-player-as-query-source path and RemotePlayer-as-pusher path are excluded by exact client selector / `RemotePlayer.noPhysics` gates.
