## Candidate differences

### 1. Sneaking player body height changes from 1.65 to 1.5

1.13.2 Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java:334–360` chooses `f = 0.6F`, `g = 1.65F` when `isSneaking()` (unless sleeping/swimming/fall-flying takes precedence), constructs the box with that height, and calls `setSize` only if `world.hasNoCollisions(null, box)` succeeds. 1.14.4 Feather: `PlayerEntity.java:109–117` maps `Pose.SNEAKING` to dynamic dimensions `0.6F × 1.5F`; `updatePlayerPose()` chooses it for sneaking when not flying and fitting, or chooses it as a clearance fallback (`:343–370`), with `Entity.java:2245–2266` applying the dimensions/shape after the synchronized pose change. A locally controlled player who can crouch under 1.65-block clearance but not stand can therefore occupy 0.15 blocks less vertical space in 1.14.4 and fit through headroom bands that the 1.13.2 crouch box cannot. This is a dimensions/pose source difference; actual block shape admission is a collision-lane dependency.

### 2. New constrained-pose fallback can shrink a blocked player to swimming dimensions

1.13.2 Feather: `PlayerEntity.updatePlayerPose()` selects only the pose-derived width/height and leaves the existing dimensions when its target box fails the no-collision query (`:334–360`); it does not then attempt a smaller pose. 1.14.4 Feather: while the swimming pose box fits, `PlayerEntity.updatePlayerPose()` first picks the player's desired pose; when the player is not a spectator or riding and the desired pose does not fit, it falls back to `Pose.SNEAKING` if that box fits, otherwise to `Pose.SWIMMING` (`:343–370`). `Pose.SWIMMING` has dimensions `0.6F × 0.6F` (`:113–114`), and its selected pose updates the current shape through `Entity.m_84943753()` (`:2245–2266`). A locally controlled player stopped by a one-block-high overhead obstruction while desiring the standing pose can thus enter the smaller swimming box at the end-of-tick pose update and pass through on a subsequent travel tick, provided the pose box fits the world collision query. The movement loop's resulting reachability depends on collision shapes/query semantics, not inspected here; this is a candidate pending that dependency closure.
