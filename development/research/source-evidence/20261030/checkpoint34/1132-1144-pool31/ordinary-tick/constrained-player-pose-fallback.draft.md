# Constrained player pose fallback
1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java`, `updatePlayerPose`, lines 334–360. It selects only the state-derived dimensions and leaves the current dimensions unchanged when the proposed shape collides.
1.14.4, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java`, `updatePlayerPose`, lines 343–370; `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, pose fit `m_87715359` lines 1628–1629, pose-box creation `m_16672198` lines 2300–2305, and dimension application `m_84943753` lines 2245–2266. If the swimming pose fits and the desired pose does not, a non-spectating, non-riding player falls back to sneaking if that fits, otherwise swimming. The swimming pose dimensions are `0.6F × 0.6F` (`PlayerEntity.java:113–114`).
Difference: 1.14.4 can reduce a blocked player to the swimming box after the failed standing/sneaking fits; 1.13.2 does not try this smaller fallback pose.
Movement meaning: a standing player halted by a one-block-high overhead obstruction can gain a 0.6-block pose box at tick-end and move through on a following travel tick if the world collision query admits that pose. Exact block/entity collision-shape dependencies remain open, so retain as a provisional candidate pending collision-lane verification.
