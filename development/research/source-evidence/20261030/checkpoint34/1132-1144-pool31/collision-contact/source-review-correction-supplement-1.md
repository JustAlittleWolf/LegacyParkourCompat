This supplement corrects factual overstatements in the immutable first-written review and drafts. The original files remain unchanged.

1. **Slipperiness read timing.** The review's section 3 correctly lists the older second read at `LivingEntity.java:1554–1558`, but the following sentence wrongly says a support transition during the move can change the sampled friction. In 1.13.2 that read is before `move(...)` at `:1575`, as is the first read at `:1540–1545`; no position change occurs between them. Retract the support-transition claim and the reference to a retained 1.13.2→1.14.2 candidate on that basis.
2. **Grounded acceleration arithmetic.** A distinct source difference remains in the acceleration factor. With default slipperiness `0.6F` (`1.13.2 Block.java:1907`; `1.14.4 Block.java:817`) and the grounded gate, binary32 evaluation of the old `t = 0.6F * 0.91F; u = 0.16277137F / (t*t*t)` yields `u = 0.9999998807907104F`; the new `0.21600002F / (0.6F*0.6F*0.6F)` yields `1.0F`. The source sites are old `LivingEntity.java:1542–1548` and new `:1841–1844,1961–1963`. This is a grounded movement-acceleration rounding candidate, not a post-move slipperiness resample. No full trajectory is asserted. Draft `ground-acceleration-friction-rounding.md` records the corrected claim.
3. **Lava contact region wording.** The lava draft's phrase “only intersects lava within the extra `0.001` contact margin” was too geometrically narrow. The newer pass checks block cells selected by the AABB inset by `0.001` and calls the LiquidBlock callback; the older query checks cells intersecting the box contracted by `0.1F,0.4F,0.1F`. The meaningful difference is that the new callback visits cells in the broader near-edge AABB region that the older material scan contracts away. Both are cell/material based; neither clips a fluid shape for this query.
4. **Stacked-fluid eye-submersion wording.** The stacked-height draft correctly identifies `8/9` versus `1.0F` for a stacked source water cell, but its eye interval claim did not account for the eye predicate's added `0.11111111F`. For source level 8, the old eye threshold is approximately one block, so this example establishes a changed body-contact threshold in `m_69693160`, not necessarily an eye-submersion difference. An eye-state change requires a stacked non-source flow level below 8, where the older level-derived height plus `0.11111111F` remains below 1.0; that narrower state should be verified separately. The general eye-submersion path does consume the context-sensitive height, but the source-level threshold example must be stated with this gate.

## Corrected movement meaning for stacked water

In 1.13.2, the contact scan sets the water-contact flag when `q + level/9.0F >= box.minY`. In 1.14.4, if the same fluid is immediately above, it instead tests against `q + 1.0F`. For a level-8 source fluid this changes body-contact admission only when the box bottom lies in the upper `1/9` of that cell. Eye-submersion has its additional `0.11111111F` margin and does not share that exact threshold; its candidate is limited to stacked flowing states with a lower level. The particular movement consequences remain source-level candidates pending a concrete reachable player state.
