## Source verdict

The corrected r1 claim is source-applicable and accepted for the exact 1.12.2-to-1.13 endpoint comparison. Its body-water contact and sampled-depth distinction, the 1.13.1/1.13.2 persistence claim, and the 1.14.4 later control are supported by the independently pinned sources.

In 1.12.2, `Entity.tick` calls `checkWaterCollisions` (`net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:381-382`). The unmounted path calls `World.applyLiquidDrag(getShape().grown(0.0, -0.4F, 0.0).contract(0.001), WATER, this)` and sets `inWater` from that result (`Entity.java:948-964`). `Box.grown` computes `minY - dy` and `maxY + dy`; `contract(a)` calls `expand(-a)`, and `expand` calls `grown` (`net/minecraft/util/math/Box.java:132-145, 279-281`). Thus the negative 0.4 shrinks both vertical ends by 0.4 before the further 0.001 contraction. `World.applyLiquidDrag` scans floor-min to ceil-max half-open cell ranges, checks the sampled upper bound against `cellY + 1 - LiquidBlock.getHeightLoss(level)`, and does not test the surface against the sampled lower face (`net/minecraft/world/World.java:1579-1605`).

In stable 1.13, `Entity.tick` calls the water-state updater (`net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:393-394`), which calls `checkWaterState`, preserves boat suppression, and writes the body-contact boolean (`Entity.java:946-976`). Its sampler contracts the original entity box by 0.001, scans the same half-open integer cell convention, accepts a tagged fluid only when `cellY + fluidHeight >= box.minY`, accumulates the maximum depth from that face, then stores that depth (`Entity.java:2507-2569`). `FluidState.getHeight` delegates to the fluid; `FlowingFluid.getHeight` is `level / 9.0F`; source water reports level 8 (`FluidState.java:29-31; FlowingFluid.java:373-376; WaterFluid.java:136-145`). I independently confirmed the 1.13.1 and 1.13.2 `Entity.java` files have the same SHA-256 and retain this sampler. The 1.14.4 Mojmap `Entity.checkAndHandleWater` keeps the unshrunk-box deflate, tagged-fluid surface-versus-minY check, and maximum-depth write (`world/entity/Entity.java:2588-2649`).

The arithmetic and loop limits follow the source methods above; these are not runtime measurements.

The arrangement can retain its source cell: the chest is `Material.WOOD`, ordinary material blocks movement by default, and the 1.12.2 flowing-liquid helper treats movement-blocking material as liquid-blocking (`block/ChestBlock.java:40-44; block/material/Material.java:71-72; block/FlowingLiquidBlock.java:186-192`).
