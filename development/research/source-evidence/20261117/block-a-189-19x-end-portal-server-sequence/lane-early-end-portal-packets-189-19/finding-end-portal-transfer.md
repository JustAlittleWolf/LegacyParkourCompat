1.9, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/server/entity/living/player/ServerPlayerEntity.java`, `changeDimension(int)`, lines 471-478; `net/minecraft/server/PlayerManager.java`, `changeDimension(ServerPlayerEntity,int)`, lines 488-513 and `changeDimension(Entity,int,ServerWorld,ServerWorld)`, lines 515-568; `net/minecraft/server/network/handler/ServerPlayNetworkHandler.java`, `teleport(...)`, lines 490-516.
Difference: In 1.8.9, when a player changes from dimension 0 to 1 and the End world has a forced spawn point, `ServerPlayerEntity.changeDimension` first calls the absolute network teleport with that point's integer X/Y/Z and yaw/pitch `0.0F`, before `PlayerManager` sends the respawn packet. In 1.9 this optional move is removed. Both releases then send the respawn packet with dimension, old-world difficulty, old-world generator type and gamemode; both choose the target world's `getSpawnPoint()` for the final End position and set yaw `90.0F`, pitch `0.0F`, then send the final absolute move. The 1.9 teleport helper additionally attaches its requested teleport ID.
Movement meaning: With a non-null forced End spawn point, the 1.8.9 client receives an extra absolute player relocation before dimension respawn; the final End relocation still targets `getSpawnPoint()` in both versions. The optional extra relocation therefore also passes through the absolute-move velocity reset described by the client handler.
