# Wall neighbor-face collision connections
1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo(WorldView, BlockPos)` (148–157), `resolveVirtualProperties(BlockState, WorldView, BlockPos)` (188–195), and `addCollisions(...)` / `getCollisionShape(...)` (92–104).
1.12, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo(WorldView, BlockPos, Direction)` (146–152), `resolveVirtualProperties(...)` (190–198), and `addCollisions(...)` / `getCollisionShape(...)` (90–102); `net/minecraft/block/FenceGateBlock.java`, `getFaceShape(...)` (175–181); `net/minecraft/block/StairsBlock.java`, `getFaceShape(...)` (140–161).
Difference: 1.11.2 connects walls to another wall, any fence gate, or a non-pumpkin solid-blocking cube. In 1.12, each queried neighbor face is classified. A non-exception `SOLID` face or `MIDDLE_POLE_THICK` face connects; `MIDDLE_POLE` additionally connects for fence gates. Gate `MIDDLE_POLE` is orientation-dependent as described by `FenceGateBlock.getFaceShape`. Resolved direction bits select unchanged wall collision boxes (including variants with 0.875 or 1.0 maximum Y).
Movement meaning: a transverse-oriented adjacent fence gate connected in 1.11.2 but fails the 1.12 directional `MIDDLE_POLE` test for that face, changing a wall's arm collision. A player intersecting that removed arm can receive different collision clipping from `World.getCollisions` during `Entity.move`. Stairs have face- and virtual-shape-dependent `SOLID` classifications in 1.12; complete state/direction combinations remain for the pair auditor.
