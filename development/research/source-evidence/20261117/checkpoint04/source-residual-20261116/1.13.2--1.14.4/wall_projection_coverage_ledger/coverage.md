## Exact remaining gates

1. **Global denominator and inheritance join remain open.** The audited census found 65 old source files with direct `getFaceShape` declarations, but that file scan is not a registered-key census. The census explicitly says the common-key denominator is a lower bound: aliases, subclasses inheriting an override, and registrations not yet joined to old effective owners remain unenumerated. The packets above close only their expressly named key/state families. There is no accepted exhaustive common registration → old effective FaceShape owner → target effective collision provider crosswalk for every common key.
2. **No full-pair wall closure/freeze.** The accepted packets establish individual wall-bit results and selected neighbor producers. They do not establish complete tick, resource, external-state, or all-neighbor update closure required for pair freeze. The provider census and its audit explicitly retain this status.
3. **No trajectory claim.** Wall-bit differences affect stored shape-selection inputs, but these packets do not measure a player trajectory. Retain source predicate, state-production, implementation, and runtime parity as separate verdicts.

Within the named census rows, current source packets account for each family listed in its “Remaining per-key unknowns” section, including `end_gateway` via `wall_portal_face_projection`. This ledger does not assert that no further keys exist outside that lower-bound list. No new provider math was introduced here; all statuses above are reconciliations of the cited immutable source evidence.
