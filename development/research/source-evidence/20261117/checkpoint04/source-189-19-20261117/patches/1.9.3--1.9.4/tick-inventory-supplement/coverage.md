## Tick closure examined

The paired code order is unchanged. A `LocalClientPlayerEntity.tick` runs its superclass chain only while the player's current chunk is loaded (`LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:143-145`). `PlayerEntity.tick` does its pre-super player setup and then calls `LivingEntity.tick` (`PlayerEntity.java:170-200`). `LivingEntity.tick` calls `Entity.tick` (`LivingEntity.java:1489-1491`); `Entity.tick` updates its glowing flag only server-side and invokes virtual `baseTick` (`Entity.java:299-304`). This dispatch reaches `LivingEntity.baseTick`, which first invokes `Entity.baseTick` (`LivingEntity.java:206-208`). The entity base phase records prior movement state, handles portal timers server-side, updates sprint particles and water collision state, then applies the client/server fire and lava/void branches (`Entity.java:307-393`). The living base phase then handles wall/world-border checks, underwater breathing and water/rain extinguishing, timers, and status-effect ticking (`LivingEntity.java:206-300`).

After that base phase, `LivingEntity.tick` runs its item-use phase and server-only equipment/attribute synchronization, then calls `mobTick` (`LivingEntity.java:1489-1551`). `mobTick` applies interpolation/smoothing gates, invokes `serverTickAi` only for locally controlled entities, chooses jump response in water -> lava -> grounded/cooldown order, damps input, performs the movement-mode dispatch through `moveRelative`, and finally pushes nearby entities (`LivingEntity.java:1645-1713`). The local player supplies input axes/jump only from `LocalClientPlayerEntity.serverTickAi` when it is the camera (`LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:586-595`); base `PlayerEntity.isLocal` is false (`PlayerEntity.java:1157`), the local subclass overrides it to true (`LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:306`), and that subclass also returns true for `isLocallyControlled` (`LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:447`). `Entity.isLogicalSideForUpdatingMovement` selects a controlling player via `isLocal`, otherwise uses `!world.isClient` (`Entity.java:2254-2256`). These client/local and server/logical-side gates are identical in the pair.

`LivingEntity.moveRelative`'s entry gate is `isLocallyControlled() || isLogicalSideForUpdatingMovement()`, then chooses dry/fall-flying, lava or water movement by the water/lava predicates and player flying ability (`LivingEntity.java:1302-1458`). Its unchanged inputs include ground block slipperiness, climbing state, chunk-loaded fallback, levitation, Depth Strider, and `getSpeed`; player `getSpeed` reads `MOVEMENT_SPEED` (`PlayerEntity.java:1393-1394`). Player movement speed is initialized to 0.1 (`PlayerEntity.java:155`), sprint toggles a movement-speed modifier (`LivingEntity.java:1180-1190`), and equipment attribute modifiers are refreshed in the server-only equipment phase (`LivingEntity.java:1515-1530`). Jump boost is read in `LivingEntity.jump` (`LivingEntity.java:1279-1289`); levitation in travel (`1396-1400`); Depth Strider through `EnchantmentHelper` (`1433-1447`). The paired attribute, item-stack, effect, and enchantment sources listed above are identical, so this supplement found no changed input or branch dependency there.

## Water/lava/material dependencies examined

`Entity.checkWaterCollisions` is called from base tick and sets `inWater` from boat status plus `World.applyLiquidDrag` over the shrunken player shape (`Entity.java:361, 845-866`). `isInWater` returns this cached flag (`845-847`). `isSubmergedIn` checks the eye-height block state's material and computes a liquid-surface threshold through `LiquidBlock.getHeightLoss` (`925-941`); `isInLava` delegates a grown player box to `World.containsMaterial` (`943-944`). The same World routines iterate block states and fluid levels, call `Block.applyMaterialDrag`, and may add the normalized liquid-flow vector scaled by `0.014` to the entity velocity (`World.java:1560-1625`; `Block.java:522`; `LiquidBlock.java:49-54`). All paired bodies/dependencies are byte-identical; no material-selection or branch-selection candidate emerged.

The player push override only suppresses pushing while sleeping (`PlayerEntity.java:1886-1890`). These generic push/knockback responses can affect a player when the player is the target, while their producer may be a non-player or server-side event; no non-player physics change is inferred as a player candidate. Player death also writes its own small vertical and directional velocity in `PlayerEntity.die` (`487-503`), but that is not an alive movement-tick branch. Position placement/respawn and passenger placement writers were observed but are not evidence of a changed reachable movement tick in this pair. None of these paired implementations differs.
