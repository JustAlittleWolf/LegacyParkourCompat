## Current source disposition

One candidate is source-confirmed: removal of the horizontal world-coordinate guard from `World.isChunkLoaded(BlockPos, boolean)` allows the local player tick to proceed with x or z outside the `[-30,000,000, 30,000,000)` block range. When the player is a spectator, inherited `PlayerEntity.tick()` sets no-clip, so the continuing local tick can apply movement beyond the prior gate. Exact reachable setup, method references and source line numbers are in `findings/player-tick-world-limit.md`.

The liquid-flow helper's changed query count/state parameter is a same-world-state refactor on the player drag route, not a separate movement finding. Rendering's `getFlowAngle` use is outside scope.
