## Current source disposition (r2)

One candidate is source-confirmed: removal of the bounds guard from `World.isChunkLoaded(BlockPos, boolean)` admits the out-of-range local-player query in 1.9.3. `LocalClientPlayerEntity.tick()` calls `super.tick()` only after that gate passes; `PlayerEntity.tick()` sets no-clip from spectator status, runs inherited movement, then clamps x/z to ±29,999,999 in the same tick. The result is a one-tick clamp back to the limit, whereas 1.9.2 skips the tick and leaves the position out of range for that tick. The finding does not claim ongoing movement beyond the limit. See the pair-specific r2 finding.

The local tick’s on-foot send path runs after `super.tick()`; the riding branch sends angles/input and vehicle movement packets. Those sends do not change the gate or the same-tick clamp. Server simulation and vehicle physics remain outside this client player-movement finding.

The liquid-flow helper's changed query count/state parameter is a same-world-state refactor on the player drag route, not a separate movement finding. Rendering's `getFlowAngle` use is outside scope. The complete paired source inventory is 1,816 shared paths plus three removed and three added paths, with 621 changed hashes among same-path sources. No additional movement delta was identified in the independent audit.
