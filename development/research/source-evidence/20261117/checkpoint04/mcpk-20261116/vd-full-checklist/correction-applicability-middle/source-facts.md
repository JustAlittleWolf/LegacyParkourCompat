### Interpretation bounded to these samples

Across these examined receivers, position-relative flags control the corresponding velocity component independently: relative X/Y/Z retains the pre-correction component; absolute X/Y/Z sets it to zero. The 1.13.2 handler updates all three components before calling `updatePositionAndAngles`; 1.16.5 computes the components, conditionally detaches a vehicle, writes raw position/history, then writes velocity; 1.19.4, 1.20.5 and 1.20.6 write position before velocity. A sampled riding distinction is visible between 1.16.5 (explicit detachment after `tickCount > 0`) and the later sampled receivers (no receiver-level vehicle check). 1.13.2 likewise has no receiver-level vehicle check. These observations do not prove the exact first cutover or behavior in any unsampled release.
