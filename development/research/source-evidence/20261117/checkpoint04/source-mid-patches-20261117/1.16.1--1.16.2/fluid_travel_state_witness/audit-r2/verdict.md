## Corrected witness checks

- **Geometry passes.** `Player.STANDING_DIMENSIONS` is 0.6×1.8 (`Player.java:112–114`); `Entity.setPos()` places the feet at Y and constructs the box to Y+height (`Entity.java:321–325`). At `(0.5,63.5,0.5)`, the box is x/z `[0.2,0.8]` and y `[63.5,65.3]`. `Blocks.LAVA` is a `LiquidBlock` using `Fluids.LAVA` (`Blocks.java:121–125`). With source lava at `(0,63,0)` and air above, `FlowingFluid.getHeight()` uses own height and `getOwnHeight()` returns `amount/9` (`FlowingFluid.java:463–475`); the source surface is therefore `63 + 8/9 ≈ 63.8888889`.
- **The corrected scan is positive.** Both versions’ `Entity.updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing()` deflate the box by `0.001`, floor/ceil the scanned cell range, and retain `max(surface - deflatedMinY, 0)` when `surface >= deflatedMinY` (`Entity.java:2633–2662` for 1.16.1; `2641–2669` for 1.16.2). Deflation raises minY from 63.5 to 63.501, so the stored lava height is positive, approximately `63.8888889 - 63.501 = 0.3878889`. This corrects r1’s reversed sign and above-surface geometry.
- **The stale old contact flag is source-supported.** Same-dimension command teleport routes `ServerPlayer` to its connection’s `teleport()` (`TeleportCommand.java:269–280`); that applies `absMoveTo()` and sends the position packet (`ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.java:907–920`). On the client, `handleMovePlayer()` applies `setPosRaw()` and `absMoveTo()` and sends the teleport acknowledgement (`ClientPacketListener.java:683–747`). The position setters rebuild the AABB but do not move the entity or call `checkInsideBlocks()` (`Entity.java:321–326`, `1104–1115`). Thus, under the report’s stated precondition (`onGround=false`, `firstTick=false`, `isTouchingLava=false`), this teleport does not refresh the old lava-contact flag before the next client tick.
- **The tick ordering produces the stated predicate difference.** `LivingEntity.tick()` calls `super.tick()` before `aiStep()` (`LivingEntity.java:2121–2124`, `2168`), and `Entity.tick()` calls `baseTick()` (`Entity.java:346–352`). `baseTick()` refreshes fluid height before AI (`Entity.java:372–374`). In 1.16.1, `isInLava()` returns only `isTouchingLava` (`Entity.java:1065–1071`); that flag is cleared and block callbacks are run by the later movement path (`Entity.java:551–553`, `803–816`, and `LiquidBlock.entityInside():178–185`). In 1.16.2, `isInLava()` returns `!firstTick && fluidHeight[LAVA] > 0` (`Entity.java:1071–1073`), which is true for the report’s already-established (`firstTick=false`) player after the positive scan.

For airborne, non-water state, the old stale false lava predicate selects zero water height and does not take a liquid-jump branch; the new true lava predicate selects the positive lava height and calls `jumpInLiquid(LAVA)`. That method adds `0.04` Y velocity (`LivingEntity.java:1900–1902`). The later `Entity.move()` contact callback follows that current AI jump choice.
