## Ordered inspected scope

1. Block callbacks into entity portal state: `NetherPortalBlock.entityInside` and `EndPortalBlock.entityInside` (`26.1.2`: `NetherPortalBlock.java:110-122`, `EndPortalBlock.java:61-75`; `26.2`: `NetherPortalBlock.java:112-124`, `EndPortalBlock.java:61-75`). Both gate on `entity.canUsePortal(false)` and call `entity.setAsInsidePortal(this, pos)`; End callback retains the server-side credits branch.
2. Portal process and cooldown writers: `PortalProcessor.processPortalTeleportation`, `getPortalDestination`, `decayTick`, `hasExpired`, entry-position update and tick flag (`PortalProcessor.java:21-68`, both versions); `Entity.setPortalCooldown`, overload, `processPortalCooldown` (`Entity.java:578-604` / `585-611`); `Entity.setAsInsidePortal` and `handlePortal` (`Entity.java:2538-2579` / `2605-2646`). Bodies are identical at both endpoints. `Entity.handlePortal` is gated by `level() instanceof ServerLevel`; `LocalPlayer.aiStep` separately decrements portal cooldown outside a level-loading screen.
3. Position/dimension transition producers and consumers: Nether portal transition-time and destination/exit construction (`NetherPortalBlock.java:124-244` / `126-246`); End destination (`EndPortalBlock.java:79-115`); entity `teleport`, same/cross-dimension handlers, `teleportSetPosition`, `getRelativePortalPosition`, `canUsePortal`, and `canTeleport` (`Entity.java:2981-3144` / `3052-3215`). `PortalProcessor.java` forwards destination unchanged. Position/rotation/delta writes and gates are otherwise method-identical.
4. Client-local portal-effect/cooldown path: `LocalPlayer.aiStep`, `getActivePortalLocalTransition`, `handlePortalTransitionEffect` (`LocalPlayer.java:767-967`, both). The loading-screen gate still covers both local effect handling and cooldown decrement; `Gui.screen()` in 26.2 returns its stored screen. Effect handling still clears an ineligible screen and consumes the inside-this-tick flag. These are API moves from `Minecraft.screen`/`Minecraft.setScreen` to `Minecraft.gui.screen()`/`gui.setScreen()`.
5. Checked helper equivalence for End destination position: in 26.1.2, `BlockPos.getBottomCenter` (`BlockPos.java:131-133`) delegates to `Vec3.atBottomCenterOf` (`Vec3.java:57-59`); 26.2 calls `Vec3.atBottomCenterOf` directly at `EndPortalBlock.java:90,110`. The shared helper remains `atLowerCornerWithOffset(pos, 0.5, 0.0, 0.5)` in both versions. The resulting coordinates are source-identical.

## Bounded result

No source-proven portal-entry, portal-time, cooldown, or transition-consumer behavior delta was found in this slice. The End portal destination replaces `spawnBlockPos.getBottomCenter()` with `Vec3.atBottomCenterOf(spawnBlockPos)` (and likewise for adjusted spawn position); the former delegates directly to that helper, whose coordinate math is unchanged. `LocalPlayer` changes screen access as GUI ownership moves, while portal cooldown decrement remains under the same loading-screen condition. These endpoint observations do not establish intermediate patch onset.
