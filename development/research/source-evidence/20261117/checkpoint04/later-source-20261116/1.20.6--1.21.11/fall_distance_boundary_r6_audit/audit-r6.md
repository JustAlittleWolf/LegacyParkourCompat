## Audited claim

The bounded behavior cutover is supported by the exact 1.21.1 and 1.21.2 sources. In 1.21.1, `Player.aiStep` reaches `super.aiStep()` without a creative-flight fall reset; `Player.travel` saves vertical velocity, calls `super.travel`, restores the damped vertical velocity, then resets fall distance (`Player.java:513+`, `1456–1477`). In 1.21.2, `Player.aiStep` resets fall distance for flying nonpassengers before `super.aiStep()` (`Player.java:543–555`), while its flying branch in `Player.travel` has no post-travel reset (`Player.java:1445–1466`). The reset-order distinction is accurately stated for the 1.21.1→1.21.2 adjacent boundary.

With creative flight on, shift contributing `-1` to vertical input, and `isFallFlying() == false`, `LocalPlayer.aiStep` adds `-abilities.getFlyingSpeed()*3.0F`; default flying speed `0.05F` yields a `-0.15F` request. The ordinary air-travel branch moves that request; `Entity.checkFallDamage` adds the magnitude of a negative, unobstructed Y displacement to `fallDistance`. The 1.21.1 wrapper resets it after travel; the 1.21.2 pre-reset leaves the new `0.15F` accumulation. The report correctly excludes collisions/effects that change the vertical request or its displacement.

For the subsequent, separately constructed SELF request, the stated values check out. `Attributes.SNEAKING_SPEED` defaults to `0.3` in both versions; `LocalPlayer` passes its float value to `input.tick`, whose keyboard path produces forward impulse `0.3F` for forward-only crouched movement. `LivingEntity.aiStep` multiplies `zza` by `0.98F`, producing binary32 `0.2940000295639038F`. With non-sprinting, non-creative flight, `Player.getFlyingSpeed()` returns `0.02F`; `getInputVector` with yaw zero and zero starting horizontal delta yields Z `0.005880000459849821`. These operations and the candidate's stated no-item-use and ordinary-input assumptions support the number. The report expressly labels it a constructed SELF request and does not claim that a whole subsequent `LocalPlayer` tick has no other vertical movement.

The edge predicate derivation is accurate: `isAboveGround` accepts `fallDistance < maxUpStep` only when the corresponding downward `canFallAtLeast` query has a collision; the query subtracts `1.0E-5F`. `maxUpStep` is `0.6F` for this nonpassenger from the `STEP_HEIGHT` default. At the post-descent feet height `0.525`, the 1.21.1 query depth `0.6` reaches about `-0.07501`, intersecting the support cube, while the 1.21.2 depth `0.45` reaches about `+0.07499`, above it. With the stated `0.001` straddle, old-profile edge backoff zeros a positive Z request of less than `0.05`; the new-profile predicate skips that loop. The full-collision support geometry and absence assumptions make the illustrated collision/no-collision outcomes valid.

`CollisionGetter.noCollision(entity, AABB)` checks block collisions, entity collisions, and—when the entity argument is non-null—world-border collision in both sources. The candidate correctly requires those other collisions to be absent and states that the support block is the only collision in the downward query.
