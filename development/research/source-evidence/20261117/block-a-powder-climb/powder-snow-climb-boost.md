# Powder Snow climb boost uses prior-tick contact

1.21.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement`, lines 2362–2372; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/PowderSnowBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 60–80.
1.21.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement`, lines 2394–2404; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/PowderSnowBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 62–95.
Difference: The climb boost still requires `horizontalCollision || jumping` and walkability (leather boots for an ordinary Player), but its Powder Snow gate changes from the current `getInBlockState().is(POWDER_SNOW)` to `wasInPowderSnow`. The 1.21.5 contact effect sets `isInPowderSnow` after travel; the entity tick snapshots that flag into `wasInPowderSnow` on the next tick. The vector order is unchanged: movement, then gate, then returned Y set to `0.2`, followed by air-travel gravity and friction.
Movement meaning: A leather-booted player can receive the climb-boost velocity on an air-travel tick with jump or horizontal collision after Powder Snow overlap was recorded on the previous tick, even if the player is no longer currently inside the block at the gate. The old current-block predicate does not admit that carried-contact case. Players without leather boots fail the Powder Snow walkability query.
