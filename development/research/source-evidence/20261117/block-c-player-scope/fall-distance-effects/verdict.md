# Independent source verdict: fall distance effect resets

Status: COMPLETED

Reviewed immutable comparison: `fall-distance-effects-source/comparison.md`, SHA-256 `4cd09d4f9e8980a3cd663c9ae50b320013af1806a69c1f2823243c4b76a52663`.

Verdict: ACCEPT the central operation difference. Minecraft 1.20 moves a single fall-distance reset for Slow Falling or Levitation from effect-specific `travel` branches into `LivingEntity.aiStep`, after `updateFallFlying()` and before ridden/ordinary travel dispatch. The new check has neither a vertical-velocity predicate nor a local-control predicate. Across every supplied snapshot from 1.20, 1.20.1, 1.20.2, 1.20.3, 1.20.4, 1.20.5, through 1.20.6, the same effect condition and single reset remain immediately before the route dispatch; no reset-gate change was found.

## Independent claims and classifications

- **Discrete effect operations:** Slow Falling's 1.19.4 reset was coupled to `deltaMovement.y <= 0.0` inside locally controlled `travel`; its gravity substitution remains direction-gated in 1.20 and 1.20.6, but no longer gates the reset. Levitation's old reset was in the ordinary travel gravity branch, after movement. The new common reset applies for either effect before movement. Climbing retains its separate `handleOnClimbable` reset.
- **Route operations:** The 1.20/1.20.6 reset precedes the `travelRidden` versus `travel` choice, so it covers water, ordinary movement, and locally eligible ridden travel independent of which branch `travel` selects. It also precedes the gliding branch for Slow Falling. Slow Falling's downward-only gravity rule remains inside `travel` and does not control this reset.
- **Authority and flight gates:** The former Slow Falling and Levitation resets were reachable only inside `travel`'s `isControlledByLocalInstance()` guard. The common `aiStep` reset has no such guard and executes before dispatch. `updateFallFlying()` occurs just before it. That function disallows fall flying while Levitation is active (`!hasEffect(LEVITATION)`), so the report's phrasing that Levitation now resets “before gliding” is not a reachable Levitation-plus-gliding witness; the pre-route reset is real, but that particular route combination must be excluded. Slow Falling plus gliding remains a reachable route because the fall-flight predicate does not reject Slow Falling.
- **Concrete movement witness:** A locally controlled player with Levitation, downward travel displacement, and no collision/resetting surface can enter ordinary travel. In 1.19.4, `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement` moves first, `Entity.checkFallDamage` adds downward displacement to `fallDistance`, then the Levitation branch resets it. In 1.20, `aiStep` resets first, then that movement adds the current downward displacement; thus the post-move value can be positive. A second valid route witness is Levitation during water travel: 1.19.4 skips the ordinary Levitation gravity/reset branch, while 1.20 clears before route dispatch and water movement can accumulate downward displacement. These are source-order witnesses, not claims about damage or a complete trajectory.
