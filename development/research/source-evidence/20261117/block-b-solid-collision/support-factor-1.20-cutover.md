## Writer order, retry, and movement readers

- 1.20 Mojmap `Entity.move` (lines 612–640) resolves collision, advances position if the clipped displacement passes the vanilla cutoff, and calls `setOnGroundWithKnownMovement(verticalCollisionBelow, clippedMovement)`. `checkSupportingBlock` (lines 567–587) queries the feet-thin AABB first. A found support or previous `onGroundNoBlocks` stores that first optional. Otherwise, if clipped movement is available, it retries the same AABB shifted by the negative clipped X/Z movement and stores the retry optional. Without a movement vector in that branch, it leaves the stored support as-is. It sets `onGroundNoBlocks` from whether the final query result is empty. The airborne branch clears the stored support and flag.
- 1.20 Mojmap `CollisionGetter.findSupportingBlock` (lines 95–109) minimizes squared distance from entity position to the block center, choosing the greater position by `compareTo` on an exact tie. It copies the selected position out of the collision iterator.
- 1.19.4 Mojmap `Entity.getBlockPosBelowThatAffectsMyMovement` is at lines 760–762, with jump/speed consumers at 744–758. 1.20 Mojmap `LivingEntity.travel` (lines 2109–2112) obtains ground friction from the same position selector used by `onSoulSpeedBlock` (lines 471–477) and by the block jump/speed reads through `Entity`. The 1.19.4 travel equivalent calls the old selector at line 2089. At the 1.20 factor offset, ordinary supports and fences retain tracked X/Z and recompute Y. Walls and fence gates retain tracked Y too.

The separate static product reconciliation by `/root/support_factor_pos_reconcile` confirmed that `GroundFrictionSamplePoint` restores the old factor query with the 1.19.4 resolver boundary. Its wording about the 1.20 fence exception is broader than the source: the factor offset `0.500001F` exceeds the fence exception's `<= 0.5` gate. This discrepancy is in the explanatory comment. The historical factor code reads the old position-column sample. This evidence covers the position operation and its callers.
