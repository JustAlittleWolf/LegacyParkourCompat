- **Strict clipping:** 1.9 `Box.intersectZ` lines 177–190 (1.9.3 lines 179 onward; 1.10 lines 183–200) requires strict X and Y overlap using `!(box.maxX <= this.minX)`, `!(box.minX >= this.maxX)`, and the corresponding Y tests before clipping.
