## Source-ordered scan and individual first-hit proof

`World.getCollisions` computes scan minima as `floor(box.min)-1` and exclusive maxima as `ceil(box.max)+1`, then loops x ascending, z ascending, y ascending (`World.java` lines 964–1001). It skips only candidates where both x and z are scan-edge coordinates. `WorldBorder.contains(BlockPos)` is strict at the outside cell: `(x+1)>minX && x<maxX && (z+1)>minZ && z<maxZ` (`WorldBorder.java` line 24). Thus the first outside block positions are x=10 at the east face, x=−11 at west, z=−11 at north, and z=10 at south.

The tangential query range is approximately `[-0.1,1.1]` on the other axis. Its padded candidate coordinates are `−2..2`, all strictly inboard of the opposite border sides at `−10` and `10`. So no tangential face contributes synthetic cells. For east, the padded scan is x=`7..11`, z=`−2..2`; x=7..9 are inboard and x=10 is first outside, producing the first synthetic cell `(10,63,−2)`. For west, scan x=`−12..−8`, z=`−2..2`; the corner `(−12,−2)` is skipped, then `(−12,−1)` is outside and is the first synthetic cell. For north, scan x=`−2..2`, z=`−12..−8`; `(−2,−12)` is skipped as a double scan edge and `(−2,−11)` is the first outside cell. For south, scan x=`−2..2`, z=`7..11`; the double edge `(−2,7)` is skipped, then `(−2,10)` is the first outside cell. In each case y=63 is first in the vertical loop. These first scan cells are distinct from the first ray-hit cells shown in the table.

For east, x=10/z=−2 and z=−1 candidates do not intersect the rays at z=.2/.8; at z=0, y=63 only face-touches the query and is strictly rejected, then y=64 is the first ray hit. For west, x=−12 scan cells miss the query; x=−11/z=−2 and z=−1 miss the rays at z=.2/.8; z=0,y=63 is face-only contact and y=64 is first hit. For north, the first z=−11 wall candidates at x=−2 and x=−1 miss the query/rays; x=0,y=63 is face-only contact and y=64 is first hit. For south, z=10 candidates at x=−2 and x=−1 miss the query/rays; x=0,y=63 is face-only contact and y=64 is first hit. Each first individual wall box therefore has maxY=65. At feet y=64, AutoJump computes `u=1`. With no Jump Boost the allowance is `o=1.2`, so `u>0.5 && u<=o` holds and the 1.12.2 client sets `ticksToNextAutojump=1` on all four sides (AutoJump lines 946–948).
