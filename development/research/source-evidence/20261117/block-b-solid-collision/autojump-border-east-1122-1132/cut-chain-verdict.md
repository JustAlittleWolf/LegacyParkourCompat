# Fresh exact-source adjudication: AutoJump border cut chain

## Verdict

The 1.12.2 → 1.13.2 draft's bounded border witness is applicable, but its claimed 1.13.2 first hit at `maxY=65` and same `ticksToNextAutojump` result are wrong. The full-cube border cells are accumulated into one `IntVoxelShape`; `getBoundingBoxes()` invokes `forEachBox`, which merges the contiguous Y run. For the stated geometry the merged box is `[10,11] × [63,67] × [0,1]`, so its first-hit `maxY` is 67, its center sample is at y=66, and (with that sample empty) the timer does not set. The 1.12.2 per-cell list instead reaches the y=64 cell first: `maxY=65`, center sample y=65, `u=1`, timer sets. This is a real source-level timer difference for the corrected witness.

The 1.13.2 → 1.14 draft's bounded endpoint result is confirmed, with a correction to its 1.14 center explanation: the intersecting outside-border AABB has both `maxX=+Infinity` and Y endpoints `(-Infinity,+Infinity)`. Its center is `( +Infinity, NaN, finite-z )`, not only NaN in Y. `BlockPos(Vec3d)` converts these to x=`Integer.MAX_VALUE`, y=0; in the client world the corresponding far-out chunk request returns the empty chunk, whose state is void air. The y=1 sample is empty, `s` remains `+Infinity`, and the timer does not set. Thus this endpoint changes the first-hit height from the finite 1.13.2 scan cap (67 in this witness) to infinity, but both versions leave the timer unchanged in the corrected empty-sample case. No intermediate patch boundary is established.

## Bounded trace

Use a stationary border centered at `(0,0)`, size 20 (`maxX=10`), loaded scan chunks, legal grounded player center `(9.7,64,0)`, width 0.6 and height 1.8, `outsideWorldBorder=false`, on ground, not sneaking/riding, nonzero forward input, yaw `-90°`, speed 0.1, no jump boost, and timer zero at AutoJump entry. The move callback may have `dx=dz=0`; the zero-displacement fallback points east. The two horizontal rays are at y=64.51 and z offsets ±0.3; their x range extends from 9.7 past x=10. The broad AutoJump query box spans x `[9.1,11.0]`, y `[64,65.8]`, z `[-0.6,0.6]`. Leave the ray corridor without other qualifying collisions. For the samples, ensure the raw world block at `(10,65,0)` (1.12.2) and `(10,66,0)` (1.13.2) is empty; the earlier draft's air assumptions at x=9 do not establish these sampled states.

- **1.12.2:** `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move` calls AutoJump after `super.move`. `World.getCollisions` scans x/z/y and substitutes stone for out-of-border cells when the entity is in the one-block shell. At x=10,z=0, the first cell intersected by the y=64.51 ray is y=64, whose box is `[10,11] × [64,65] × [0,1]`. The box center floors to `(10,64,0)`; the single `t=1` clearance sample reads y=65. Empty leaves `s=65`; `u=65-64=1`, satisfying `!(u<=0.5F) && !(u>1.2F)`, so the timer is assigned 1.
- **1.13.2:** The inside-state border-shell gate is true because the entity position is strictly inside the border expanded by one block. The finite collision scan has integer bounds x `[8,12)`, y `[63,67)`, z `[-2,2)`. Since x=10 lies outside the border, all four y cells 63,64,65,66 at `(10,y,0)` enter the full-cube `VoxelSet`. `IntVoxelShape` maps its integer extent to these world coordinates. `VoxelShape.getBoundingBoxes()` delegates to `forEachBox`; the contiguous four-cell Y column merges to one box with `maxY=67` (not 65). The box center is y=65, so the `t=1` sample is y=66. Empty leaves `s=67`; `u=3>1.2F`, so the timer is not assigned.
- **1.14:** The border is an actual complement shape built from a full shape minus the finite border interior with Y `(-Infinity,+Infinity)`. `WorldView.getCollisions(entity, queryBox, ...)` adds it first exactly when the entity AABB touches the border shell under `contract(1e-7)` / `expand(1e-7)` overlap checks. A post-move player AABB face at x=10 meets that gate, and the outward query overlaps its east exterior. The first ray-crossed border AABB has `maxY=+Infinity`; the AutoJump float cast gives `s=+Infinity`. Its center calculation gives x=+Infinity and y=NaN. `BlockPos(Vec3d)` floors +Infinity to `Integer.MAX_VALUE` and NaN to 0. The client world's far-out chunk request returns its `EmptyChunk`; `getBlockState` there is void air, so the y=1 sample is empty. `s` remains +Infinity and `u>1.2F`, so the timer is not assigned.

These conclusions use source operation order, scan bounds and numeric conversions only. They establish no runtime parity or broader movement coverage.
