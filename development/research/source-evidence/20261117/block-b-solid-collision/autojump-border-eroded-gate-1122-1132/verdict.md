# Independent verdict: 1.12.2–1.13.2 AutoJump border witness

Candidate report SHA-256: `e830cb9ccff49340d09ac9a5a84dc84ce9f50f3ba2ef2476d66eef594b3e74a8` (matched before review).

## Verdict

**Positive source finding, with conditional applicability.** I independently derived and then checked the bounded witness. With the stated reachable tick state and loaded blocks, 1.12.2 sets `ticksToNextAutojump` to 1 while 1.13.2 returns without setting it. This verdict accepts only this exact 1.12.2 → 1.13.2 endpoint comparison; it does not establish the intermediate patch cutover or runtime parity.

## Independently checked evidence

- Exact source identity: 1.12.2 Ornithe Feather build.2 source manifest SHA-256 `b8a37ccfccd2aac5f40f5e34fec85873dbdfa93a266043c103e592e4a8c949da`; 1.13.2 build.2 manifest SHA-256 `2c8cfb646bf622fb26ac0ca0cb5e02a354fb512e29f8b1010f1aaacd509e1211`. Both match their ready markers. I rehashed the relevant local source files against their manifest entries. Ready/provenance records identify the exact release IDs, Feather mapping inputs, and Vineflower 1.12.0 output. The 1.12.2 verification record notes a regenerated derived remapped intermediate difference, but reports zero source-file differences and no raw-input differences.
- The local-player `move` wrapper calls `autoJump` after superclass movement and passes the actual post-collision displacement cast to float. For the stipulated accepted `dx≈0.10`, the magnitude-squared gate is about `0.01`, above the `0.001` input/yaw fallback threshold. Eastward movement aligned with yaw -90 passes the direction gate. Speed II applies a `+0.2` operation-2 modifier per level to the player's `0.1` base speed, so level II gives `f=0.14`; `p=max(f*7,1/l)≈0.98`. Crucially, the endpoint is `this.x + dx + p`: `8.99 + 0.10 + 0.98≈10.07`.
- With a static square border `[-10,10]` in X/Z, both border-center predicates accept x=8.89 and x=8.99: when the prior flag is false the upper bound is 11; when true it is 9. z=0.5 is inside both the expanded and eroded Z intervals. Thus both versions classify the player as inside for this call regardless of the prior outside-border flag; both finish with the flag false. The collision query consequently treats loaded block positions at x=10 as synthetic full collision blocks even though their vanilla state is AIR.
- The player-width-expanded AutoJump box reaches x≈10.67 and has vertical bounds y=64..65.8. Its two lateral eastbound rays are at z≈0.2 and z≈0.8, y=64.51; each crosses the synthetic x=10,z=0 wall cell. The player remains supported at feet y=64 by the loaded floor at y=63. AutoJump's direct headroom reads at the player column (y=65,66), and its obstacle-clearance read at (x=10,y=65,z=0), must be real loaded AIR; these reads do not replace the vanilla block state with the synthetic border block.
- In 1.12.2, `World.getCollisions` loops x, then z, then y and appends individual collision boxes. The first ray-intersecting border cube is x=10,z=0,y=64, with `maxY=65`; relative obstacle height is 1.0, within the no-Jump-Boost allowance of 1.2, so the timer is set to 1. In 1.13.2, `WorldView.getBlockCollisions` stores outside-border full voxels and returns their aggregate `IntVoxelShape`. For this query its scan ranges are x=7..11, y=63..66, z=-2..2 (upper bounds exclusive after padding). Loaded out-of-border cells at x=10 include the full y=63..66 run; `VoxelShape.getBoundingBoxes()` yields a ray-intersecting aggregate box with `maxY=67`. AutoJump uses that box's full top, giving relative height 3.0, above 1.2, and returns before writing the timer.

## Applicability and limits

The witness requires AutoJump enabled, countdown zero, on-ground, not sneaking or riding, nonzero raw movement input, accepted eastward `dx≈0.10,dz≈0`, yaw -90, Speed II, no Jump Boost, the stated border, and loaded support/headroom/clearance blocks. The exact accepted displacement is a condition of the witness; source does not imply every ordinary input produces that delta. The old/new result is source-derived and conditional on those states. No launch or runtime reproduction was performed.

Primary source members: 1.12.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move/autoJump`, `World.getCollisions/isWithinBorder`, `WorldBorder.contains(BlockPos)`, `StatusEffect` Speed modifier, and `PlayerEntity.initAttributes/getSpeed`; 1.13.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.move/autoJump`, `WorldView.getBlockCollisions/getCollisions/collidesWithBorder`, `WorldBorder.contains(BlockPos)`, `VoxelShape.getBoundingBoxes`, `VoxelSet.forEachBox`, `StatusEffect` Speed modifier, and `PlayerEntity.initAttributes/getSpeed`.
