# AutoJump border applicability in exact 1.14.4 to 1.15.2

Both exact Mojmap endpoints admit the stationary border candidate in the same thin contact shell. On the first iterator advance, the border must overlap the current entity box inflated by 1e-7 and clear that box deflated by 1e-7. Swept-probe overreach alone is insufficient. When admitted, the unclipped synthetic border comes before cursor block candidates and retains infinite Y. Its horizontal boundary uses the same floor and ceil construction in both releases.

The later [decomposition addendum](decomposition-addendum.md) and [independent verdict](west-origin-verdict.md) resolve the sampled block for the audited face cases. The unbounded boxes are emitted west, negative Z, positive Z, and east. In Minecraft coordinates, negative Z is north and positive Z is south. The archived reports transpose those two labels; their intervals, output order, sample coordinates, and disposition stand.

For the loaded west tangent at X=0, the first box center is NaN on all axes and floors to the origin. The one clearance read at (0,1,0) sees a full cube and replaces infinite maxY with 2. At feet Y=1, rise 1 arms the timer in both exact releases. The player is supported at (0,0,-1) and clear of the sampled cube. For the audited centered negative-Z, positive-Z, and east face cases, center X is positive infinity. The converted MAX X sample resolves to the client's out-of-range empty chunk, leaving infinite height and suppressing the timer.

This is a bounded no-difference result for the named faces and clearance premises. Corners, other shapes, intermediate aliases, runtime parity and the [later border timeline](../../../20261028/autojump-border-1152/comparison.md) remain separate.
