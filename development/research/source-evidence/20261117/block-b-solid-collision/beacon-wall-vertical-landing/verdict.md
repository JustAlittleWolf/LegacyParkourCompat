# Independent source verdict: Beacon–wall vertical landing

**Verdict: confirmed for the bounded source claim.** The exact Beacon-neighbor predicate changes the ordinary wall state: the proposed vertical SELF move is unobstructed in 1.13, 1.13.1 and 1.13.2, but clips on the north arm in 1.14, 1.14.4 and 26.2. This reviews one lawful move and its collision dependencies; it does not freeze full-pair coverage or establish an intermediate patch cutover.

Reviewed author proof identity: `beacon-wall-vertical-landing/proof-r2.md`, SHA-256 `846a384a6b5609c73f957fdcfd5acab2c15710aa3964b8e31eb4a2240001c8b6` (matched).

## Independent trace

Coordinates: wall W=(0,64,0), Beacon N=(0,64,-1); other horizontal neighbors and W-above are loaded AIR; wall is dry. Player width 0.6, center x=.5,z=-.1, feet y=65.65. Its box is x=[.2,.8], z=[-.4,.2]. The bounded operation is one ordinary `SELF` move with dx=dz=0, dy approximately -.3, starting ground-free. At its start the feet are above both the wall collision top (65.5) and Beacon top (65), so this is not an initially overlapping placement.

- **1.13 / 1.13.1 / 1.13.2 Feather:** `WallBlock.getPlacementState` tests the north Beacon's south-facing `FaceShape`, but `shouldConnectTo` also applies `isExceptionForConnection`; that delegates to `Block.isExceptionForAttachment`, whose `isExceptionForPlacement` explicitly includes `Blocks.BEACON`. The secondary middle-pole/fence-gate case does not apply. Therefore NORTH=false; AIR makes E/S/W false. The UP formula evaluates true with all four booleans false. `WallBlock.getCollisionShape` selects the no-arm post; `PaneBlock.makeShapes` puts its z interval at [4,12]/16=[.25,.75]. The player's z-max .2 clears the post. Beacon's full-block collision ends at y=65, below the move's requested final feet y≈65.35. The move reaches y≈65.35 without collision.
- **1.14 / 1.14.4 Feather:** north placement passes the exact opposing face-solid result (south face of the north neighbor). `Block.isExceptionForAttachment` no longer lists Beacon; Beacon has its default full collision shape and the tested face is full, so NORTH=true. Other side bits are false; UP=true. Both wall constructors use collision-shape height 24/16. The north arm spans local x=[5,11]/16, z=[0,11]/16, y=[0,24]/16. It overlaps the player box horizontally, while the central post at z≥.25 does not. The downward movement is clipped when the feet reach 65.5, before reaching the Beacon top at 65.
- **26.2 unobfuscated:** the wall uses `isFaceSturdy(..., SOUTH)` and its connection-exception list does not include Beacon. The full Beacon support face connects north. With air above, the state calculation gives UP=true and NORTH=LOW (other sides NONE). Although LOW's outline arm is shorter, `collisionShapes` are built with `(24,24)`, so the LOW arm collision top remains 65.5. The same overlap and vertical clip follow.

For the one-axis move, the legacy resolver calculates only the downward Y offset before the zero X/Z components; modern `Entity.collide` likewise resolves Y against block collision shapes. No step-up branch can change the result: requested horizontal movement is zero, and the reviewed legacy/1.14/modern guards require horizontal displacement or collision. This does not require a tick simulation. In 1.13.x the resulting vertical segment stays above the Beacon top; in the tested 1.14, 1.14.4 and 26.2 sources the wall arm stops it even earlier.
