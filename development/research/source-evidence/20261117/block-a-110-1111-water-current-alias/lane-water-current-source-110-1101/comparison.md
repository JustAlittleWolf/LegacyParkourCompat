# 1.10 → 1.10.1 ordinary water-current comparison

Scope: the LocalPlayer-reachable, non-falling ordinary-water current path only. This is a bounded no-difference result, not a full-pair movement audit. Lava, falling-water special handling, eye/submersion geometry and other collision geometry are outside this assignment.

## Exact source identity

Both canonical mapping roots are `decompiled_minecraft/<release>/ornithe-feather/`. Their `.decompile-provenance.json` manifests identify the matching Mojang client release metadata and exact Feather mapping metadata/artifact. The Feather artifacts are 1.10 `build.2` and 1.10.1 `build.2`. Every cited Java file's manifest `sources` SHA-256 was recomputed from the local UTF-8 file and matched. `source-roster.json` records the source and manifest hashes plus equal SHA-256 digests for the checked method bodies; its SHA-256 is `4aa1b97837a9ff3c6bd466a598cf607a0ea35ae497d6892a5b7ecf585ba74c24`.

## Compared behavior

- **Caller box and water scan:** `Entity.checkWaterCollisions` passes `getShape().grown(0.0, -0.4F, 0.0).contract(0.001)` to `World.applyLiquidDrag` for water (Entity.java:862–866). The world floors each minimum and ceils each maximum, then scans `[floor(min), ceil(max))` in x, y, z order. Before scanning, it requires `isAreaLoaded(i, k, m, j, l, n, true)` (World.java:1564–1582). The shared `isAreaLoaded` implementation rejects only when `maxY < 0` or `minY >= 256`, shifts x/z bounds to chunk coordinates and checks the chunk ranges inclusively (World.java:216–235). Thus the scan's maximum block coordinate is exclusive, while the loaded-area chunk check includes the chunk containing the ceiled maximum; at a chunk boundary that can require the neighboring chunk even though the scan does not visit its first block.
- **Per-cell flow and aggregation:** A scanned cell contributes only when its material matches the requested water material and its top test passes (`ceil(bounds.maxY) >= y + 1 - getHeightLoss(level)`). The block's `applyMaterialDrag` adds that cell's `getFlow` result to the aggregate (World.java:1583–1590; LiquidBlock.java:131–175). For non-falling water, `getFlow` walks horizontal directions in `Direction.Plane.HORIZONTAL` order. A same-material side neighbor of depth `j` contributes direction offsets scaled by `j - i`, where `i` is this cell depth. If the side neighbor has no water and does not block movement, water depth `j` directly below it contributes offsets scaled by `j - (i - 8)`. The resulting per-cell vector is normalized before `applyMaterialDrag` adds it to the aggregate. World sums these per-cell normalized vectors in scan order and, after releasing the pooled position, normalizes the total only when its length is positive.
- **Impulse gate and timing:** After the scan, the normalized aggregate is multiplied by `0.014` and added to the entity's x/y/z velocity only when its length is positive and `entity.hasLiquidCollision()` is true (World.java:1596–1605). PlayerEntity defines that gate as `!abilities.flying` (PlayerEntity.java:1736–1738); flying therefore suppresses the current impulse, while the liquid-contact boolean may still be returned. `Entity.checkWaterCollisions` stores that boolean in `inWater` (Entity.java:862–876). Entity base tick calls this check before returning (Entity.java:312–369); LivingEntity tick reaches `mobTick` later (LivingEntity.java:1525–1588), and LocalClientPlayerEntity's tick delegates through `super.tick()` before its local movement work (LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:152–166). The current impulse is consequently in velocity before that tick's later player travel/movement consumer. `LivingEntity.moveRelative` has the distinct player-flying bypass for water movement controls (LivingEntity.java:1332–1335, 1470–1481).

## Result

No difference was found between 1.10 and 1.10.1 in the assigned path. The checked `World.applyLiquidDrag`, `World.isAreaLoaded`, `LiquidBlock.getFlow`, `LiquidBlock.applyMaterialDrag`, `Entity.checkWaterCollisions`, Entity/LivingEntity base-tick and movement ordering, LocalClientPlayerEntity tick/mob-tick, and `PlayerEntity.hasLiquidCollision` bodies have matching method-body SHA-256 values in the roster. The full files for LiquidBlock, Entity, LivingEntity, LocalClientPlayerEntity and PlayerEntity also have matching manifest/source hashes. World.java differs as a file, but the bounded current, area-loaded and flow-related methods listed above match.
