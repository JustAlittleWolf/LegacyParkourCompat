# Precise source claim: falling-water fence-face boundary

For the inspected canonical exact sources, the dry oak-fence falling-water side cue changes between 1.11.2 and 1.12.0. In 1.11.2, `LiquidBlock.isFaceSolid` rejects same-material neighbors, accepts UP, rejects ICE, then delegates to the base material-solidity predicate; since oak fence is registered as `Material.WOOD`, a horizontal oak-fence neighbor qualifies regardless of its partial face. Starting in 1.12.0, after the same-material, UP and ICE branches, the helper also rejects attachment exceptions and stairs and requires `BlockState.getFaceShape(world,pos,face) == FaceShape.SOLID`. Oak fence remains a `Material.WOOD` fence registration, but its horizontal face provider returns `FaceShape.MIDDLE_POLE`, so the same witness no longer qualifies. This exact rejection is present in 1.12.1, 1.12.2, 1.13, 1.13.1, 1.13.2, Feather 1.14, Yarn 1.14.1/.2/.3, and Mojmap 1.14.4, where the face check becomes a full collision-face test.

The sampled falling branch retains `LEVEL >= 8` through 1.13 and the same horizontal direction for the neighbor and above-neighbor queries, adding `normalize() + (0,-6,0)` and normalizing afterward. The explicit same-fluid/material, UP, and ice branches remain ahead of the general face query; 1.13+ uses fluid type rather than material identity. The fence witness does not depend on those three branches.

Scope: source-level dependency for the era-existing dry fence only. First stable observed onset among the listed exact versions: **1.12.0**. This does not claim uninspected versions or establish the generic water upper bound.
