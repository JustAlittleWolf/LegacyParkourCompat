Difference: Legacy falling flow (`LEVEL >= 8`) checks each horizontal neighbor and its upper block using the same horizontal direction, then adds `(0,-6,0)` to the normalized horizontal flow on a hit and normalizes the result. Its face predicate treats every `Material.isSolid()` neighbor as solid on every side, except for same-material liquid, `UP`, and ice handling in `LiquidBlock`. 26.2 retains the same-direction face query and flow normalization but asks whether that specific support face is full; same-fluid and ice exceptions remain. The exact introduction release is unknown between the inspected 1.11.2 and 26.2 endpoints.

Movement meaning: With one falling-water cell (`LEVEL=8` historically; `FALLING=true` in 26.2), no horizontal water gradient, and a dry oak fence beside it as the only side candidate, the legacy material test accepts the fence (`1.8 Block.java:936`, `Material.WOOD`; `Material.java:55-57`) and gives that water cell a downward flow vector. In 26.2 the fence's narrow post and optional arms do not make a full horizontal support face (`Blocks.java:2021-2026`; `FenceBlock.java:37-42`; `CrossCollisionBlock.java:46-68`), so that cell's flow stays zero. A non-flying ordinary `LocalPlayer` inherits the player fluid-current path and can receive the legacy downward current, while this cell contributes no 26.2 current. This witness isolates the face predicate and does not establish an intermediate patch cutover.
