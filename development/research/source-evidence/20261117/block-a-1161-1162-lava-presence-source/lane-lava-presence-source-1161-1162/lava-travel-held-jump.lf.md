# Lava travel and held-jump response
1.16.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)` 1912–1976 (lava branch 1957–1976), `jumpInLiquid` 1900–1902, held-jump block 2424–2451.
1.16.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)` 1914–1978 (lava branch 1959–1978), `jumpInLiquid` 1902–1904, held-jump block 2426–2453.
Difference: The compared travel method, held-jump block and `jumpInLiquid` body are exact text matches after CRLF and line-offset normalization. Their `isInLava()`/fluid-depth inputs changed with the Entity lava-presence producer: 1.16.1 can branch on the prior movement's lava callback flag even when cached lava depth is zero; 1.16.2 requires `!firstTick` and cached lava depth strictly greater than zero. Travel's lava branch also requires effective AI or local control, affected-by-fluids and inability to stand on the block-position fluid. It moves relatively at `0.02F`, moves, then for lava depth `<= getFluidJumpThreshold()` multiplies velocity by `(0.5, 0.8F, 0.5)` and applies fluid-falling adjustment; deeper lava scales the whole vector by `0.5`. Both paths then add `-d / 4.0` vertical gravity unless no-gravity, and the horizontal-collision escape may set Y to `0.3F`. Held jump is gated by `jumping && isAffectedByFluids`; it selects lava depth when `isInLava()`, otherwise water depth. The nested branch can call `jumpInLiquid(LAVA)`, whose unchanged implementation adds `0.04F` to vertical velocity. Under the old flag, a midair held jump can reach that lava impulse with zero cached lava depth; under the new predicate a zero-depth cache cannot select it.
Movement meaning: The branch source itself did not change, but the producer change changes when a locally controlled player receives lava drag/falling adjustment or the held-jump vertical impulse. The visible difference depends on lava depth, ground state, local-control/AI and fluid-affect/stand-on gates. This finding refers to the producer/predicate finding for the changed input semantics.
