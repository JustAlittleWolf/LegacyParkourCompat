# Underwater boat passenger water-current response

1.19.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateInWaterStateAndDoWaterCurrentPushing`, lines 1069-1083; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 2797-2862.
1.19.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateInWaterStateAndDoWaterCurrentPushing`, lines 1074-1087; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 2826-2890; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `rideTick`, lines 1704-1709; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientLevel.java`, `tickPassenger`, lines 270-280; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, lines 2052-2095.
Difference: In 1.19.2, all boat passengers set `wasTouchingWater=false` and skip water-height/current sampling. In 1.19.3, only surfaced-boat passengers do; an underwater-boat passenger is measured against water and, when the player's deflated box intersects loaded water with nonzero aggregate flow, fluid pushing is enabled, and the flow vector passes the method's thresholds, the current-derived vector is added to the player's `deltaMovement` at scale `0.014`. The paired predicate remains surfaced-only in 1.19.4 and 1.20.1. Flight disables the player fluid-push and fluid-affectability gates.
Movement meaning: On the logical client, passenger `rideTick()` zeros velocity before the player tick; base tick then writes eligible water current into the player delta, and `LivingEntity.travel()` consumes and friction/fall-adjusts it. `positionRider()` later restores the rider's vehicle-relative position but does not clear delta. The resulting player `deltaMovement` differs at the end of that passenger tick. A following passenger tick zeros it, and the authoritative ordinary dismount correction with no relative velocity flags also zeros it; no persistent post-dismount trajectory is claimed. The shift-down water control is an unchanged, conditional consequence of the new water-state predicate and is not part of this independent current-input finding.
