## Blockers

- The immutable draft overstates the Camel witness unless it says the `UPDATE_1_20` feature must be enabled.
- The source proof establishes that `stopRiding()` does not clear sprint and that a sprinting jump receives the impulse. It does not, by itself, establish an end-to-end client dismount-to-jump path that survives the intervening `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` stop gates. Preserve this as a conditional handoff claim unless that path is separately proven.
