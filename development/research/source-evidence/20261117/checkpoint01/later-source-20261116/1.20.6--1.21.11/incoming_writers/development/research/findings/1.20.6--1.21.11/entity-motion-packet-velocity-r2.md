# Entity-motion packet velocity representation

1.20.6, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.20.6/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleSetEntityMotion`, lines 515–521; `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundSetEntityMotionPacket.java`, constructor and wire codec, lines 20–46; `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerEntity.java`, hurt-marked send and `broadcastAndSend`, lines 206–213 and 307–312.
1.21.11, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleSetEntityMotion`, lines 604–610; `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundSetEntityMotionPacket.java`, payload and codec, lines 14–34; `net/minecraft/network/LpVec3.java`, `read`/`write`/`sanitize`/`pack`/`unpack`, lines 24–75; `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerEntity.java`, hurt-marked send, lines 216–219.

Difference: both handlers look up the packet entity ID in the client level and call `lerpMotion` only when that entity exists. In 1.20.6 the packet constructor clamps each server-produced component to ±3.9, truncates `(int)(component * 8000.0)`, and writes shorts; the client divides the decoded signed shorts by `8000.0`. In 1.21.11 the packet carries a `Vec3` encoded as `LpVec3`: the writer maps NaN to zero, clamps components to ±1.7179869183E10, selects a shared scale from the maximum absolute component using `ceil`, quantizes normalized axes with `Math.round` to 32766 steps, and the reader unpacks and rescales them. The component representation and quantization therefore differ. The packet handler remains generic for any resolved entity; the old `ServerEntity.broadcastAndSend` and new `sendToTrackingPlayersAndSelf` both provide a self-send path when the hurt-marked entity is a `ServerPlayer`.

Movement meaning: when a received packet's entity ID resolves to `minecraft.player`, the packet directly replaces local-player velocity. The different cap, shared-scale encoding, and quantization can produce different velocity doubles and thus affect subsequent player movement. The handler is not player-only; it also routes velocity updates to other client-level entities.
