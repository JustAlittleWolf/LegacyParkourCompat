# Bubble-column above-contact classification
1.21.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 50-76; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleCol` and `onInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2483-2505; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, `SHORT_GRASS` registration, lines 698-710.
1.21.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 51-59; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `handleOnAboveBubbleColumn` and `handleOnInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2459-2500.
Difference: 1.21.4 dispatches above only when the block above is air. 1.21.5 dispatches above when the above block has an empty collision shape and empty fluid; otherwise it dispatches inside. The collision-shape query passes the bubble cell's position, not the above cell's position. `SHORT_GRASS` is non-air, position-independent here because it is registered `.noCollission()`, and dry, so conditional on the bubble callback being admitted it selects inside in 1.21.4 and above in 1.21.5. With `dragDown=false` and callback-entry y velocity `0.65`, above yields `min(1.8, 0.65+0.1)=0.75`; inside yields `min(0.7, 0.65+0.06)=0.70` and resets fall distance. Both response formulas predate the predicate change. This above-contact classification is distinct from the short-sweep gate first found in 1.21.10.
Movement meaning: A player whose bubble contact reaches `entityInside` can receive the above-column adjustment beginning in 1.21.5 even with a non-air dry block above that has no collision shape.
