Difference: 1.21.9 enumerates short nontrivial movement candidates from the final box translated back by `-delta`, along the segment and in the final box. Its entity-inside callback has no precision argument. 1.21.10 keeps that candidate traversal but computes `movedFar` as `from.distanceToSqr(to) > Mth.square(0.9999900000002526)` and passes `movedFar || deflatedTargetBox.intersects(blockPos)` to the still-invoked `state.entityInside(...)`. BubbleColumnBlock now encloses both above and inside response branches in `if (isPrecise)`. For BubbleColumn, the default entity-inside shape is the identical `Shapes.block()` object, so the caller's identity branch short-circuits before `collidedWithShapeMovingFrom`; ordinary collision shape being empty does not remove the entity-inside candidate. The iterator's small-motion sweep threshold changed earlier, at 1.21.9, from `Mth.square(0.99999F)` to `Mth.square(1.0E-5F)`; this prerequisite is separate from the response gate.
Movement meaning: A standard player box at feet y=0.98, centered x=z=0.5 in bubble `(0,0,0)`, can first contact from rest in a stationary water tick. Fluid falling adjustment takes y from 0 to -0.005, then the admitted above response with `dragDown=false` sets it to 0.095. On the next tick, water travel moves by 0.095 to feet y=1.075; its post-move y becomes approximately `(0.095 × 0.8F) - 0.08/16 ≈ 0.0710000011`. The final deflated box minY is 1.07499 and misses the bubble cell, yet the 1.21.9 iterator visits that cell from the target box translated back to start (minY 0.97999); the full-shape identity admits it. The 1.21.9 above response then produces approximately 0.1710000011, below the 1.8 cap. In 1.21.10 `entityInside` is still invoked with `isPrecise=false`, so neither direct branch runs and y remains approximately 0.0710000011. This separates the 1.21.9 traversal prerequisite from the 1.21.10 bubble response gate and accounts for actual water travel order.
