Difference: in the 1.12.2 water branch, after moving, `velocityY` is multiplied by `0.8F`, then fixed `0.02` is subtracted when gravity is enabled. In 1.13, `d` is `0.08`, or `0.01` when pre-movement Y is nonpositive and Slow Falling is active; after the same damping, gravity is applied only when gravity is enabled and the player is not sprinting, as `d/16.0`. Before subtraction, 1.13 assigns `-0.003` when post-damping Y is nonpositive, `abs(Y - 0.005) >= 0.003`, and `abs(Y - d/16.0) < 0.003`. For ordinary non-sprinting movement this changes the decrement from `0.02` to `0.005`; sprint suppresses the 1.13 gravity operation. Damping precedes the clamp-or-subtraction; the horizontal-collision response follows it.
Movement meaning: for an unmounted local player in water, not flying or swimming, with gravity enabled and no vertical collision altering movement, downward velocity receives less gravity in ordinary non-sprinting travel in 1.13+. A reachable 1.13 clamp input is pre-movement Y `-0.002` with Slow Falling and non-swimming state: player wrapper skips swim Y adjustment, `0.8F` damping gives `-0.0016`, `d/16.0=0.000625`, and the LivingEntity clamp sets Y to `-0.003`. Patch introduction is bounded only to after 1.12.2 through 1.13.

The 1.14.4 control retains `0.8F` damping and `d/16.0`, but its clamp uses `bl`, the sign of Y snapshotted at travel entry, while 1.13 tests current post-damping Y. It does not prove the clamp predicate is identical.
