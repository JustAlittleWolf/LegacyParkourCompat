# Sprint-dependent horizontal drag in water

1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.12.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative(float,float,float)`, lines 1555–1579; `getBaseMovementSpeedMultiplier()` lines 1421–1422.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.13/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative(float,float,float)`, lines 1598–1629. The same body occurs in 1.13.1/1.13.2 lines 1617–1654 and 1.14.4 Mojmap `LivingEntity.travel(Vec3)` lines 1880–1907.
Difference: 1.12.2 starts water drag `f` at the base multiplier `0.8F`, regardless of sprinting. In 1.13+, `f` starts at `0.9F` while sprinting and otherwise uses the `0.8F` base. Depth Strider level `h` is capped at `3.0F`, halved off-ground, and for positive `h` updates horizontal drag with `f += (0.54600006F - f) * h / 3.0F`. The separate input scale starts at `g=0.02F` and uses `g += (getSpeed() - g) * h / 3.0F`. Dolphin’s Grace then assigns `f=0.96F`. After moving, double X/Z velocity is multiplied by float `f` widened to double; Y damping is separately `0.8F`. In Feather 1.12.2/1.13 `PlayerEntity.getSpeed()` reads the movement-speed attribute (lines 1407–1409 / 1483–1486); producers are outside this finding.
Movement meaning: with no Depth Strider or Dolphin’s Grace, a post-move double X of `0.1` becomes `0.0800000011920929` after `0.8F` multiplication or `0.08999999761581422` after `0.9F`. Thus sprinting preserves more horizontal velocity in 1.13+. For Depth Strider level 3, Java float arithmetic yields `0.54600006F` nonsprinting and `0.546F` sprinting on ground; airborne (`h=1.5F`) it yields `0.67300004F` nonsprinting and `0.723F` sprinting. Dolphin’s Grace subsequently sets the drag to `0.96F`. Patch introduction is bounded only to after 1.12.2 through 1.13.
