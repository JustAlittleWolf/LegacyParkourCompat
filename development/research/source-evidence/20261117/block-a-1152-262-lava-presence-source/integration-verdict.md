Approved for the ordinary local client player lava-presence operation in 1.15, 1.15.1 and 1.15.2. The reviewed witness uses an airborne, unmounted, nonflying player and the vanilla packaged lava tag. The change does not establish runtime parity.

- Existing `EntityMixin` dispatches `afterMove` after position, collision and fall handling, before block-speed scaling. The no-physics branch and the zero-limited piston return bypass it, preserving the previous flag. Processed movement clears the flag before the historical inclusive scan. The original inset endpoints are floored separately. The historical vertical overlap gate and the full original horizontal chunk range are checked before reading. Cells outside historical Y levels `[0, 256)` cannot contribute. Missing client columns supply empty-chunk AIR through the direct block lookup, as in the 1.15 client path.

This retains the tested distinction from the derived fluid state, including the source-versus-flowing identities in the exact witness. The predicate adds no depth or surface test.
- Existing `EntityLavaPresenceMixin` reads the retained flag for the local client player. Its ordinary base-tick lava check and the next living local held-jump and travel decisions see that state. For the audited otherwise dry, airborne inputs the held jump reaches the lava jump operation even with zero tracked height because the shallow ground check requires on-ground. Travel reaches the existing lava branch. Body/eye state, native fluid depth, current, server authority and other movement operations keep their existing implementation. The evidence does not cover downstream trajectory equivalence for other states or custom replacement of tag membership.
