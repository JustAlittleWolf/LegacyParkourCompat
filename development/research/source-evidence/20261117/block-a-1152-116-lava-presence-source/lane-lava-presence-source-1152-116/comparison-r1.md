## Exact tick and movement ordering
In both releases, `LocalPlayer.tick` delegates through `Player.tick` to `LivingEntity.tick`; `LivingEntity.tick` calls its superclass tick/base-tick work before `aiStep`. The base tick refreshes old water height/state in 1.15.2 (`Entity.java:348–364`) and clears/recomputes the per-tag `fluidHeight` map in 1.16 (`Entity.java:354–374`). In `LivingEntity.aiStep`, the jump section precedes its call to `travel` (1.15.2 `:2264–2287`; 1.16 `:2423–2457`). The lava flag is a separate cache: `Entity.move` resets it immediately before `checkInsideBlocks` at the end of the processed-move path (1.15.2 `Entity.java:533–540`; 1.16 `:551–558`). The travel branch admission and the earlier jump decision in that aiStep pass have already been selected before travel's `move` reaches this reset/callback. A processed move's callback result can feed a later consumer pass, subject to another processed move changing it first.

`Entity.move` does not reach reset/callback in either early path: `noPhysics` updates the box and location in its separate branch (1.15.2 `:450–454`; 1.16 `:469–473`), and a `PISTON` move limited to `Vec3.ZERO` returns before the processed-move body (1.15.2 `:455–460`; 1.16 `:474–479`). `checkInsideBlocks` invokes block-state `entityInside` callbacks for positions inside its box/chunk scan (1.15.2 `Entity.java:792–823`; 1.16 `:803–828`).

The reachable local-player input path is `LocalPlayer.tick` → `Player.tick` → `LivingEntity.tick`; the LocalPlayer override `isEffectiveAi()` returns true (1.15.2 `LocalPlayer.java:490–492`; 1.16 `:519–521`). During LivingEntity's ai step, the effective-AI path invokes virtual `serverAiStep`; `LocalPlayer.serverAiStep` copies `input.jumping` into `jumping` under `isControlledCamera` (1.15.2 `LocalPlayer.java:607–618`; 1.16 `:634–645`). The LocalPlayer `aiStep` override delegates to Player `aiStep` (`:778` / `:813`), whose override delegates to LivingEntity `aiStep` (1.15.2 `Player.java:497–515`; 1.16 `:483–500`). The copied jump input therefore reaches the held-jump consumer when the LocalPlayer is the controlled camera. The assignment is in `serverAiStep`, not `aiStep`.

## Player travel path precision
`Player.travel` is overridden in both compared releases. In 1.15.2 it is at `Player.java:1448–1478`; in 1.16 it is at `Player.java:1380–1410`. Both versions delegate to `super.travel` in the ordinary non-flying branch and also call it in the flying branch; the flying branch then adjusts vertical movement. Therefore do not describe 1.16 as lacking a Player travel override. For 1.16, `Player.isAffectedByFluids()` returns `!abilities.flying` (`Player.java:1005–1007`), and inherited `LivingEntity.canStandOnFluid` returns false. Thus the new inherited lava travel gate applies to non-flying Player movement; flying Player movement fails its fluid-affect gate. This corrects the audit's no-override statement without changing the accepted bounded LivingEntity difference.

## Accepted bounded differences
1. **Presence producer:** 1.15.2 lava `LiquidBlock.entityInside` unconditionally sets the Entity lava flag; 1.16 computes the fluid surface and AABB predicate `aABB.minY < f || f > aABB.maxY` before setting it. Preserve the literal float/double test. For an ordered AABB the second disjunct is subsumed by the first. The processed-move reset/callback order remains, with early bypasses above.
2. **Travel consumer:** both releases have a lava movement path after water/flying admission. 1.16 additionally gates it on `isAffectedByFluids()` and `!canStandOnFluid(...)`; its cached lava immersion `<= getFluidJumpThreshold()` selects Y multiplier `0.8F` plus fluid falling adjustment, while the deeper case uses uniform `scale(0.5)`. The flag and fluid-height map are refreshed through distinct paths and at different points.
3. **Held-jump consumer:** 1.15.2 uses its water-height/ground split and the lava flag without a lava-height test for the liquid impulse. 1.16 adds `isAffectedByFluids`, chooses cached height from lava or water, and uses the eye-height-derived threshold (`0.0` below eye height `0.4`, otherwise `0.4`) and ground state to select liquid impulse or ground jump. The liquid impulse remains `add(0.0, 0.04F, 0.0)`.

Fluid tag contents and membership, other blocks/callers, other patches, full-pair inventory and independent full-pair audit remain outside this comparison.
