# Lava travel admission and velocity
1.15.2 Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.15.2/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, lines 1831–1841 and 1913–1925; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel`, lines 1448–1478.
1.16 Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.16/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, lines 1908–1913 and 1957–1976; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getFluidJumpThreshold`, lines 2704–2706; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel`, lines 1380–1410 and `isAffectedByFluids`, lines 1005–1007.
Difference: In 1.15.2, LivingEntity travel is inside `isEffectiveAi() || isControlledByLocalInstance()`. The lava path is reached through nested admission `!isInWater() || (this instanceof Player && flying)`, then `isInLava() && !(this instanceof Player && flying)`; its body does `moveRelative(0.02F, input)`, moves with the current delta, scales delta by `0.5`, adds `(0.0, -d / 4.0, 0.0)` when gravity applies, then on horizontal collision sets Y to `0.3` if the raised-position `isFree` check succeeds. There is no lava-height branch in that body. In 1.16, the independent lava branch is admitted by `isInLava() && isAffectedByFluids() && !canStandOnFluid(fluidState.getType())`, following the water branch. After moving, `getFluidHeight(FluidTags.LAVA) <= getFluidJumpThreshold()` selects `multiply(0.5, 0.8F, 0.5)` followed by `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(d, bl, ...)`; otherwise it selects `scale(0.5)`. Gravity and horizontal collision/free-space response follow. `getFluidJumpThreshold()` is `getEyeHeight() < 0.4 ? 0.0 : 0.4`.
Player applicability: `Player.travel` is overridden in both releases and delegates to `super.travel` on its ordinary non-flying branch. The 1.16 override exists at `Player.java:1380–1410`; it is not absent. `Player.isAffectedByFluids()` returns `!abilities.flying`, while inherited `canStandOnFluid` returns false. The inherited 1.16 lava branch thus applies to non-flying Player movement; flying Player movement fails the fluid-affect gate. The 1.15.2 Player override also delegates to `super.travel` on the non-flying branch; its flying branch applies a later vertical adjustment. No post-travel vertical adjustment occurs on the ordinary non-flying branch.
Movement meaning and timing: For a non-flying Player admitted to lava travel, the 1.16 result depends on cached lava immersion relative to the eye-height threshold: shallow lava uses Y damping 0.8F and fluid falling adjustment; deeper lava uses uniform half scaling. The fluid-effect and can-stand-on-fluid gates also qualify branch admission. The callback cache is refreshed during a processed `Entity.move` after the current aiStep's jump decision and after the travel branch admission; the separately refreshed fluid-height map is available from baseTick. A newly set flag can affect a later consumer pass, subject to intervening moves. No trajectory was simulated.
