1.21.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` 598–706 and `checkInsideBlocks` 1001–1034; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/SweetBerryBushBlock.java`, `entityInside` 80–91.
1.21.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, travel and `applyEffectsFromBlocks` call 2178–2213 and 2771–2775; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` 633–637, `applyEffectsFromBlocks` 736–767 and `checkInsideBlocks` 1038–1061; `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `boxTraverseBlocks` 172–245; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/state/BlockBehaviour.java`, `getEntityInsideCollisionShape` 353–355; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/SweetBerryBushBlock.java`, `getShape` 36–38, 54–60 and `entityInside` 79–93; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, standing dimensions 136 and `makeStuckInBlock` 1582–1586.
Difference: 1.21.1 checks cells in the final AABB. 1.21.2 also traverses movement segments with squared length at least `Mth.square(0.99999F)`. The age-1 bush's actual collision shape ends at local z=15/16, while the default entity-inside shape provider returns `Shapes.block()`, causing the swept scan to skip its movement-shape filter. A non-flying player can therefore trigger the bush callback from an intermediate candidate and store its multiplier after the current travel, for use on the next move.
Movement meaning: with a bush at `(1,1,0)`, a standing player can move from `(0.5,1.1,1.25)` to `(2.6,1.3,1.25)`. Its z range `[0.95,1.55]` stays 0.0125 blocks clear of the age-1 collision shape's maximum z=0.9375, while the swept scan's expanded DDA still enumerates cell `(1,1,0)`. The final x range `[2.3,2.9]` misses the bush's `[1,2]` x range. A qualifying `(2.1,0.2,0)` segment is a conditional source witness: vanilla explosion impulses add to local-player velocity, but the inspected source does not establish that several impulse packets are applied before one specific travel tick.
