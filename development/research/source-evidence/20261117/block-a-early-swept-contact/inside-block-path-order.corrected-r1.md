Difference: 1.21.1 invokes final-box-only block callbacks within `Entity.move`, before that move's block-speed-factor update, and skips the whole scan unless the final box's block range has loaded chunks. 1.21.2 invokes callbacks after `LivingEntity.travel` for the local client instance, first applying `stepOn` when grounded, then traversing cells along the default pre-tick `oldPosition()`-to-post-travel position segment before final-box cells, with position de-duplication and no explicit loaded-chunks guard. `boxTraverseBlocks` switches to swept candidates when squared displacement is at least `Mth.square(0.99999F)` (binary32 `0x3F7FFEB0`, `0.99997997283935546875`; equality enters); DDA clips at most 17 cells before adding entity extents. The exact introduction is bounded to the compared releases.
Movement meaning: A reachable free-fall witness passes a standing LocalPlayer through COBWEB at `(0,0,0)`, centered at `x=z=0.5`, with both endpoint boxes outside it. Under ordinary open-air assumptions and default gravity 0.08, 72 updates from vertical velocity zero yield about `-3.00470152646` for the next downward move. Starting that segment with feet at `y=1.1` (head `2.9`), it ends with feet about `-1.90470152646` and head about `-0.10470152646`. COBWEB has no ordinary collision but its default entity-inside shape is the full block, so 1.21.2's swept path callback sets stuck speed `(0.25,0.05F,0.25)`; the 1.21.1 final-box scan misses it. For steady ordinary LocalPlayer this multiplier is consumed at the next travel move on the following tick. Runtime parity was not tested.
