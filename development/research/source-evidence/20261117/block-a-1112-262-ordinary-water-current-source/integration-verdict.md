# Ordinary water current integration verdict

Source-only integration approved for the bounded ordinary nonfalling water current of unmounted, nonflying local players. The historic scan is restored for 1.10, 1.10.1, 1.10.2, 1.11, 1.11.1 and 1.11.2. The release introduction date and runtime parity remain open.

- Candidate `e5652004938e1406676de1c8c97b1bb16541332f` descends from the sanitized root `352cb5167dc6073cb76e11ec4b323a30af71f1d3`. Product commit `f4c9163e2716bae7b5471dc67f2e6520ce541df4` and tree `e85e084633ed7c3cfb4c9418c6249e1cee3a0a74` use the existing providers and traversal instead of the candidate's parallel interfaces.
- Evidence parent `eeb2ea6db1a3d3162e2a6b58e88ad12399685aba` retains the original continuity, endpoint and packaged-tag commits `4df1b8624a4ef35eaccaff5380fd03052822131a`, `7836abf8b8cba86b0a43fc6e444067040fb2a141`, `75f8eeba53cee57305d8e39390d992482d4da97a`, `24fcf61aac112219f0d7efdcb930730cdddbd63f` and `68f85b2a5bb9f276a682bc8a50ac23f441b673e9` on official main `aae9c86464638cb5ed7faa535c92569b89484906`.
- Resolver review confirmed that the dedicated 1.8.x floor provider still wins. The moved ceil provider covers 1.9.x through 1.11.2, and the moved area policy covers 1.8.x through 1.11.2. Both moved policies stop resolving at the 1.12 profile. Existing 1.12 and later water-current and body-contact providers keep their own selection.
- Ornithe Feather `Entity.checkWaterCollisions`, `World.applyLiquidDrag` and `World.isAreaLoaded` use the narrowed and inset box, flooring the minimum and passing the rounded exclusive maximum unchanged to the area predicate. A maximum of `-0.01` becomes `0` in the ceil-era scan, so it satisfies the upper part of `maxY >= 0 && minY < 256`. The current traversal still treats block cells outside historical Y levels `[0, 256)` as absent. At an exactly integral maximum of `2.0` the 1.8 rule ends at `3` and the 1.9–1.11.2 rule ends at `2`.
- `WaterScan` still reads via `Level.getBlockState`, whose direct client chunk read supplies empty-chunk AIR for missing columns. `EntityWaterMixin` computes current independently after the tracker update and applies its historical vector at the first water predicate. `EntityMixin` suppresses the later native WATER current write when that result replaced current. The old addition therefore does not depend on the native FULL-chunk or water-contact preflight and is applied once. Eye/depth sampling, body contact, lava and the native route for unsupported features or falling dependencies retain their own behavior.
- The accepted source-derived channel witness is `0.014` west in 1.11.2 from the positive normalized sum. The pinned 26.2 client default contains flowing water and the 26.2 shallow contribution reaches the native `0.0045000000000000005` minimum. Custom effective tags remain outside the verified resource scope.
