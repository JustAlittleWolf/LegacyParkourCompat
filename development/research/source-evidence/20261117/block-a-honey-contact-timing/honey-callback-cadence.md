# Honey block callback moves after ordinary travel

1.21.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move`, line 705 calls `tryCheckInsideBlocks` after movement, before the `getBlockSpeedFactor` velocity multiplier; `checkInsideBlocks`, lines 1001-1028.
1.21.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel` and `travelInAir`, lines 2178-2221; `tick`, line 2775. `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `applyEffectsFromBlocks`, lines 736-767, and `checkInsideBlocks`, lines 1038-1072.

Difference: In 1.21.1 each ordinary `Entity.move` reaches the inside-block callback before subsequent travel velocity handling. In 1.21.2 `Entity.move` no longer calls it. `LivingEntity.tick` calls `applyEffectsFromBlocks` only after `travel` returns, and only when the entity is server-side or locally controlled. An ordinary dry, non-fall-flying local player takes `Player.travel` to `LivingEntity.travelInAir`; gravity and friction/drag are applied there before the callback. Fluid and fall-flying travel take other branches. The new callback helper rejects removed or `noPhysics` entities, checks collected movement segments, deduplicates visited block positions per batch, and invokes each eligible block's `entityInside` before `onInsideBlock` and before adding the state to the collector. It uses a `1.0E-5F` deflated entity box and shape/movement gating, with no explicit whole-box `hasChunksAt` guard.

Movement meaning: the HoneyBlock callback moves from within a movement operation to after ordinary air travel's movement and velocity updates. A callback's velocity effect therefore cannot alter that same travel's movement/drag. A block position traversed by multiple recorded segments is visited at most once in the batch. The per-block range/applicability change is separately recorded in `honey-block-inset.md`.
