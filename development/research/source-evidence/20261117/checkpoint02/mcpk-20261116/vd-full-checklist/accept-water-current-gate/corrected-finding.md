# Flowing-water current level and depth

1.13.0, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.13/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `m_69693160` and `checkWaterState`, lines 2507–2567 and 960–976; `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow`/`getHeight`, lines 39–84 and 374–376; `net/minecraft/fluid/state/FluidState.java`, `getHeight`, lines 29–31. The relevant scan, fluid-height and flow bodies remain identical in 1.13.1 and 1.13.2.

Difference: In 1.12.2, `Box.grown(0.0, -0.4F, 0.0)` sets the query's `minY` to the entity box's `minY + 0.4F` and `maxY` to `maxY - 0.4F`; `.contract(0.001)` shrinks it a further `0.001` at each side. It therefore samples a vertically contracted box, not a box extended downward. `World.applyLiquidDrag` already checks `isAreaLoaded(...)` and returns false before scanning when the area is unavailable. Within loaded areas it scans material-water cells and accepts a cell when `ceil(query.maxY) >= cellY + 1 - getHeightLoss(LEVEL)`, accumulates their flow vectors, normalizes the sum, and adds `0.014` times that direction to velocity when nonzero and `hasLiquidCollision()` is true. In 1.13.0 the current scan uses the entity box contracted by `0.001`, with its own existing loaded-area early return. It qualifies water-tagged fluid cells when `cellY + fluidState.getHeight() >= box.minY`, tracks maximum immersion depth, scales each contributing flow by that running depth while it is below `0.4`, averages by contributor count, and applies `0.014` times the result. It skips normalization for `PlayerEntity` (non-player vectors are normalized). `FluidState.getHeight()` delegates to the fluid; flowing-water height is `level / 9.0F`. `FlowingFluid.getFlow()` derives a normalized per-cell direction from neighboring fluid heights and the falling-fluid/solid-face rule. Thus level affects surfaces, qualifying cells and flow directions; shallow immersion and aggregation also affect the player's current magnitude. The loaded-area early return is not a 1.13 addition.

Movement meaning: The scan geometry and cell-surface predicate differ: 1.12.2's vertical query is contracted by `0.4F` at both ends before its rounded-top test, while 1.13.0 scans almost the full entity-box height and tests each fluid surface against `box.minY`. A non-flying player can therefore receive current at different boundary positions. For multiple contributing cells, or shallow immersion, 1.13.0's unnormalized averaged/scaled player vector can also produce a different velocity delta from 1.12.2's normalized aggregate. A single full-depth contributing cell can still produce the same `0.014` magnitude. The source-checked boundary is 1.13.0 (scan, current math and relevant dependencies are unchanged in 1.13.1 and 1.13.2); no earlier patch boundary is claimed.
