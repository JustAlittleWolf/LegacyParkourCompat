# Powder snow stuck speed during piston movement
1.17.1, Mojang mappings: `decompiled_minecraft/1.17.1/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/level/block/PowderSnowBlock.java`, `entityInside`, line 56; `decompiled_minecraft/1.17.1/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `makeStuckInBlock`, line 2150, and `move`, around lines 542–551.
26.2, unobfuscated: `decompiled_minecraft/26.2/unobfuscated/net/minecraft/world/level/block/PowderSnowBlock.java`, `entityInside`, line 71; `decompiled_minecraft/26.2/unobfuscated/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `makeStuckInBlock`, line 2943, and `move`, around lines 728–735.
Difference: Powder snow stores `(0.9F, 1.5, 0.9F)` after resetting fall distance. After piston limiting, both versions enter stuck handling only when `stuckSpeedMultiplier.lengthSqr() > 1.0E-7`; smaller deltas do not consume the stored multiplier. A piston vector returned as zero by `limitPistonMovement` returns from `Entity.move` before this gate in both versions. When the gate passes, 1.17.1 multiplies requested movement for every mover. 26.2 multiplies it only when `moverType != MoverType.PISTON`, while still consuming and clearing the multiplier and zeroing delta movement for piston moves.
Movement meaning: When a player with a pending powder-snow stuck multiplier is moved by a piston and the limited vector reaches the stuck gate, 1.17.1 damps the requested movement by the vector, while 26.2 skips that scaling for piston movement. Normal self-movement above the gate threshold still scales in both. The exact release introducing the mover-type gate is not established by this endpoint comparison. Powder snow exists beginning in 1.17; do not model it before its introduction.
