# Stale water height after out-of-height preflight
1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateInWaterState()` and `checkAndHandleWater(Tag<Fluid>)`, lines 957-972, 2593-2603, 2650-2651; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep()` and `travel()`, lines 1907-1911, 1927-1956, 2266-2276; `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `tick()` and `serverAiStep()`, lines 179-197, 607-618; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, standing dimensions, line 111; `net/minecraft/world/level/LevelReader.java`, `hasChunksAt` and `hasChunkAt(BlockPos)`, lines 147-175; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientLevel.java`, `hasChunk`, lines 240-242.
1.16.0, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing()`, lines 947-972; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep()` fluid-jump branch, lines 2424-2448.
Difference: 1.15.2 stores a sampled depth in primitive `waterHeight`; a failed six-int preflight returns before overwriting it. Its caller only clears `wasInWater`, leaving prior positive depth available. 1.16.0 changes to the `fluidHeight` map and clears it before either sampler each tick, so the same failed preflight leaves the absent depth at zero.
Movement meaning: Conditional but locally reachable without a position packet or custom dimension. In a map with vanilla water at Y=0, a standing player at feet Y=0 samples positive water depth. With feet at Y=0, the deflated max Y is about 1.799; the old preflight only fails below it when `ceil(maxY) < 0`, which requires the following deflated max Y to be at most -1. Thus the tick must move the player downward by about 2.8 blocks. This is physically available after a long fall: air travel updates vertical velocity as `(vy - 0.08) * 0.98`, which approaches -3.92 (`LivingEntity.java:1907-1911`); when the Y=0 sample marks the player in water, the water branch calls `move()` with that current velocity before water drag (`LivingEntity.java:1927-1956`). An entry speed below -2.8 blocks/tick clears the box below the integer gate in one ordinary movement step. The old integer gate then fails, `wasInWater` becomes false, but `waterHeight` stays positive. LocalPlayer still ticks because its chunk admission queries ClientLevel at Y=0 and ClientLevel.hasChunk returns true. A jump input is copied into `jumping` before `LivingEntity.aiStep`; its jump branch reads positive `waterHeight` without testing `isInWater`, causing `jumpInLiquid(WATER)` while below the world. The 1.16.0 map clear removes that stale jump. This condition is possible in a void/map build with water at the lower boundary; generated-world bedrock at Y=0 does not provide the setup.
