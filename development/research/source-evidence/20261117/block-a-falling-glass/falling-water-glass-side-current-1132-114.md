# Falling-water glass-side current restored
1.13.2, Feather generation 2: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow` and `isSolidFace` (54–122); `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, `isExceptionForPlacement` and `isExceptionForAttachment` (241–256).
1.14, Feather generation 2: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow` and `isSolidFace` (54–114); `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, `isFaceSolid` and `isFaceFull` (391–400).
Difference: 1.13.2's fluid face helper returns false for attachment exceptions, including ordinary and stained glass. In 1.14 it removes that exclusion and tests the face of the block's collision shape instead; the era's glass and stained-glass blocks use full-block shapes, so their side faces return solid. The falling-fluid branch continues to test the horizontal neighbor and the block directly above it, adding `(0,-6,0)` before the final normalization when either face is solid.
Movement meaning: In the same isolated falling-water state with zero horizontal gradient, a glass side again contributes `(0,-1,0)` and restores a `-0.014` Y current addition for a sufficiently immersed local player. Patch introduction is only bounded to after 1.13.2 and by 1.14.
