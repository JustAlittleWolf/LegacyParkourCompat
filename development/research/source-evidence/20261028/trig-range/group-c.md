## Compared source ranges and behavior

`net/minecraft/util/Mth.java`, 1.20.5/1.20.6: lines 32–54; 1.21/1.21.1: lines 32–54. All four have a `float[65536]` table. Initialization iterates integer indices and stores `(float)Math.sin(index * Math.PI * 2.0 / 65536.0)`. Both entry points accept `float`, return `float`, calculate an `int` index using `(int)(angle * 10430.378F)` (cos adds `16384.0F` before cast), then mask with `65535` to index `SIN`.

`net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, 1.20.5/1.20.6: lines 1311–1326; 1.21/1.21.1: lines 1350–1365. `moveRelative(float, Vec3)` passes `this.getYRot()` to `getInputVector` and adds the resulting vector to delta movement. `getInputVector(Vec3, float, float)` preserves the squared-length `< 1.0E-7` zero gate, conditional normalization for length-squared `> 1.0`, scaling by the float magnitude, and float `Mth.sin`/`Mth.cos` calls. The conversion is `yaw * (float) (Math.PI / 180.0)` and is evaluated as float; X/Z expressions remain `x*cos - z*sin` and `z*cos + x*sin`, with Y passed through.
