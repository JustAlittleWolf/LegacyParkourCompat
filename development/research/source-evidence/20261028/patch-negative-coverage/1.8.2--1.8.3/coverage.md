## Ordered coverage and comparison

1. **Ordinary client tick and input:** `net/minecraft/client/Minecraft.java`, `Minecraft.tick`; `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tick` and `sendMovementToServer`; `Input.java`, `tick`; `KeyboardInput.java`, `tick`; `GameOptions.java` key bindings. The local player, input, and options files have byte-identical source hashes across endpoints. `Minecraft.java` differs only in release text (window title and request header); its `tick` implementation is outside the changed hunks.
2. **Local-player and inherited tick state:** `LocalClientPlayerEntity.tick` dispatches through its superclass; `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/ClientPlayerEntity.java`, `net/minecraft/entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java`, and `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java` were checked. All are byte-identical across endpoints. Player tick, living tick, status-effect ticking, movement statistics, and player-specific movement branches therefore have no source delta.
3. **Jump and travel branches:** `LivingEntity.jump`, `jumpInWater`, `jumpInLava`, `moveRelative`, `getSpeed`, `setSpeed`, and `isClimbing` are unchanged. This covers jump boost addition, sprint-jump impulse, ground slipperiness, air movement, water depth-strider handling, lava movement, ladder clamps, and collision-triggered climb velocity. `PlayerEntity.moveRelative` / movement-stat helpers are also unchanged.
4. **Position, velocity, collision, and box writers/consumers:** `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, including `tick`, `move`, `setPosition`, `setPositionFromShape`, `setShape`, `updateVelocity`, `isSubmergedIn`, and sprinting-effect/fluid predicates, is byte-identical. `net/minecraft/util/math/Box.java`, `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, and `net/minecraft/client/world/ClientWorld.java` are byte-identical, covering shape math and the world collision/liquid query dependencies.
5. **Block/environment movement inputs:** `Block.java` and the registered block source set were included in the full manifest comparison. Relevant providers checked by hash include `CobwebBlock`, `LadderBlock`, `LiquidBlock`, `FlowingLiquidBlock`, `SlimeBlock`, `SoulSandBlock`, `IceBlock`, `PackedIceBlock`, `PistonHeadBlock` (and the piston-base implementation). No collision shape, slipperiness, fluid, or movement callback provider changed. All block source files except unrelated spawner code are unchanged.
6. **Predicates, modifiers, and direct player responses:** `LivingEntity`, `PlayerEntity`, `ArmorItem`, `StatusEffect`, and `EnchantmentHelper` dependencies were checked. The only `EnchantmentHelper` hunk clamps negative protection to zero in the damage-protection calculation; depth-strider lookup and movement use are unchanged. The only `ThornsEnchantment` hunk adds a null guard around damage/sound. Neither changes player velocity/position or movement predicates. No changed source hunk modifies a direct player movement response.
7. **Changed-file audit:** The two manifests list the same 1,612 relative sources. Only 16 source-file hashes differ: `block/spawner/MobSpawner.java`, `client/gui/overlay/DebugOverlay.java`, `client/gui/screen/game/DeathScreen.java`, `client/gui/screen/menu/TitleScreen.java`, `client/Minecraft.java`, `client/options/ServerListEntry.java`, `enchantment/EnchantmentHelper.java`, `enchantment/ThornsEnchantment.java`, `entity/projectile/ThrownEntity.java`, `realms/RealmsSharedConstants.java`, `server/command/handler/CommandRegistry.java`, `server/integrated/IntegratedServer.java`, `server/MinecraftServer.java`, `server/network/handler/RemoteHandshakeNetworkHandler.java`, `snooper/Snooper.java`, and `util/crash/CrashReport.java`. Their hunks are version text/UI, server/command/spawner behavior, throwable owner lookup exception handling, or damage-related protection/Thorns logic. None changes ordinary client-player movement; throwable motion and other non-player physics remain excluded.
