Difference: 1.15.2 tests `level.noCollision` with the full pose-derived box. 1.16.5 first calls `deflate(1.0E-7)` on all six faces. `Player.updatePlayerPose` still tries swimming fit, then the requested pose, then crouching, then swimming fallback. `LocalPlayer.aiStep` calculates crouching/slow-movement state before `input.tick`, so releasing shift on tick 35 still supplies the crouch-scaled input for that tick.

Corrected witness: use a continuous ordinary full-block floor with top at y=0, top slabs at y=1 in block column x=0 and block rows z=0, 1 and 2, and open space at x>=1 above the floor. Begin after an ordinary grounded rest: player feet at y=0, center x=z=0.5, `onGround=true`, zero horizontal velocity and `deltaMovement.y=-0.0784000015258789`. Keep standing dimensions 0.6F wide by 1.8F high, no effects, liquids, flight, entities, sprint or jump. Hold forward at yaw -21.4453125F for 35 ticks, shift on ticks 1–34 and release it on tick 35. On that tick the slowdown argument is evaluated before `KeyboardInput.tick` reads the released key state.

The nonzero initial vertical velocity is required. On each tick, the negative attempted Y move is clipped by the floor, which sets `onGround` because `verticalCollision` is true and the requested Y displacement is negative. The ordinary block's `updateEntityAfterFallOn` zeroes Y velocity; `LivingEntity.travel` then subtracts gravity 0.08 and multiplies by 0.98F. This reproduces the resting value -0.0784000015258789 at the next tick boundary. Starting with Y velocity zero would not reproduce the grounded recurrence and would not be a valid ordinary-resting initial state.

The horizontal arithmetic is unchanged by that correction. `KeyboardInput`'s crouch-scaled forward input is 0.2940000295639038; `getFrictionInfluencedSpeed` gives 0.10000000149011612 on the 0.6F-friction floor. The standing candidate has minX=0.9999998805017865, so it overlaps the x=0 slab column by 1.194982135466205E-7. Its tick-35 Z span, computed from the reconstructed center and player width, is approximately [2.2371136931594165, 2.8371137170012743]. The full Z path starts at center 0.5 and monotonically reaches 2.5371137050803454; therefore rows z=0, 1 and 2 are needed so the slab shape covers the whole-body query at the endpoint. A one-row z=0 arrangement would not support the endpoint claim.

The 1.15.2 query retains this slab overlap and rejects standing; crouching fits because its top is at y=1.5. In 1.16.5, deflation changes the X overlap to about 1.94982135E-8, within the 1.0E-7 coordinate coalescing used by the shape merger, so the candidate is accepted. Swimming and crouching remain available in both versions. This is static source arithmetic, not a game/TAS parity result.
