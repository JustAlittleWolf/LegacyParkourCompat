# Source audit: nonfalling Oak leaves in the liquid side-flow gate

Status: **COMPLETED — confirmed Oak movement-blocking dry-side provider for the source operation.**

## Bounded question and verdict

Checked only the dry horizontal-neighbor / one-below contribution in ordinary, nonfalling water `LiquidBlock.getFlow`, using exact Ornithe Feather sources for 24 stable releases (`1.8`, `1.8.1`–`1.8.9`, `1.9`–`1.9.4`, `1.10`–`1.10.2`, `1.11`–`1.11.2`, `1.12`–`1.12.2`). The candidate is only the legacy `leaves` block, `LeavesBlock` metadata variant 0 (Oak); `leaves2` / other species are outside this finding.

Source conclusion: **Oak leaves are a confirmed movement-blocking provider for the dry-side gate**. In every checked release the gate calls `getMaterial().blocksMovement()`; Oak leaves use `Material.LEAVES`, a plain `Material` whose inherited method returns `true`. A dry Oak-leaf side therefore blocks the lower-cell sample exactly as dry full glass does. This is a provider result for the requested operation, not a no-code or exclusion decision; product code was not inspected. Transparency, opacity 1, render solidity, collision shape, and apparent full-block geometry are not proxies for the queried predicate.

### Ordinary stable placed-leaf fixture

Use the ordinary Oak leaves item at `(1,64,0)`, east of the player-query water cell. Across all checked releases, `LeavesItem.getBlockMetadata(metadata)` returns `metadata | 4`; `BlockItem` passes that result into `getPlacementState`. For Oak item metadata 0, block metadata is 4: `LeavesBlock.getVariant` masks to variant 0 / Oak, and `DECAYABLE` is false because `(metadata & 4) == 0` is false. It is a normally placed, persistent/non-decaying leaf; no log-support workaround or custom state is required.

### Isolated local-player query and stable positive gradient

Use a loaded ordinary local-player geometry centered at `(0.5,64,0.5)` with the standing-player box and water scan documented in the supplied glass audit (`evidence.md`, commit `f2298d2fd3f5b8959b741e502b6c367e2f213080`). Set the center water `(0,64,0)` to level 0, with exactly one same-height north water face `(0,64,-1)` at level 1. Place persistent Oak leaves at the dry east side `(1,64,0)` above an ordinary level-0 water source `(1,63,0)`. Keep west/south around the center, the center floor, north-cell side/floor neighbors, and lower-source side/floor neighbors as ordinary solid blocks. Load all touched cells.

The phase assumption is one client movement water query over an already initialized, loaded block-state snapshot, with no concurrent world mutation. The player water query includes the center source but not the north face or lower source, per the reference scan bounds. `getFlow` directly samples the north water neighbor at depth 1 against center depth 0, contributing direction north times `1 - 0 = 1`: raw `-Z` current. The east Oak leaf is non-water, but `Material.LEAVES.blocksMovement()` is true, so it blocks the east-down read of the lower source. Other dry solid directions also contribute nothing. Thus the leaf setup has a nonzero historical center current independently of the candidate lower sample; no `LEVEL >= 8` falling branch is reached. All three water states (center level 0, north level 1, lower source level 0) are ordinary non-falling states.

The leaf and water front are stable under the exact liquid source. In all checked releases, `FlowingLiquidBlock.isLiquidBlocking` returns the block material's `blocksMovement()` for leaves (with unrelated portal/structure-void exceptions in later versions); `canSpreadTo` requires `!isLiquidBlocking`, so water cannot replace or flow through the leaf. The center source cannot spread into its north neighbor because that is already water of the same material; its other horizontal choices are solids or the blocking leaf, and its floor blocks downward spreading. For the level-1 north neighbor, the center level-0 source is its sole lower horizontal depth; the update calculation yields `0 + 1 = 1`, preserving the level-1 face, while its other directions/floor are blocked. The lower source is enclosed by its solid floor and horizontal walls, with the leaf above it; the liquid update spreads only downward or horizontally. Oak item placement sets `DECAYABLE=false`, so the leaf itself is persistent.

As a separate query-time counterfactual only, replace the east leaf with air while keeping the same source snapshot. Air admits the lower read: for center depth `i=0` and lower depth `j=0`, the dry-side weight is `j - (i - 8) = 8`; this adds raw `+8 X` to the independent raw `-1 Z` gradient before `getFlow` normalizes the vector. Do not treat east-side air as a stable arrangement after a center-source update. No runtime claim is made.
