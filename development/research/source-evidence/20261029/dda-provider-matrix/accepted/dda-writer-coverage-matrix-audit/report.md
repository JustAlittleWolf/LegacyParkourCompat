## Method and status key

The class inventory is `.native-source/20261028/collision-next/dda-provider-closure/full-ray-provider-inventory.md`. Exact old numeric registrations below were checked against 1.8.9 `net/minecraft/block/Block.java` (canonical SHA-256 `EA10F05106A3CF7189AEC85236A7ECF9C7106A717F37BED583B5EA4ADEFFA528`), including the complete 0–197 registration region. The inventory's caller, flags, virtual dispatch, update order, and early-stop facts are retained from that accepted source report and `.native-source/20261028/collision-next/dda-legacy-provider-closure/provider-and-hit-contract.md`. The default-provider closure remains authoritative for constructor bounds, world/neighbor reads, and cache-history requirements. The artifact hashes in `artifact-hashes.json` were recomputed from current files for every referenced report, review, and source manifest.

- **A** — bounded source claim has an independent ACCEPT review. This is not whole-operation DDA acceptance.
- **Partial** — only the listed subset of IDs or operation inputs is closed.
- **Gap** — no current reviewed source closure in this bundle for the listed ID/input.
- **Cache consumer: yes** — accepted direct movement-consumer evidence proves a ray-written shared bound can feed ordinary block collision later.
- **Cache consumer: excluded** — source proves the ray writer is bypassed, or the block contributes no movement collision through its provider. “No” here is a bounded source fact, not a general no-difference claim.
- **Cache consumer: open** — provider presence alone does not prove whether movement reads the same mutable bounds.

`ID → old key` pairs below are exact old registrations, not aliases inferred from names. “Current alias/state” means the family report covers the stated subset; it does not imply neighbor, block-entity, chunk, or invocation context is reconstructed for an adapter.

## Ray eligibility and classes outside the mutable-writer set

The ordinary camera caller is source-bound to `allowLiquids=false`, `ignoreBlocksWithoutCollision=false`, `allowHitInStartPosition=true`. `AirBlock` ID0, `FireBlock` ID51, and `LiquidBlock` IDs8–11 fail the old ray-eligibility gate for that caller; the old `Block.rayTrace`/`updateShape` provider is not invoked. Their null movement collision providers are separately source-closed with independent ACCEPT reviews in `dda-ray-rejected-family-0-51` and `dda-fluid-family-8-9-10-11`. These are excluded from the cache-consumer subset, but a complete adapter must preserve their old eligibility result.

The generic `Block.rayTrace` contract is source-closed as writer-then-clip, with virtual `updateShape`; it is not a usable global adapter without exact old identity/state, constructor bounds, and old-world inputs for each visited cell. `StairsBlock.rayTrace` loops internal sub-boxes and selects its own result; rail, door, and trapdoor overrides repeat `updateShape` before delegating. `MovingBlock.rayTrace` returns null and bypasses its inventory-listed `updateShape` writer. Those dispatch distinctions remain necessary even where local aliases are closed.

## Cache-consumer subset and explicit exclusions

Only **Anvil ID145** and **Chest ID54** have accepted direct evidence that the ray-written shared old bounds are consumed by later ordinary player movement collision. ID146 is not included in the ID54 crosswalk evidence. The source inventory's separate Stem candidate is not a movement-cache consumer: the accepted age-crop report states `StemBlock` inherits `PlantBlock.getCollisionShape() == null`. The other explicit exclusions above are based on a proven null/empty movement provider, a ray-rejected gate, a refresh-before-clip provider, a state-derived movement getter, or a ray override that returns null. For remaining writers, cache-consumer status stays **open** until the old collision getter/addCollisions path and current source provider are composed with that writer.

## Adapter gate from this matrix

The family reports close many local aliases, states, and individual providers, but do not close one general `CellVisitor`. Remaining specific identity/state gaps are ordinary pressure plates 70/72, trapped chest 146, iron bars 101, and red-sandstone stairs 180. More broadly, the accepted default-provider closure still requires an exhaustive lossless old-ID/state→current-state/provider-input map for every camera-eligible block, all constructor and query-time bound writes, and old-equivalent neighbor/paired-cell/block-entity reads. The old DDA's exact visit order, writer sequence, miss writes, custom provider internals, and first-non-null stop must be preserved. Current-family source reports do not establish lifecycle-wide cache validity, movement consumption for every writer, or whole traversal parity.

This is a source coverage snapshot only. It identifies the remaining provider and state inputs; it neither declares the pair frozen nor certifies any product implementation.
