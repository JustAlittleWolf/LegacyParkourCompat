## Findings and closure

No movement-related Java source difference was found in the 1.20 → 1.20.1 comparison. The observed source changes are the 11 paths listed above; `ChunkStatus` is the sole indirect movement-adjacent candidate because chunk availability gates local ticking, unloaded-chunk fluid work, and a client-side land-travel fallback. Its changed method preserves the ProtoChunk status assignment only on the `Either` left result, but the downstream status-to-availability relationship has not been independently traced.
