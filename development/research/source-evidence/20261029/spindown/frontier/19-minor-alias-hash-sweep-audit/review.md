No write intervenes in this tick branch; the redundant read and API refactor are source facts, but no player movement consequence is established here.

The review must not imply every `getBlockState` behavior changed without tracing its own method; the cited predicates establish a real horizontal world-access delta, while player reachability through collision/world-border paths remains unresolved. This remains a candidate, not a movement finding.
