## Source-level difference

B adds a PISTON-specific cumulative requested-movement limiter keyed by world time and entity. A has no mover-type branch or corresponding accumulator in `Entity.move`. For a non-noClip entity, B resets the accumulator on a changed world time, then clamps each nonzero axis's accumulated request to `[-0.51,0.51]`, forwards only the difference from the previous accumulator, and returns before ordinary collision resolution when the forwarded magnitude is `<= 1.0E-5F`. The accumulator is updated before the early return. A forwards each piston caller request to ordinary collision resolution without this limiter. Because the cap runs before collision handling, this finding is about requested movement input, not a cap on realized position change.

## Reachability and dependencies

The paired moving-piston block entity selects entities intersecting its movement area, skips only `PistonMoveBehavior.IGNORE`, and calls `Entity.move` with the piston direction delta. `PlayerEntity` inherits `Entity#getPistonMoveBehavior() == NORMAL` on both sides; its tick sets `noClip` to the spectator flag. The bounded precondition is a local non-spectator player present in that entity list and receiving same-axis piston requests during one `World.getTime()` value; B routes each request through the PISTON branch. Piston progress, candidate-area construction and tick order are traced in S4-piston-collision-geometry; broader collision-provider coverage remains open under D-COLLISION. The cap itself precedes collision resolution, so the exact collision shape does not determine whether the request is capped once the caller has supplied it.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source proves a new per-world-time cumulative cap on piston-driven requested movement for the bounded player path. Whether a different forwarded request produces a different final player box depends on the collision result and repeated-call geometry; no runtime trajectory is claimed. `0.51` cap and `1.0E-5F` early return are source constants, not inferred outcomes.
