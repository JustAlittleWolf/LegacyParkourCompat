## Player consequence and scope

The fixed-state trapdoor collision shape is the same at A/B: closed uses the horizontal 3/16 plate, open uses the vertical 3/16 panel selected by `FACING`. The ordinary entity path consumes the current state shape (`Entity#move` → `collide` → `BlockCollisions#computeNext` → `BlockState#getCollisionShape`); the paired source hashes for Entity and BlockCollisions are recorded in F-09 and match the published manifests. A player can therefore encounter a different collision shape if vanilla redstone produces a different `OPEN` state. This is a possible downstream movement consequence, not a changed player movement operation. Project scope keeps vanilla block states and excludes redstone/block-state lifecycle production, so no implementation-eligible player delta is established by F-09. Runtime parity was not tested.
