## Required revision

The snapshot's coordinate expression and its derived overlap numbers are not internally consistent. It writes `playerY = (64 + (1.0F/9.0F)) - 0.003`. Under Java promotion rules, the `int + float` addition is performed as `float` before the `double` subtraction. That yields `playerY ≈ 64.10811450195312`, A overlap `≈ 0.002`, scaled A magnitude `≈ 1.4E-5`, B height `≈ 0.00299660998583`, and B squared current `≈ 8.9796714E-6`. The snapshot's stated A overlap `≈ 0.00200339` and B height `≈ 0.003` instead correspond to `playerY = topB - 0.003` where `topB = 64.0D + (1.0F/9.0F)`.

Both coordinate interpretations still pass the cited strict branches, so this does not invalidate the source difference. However, the assignment requires exact operation/cast verification and the immutable finding presents its values as a reproducible witness.
