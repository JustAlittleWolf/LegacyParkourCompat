## Source-level difference

A promotes the float base jump power and the double-typed effect contribution to double before adding, then assigns the double result to vertical velocity. B evaluates the base, Jump Boost contribution and their sum as floats, rounding their sum to float before promotion at the `setDeltaMovement` call. With Jump Boost amplifier 0 and block jump factor 1.0, A sums the binary float values for `0.42F` and `0.1F` in double precision; B rounds their sum back to float. The assigned vertical values therefore differ by float-rounding precision under that reachable precondition. B also sources its base from a syncable jump-strength attribute whose default matches `0.42F`.

## Reachability and dependencies

Grounded player jump processing reaches `LivingEntity.jumpFromGround()` through the Player override; `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` can also reach it on the flight-activation path documented in finding `MC1194-1206-01`. Jump Boost is the direct effect dependency. The base jump-strength attribute is server-synchronized in B; any nondefault server value is an external input and is not assumed here. Block jump-factor behavior remains a Stage 5 dependency.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: under the stated Jump Boost condition, the arithmetic path rounds at different precision before writing vertical velocity. The magnitude is a small numeric delta and its later trajectory has not been tested. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
