# Cocoa age-two collision shape
1.10.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CocoaBlock.java`, `EAST_SHAPE` lines 21–25, corresponding `WEST_SHAPE`/`NORTH_SHAPE`/`SOUTH_SHAPE` lines 26–40, and `getShape` lines 77–90.
1.11, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CocoaBlock.java`, same ranges. Collision insertion is `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, `addCollisions`/`addCollision`/`getCollisionShape` lines 342–358 in 1.10.2 and 353–369 in 1.11. The ordinary grounded player path is `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, lines 1385–1421 in 1.10.2 and 1439–1475 in 1.11; its `Entity.updateVelocity` implementation is lines 960–974 and 982–996 respectively, followed by `Entity.move` at lines 446–530 and 455–545.
Difference: `AGE` remains bounded to 0–2 and `getShape` selects the facing-specific box in both versions. At age 2, the 1.10.2 boxes duplicate age 1; 1.11 expands the box toward the side away from its support and downward. For EAST-facing cocoa at `(0,0,0)`, the age-2 local box changes from `[0.5625,0.3125,0.3125]–[0.9375,0.75,0.6875]` to `[0.4375,0.1875,0.25]–[0.9375,0.75,0.75]`. The other facings have the corresponding reflected expansion.
Movement meaning: A one-tick ordinary grounded walking witness needs no initial overlap or unusual speed. Place EAST-facing age-2 cocoa at `(0,0,0)` with its required jungle-log support at `(1,0,0)`, a stone floor at `y=-1`, and clear space elsewhere. A standing player with the normal 0.6-wide, 1.8-high box centered at `(0.1,0,0.5)` starts with AABB `[-0.2,0,0.2]–[0.4,1.8,0.8]`: it does not overlap the cocoa shape in either release, the floor touches only its feet, and the support log begins at `x=1`. With zero horizontal velocity, no sprint/jump/sneak, and forward input aimed along +X, grounded travel on the stone floor applies the normal player speed (`0.1`) before calling `Entity.move`; its requested X displacement is approximately `+0.1` in both releases. The swept AABB therefore reaches `maxX≈0.5`. In 1.11 that intersects the cocoa beginning at `x=0.4375`, so X clipping allows only `0.4375−0.4=0.0375` and ends at center `x=0.1375`. In 1.10.2 the swept box ends before the old shape begins at `x=0.5625`, so the full ordinary step ends at center `x≈0.2`. `Block.getCollisionShape` returns the state shape and collision insertion offsets it to world coordinates before adding it when it intersects the query box. The grounded move enumerates collisions from its directional box expansion and clips Y before X before Z. This establishes a reachable per-tick walking divergence; it does not claim a precise later trajectory.
