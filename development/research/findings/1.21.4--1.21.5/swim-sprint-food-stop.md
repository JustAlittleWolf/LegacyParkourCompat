# Swimming sprint loses the grounded and sneak exemption for insufficient food
1.21.4, Mojmap: `1.21.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep`, lines 713–723; `hasEnoughFoodToStartSprinting`, lines 1073–1074.
1.21.5, Mojmap: `1.21.5/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep`, lines 736–743; `shouldStopSwimSprinting`, lines 846–851; `hasEnoughFoodToSprint`, lines 1081–1082.

Difference: For an already swimming, sprinting player in water, 1.21.4 stops for insufficient food only when both `!onGround()` and `!input.keyPresses.shift()` hold: `(!forward || !food) && !ground && !shift`. In 1.21.5 `!food` is an unconditional disjunct in `shouldStopSwimSprinting`. For an unmounted player the food predicate is unchanged: food level greater than `6.0F` or permission to fly. Adjacent stable releases establish 1.21.5 as this boundary; it is separate from the 1.14.2 forward threshold change.

Movement meaning: A survival player already sprint swimming in a deep pool can hold the sneak key while their supplied food level becomes six. No item or other entity is needed. Hold forward or release it; leave the player in water, unmounted and without blindness. In 1.21.4 sneak makes the food-related stop branch false and sprint remains set. In 1.21.5 food failure clears sprint even with sneak held. This finding concerns the movement predicate reading food state, not production or calculation of hunger. The immediate source consequence is the sprint state write; trajectory parity has not been measured.

26.2, published unobfuscated names: `26.2/unobfuscated/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `shouldStopSwimSprinting`, lines 927–928, still stops unconditionally on `!isSprintingPossible(true)`; `isSprintingPossible`, lines 1135–1138, reads `hasEnoughFoodToDoExhaustiveManoeuvres` for unmounted players. `world/entity/player/Player.java` lines 1592–1593 and `world/food/FoodData.java` lines 92–93 preserve the greater-than-six-or-flight food gate.
