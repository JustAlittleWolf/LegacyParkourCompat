# Bubble-column contact treats collisionless dry overhead blocks as above contact in 1.21.5

1.21.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside` lines 50–76; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleCol` and `onInsideBubbleColumn` lines 2483–2505; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, bubble response override lines 358–368.
1.21.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside` lines 51–57; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, bubble response helpers lines 2455–2499; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, bubble response override lines 373–383; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, `SHORT_GRASS` registration lines 709–721; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/state/BlockBehaviour.java`, default fluid and empty collision behavior lines 217–219, 310–312.
Difference: In 1.21.4, any non-air block immediately above a bubble column selects `onInsideBubbleColumn`; air selects `onAboveBubbleCol`. In 1.21.5, the block above selects `onAboveBubbleColumn` when its collision shape and fluid state are both empty. A player intersects the bubble column's entity-inside shape even though the column has no physical collision; non-flying players receive the selected response. Dry `SHORT_GRASS` above an upward column is a concrete reachable precondition. The inside response adds 0.06 to vertical velocity, caps it at 0.7, and resets fall distance; the above response adds 0.1, caps it at 1.8, and does not reset fall distance. The upward column has `DRAG_DOWN=false`.
Movement meaning: At zero vertical velocity, a non-flying player in an upward bubble column under dry short grass receives +0.06 Y velocity in 1.21.4 and +0.1 in 1.21.5; subsequent upward movement therefore starts from a 0.04 higher velocity in 1.21.5. The callback also changes the upward cap and fall-distance reset behavior. The demonstrated branch applies only to non-air overhead blocks with empty collision and fluid states. The stable cutover is 1.21.5: the old classification is in 1.21.4 and the new classification in the next stable release, 1.21.5.
