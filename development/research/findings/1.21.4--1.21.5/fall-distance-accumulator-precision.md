Difference: `fallDistance` changes from `float` to `double` in 1.21.5. The ordinary downward writer still casts each negative movement delta to `float` before subtracting it. Thus each input increment retains float rounding, while the accumulated sum changes from float arithmetic in 1.21.4 to double arithmetic in 1.21.5. The same casted-delta writer is present in 1.21.3 and 26.2; this does not establish an intermediate release boundary beyond the compared pair.
Movement meaning: `Player.maybeBackOffFromEdge` is reached from `Entity.move` before its fall-distance callback and, for a shift-key-held SELF/PLAYER move with nonpositive vertical delta, checks `isAboveGround`; that predicate directly compares `fallDistance` against the step height. A preceding downward move can therefore feed the new accumulator value to the next ordinary sneak-edge decision. This is conditional: when the player is on ground, `isAboveGround` short-circuits on `onGround()`, and 1.21.4's local movement gate skips its fall-distance callback. Source establishes the different stored arithmetic and direct consumer, not a specific trajectory or a runtime divergence.
