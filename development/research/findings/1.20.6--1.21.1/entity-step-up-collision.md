# Step-up collision candidate heights
1.20.6, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `Entity.collide(Vec3)` lines 874-898 and `Entity.collideBoundingBox(...)` lines 900-914.
1.21.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `Entity.collide(Vec3)` lines 875-904, `collectCandidateStepUpHeights(...)` lines 906-925, and `collectColliders(...)` lines 932-946.
Difference: When an entity has `maxUpStep() > 0`, is grounded or has a downward collision, and horizontal movement is clipped, 1.20.6 tests two routes based on the full `maxUpStep()` height and chooses the farther horizontal result, then adds a downward collision adjustment. 1.21.1 gathers Y coordinates from the collision shapes, casts candidate offsets to `float`, filters them against the downward clip and step limit, unstable-sorts them, then returns the first candidate whose horizontal distance exceeds the baseline; it uses the new collider collection helper to add the world-border and block shapes for the expanded step box.
Movement meaning: A player satisfying the step-up gate can receive a different step height and horizontal displacement when the available collision-shape heights do not match the old full-height routes. The player's inherited entity movement path reaches this collision resolver.
