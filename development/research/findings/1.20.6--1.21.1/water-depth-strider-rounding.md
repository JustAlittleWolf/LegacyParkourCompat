# Depth Strider water input-factor rounding

1.20.6, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lines 2112–2129; `net/minecraft/world/item/enchantment/EnchantmentHelper.java`, `getDepthStrider(LivingEntity)`, lines 184–185; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `createAttributes()` lines 221–225 and `getSpeed()` lines 1511–1514; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, sprint speed modifier lines 134–136 and `setSprinting(boolean)` lines 2008–2014; `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffects.java`, Slowness movement modifier lines 22–25.
1.21.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lines 2091–2112; `net/minecraft/world/item/enchantment/Enchantments.java`, Depth Strider water-movement-efficiency effect, lines 346–365; `net/minecraft/world/entity/ai/attributes/Attributes.java`, `WATER_MOVEMENT_EFFICIENCY`, lines 101–102; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `createAttributes()` lines 222–226 and `getSpeed()` lines 1494–1497; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, sprint speed modifier lines 140–142 and `setSprinting(boolean)` lines 1994–2000; `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffects.java`, Slowness movement modifier lines 25–29.

Difference: In 1.20.6, water travel reads Depth Strider as a float level, caps it at `3.0F`, halves it off-ground, and computes the input factor as `(target - 0.02F) * level / 3.0F` using left-to-right float operations. In 1.21.1, Depth Strider adds `LevelBasedValue.perLevel(0.33333334F)` to the water-movement-efficiency attribute; water travel casts that attribute value to float, halves it off-ground, and computes `(target - 0.02F) * factor` without the cap or division.

For an equal-input player witness, use default player attributes (movement speed `0.1F`), Depth Strider II boots, Slowness I, sprinting, grounded in water with fluid movement enabled and no stand-on-fluid override, no Dolphin's Grace, and a forward input vector `(0, 0, 1)`. Vanilla sprint adds `+0.3F` and Slowness I adds `-0.15F` as total movement-speed modifiers, so `getSpeed()` returns `0.1105F` in both versions. Grounded state avoids the off-ground halving. With the same target `0.1105F`, Depth Strider II gives the old level factor `2.0F` and the new attribute-derived float factor `0.6666667F`. The old input factor evaluates as `0.02F + ((0.1105F - 0.02F) * 2.0F) / 3.0F = 0.08033333F`; the new factor evaluates as `0.02F + (0.1105F - 0.02F) * 0.6666667F = 0.08033334F`. Thus equal state and inputs differ by one float ULP in the water input-acceleration coefficient. Grounded Depth Strider II remains below the old cap of 3, so this witness isolates the interpolation-rounding change from the removed cap.

Movement meaning: The different coefficient changes the player's water movement input acceleration; the water-friction interpolation also uses the changed coefficient procedure. This finding establishes the old operation in the exact 1.20.6 Mojmap source and the attribute-backed operation in the exact 1.21.1 Mojmap source. It does not establish applicability for other release or mapping representatives. The introduction boundary supported by these sources is `1.20.6 < introduction ≤ 1.21.1`.
