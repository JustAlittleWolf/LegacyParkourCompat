# Piston movement bypasses stuck-speed scaling
1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 446-465 and `makeStuckInBlock` lines 1982-1985; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/piston/PistonMovingBlockEntity.java`, direct entity push lines 140-153.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 670-700 and `makeStuckInBlock` lines 2788-2791; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/piston/PistonMovingBlockEntity.java`, `moveEntityByPiston` lines 185-192; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/WebBlock.java`, `entityInside` lines 27-34.
Checkpoints (Mojmap `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move`): 1.16.5 lines 485-505; 1.20.6 lines 597-620; 1.21.1 lines 598-622; 1.21.3 lines 619-643; 1.21.8 lines 663-681; 1.21.9 lines 670-691. Each scales piston movement by a nonzero `stuckSpeedMultiplier` after piston limiting, then clears the multiplier and zeroes delta movement. In 1.21.10 lines 678-694, the same state is cleared and velocity is zeroed, but the vector multiplication is skipped only for `MoverType.PISTON`. `net/minecraft/client/Minecraft.java`, `tickLevel` lines 1782-1784, ticks `ClientLevel.tickEntities` before `Level.tickBlockEntities`; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `applyEffectsFromBlocks`/`checkInsideBlocks` lines 801-850 and 1137-1159, reaches block callbacks such as `WebBlock.entityInside` and `makeStuckInBlock` before the piston block entity calls `moveEntityByPiston`. The stable difference is present in 1.21.10 and absent in 1.21.9. Before the field-based range, 1.8.9 through 1.13.2 use the separate one-shot `inCobweb` state; the 1.8.9 player path also scales direct moving-piston displacement (`net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 371-386; `net/minecraft/block/entity/MovingBlockEntity.java`, lines 99-108). This earlier web mechanism is separate from `stuckSpeedMultiplier`.
Movement meaning: A player with a pending stuck-speed multiplier when a piston block entity pushes them gets component-scaled, piston-limited displacement in the checked 1.14.4–1.21.9 profiles. In 1.21.10 the piston-limited displacement is preserved, while the pending multiplier is still cleared and velocity is still zeroed. The earliest profile in the assigned field-based range is 1.14.4; the first stable release with the skip is 1.21.10.
