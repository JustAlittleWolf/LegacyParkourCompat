# Wall neighbor connectivity predicate

1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo` (148–158), `resolveVirtualProperties` (188–195), and the wall shape/collision arrays and selectors (30–84, 86–104); `net/minecraft/block/StairsBlock.java`, `isCube` (169–172). `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (564); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, block collision dispatch (997–1001).
1.12, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo` (146–160), `resolveVirtualProperties` (190–196), and the wall shape/collision arrays and selectors (28–82, 84–102); `net/minecraft/block/StairsBlock.java`, `getFaceShape` (140–160) and `isCube` (169–172). `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (567); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, block collision dispatch (1001–1005). `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, attachment exceptions (219–234). 1.12.1 and 1.12.2 have byte-identical `WallBlock.java` and `StairsBlock.java` source to 1.12, with the same methods and line ranges.

Difference: 1.11.2 accepts the same wall and fence gates after a barrier rejection; other neighbors must have solid-blocking material and `isCube()`, and pumpkin material is excluded. 1.12 resolves each directional neighbor by the wall-facing side and accepts a `SOLID` face unless the block is an attachment exception, or the specified middle-pole/gate face cases. A straight stair's facing side is `SOLID` in 1.12 while stairs still return `isCube() == false`; stairs are not in the attachment-exception list. For a wall at `(0,0,0)` and a straight stair at `(1,0,0)` facing west, 1.11.2 leaves the east connection off, while 1.12 resolves the stair's west face as solid and turns the east connection on. The first observed stable release with the new predicate is 1.12; its source is unchanged in 1.12.1 and 1.12.2. The exact introduction is bounded by `(1.11.2, 1.12]`.

Movement meaning: The wall's cardinal flags feed the same 16-entry shape index and collision table in both releases; the table itself is unchanged. For the witness, an east-only mask selects the existing collision box `(0.25,0,0.25)..(1,1.5,0.75)` instead of the center-only box `(0.25,0,0.25)..(0.75,1.5,0.75)`. A normal 0.6-wide, 1.8-high player box centered at `(1.1,1,0.5)` overlaps the added east arm but not the center-only box. `Entity.move` submits the player box expanded by the requested displacement to `World.getCollisions`, which dispatches `BlockState.addCollisions` with `forceShape=false`; the wall then resolves the neighbor flags and adds the selected collision box. Thus this reachable wall/stair arrangement can add a player collision constraint in 1.12. This finding concerns the predicate's selection of the existing mask, not a new mask geometry.
