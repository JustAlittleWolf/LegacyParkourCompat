# Farmland demotion displacement
1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.11.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/block/FarmlandBlock.java`, `setDirt`, lines 71–79; callers `tick`, `onFallenOn`, `neighborChanged`, and `onAdded`.
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.12.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/block/FarmlandBlock.java`, `setDirt`, lines 72–80; the same callers remain.
Difference: after replacing farmland with dirt, 1.11.2 queries entities in the full dirt collision box and sets every returned entity's Y directly to that box's top. 1.12.2 queries only the upper 1/16 block slice and teleports each returned entity upward by `min(1/16, slice.maxY - entityBox.minY) + 0.001`. `onFallenOn` invokes this only off the client; the state-demotion callers `tick`, `neighborChanged`, and `onAdded` have no client-side gate in this class.
Movement meaning: when client-side farmland demotion is reached while a player intersects the newly solid top slice, the player is displaced by the changed amount and selection gate. The 1.11.2 path can also reposition any entity intersecting the dirt volume to the block top; the 1.12.2 path limits the query to the newly occupied top slice and preserves the entity's relative position while lifting it clear.
