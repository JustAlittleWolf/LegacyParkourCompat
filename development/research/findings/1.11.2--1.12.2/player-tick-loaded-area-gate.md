1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.12.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/world/World.java`, `tickEntities` lines 1278–1292 and `tickEntity`, lines 1412–1434; local player entry is `decompiled_minecraft/1.12.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tick`, lines 165–167.
Difference: `tickEntities` still dispatches regular entities through `tickEntity(entity)`. In 1.11.2, `tickEntity` requires a 32-block area around every entity to be loaded before it updates position history or ticks an in-chunk entity. In 1.12.2, that area check is skipped for every `PlayerEntity`; the in-chunk gate remains. `LocalClientPlayerEntity.tick` separately requires its current chunk to be loaded in both releases.
Movement meaning: with the local player's current chunk loaded and `inChunk` true, but another chunk within the surrounding 32-block area missing, 1.11.2 suppresses the local player tick while 1.12.2 executes it. The local input, sprint/jump decisions, travel, collision move and movement packet path therefore run on 1.12.2 for this reachable chunk-loading state.
