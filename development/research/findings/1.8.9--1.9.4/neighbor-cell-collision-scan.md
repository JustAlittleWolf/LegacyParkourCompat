Difference: 1.8.9 scans each horizontal axis from `floor(min)` to exclusive `floor(max + 1.0)`. Starting in 1.9, the list-returning collision method scans from `floor(min) - 1` to exclusive `ceil(max) + 1`, then prunes horizontal corner columns and vertical edge cells. The piston provider also changes: its 1.8.9 EAST arm has local X bounds `[0, 0.75]`; from 1.9 the EAST arm has local X bounds `[-0.25, 0.75]`. Thus the newer arm can extend into the cell west of its owner, and the expanded scan can query that provider. The 1.9 scanner and arm geometry persist in 1.9.1 through 1.9.4.

Movement meaning: For a player with current box X `[0.1, 0.7]`, Y `[10, 11.8]`, Z `[0.2, 0.8]`, `onGround == false`, and requested movement `(0.2, 0, 0)`, `Entity.move` asks the world for collisions against the swept box expanded by that movement. With an EAST piston head at `(1, 10, 0)`, an otherwise empty collision area, a loaded chunk, and the candidate cells inside the world border, 1.8.9 scans only X cell 0 for this swept box and retains `dx = 0.2`. From 1.9, cell `(1, 10, 0)` is included: it is a horizontal edge candidate (`q == 1`), not a pruned corner, and Y=10 survives vertical pruning. Its EAST arm starts at world X `1 - 0.25 = 0.75`, so `Entity.move` clips X to `0.75 - 0.7 = 0.05`. With zero vertical movement and `onGround == false`, the step-entry condition is false. This is a conditional source-level clipped-displacement difference for the stated player move and collision context.
