# Lava material scan upper bound changes in 1.9.0

1.8.9, Ornithe Feather Gen2 build 2: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `isInLava`, lines 840–842; `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, `containsMaterial`, lines 1529–1549; `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `moveRelative`, lines 1119–1123, and lava travel jump branch, lines 1432–1439.
1.10.2, 1.11.2 and 1.12.2, Ornithe Feather Gen2 build 2: their `Entity.isInLava` wrappers retain the same contraction; `World.containsMaterial` also retains the exclusive `ceil(max)` limits (respectively lines 1614–1639, 1615–1640 and 1623–1648). In 1.13.2, `World.containsMaterial`, lines 1310–1332, still uses those `ceil(max)` limits, while `Box.contract`, lines 222–224, delegates to `grown(-dx, -dy, -dz)`.
Difference: `isInLava` queries the box contracted horizontally by 0.1 and vertically by 0.4 in each inspected release. The independent scan operation changes from `floor(max + 1.0)` as the exclusive limit in 1.8.9 to `ceil(max)` in 1.9.0. The latter is present in the inspected 1.9.1–1.9.4 sources and remains in the queried 1.10.2–1.13.2 projections. At an integral contracted-box maximum, the former scans the block at that maximum and the latter does not. The box contraction remains geometrically the same when 1.13.2 spells it as `contract(0.1, 0.4, 0.1)`.
Movement meaning: with lava occupying a block at an integral upper boundary of the contracted query box, 1.8.9 can report lava while 1.9.x reports dry. The query feeds the living entity's lava movement branch and lava jump handling; the scan-bound change is separate from the later cached-presence operation.
