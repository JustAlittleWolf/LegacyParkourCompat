# Wall neighbor connection mask
1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.11.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo` (148–157), mask resolution (188–194), and collision table (48–70) and selection (92–104); `net/minecraft/block/material/Material.java`, `isSolidBlocking` (103–105) and TNT material (26); `net/minecraft/block/TntBlock.java`, constructor (23–28); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, entity collision assembly (997–1014); `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (462, 567).
1.12, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.12/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo` and exceptions (146–160), mask resolution (190–196), collision table (46–68) and selection (90–102), and wall face shape (205–207); `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, attachment exceptions (220–234), default cube and face shape (252, 362–364); `net/minecraft/block/FenceBlock.java`, neighbor predicate and face shape (124–134, 202–204); `net/minecraft/block/PaneBlock.java`, neighbor predicate and face shape (136–143, 206–208); `net/minecraft/block/FenceGateBlock.java`, face shape (175–179); `net/minecraft/block/material/Material.java`, TNT material and `isSolidBlocking` (26, 103–105); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, entity collision assembly (1001–1018); `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (462, 567).
Difference: 1.11.2 connects to a horizontal neighbor when it is the same wall or a fence gate, or its material is solid-blocking and its state is a cube, except pumpkin material; barriers never connect. 1.12 replaces this with direction-specific face shapes: a non-exception solid face connects; a thick middle pole connects; a middle pole connects only for a fence gate. Its exceptions include block attachment exceptions, barrier, melon, pumpkin and lit pumpkin. Fence and pane neighbors remain disconnected (their horizontal faces are middle-pole and thin-middle-pole); gates and walls remain connected (middle-pole gate and thick-middle-pole wall). TNT is a concrete changed input: `TntBlock` uses translucent `Material.TNT`, so in 1.11.2 `isSolidBlocking` is false and it does not connect; in 1.12 TNT has the default solid face shape and is not in the wall exception list, so it connects. Mask resolution writes the same four directional bits in both versions. The unchanged shape-index and collision-box tables then select a different wall box for that mask. In ordinary player movement, `Entity.move` queries `World.getCollisions` with the player's expanded bounding box, and `World` calls each block state's `addCollisions`; wall `addCollisions` resolves its virtual properties against neighboring world states before selecting and appending its collision box. A path that intersects the newly included horizontal wall arm beside TNT therefore receives a different collision result. Patch introduction is unknown within 1.11.2–1.12.
Movement meaning: For fixed vanilla states, the 1.12 wall connection mask uses directional face geometry instead of the old material-and-full-cube predicate. TNT adjacency can add a wall arm to the box used by ordinary player movement and change position clipping.
