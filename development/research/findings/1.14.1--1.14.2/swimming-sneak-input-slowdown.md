1.14.2, Yarn `1.14.2+build.7-v2`: `1.14.2/yarn/net/minecraft/client/network/ClientPlayerEntity.java`, `isInSneakingPose`, lines 577–586, and `tickMovement`, lines 614–623; `1.14.2/yarn/net/minecraft/client/input/KeyboardInput.java`, `tick`, lines 13–26; `1.14.2/yarn/net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `wouldPoseNotCollide`, lines 1581–1583, `shouldLeaveSwimmingPose`, lines 1771–1777, and `method_20343`, lines 2250–2256; `1.14.2/yarn/net/minecraft/entity/player/PlayerEntity.java`, pose sizes, lines 108–117, and `updateSize`, lines 326–354.

Difference: The local sneak-pose predicate adds `!isSwimming()` in 1.14.2. Both versions otherwise require flight disabled and a collision-free crouch-sized box, then either the previous input's sneak intent or a blocked standing-sized box. There is no riding guard in this predicate. Before polling input, the client combines it with the unchanged crawl predicate: current pose is swimming and the body is not in water. Keyboard input then polls current sneak intent and scales each movement impulse by `(float)(impulse * 0.3)` when not spectator and either current sneak intent or the combined pre-poll predicate is true. Item-use slowdown follows this step. Actual pose selection is separate: swimming state selects the swimming pose before sneak state is considered, with collision fallback and spectator/riding bypass retained.

Movement meaning: A non-flying, non-spectator local player already swimming in water under a ceiling can fit the 0.6-by-1.5 crouch box while the 0.6-by-1.8 standing box is blocked. With forward held and current sneak released, the older predicate supplies slowdown despite swimming, producing positive forward impulse `0.3F`; 1.14.2 excludes swimming from that predicate and produces `1.0F` before any item-use slowdown. The dry swimming-pose crawl branch remains eligible for slowdown, and current sneak input still causes slowdown in both versions. This changes an existing local input response independently of the sprint-forward eligibility change.
