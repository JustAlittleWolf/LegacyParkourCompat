# Player correction rotation normalization
1.19.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleMovePlayer`, lines 563–628; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `absMoveTo(double, double, double, float, float)`, lines 1194–1199.
1.19.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleMovePlayer`, lines 592–667; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `setXRot`/`setYRot`, lines 3082–3100.
Difference: For clientbound player position corrections, 1.19.2 applies relative yaw/pitch deltas before calling `absMoveTo`, which stores yaw modulo 360 degrees and clamps pitch to [-90, 90] before modulo 360; it then copies the resulting angles into the old-angle fields. 1.19.3 instead applies relative values directly through the finite-checking setters (and increments the old-angle fields by raw deltas), or stores absolute values directly; it does not wrap yaw or clamp pitch. It acknowledges the teleport and responds with the resulting player angles in both versions.
Movement meaning: A correction carrying yaw outside one turn or pitch outside [-90, 90], or a relative correction that crosses those bounds, leaves different player rotation state in 1.19.3. That state is used by subsequent player movement input and is echoed in the client movement response. The path is the client packet handler after its same-thread gate; relative behavior is selected by the packet's Y_ROT/X_ROT flags.
