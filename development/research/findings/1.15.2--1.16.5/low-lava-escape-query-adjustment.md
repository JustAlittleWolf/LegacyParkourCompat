# Low-lava escape query adjustment
1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lava branch and `isFree` escape query, lines 1913–1925.
1.16.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lava branch and `isFree` escape query, lines 1959–1978; `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(double, boolean, Vec3)`, lines 2074–2087.
Difference: For an affected entity in lava whose fluid height is at or below `getFluidJumpThreshold()`, 1.16.5 scales movement by `(0.5, 0.8F, 0.5)` and applies fluid-falling adjustment before the horizontal-collision free-space query. 1.15.2 always scales by `0.5`, applies its `-gravity/4` adjustment when gravity is enabled, and queries that resulting movement directly. Above the 1.16.5 threshold, its lava branch retains the `0.5` scale. Both versions require `horizontalCollision` and a successful `isFree` result before setting vertical movement to `0.3F`.
Movement meaning: With horizontal collision in low lava, the offset passed through `isFree` can differ because the candidate vertical movement is computed differently. That can change whether the escape query succeeds and therefore whether the player receives the `0.3F` upward response. The `Entity.isFree` delegation itself is unchanged across these endpoints.
