# Low-lava escape query adjustment: stable introduction boundary

## Source finding

1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, lava branch, lines 1831–1924. `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `isFree(double,double,double)` and its AABB predicate, lines 442–446.

1.16.0, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)`, low-lava branch, lines 1912–1974; `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(double,boolean,Vec3)`, lines 2072–2084. `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing(Tag,double)`, `getFluidHeight(Tag)`, `getFluidJumpThreshold()`, and `isFree`, lines 2633–2712 and 453–457.

Difference: In 1.15.2, an ordinary non-flying player in lava moves relatively at `0.02F`, performs `move`, halves all movement components, adds `-d / 4.0` to Y when gravity is enabled, then—only when `horizontalCollision`—tests `isFree` with that resulting vector and sets Y to `0.3F` on success. In 1.16.0, the lava branch is gated by `isInLava() && isAffectedByFluids() && !canStandOnFluid(fluidState.getType())`. When cached lava height is at or below `getFluidJumpThreshold()`, it instead multiplies movement by `(0.5, 0.8F, 0.5)`, applies `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(d, bl, ...)`, then adds gravity `-d / 4.0`; only then does the same horizontal-collision/free-space gate test and possible `0.3F` response. Above the threshold, the new branch retains uniform `0.5` scaling before gravity. For a standing ordinary player `getFluidJumpThreshold()` is `0.4`; `canStandOnFluid` is false. The 1.16 helper subtracts `d / 16.0` from Y (subject to its exact `-0.003` special case) when gravity is enabled and the player is not sprinting. That helper is absent from both 1.15.2 `Entity.java` and `LivingEntity.java`.

The actual first stable release carrying the changed operation is **1.16.0**. This is an exact 1.15.2-to-1.16.0 source comparison, rather than a boundary inferred from the 1.16.5 endpoint. The affected `Entity.isFree` predicate is textually unchanged: both endpoints translate the current bounding box, then require `level.noCollision(this, box) && !level.containsAnyLiquid(box)`.

Movement meaning: The candidate offset supplied to `isFree` can change. A blocked query leaves the downward lava movement intact; a clear query replaces its Y velocity with `0.3F`. Thus the 1.16 low-lava math can change both the queried space and the resulting player velocity.

## Ordinary local-player reachability and gates

- `client/player/LocalPlayer.java` marks the local player as effective AI (`isEffectiveAi() == true`; 1.15.2 line 490, 1.16.0 line 519), satisfying the outer `LivingEntity.travel` gate on the logical client. `LivingEntity.aiStep()` calls `this.travel(new Vec3(this.xxa, this.yya, this.zza))` (1.15.2 line 2287; 1.16.0 line 2457).
- `Player.travel(Vec3)` delegates to `super.travel(vec3)` in its ordinary non-flying branch (1.15.2 lines 1448–1477; 1.16.0 lines 1380–1409). The witness uses a standing, non-swimming, non-flying, non-sprinting player who is not a passenger or fall-flying.
- `LivingEntity.isAffectedByFluids()` returns true (1.16.0 line 487), and `canStandOnFluid` returns false (line 1908). Standing Player eye height is `1.62F`; the threshold therefore evaluates to `0.4` (`Player.java` lines 112, 1815–1824; `Entity.java` lines 2700–2705).
- In 1.16.0 `Entity.baseTick()` calls `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing()` before AI travel (line 372). Fluid height is calculated from the deflated player box and each tagged fluid surface as `max(surfaceY - aabb.minY, currentHeight)` (lines 2633–2698), then exposed by `getFluidHeight`. This closes the height-state dependency for the witness below.
- `Entity.move` sets `horizontalCollision` when requested X/Z movement is clipped and zeroes the clipped horizontal velocity component (1.15.2 lines 450–498; 1.16.0 lines 469–517). `isFree` then checks the translated box for collision and any liquid; the predicate is unchanged between the endpoint bodies.

## Concrete vanilla-state witness

Use ordinary standing dimensions (`0.6F × 1.8F`, 1.15.2 `Player.java:111` and 1.16.0 `Player.java:112`) and a client local player centered at `(0.5, 64.70, 0.5)`, with pre-travel velocity `(0.50, -0.10, 0)`, eastward travel input, gravity enabled, and no sprint/flying/swimming/fall-flying. Put a still lava source at block `(0,64,0)`, stone around that cell at its horizontal sides and below so the source has no fluid current, a stone wall starting at `x=1` to clip eastward movement, and a stone ceiling block at `(0,67,0)`. All are era-existing vanilla states. The player box initially has X/Z bounds `[0.2,0.8]` and Y bounds `[64.70,66.50]`; it is clear of the wall and ceiling. The fluid-height update sees the lava surface at Y=65 and a deflated lower bound of 64.701, so its lava height is about `0.299`, positive and below `0.4`. The player is already past its first tick, so the 1.16.0 `isInLava()` predicate sees the positive cached lava height; the player cannot stand on lava and has `bl == true` because pre-travel Y is negative.

Eastward movement clips at the wall after about `0.2` blocks, setting horizontal velocity to zero and `horizontalCollision` true; downward movement by `0.10` remains clear. After `move`, player Y is 64.60 and the saved pre-move Y is 64.70. In 1.15.2 the lava branch yields Y velocity `(-0.10 × 0.5) - 0.08/4 = -0.07`; its `isFree` Y offset is approximately `-0.07 + 0.6F - 64.60 + 64.70 = 0.630000024`. The translated standing box reaches about Y=67.03 and intersects the stone ceiling beginning at Y=67, so `isFree` is false and there is no `0.3F` escape response.

In 1.16.0, low-lava scaling yields `-0.10 × 0.8 = -0.08`. With `d=0.08`, the falling helper takes its ordinary branch and produces `-0.08 - 0.005 = -0.085`; gravity then yields `-0.105`. Its query offset is approximately `-0.105 + 0.6F - 64.60 + 64.70 = 0.595000024`. The translated box reaches about Y=66.995, below the ceiling block's bottom at Y=67, and is above the lava surface; with its X/Z box still clear of the wall boundary, `isFree` is true and the player receives Y velocity `0.3F`. The 0.035-block query-height difference therefore changes the response in a concrete reachable state.
