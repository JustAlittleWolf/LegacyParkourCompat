1.21.9, Mojmap: `world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 693-710, `checkInsideBlocks` lines 1134-1222; `world/level/BlockGetter.java`, traversal lines 171-266. 1.21.10, Mojmap: `world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks` lines 1137-1227; `world/level/BlockGetter.java`, traversal lines 171-266. 26.2, published unobfuscated names: `world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks` lines 1278-1379; `world/level/BlockGetter.java`, traversal lines 175-287.

Difference: 1.21.8 already truncates long traversal. Each segment's `BlockGetter.addCollisionsAlongTravel` advances one DDA axis, then executes `if (counter++ > 16) break` before clipping that crossing. At most 17 crossing attempts reach clipping, carrying visitor indices 1 through 17; attempts with empty `AABB.clip` still consume the counter. On truncation the counter becomes 18. The wrapper then visits unvisited cells of the segment's deflated endpoint box with index 19. A segment shorter than `0.99999F` takes the endpoint-only branch with index 0. The internal limit resets for every component segment; Entity has no additional budget shared across the record's axes. This is neither an uncapped trajectory nor a limit on individual block cells.

In 1.21.9 the internal DDA limit is absent. Entity instead initializes a remaining budget of 16 per movement record. The iterator emits the start box at index 0, increments its index only after a successful DDA clip, and emits remaining endpoint cells at the final index plus one. Entity rejects a visit when its index is at least the remaining budget, before reading the block state. Its segment helper returns the last accepted index plus one (the initially zero atomic counter means even an immediately rejected or empty traversal returns 1). That return is subtracted before the next component segment. Multiple cells share an index; air and previously visited cells still update the accepted index. Once the record's remaining budget is nonpositive, Entity separately checks the record's actual endpoint with a stationary segment and budget 1, allowing its index-0 cells through the shared Entity visited set. Later component helpers are still called with the exhausted budget. The same budget contract is present in 1.21.10 and 26.2.

Applicability: Exact 1.21.6 and 1.21.7 Mojmap `Entity.java` and `BlockGetter.java` are byte-identical to 1.21.8. Thus the 1.21.6-1.21.8 record-level recipe is established. 1.21.5 Mojmap `Entity.java` lines 655-680, 768-801 and 1079-1121 and `BlockGetter.java` lines 172-252 establish the same internal 17-attempt segment cap and endpoint completion, but a different recording contract: `move` immediately splits the resolved displacement into component records and sets the final position through those component additions; it keeps batches and has no 100-record compression. In 1.21.6-1.21.8, `move` first adds the full resolved vector to the position; later callback traversal reconstructs components from stored `to - from`, and compression can create an unsplit record. Consequently the full 1.21.8 Entity loop must not be assigned to 1.21.5 merely because the old segment budget is shared.

Movement meaning: A nonpassenger local player can receive finite horizontal delta movement 20 through `ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket` / `PositionMoveRotation` and `ClientPacketListener.handleMovePlayer` / `setValuesFromPositionPacket` (1.21.8 lines 769-795; 1.21.9 lines 764-790). `PositionMoveRotation.STREAM_CODEC` lines 12-23 uses `Vec3.STREAM_CODEC`, and `calculateAbsolute` does not clamp that component. `LivingEntity.aiStep` travels before applying block effects (1.21.8 lines 2815-2822; 1.21.9 lines 2845-2852). In loaded clear space, a straight first move from x=0.5 to x=20.5 can therefore cross a cobweb at x=16, away from the final box: the old segment can visit its crossing index 16, whereas the new 16-budget rejects crossing index 16 and checks the distant endpoint instead. `WebBlock.entityInside` calls `makeStuckInBlock`, so this can change the next movement's multiplier. Both versions can omit other intermediate cells on still longer paths. This is a static source witness, not a runtime parity claim.
