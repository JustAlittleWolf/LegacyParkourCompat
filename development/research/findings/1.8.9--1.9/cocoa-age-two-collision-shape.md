# Cocoa age-two collision shape and grounded X clipping

1.8.9, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CocoaBlock.java`, `isSupported` (41–45), `getCollisionShape` (58–61), and `updateShape` (70–89); `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (371–459, 460–540); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, `getCollisions` (891–925).
1.9, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CocoaBlock.java`, EAST age shapes (21–25), `isSupported` (60–64), and `getShape` (77–89); `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` (441–532, 533–621); `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, `getCollisions` (899–940).
Difference: 1.8.9 calculates EAST age two with `j=8`, `k=9`, yielding local bounds `[0.4375,0.1875,0.25]–[0.9375,0.75,0.75]`. 1.9’s EAST shape array selects `[0.5625,0.3125,0.3125]–[0.9375,0.75,0.6875]` at both ages one and two. Both support EAST-facing cocoa by checking the block one position EAST for a jungle log. The same smaller age-two box and support rule are present in the inspected 1.9.1, 1.9.2, 1.9.3 and 1.9.4 sources. Entity movement gathers collision boxes for the swept box, clips Y then X then Z, and may try the ordinary 0.6 step branch when grounded. In the fixed witness (cocoa at `(0,0,0)` EAST age two, jungle log at `(1,0,0)`; player box `[-0.2,0,0.2]–[0.4,1.8,0.8]` resting on a floor at `y=0`; ordinary non-sneaking +X movement requests `dx=0.1`), the 1.8.9 cocoa intersects the swept box and clips X to `0.0375` (player center ends at `x=0.1375` if its start center is `0.1`). Its 0.6 step attempt still overlaps the cocoa’s top at `y=0.75`, so it cannot improve the horizontal displacement. In 1.9+, the swept maximum `x=0.5` remains below cocoa’s minimum `x=0.5625`; cocoa does not clip this move.
Movement meaning: for this reachable fixed-profile grounded player path, the 1.8.9 age-two cocoa clips +X while every inspected stable 1.9 patch through 1.9.4 allows the full requested +0.1.
