Difference: In 1.13, `autoJump` visits bounding boxes from one collision shape. `WorldView.getBlockCollisions` OR-joins partial block shapes by column/volume, optimizes that union, then joins the full-cube aggregate. In 1.13.1, `autoJump` flattens boxes from the streamed individual translated shapes and stops at the first ray hit. The region iterator advances X first, so the x=1 slab is streamed before the x=2 slab. Adjacent top slabs therefore form a merged x=1..3 box in 1.13, but remain separate components in 1.13.1.

## Legal fixture and exact probe geometry

Use an ordinary player with width `0.6F`, height `1.8F`, default movement speed attribute `0.1F`, no effects, yaw `-90°`, forward input `(0, 1)`, AutoJump enabled, cooldown zero, on ground, not sneaking/riding, and no entities. Let a collision-free +X move take the player from `(x=0.59, feetY=64, z=0.5)` to the post-move pose `(x=0.69, feetY=64, z=0.5)`, with actual displacement `(dx=0.1F, dz=0)`. The move method calls `autoJump` with the post-minus-pre displacement; the input vector is nonzero and the resulting direction faces forward, so neither early gate rejects it. This is a source-level ordinary-input path, not a runtime observation.

Place a full, clear support floor at block y=63. Place dry top slabs at `(1,64,0)` and `(2,64,0)`. Place a no-eye End Portal Frame at `(2,65,0)`. Keep the player's initial headroom cells `(0,65,0)` and `(0,66,0)` empty; keep the approach and all other queried cells clear. At post-move x=0.69, a width-0.6 player has horizontal extent approximately `[0.39,0.99]`, so it does not overlap the x=1 slab. At its pre-move x=0.59 it is also clear. The support tops meet feetY=64.

For `autoJump`, `vec3d=(0.69,64,0.5)`, `vec3d2=(0.79,64,0.5)` (the method adds actual dx to the already post-move position), and normalized `vec3d4` is +X. With `f=0.1F`, `g=(float)(dx²)=0.010000001` approximately, `l=(float)fastInvSqrt(g)` approximately 10, and `p=max(f*7,1/l)=0.7F`. The search endpoint is about x=1.49. The search `Box` is grown by `q=width=0.6F`, yielding x bounds about `[0.09,2.09]`, y bounds `[64,65.8]`, and z bounds `[-0.1,1.1]`. Both slab collision shapes overlap this query and are admitted. Values are approximate only where the source uses float inverse-square-root arithmetic; the x/y/z margins are large enough that rounding cannot change admission.

The raised side rays begin at `(0.69,64.51,0.2)` and `(0.69,64.51,0.8)`, and end at about `(1.49,64.51,0.2)` and `(1.49,64.51,0.8)`. These are derived from raising endpoints by `0.51F` and offsetting by half width (`0.3F`) perpendicular to +X. The top slab shape is `[x,x+1] × [64.5,65] × [0,1]`, so both rays hit the x=1 slab. They end before x=2, so neither hits the x=2 slab. The query includes both components, but ray eligibility includes only the first slab component in the 1.13.1 stream.

The first hit sets `s=65`. Its box center is `(1.5,64.75,0.5)`; `new BlockPos(Vec3d)` floors this to `(1,64,0)`. Since there is no Jump Boost, `o=1.2F` and the loop executes only `t=1` (`1 < 1.2F`). It reads `(1,65,0)`, which is empty; `s` remains 65, so `u=s-feetY=1.0F`. The strict timer bounds accept it (`u>0.5F` and `u<=1.2F`) and set `ticksToNextAutojump=1` in 1.13.1.

In 1.13, the adjacent slabs' joined/optimized shape has bounding box `[1,3] × [64.5,65] × [0,1]`, which is intersected by the same rays. Its center `(2,64.75,0.5)` floors to `(2,64,0)`. At `t=1`, the secondary read is `(2,65,0)`, the no-eye frame. Its collision shape has height 13/16, so absolute top is `65 + 13/16 = 65.8125`; this overwrites `s`. Then `s-feetY=1.8125F > o`, so the method returns before setting the timer. An eye must be absent: with an eye, the frame's collision shape is taller and remains an early-return case, but this witness specifically relies on its 13/16 body top.

The initial empty-headroom gate reads `(0,65,0)` and `(0,66,0)`, not the frame at x=2; it passes. The frame is encountered only by the post-hit center-column lookup. This resolves the former fixture's ambiguity around the post-move position and makes all slab/frame positions legal without embedding the player.
