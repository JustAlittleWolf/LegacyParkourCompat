# Direct local player position correction changes its write gate and interpretation

1.21.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.handleMovePlayer` (620–690), `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket` (10–49), and `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.setXRot` (3363–3368).

1.21.2 and 1.21.3, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.handleMovePlayer` (706–715) and `setValuesFromPositionPacket` (717–735); `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket` (12–25); `net/minecraft/world/entity/PositionMoveRotation` (10–20, 35–62); `net/minecraft/world/entity/Relative` (10–24); and `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.setXRot` (3489–3494).

Difference: In 1.21.1, the local-player handler applies the received position, delta movement, and rotation without checking whether the player is a passenger. Relative position axes add their payload to the current position and preserve that axis of existing velocity; absolute axes set position and zero that velocity component. Relative angles add to current rotation. In 1.21.2 and 1.21.3, correction writes run only when `!player.isPassenger()`. For a passenger, the handler replies with its current `PosRot` and accepts the teleport id without applying the correction. The packet now carries a `PositionMoveRotation` containing position, delta movement, yaw, and pitch, with relative flags for position, delta movement, and rotation. Its absolute-value calculation combines relative position/rotation and velocity components, including rotation of prior movement for `ROTATE_DELTA`. The shared `Entity.setXRot` also changes: 1.21.1 rejects non-finite pitch and stores finite values as-is, while 1.21.2 and 1.21.3 additionally apply `% 360` and clamp to `[-90, 90]`. Thus a finite absolute pitch of 360° stores as 360° in 1.21.1 and 0° in 1.21.2/1.21.3. The response order changes from `AcceptTeleportation` then `PosRot` in 1.21.1 to `PosRot` then `AcceptTeleportation` in 1.21.2/1.21.3.

Movement meaning: The ordinary `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.teleport` path sends this packet family in all three versions (1.21.1: 989–1006; 1.21.2/1.21.3: 1007–1019); in 1.21.2/1.21.3, `ServerPlayer` calls that path (1046, 1069, 1198). A vanilla correction can therefore reach the local-player handler. When the player is a passenger, the newer handler leaves local position, velocity, and rotation unchanged by the correction and replies with its existing state; 1.21.1 applies the payload. This finding is limited to direct local-player corrections. It does not establish reachability of every relative-velocity flag combination, entity synchronization, removed-vehicle handling, respawn, or standalone rotation. The changed behavior is present in 1.21.2 and unchanged in 1.21.3; 1.21.1 retains the earlier behavior.
