# Glide fall-distance limiting moves before fluid travel

1.21.3, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.21.3/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep`, lines 2754–2776; `updateFallFlying`, lines 2814–2840; `travel`, lines 2178–2189; `travelInFluid`, lines 2216–2266. `Entity.java`, `checkSlowFallDistance`, lines 2484–2488; `checkFallDamage`, lines 1216–1231; `baseTick`, lines 476–479.

Difference: Both versions limit fall distance to `1.0F` when the current vertical velocity is strictly greater than `-0.5` and accumulated fall distance is strictly greater than `1.0F`. In 1.21.1, that operation occurs inside the fall-flying branch of `travel`, after water and lava branches have taken precedence. In 1.21.3, it occurs in `updateFallFlying`, invoked by `aiStep` when the fall-flying flag is set, before the effect reset and travel dispatch. It consequently also runs on a gliding player whose travel selects lava physics. The threshold and assigned value did not change. Exact boundary evidence follows below.

Movement meaning: A local, non-flying, non-passenger player can retain the fall-flying flag while entering lava: neither version's client `updateFallFlying` clears that flag, and lava does not reset fall distance to zero. Consider airborne lava travel with no water, slow-falling or levitation, vertical velocity above `-0.5`, and fall distance still above one after the lava tick's halving. A sufficiently long preceding steep glide followed by lava drag can supply those conditions. In 1.21.1 the lava branch skips the limiter; in 1.21.3 the pre-travel limiter sets the state to one. Subsequent downward `move` adds the float-cast displacement to those different starting states. This establishes a direct player state difference without relying on fall-damage calculation. Native ability flight has separate player resets (after travel in 1.21.1 and before `super.aiStep` in 1.21.3); the witness has that ability disabled.

Boundary evidence: Exact 1.21, Mojmap, `decompiled_minecraft/1.21/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel`, lines 2091–2153; `aiStep`, lines 2664–2678; `updateFallFlying`, lines 2716–2741, retains the old placement. These exact stable releases establish 1.21.2 as the first stable release with the relocated limiter and establish continuity of this bounded player state behavior through 1.21.3.
