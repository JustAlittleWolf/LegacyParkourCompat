Difference: In 1.21.1 the normal physics-enabled `Entity.move` path invokes `checkInsideBlocks` after movement; the `noPhysics` fast path and zero-vector piston early return bypass it. It scans the final bounding box deflated by `1.0E-7` and unconditionally calls `entityInside` and `onInsideBlock` for every bounded-loop block state, including air; it does not test an inside shape or deduplicate positions across moves. In 1.21.2 onward, `LivingEntity.aiStep` calls `applyEffectsFromBlocks` only on the logical server or for the controlled local instance. The routine skips removed entities and `noPhysics` entities, records movement segments, then traverses their block positions using the final bounding box deflated by `1.0E-5F`; it skips air and already-visited positions. The default inside shape is `Shapes.block()`. Non-full inside shapes require the movement segment to collide with the shape before callbacks run. `EndPortalBlock` supplies the block's shape and its callback sets inside-portal state when `canUsePortal(false)`; `NetherPortalBlock` uses the default full-block inside shape and the same portal-state callback. `TripWireBlock` supplies its block shape, but its callback is server-only.
Movement meaning: For a living local player controlled by this client, movement through a portal cell can dispatch its callback in 1.21.2 onward even if the final box has left that cell; End Portal dispatch additionally depends on crossing its inside shape, while Nether Portal uses the full-cell inside shape. In 1.21.1 only cells overlapping the final box at each `move` callback are visited, independent of shape; air positions are also passed to the no-op/default block callbacks. Tripwire's new callback gate has no client-side movement response.
