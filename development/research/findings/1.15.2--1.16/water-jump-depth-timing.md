# Retained water depth no longer selects water jumps after a skipped refresh

1.15.2, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.15.2/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `baseTick`, lines 348–363; `updateWaterState` / `updateInWaterState`, lines 943–972; `checkAndHandleWater`, lines 2593–2651. `world/level/LevelReader.java`, `hasChunksAt`, lines 157–182. `world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep`, lines 2207–2287; `jumpInLiquid`, lines 1823–1825. `client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `tick`, lines 179–181; `serverAiStep`, lines 607–618; `aiStep`, lines 625–647 and 778.

1.16, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.16/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `baseTick`, lines 357–374; `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing` / `updateInWaterStateAndDoWaterCurrentPushing`, lines 947–970; `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing` / `getFluidHeight`, lines 2633–2701. `world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep`, lines 2366–2458; `jumpInLiquid`, lines 1900–1902. `client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `serverAiStep`, lines 634–645; `aiStep`, lines 652–678 and 813.

Difference: Both versions refresh water state during the inherited entity tick, then sample current input in the local player's AI step and copy its jump flag before jump selection. In 1.15.2, the water-depth scalar is written only after a completed fluid scan. A failed `hasChunksAt` gate returns before that write, leaving previous depth while `updateInWaterState` sets `wasInWater` false. Jump selection nevertheless reads the scalar without requiring `isInWater()`: positive retained depth selects a liquid jump whenever airborne, or when grounded with depth greater than the double constant `0.4`. This adds the float constant `0.04F` to vertical velocity. In 1.16, the base fluid update clears the tagged depth map before the scan; water-jump selection additionally requires `isInWater()` and positive selected depth. A skipped scan cannot select a water jump from retained depth.

Movement meaning: A player can cross the top build-height query boundary in one jump from a waterlogged chest at block Y=255. The chest top is Y=255.875, while its source water has height `8 / 9.0F`, giving positive depth below `0.4`. With grounded jump input and zero jump delay, the ground-jump branch sets upward velocity to `0.42F`; water travel moves before applying drag, crossing Y=256. The following scan fails the `minY < 256` query gate. In 1.15.2, held jump then adds `0.04F` from the stale positive depth despite the water-state flag being false. In 1.16, it does not. This is a source-derived reachable consequence, not a runtime parity result.
