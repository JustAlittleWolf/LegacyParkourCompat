# Moving slime block player velocity packet

- 1.15.2, Mojmap, `net/minecraft/world/level/block/piston/PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:98–153` (`moveCollidedEntities`). A moving slime block sets the movement-axis velocity to the direction step for eligible queried entities, preserves the other two components and continues to displacement handling.
- 1.16 and 1.16.1, Mojmap, `PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:101–159` (`moveCollidedEntities`). Immediately after the same velocity write, the method sends `ClientboundSetEntityMotionPacket(entity)` to a recipient that is a `ServerPlayer`, before computing push displacement.
- 1.16, Mojmap, `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundSetEntityMotionPacket.java:19–42` (constructors and serialization), `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java:568–578` (`handleSetEntityMotion`) and `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java:1766–1768` (`lerpMotion`). The packet captures all three server-side velocity components, clamps each to `[-3.9, 3.9]`, multiplies by `8000.0` and truncates to an integer serialized as a short. The client resolves the entity ID, divides each component by `8000.0` and overwrites the recipient's velocity. In 1.15.2 the same receiver exists at `ClientPacketListener.java:554–564` and `Entity.java:1733–1735`, without this explicit piston send.
- 1.16.2, Mojmap, `PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:102–165` (`moveCollidedEntities`), and 26.2, unobfuscated, `PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:120–190` (same member). The moving-slime branch skips `ServerPlayer` entirely, so this sender is absent. A qualifying local player still receives the local movement-axis assignment with its other two components preserved.

The 1.16 and 1.16.1 change can replace the local player's entire velocity at packet handling time, including the two components the piston leaves alone locally. The untouched server components and arrival order are external inputs. The current native local response corresponds to the 1.15.2 velocity assignment for this operation.
