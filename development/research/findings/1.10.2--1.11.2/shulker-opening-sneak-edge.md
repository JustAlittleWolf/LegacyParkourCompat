# Shulker opening bypasses player sneak-edge backoff

1.10.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/mob/monster/ShulkerEntity.java:231-296` calls untyped `entity.move(k,l,m)` for an intersecting entity on a positive WEST-opening delta; `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:468-482` applies grounded-player sneak-edge reduction without a mover-type gate.
1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/living/mob/monster/ShulkerEntity.java:246-311` calls `entity.move(MoverType.SHULKER,i,j,k)`; `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:519-532` applies the edge reduction only to `SELF` or `PLAYER`. The isolated bottom EAST stair collision boxes are defined and resolved by `net/minecraft/block/StairsBlock.java:33-42,67-102,311-348`; strict overlap and positive-X clipping are in `net/minecraft/util/math/Box.java:185-203,245-251`.
Difference: With a WEST-attached Shulker at `(0,1,0)`, a legal `.9F→.95F` progress update gives `e≈0.9755252898`, `d≈0.9938471615`, and a positive X push `h≈0.0183218718`. A sneaking grounded player with feet at `y=1`, `minX≈1.9838397`, and center `z=.5` strictly intersects the grown Shulker shape and is supported by the EAST upper step of an isolated bottom stair at `(1,0,0)`. In 1.10.2, the `-1` support probe after the candidate push clears the stair and Shulker collision boxes, so the `<.05` backoff reduces X to zero. In 1.11.2, the `SHULKER` mover skips the edge gate; the stair face-touches the movement query and the already-overlapping Shulker box does not clip positive X, so the immediate accepted displacement is `h`. This is one source-resolved move, not a prior or subsequent trajectory. The first inspected release with the typed call and gate is 1.11; 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 retain it.
