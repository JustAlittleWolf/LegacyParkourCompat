A witness away from the border and world limits satisfies both paths. The first stable changed release is 1.11.1, established by the immediately preceding stable 1.11 and unchanged 1.11.2 behavior.

The 1.11, 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 dynamic path is block-state `addCollisions` -> `MovingBlock.addCollisions` -> block entity `addCollisions` -> moved fence state `addCollisions`. It inverse-translates the query, appends intersecting fence boxes, then forward-translates only the appended list suffix. The center post is always emitted before conditional NORTH/EAST/SOUTH/WEST arms. Its translated box is `[x+0.875,x+1.125] × [y,y+1.5] × [z+0.375,z+0.625]`. The center post has no neighboring-block dependency. In 1.11 the fence resolves neighbor connections, whereas 1.11.1/1.11.2 receive `forceShape=true` and retain stored connection flags; neither distinction removes the center post.

A concrete normal-player swept query avoids idealized decimal player dimensions: let the player have width `0.6F`, height `1.8F`, center x `x+1.55`, center z `z+0.5`, bottom y `y+1.3`, and request x movement `-0.2`, y/z movement zero. The x bounds of `playerBox.expanded(-0.2,0,0)` are approximately `[x+1.049999988,x+1.850000012]`, y bounds `[y+1.3,y+3.099999952]`, and z bounds `[z+0.199999988,z+0.800000012]`. Thus the moving cell lies on the lower x perimeter, at the lower padded y row, with interior z. It strictly intersects the translated center post. Choose loaded chunks, a position well within the world border, no other intersecting entities or blocks, `noClip=false`, no cobweb, and an airborne nonsneaking player; the normal movement query is reached without sneak-edge adjustment or stepping.

1.11 skips this candidate cell; 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 append its center post. This adjacent comparison isolates the scan change. The `Box.intersectX` consumer can then shorten the negative x displacement because the initial player box lies to the right of the post and overlaps its y/z ranges.

The 1.10.2 endpoint also skips the cell, but its provider must not be conflated with the 1.11 provider: `MovingBlock` inherits base block collision collection, whose dynamic collision-shape lookup reaches `MovingBlockEntity.getShape`. That method unions the moved state's ordinary `getShape` at current and last progress. Fence ordinary `SHAPES` have height 1.0, unlike the independently emitted 1.5-high collision post. Consequently this endpoint witness is not evidence that changing only 1.10.2's scan mask would produce the newer box.
