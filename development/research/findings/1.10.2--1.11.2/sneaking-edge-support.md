Difference: The 1.8.9, 1.9.4, 1.10 and 1.10.2 movement methods all gate this support-preserving adjustment on `onGround && isSneaking() && this instanceof PlayerEntity`. They reduce X first, then Z, then both diagonally, probing `World.getCollisions(this, getShape().moved(...))` with a fixed `-1.0` Y offset. Each retreat iteration subtracts/adds `0.05` or zeroes the remaining component when it is in `[-0.05, 0.05)`. At 1.11, `SELF` and `PLAYER` mover types are additionally required and the probe's Y offset becomes `-this.stepHeight`; the X/Z/diagonal order and retreat arithmetic remain the same through 1.11.2. `LivingEntity` initializes `stepHeight` to `0.6F`. The source bounds the introduction to after 1.10.2 and by 1.11; no intermediate release was inspected to narrow it.
Movement meaning: Local player movement runs from the `LocalClientPlayerEntity` tick path through `LivingEntity` movement and invokes `Entity.move`; with grounded sneaking input, empty lowered-box collision probes cause the corresponding requested displacement component to retreat until support is found or the component reaches zero. In 1.8.9–1.10.2 the support sample is always one block below the player box; from 1.11 it is `stepHeight` below for `SELF`/`PLAYER` movement. The provider chain is `World.getCollisions` → block-state collision collection (`BlockState.addCollisions` / `Block.addCollisions` in these mappings), so the probe tests collision shapes while retaining vanilla block states.
