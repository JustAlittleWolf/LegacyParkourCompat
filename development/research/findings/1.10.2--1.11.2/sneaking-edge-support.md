# Sneaking edge support uses step height
1.10.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.10.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tick` lines 152–163, `serverTickAi` lines 600–611, and `mobTick` lines 620–790; `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `tick` lines 1525–1588, `mobTick` lines 1681–1755 and `moveRelative` lines 1332–1500; `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 446–495.
1.11.2, Ornithe Feather: `decompiled_minecraft/1.11.2/ornithe-feather/net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `tick` lines 159–170, `serverTickAi` lines 612–623, and `mobTick` lines 632–790; `net/minecraft/entity/living/LivingEntity.java`, `tick` lines 1581–1644, `mobTick` lines 1737–1803 and `moveRelative` lines 1388–1549; `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 459–548.
Difference: For a sneaking player on the ground, 1.10.2 checks support by querying `World.getCollisions` with the candidate player box lowered by exactly `1.0` block on X, Z and diagonal adjustment loops. In 1.11.2, `SELF` and `PLAYER` movement types enter this player-only gate, and the same queries lower the box by `stepHeight` instead. The 0.05-block retreat loop and axis order remain the same. `LivingEntity.mobTick` preserves the jump/travel call order; its ordinary travel branches now call `move(MoverType.SELF, ...)`.
Movement meaning: Local camera movement inputs flow through `LocalClientPlayerEntity.serverTickAi` → `LivingEntity.mobTick` → `moveRelative` → `Entity.move`; when grounded and sneaking, support edges are tested at a different vertical offset, so movement can be retained or curtailed differently near an edge. `World.getCollisions` still resolves block states through `BlockState.addCollisions` to block collision shapes in both releases.
