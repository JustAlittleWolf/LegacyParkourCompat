Difference: 1.10.2 finds entities intersecting the moving block's unioned shape and pushes each by the overlap distance plus `0.01`, using ordinary `Entity.move`. 1.11.2 advances the piston shape for the tick, derives a per-shape swept movement area from the progress delta, computes the translation required to clear intersecting entity boxes, then calls `move(MoverType.PISTON, ...)`. That movement type accumulates per-axis piston displacement per world tick, clamps the total to `[-0.51, 0.51]`, and ignores deltas at or below `1.0E-5F`. The collision provider also routes moving-block collision queries through `MovingBlockEntity.addCollisions`, including the carried block or source piston head shape.
Movement meaning: A player intersecting a moving piston can receive a different per-tick displacement and collision response in 1.11.2. The block-entity tick calls this response without a client-side exclusion, so it also reaches the local client player's position path.
