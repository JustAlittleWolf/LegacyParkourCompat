Difference: 1.10.2 finds entities intersecting the moving block's unioned shape and pushes each by the overlap distance plus `0.01`, using ordinary `Entity.move`. 1.11.2 advances the piston shape for the tick, derives a per-shape swept movement area from the progress delta, computes the translation required to clear intersecting entity boxes, then calls `move(MoverType.PISTON, ...)`. That movement type accumulates per-axis piston displacement per world tick, clamps the total to `[-0.51, 0.51]`, and ignores deltas at or below `1.0E-5F`. The collision provider also routes moving-block collision queries through `MovingBlockEntity.addCollisions`, including the carried block or source piston head shape.
Movement meaning: A player intersecting a moving piston can receive a different per-tick displacement and collision response in 1.11.2. The block-entity tick calls this response without a client-side exclusion, so it also reaches the local client player's position path.

## Bounded cobblestone stair cutover

On the second eastward half-step of a dry bottom north-facing straight cobblestone stair, the 1.10.2 full-outline scan requests +0.36 X for the ordinary player at feet Y=0.5 and minimum X/Z=0.65. Stable 1.11, 1.11.1 and 1.11.2 still include that player in the broad query. The lower component only touches the feet and the north tread ends before the player box. Their strict per-box tests leave the piston request at zero. The existing 1.10.2 stair change and the native per-box path already cover this fixture. The interpenetrating full-stone request remains an abstract comparison. ## Full-stone first-tick starting-state gate

For the exact 1.10.2 and 1.11 full-stone fixture, the synchronized vanilla piston event installs the progress-zero moving collision before the ordinary client tick can move the player. With an initially nonpenetrating player and no overriding position write, ordinary collision resolution keeps the player out of the interior start required for the abstract +1.31 X request. A legal east-face contact at X=0 requests +0.51 X in both versions. This closes the fixed full-stone starting-state question.
