# Soul sand now damps horizontal player velocity
1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, end of the movement/tick path after `checkInsideBlocks`, lines 535–550; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, `SOUL_SAND`, lines 577–580.
1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, end of the movement/tick path, lines 535–548, and `getBlockSpeedFactor`, lines 590–596; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `getBlockSpeedFactor`, lines 2028–2030; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Blocks.java`, `SOUL_SAND`, lines 601–604.
Difference: After block callbacks, 1.15.2 multiplies the entity's X and Z velocity by its block speed factor. The factor is taken from the block at the entity position, falling back to the block below that affects movement when the first factor is 1.0. The player override disables this factor while ability-flying or fall-flying. Soul sand changes from default block properties in 1.14.4 to `speedFactor(0.4F)` in 1.15.2. A grounded, non-flying player standing on soul sand therefore reaches the new multiplier through the fallback block position.
Movement meaning: For an ordinary grounded player on soul sand, 1.15.2 leaves 40% of the horizontal velocity after this movement call for subsequent movement to consume; 1.14.4 does not apply this block-based damping.
