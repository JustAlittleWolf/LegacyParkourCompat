1.15.1 and 1.15.2, Mojmap: the same `Blocks.java` SOUL_SAND registration at lines 601–604, absent `SoulsandBlock.entityInside`, same `Entity.java` factor path at lines 543–602, same `Player.java` flight gate at lines 2028–2030 (1.15.2), and same double-argument `Vec3.multiply` implementation at lines 96–98 (1.15.2).
Difference: 1.14.4 registers Soul Sand without a speed factor and `SoulsandBlock.entityInside` multiplies the entity's X and Z velocity by the double literal `0.4` whenever the entity's block-contact scan invokes the callback for a Soul Sand cell. In 1.15, that callback is removed; the block is registered with `speedFactor(0.4F)`, and after `checkInsideBlocks()` returns, `Entity.move` multiplies X and Z velocity once by `getBlockSpeedFactor()`. The factor lookup uses the block at `new BlockPos(this)`, falling back to `getBlockPosBelowThatAffectsMyMovement()` when its factor is `1.0`, except when the current block is water or a bubble column. The player override returns `1.0F` while ability-flying or fall-flying. The new factor is a `float` (`0.4F`) widened to `double` for `Vec3.multiply(double, double, double)`, so one factor is `0.4000000059604645`; the old callback passes double `0.4` (`0.4000000000000000` as rounded for display). The exact stable-version boundary is 1.15: the registered factor and removed callback are present in 1.15 and retained in inspected 1.15.1 and 1.15.2.
Movement meaning: For an ordinary local player standing on Soul Sand, both versions apply one horizontal `0.4`-class multiplier for one intersected Soul Sand cell; 1.14.4 applies the double literal in the cell callback, while 1.15+ applies the widened float after block-contact callbacks. The factor precision therefore differs even in the one-cell case. If multiple Soul Sand cells are contacted, 1.14.4 repeats the per-cell multiplication, while 1.15+ performs one post-scan multiplication from the selected current/support block. Ability-flying and fall-flying players bypass the new player factor response, while the old block callback has no such player-specific gate. The stable transition is established at 1.15; this finding covers Soul Sand, an old-era block, rather than blocks introduced after 1.14.4.
