# Elytra jump-edge local fall-flying start

1.9–1.14.4, Ornithe Feather, Yarn and Mojmap as available. The local jump-edge branch is in `LocalClientPlayerEntity.mobTick` in 1.9 (lines 703–708), `ClientPlayerEntity.tickMovement` in 1.14 Yarn (lines 697–702), and `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` in 1.14.4 Mojmap (lines 707–712).
1.15–1.15.2, Mojmap. The branch is in `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` (lines 715–719), and the local flag write is in `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `tryToStartFallFlying` and `startFallFlying` (1.15.2 lines 1578–1592).

Difference: From 1.9 through 1.14.4, a rising jump input sends a start request when the player is airborne, has strictly negative vertical velocity, is neither fall-flying nor ability-flying, and wears a usable chest Elytra. The gate reads ability flight after the same-tick toggle logic. This branch leaves the local fall-flying flag unchanged. Starting in 1.15, the client admits zero and positive vertical velocity, excludes passengers and same-tick ability-flight toggles, and sends the request only after the helper sets local flag 7. The helper checks airborne state, existing fall-flying, water contact and chest Elytra usability. 1.15.2 also rejects climbable contact before the equipment check.

Movement meaning: A dry survival player with a usable chest Elytra can release jump and press it again while still rising from an ordinary jump. In 1.15 the local flag lets travel select fall-flying in the same tick. In 1.14.4 the rising player fails the negative-velocity gate. A ladder provides a separate 1.15.2 witness because the new climbable gate can suppress that immediate local start. The earlier request predicate contains no passenger test, but the ordinary ride tick clears the player's vertical velocity before player tick and does not establish a reachable mounted descending request.
