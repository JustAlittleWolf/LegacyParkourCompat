1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, `update()`, lines 54–85, and `tick(LivingEntity)`, lines 111–121; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tickEffects()`, lines 605–620, `hasEffect()` / `getEffect()`, lines 752–758, and `jumpFromGround()`, lines 1752–1768; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleUpdateMobEffect()` and `handleRemoveMobEffect()`, lines 1491–1509 and 1605–1611.

1.15, 1.15.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, `update()` and `tick(LivingEntity)`, lines 54–85 and 111–121 (no hidden-effect chain).

1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/item/alchemy/Potions.java`, `LEAPING` and `STRONG_LEAPING`, lines 21–23; `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, `update()` and `tick(LivingEntity, Runnable)`, lines 66–109 and 135–157; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `addEffect()`, `tickEffects()`, `hasEffect()` / `getEffect()` and `jumpFromGround()`, lines 729–745, 573–588, 720–727 and 1803–1817; `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `onEffectAdded()`, `onEffectUpdated()` and `onEffectRemoved()`, lines 1031–1055; `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundUpdateMobEffectPacket.java`, constructor/write, lines 19–59; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleUpdateMobEffect()` and `handleRemoveMobEffect()`, lines 1523–1541 and 1641–1647.

Difference: Under the same ordered 1.15.2 server-emitted effect-update/removal event stream, compared by decoded effect fields through each release's receive procedure, first apply Jump Boost amp 0 and then amp 1 while the weak instance has more than 1800 ticks remaining. The 1.15.2 update keeps that weaker instance hidden beneath the 1800-tick strong instance; 1.15 and 1.15.1 do not have hidden-effect state. The 1.15.2 server sends an amp-0 update when the strong instance expires, then sends REMOVE after the restored weaker duration elapses. On the 1.14.4 client, a lower-amplifier update does not replace amp 1, and client-side `tickEffects()` leaves the expired amp-1 map entry until REMOVE is handled. On the 1.15.2 client, the pre-existing hidden amp-0 instance is restored when local amp 1 expires. If local expiry occurs before REMOVE is handled, an eligible grounded `jumpFromGround()` reads amp 1 in the old procedure and amp 0 in the new procedure. For weaker remaining duration `w` when strong is added, the server fallback lasts `r = w - 1800` ticks (`1..1800`); the amp contributions are `+0.2F` and `+0.1F` respectively. This is a client-procedure comparison and makes no cross-version wire-compatibility claim.
