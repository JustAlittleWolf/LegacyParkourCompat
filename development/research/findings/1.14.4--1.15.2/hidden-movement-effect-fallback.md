# Hidden effect fallback affects jump boost
1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, `update` (59–84) and `tick` (111–125); `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tickEffects` (605–620) and `jumpFromGround` (1752–1768).
1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/effect/MobEffectInstance.java`, `update` (71–108) and `tick` (135–158); `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tickEffects` (573–587), `addEffect` (729–744), `jumpFromGround` (1803–1817), and `aiStep` jump dispatch (2265–2275); `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleUpdateMobEffect` (1523–1539).
Difference: 1.14.4 has no hidden effect field or fallback: `update` changes the primary effect's amplifier/duration, and `tick` only decrements that duration. 1.15.2 stores an existing weaker effect as `hiddenEffect` when a stronger, shorter effect replaces it; `tickDownDuration` decrements the hidden chain, and when the primary duration reaches zero, `tick` restores the hidden effect's details and invokes its callback. Client `LivingEntity.tickEffects` calls that overload without a logical-side restriction. Clientbound effect updates construct an instance and call `LivingEntity.addEffect`, whose `update` can construct this stack locally. The client-side player jump path reaches `jumpFromGround`, which reads `hasEffect(JUMP)` and the current `getEffect(JUMP).getAmplifier()` to set vertical velocity.
Movement meaning: after a short stronger Jump Boost is applied over a longer weaker Jump Boost, the client restores the weaker amplifier when the stronger one expires; a subsequent grounded player jump therefore receives the weaker boost's vertical impulse. This is a direct local movement response to the vanilla effect state and does not depend on client attribute production. The source change is absent in 1.15 and 1.15.1 (their `MobEffectInstance.java` hash matches 1.14.4) and present in 1.15.2; this bounds the observed representative transition to after 1.15.1 and by 1.15.2, without claiming an unexamined intervening release boundary.
