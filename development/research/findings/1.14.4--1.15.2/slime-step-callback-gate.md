# Flying players reach slime step damping by 1.15

1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 503–520; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `makeStepSound` lines 1646–1648; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/SlimeBlock.java`, `stepOn` lines 43–52.
1.15.2, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 478–501; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `travel` lines 1463–1475; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/SlimeBlock.java`, `updateEntityAfterFallOn` and `stepOn` lines 24–50; `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, grounded flight shutdown lines 778–782. The changed callback gate is present in 1.15 and the relevant `Entity.move` and `SlimeBlock` bodies are unchanged in 1.15.1 and 1.15.2.
Difference: In 1.14.4, `Entity.move` invokes `stepOn` only inside the step-sound/passenger gate; `Player.makeStepSound()` returns false while the player is flying. From 1.15, `Entity.move` invokes `stepOn` under a separate grounded and not-stepping-carefully gate before the step-noise gate. Thus a flying player landing on slime while not pressing shift can reach `SlimeBlock.stepOn` in 1.15, though not in 1.14.4. For a low-speed landing, slime multiplies horizontal velocity by `0.4 + abs(deltaY) * 0.2`. The flying `Player.travel` path preserves the horizontal velocity change, and `LocalPlayer.aiStep` then disables ordinary flight when grounded.
Movement meaning: A flying local player with nonzero horizontal velocity and a small downward velocity can land on slime without sneaking. The 1.15 callback reduces horizontal velocity on contact; 1.14.4 skips that damping callback. The stable cutover is 1.15: the old gate is present in 1.14.4 and the new gate in the next stable release, 1.15; 1.15.1 and 1.15.2 retain it.
