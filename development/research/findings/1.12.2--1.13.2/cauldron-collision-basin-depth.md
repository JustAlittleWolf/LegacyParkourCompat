# Cauldron collision basin begins lower
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CauldronBlock.java`, collision boxes and `addCollisions`, lines 35-52.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/CauldronBlock.java`, `INSIDE_SHAPE`/`SHAPE` construction and `getShape`, lines 34-44. 1.13.2 retains these same shape coordinates and subtraction operation.
Difference: The 1.12.2 collision bottom fills the block to Y=5/16, with four side strips extending to the full block height. First verified in 1.13, the shape subtracts an interior beginning at Y=4/16 from a full-block shape, so the open basin starts one sixteenth lower; 1.13.2 retains the same coordinates and subtraction operation.
Movement meaning: When a player overlaps the cauldron cavity near its floor, the collidable basin boundary differs by 1/16; the shape reaches player `Entity.move` through the world block-collision query and axis resolution (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).

Concrete local-player witness: Put a dry Cauldron with LEVEL=0 at `(0,0,0)` and no other colliders. A normal local player has dimensions `0.6F × 1.8F` (1.12.2 `PlayerEntity.java:399`; 1.13.2 `PlayerEntity.java:450`); `Entity.setPosition` centers the width around x/z and uses y as the feet coordinate (1.12.2 `Entity.java:298-306`; 1.13.2 `Entity.java:309-317`). Centered at `(0.5,0.5,0.5)`, its x/z bounds are approximately `0.199999988..0.800000012`, strictly inside the cauldron's `0.125..0.875` wall opening, with feet above both floors. The old callback emits the 5/16 base and four side components (`CauldronBlock.java:31-38,47-53`), while the new collision shape subtracts an interior beginning at y=4/16 (`CauldronBlock.java:34-45`).

For an ordinary airborne request `(dx,dy,dz)=(0,-0.30,0)`, the old wall boxes miss the player's horizontal footprint and the base clips Y to `0.3125−0.5 = −0.1875`; feet finish at y=0.3125 (`World.java:1001`; `Entity.java:567-595`; `Box.java:205-221`). In 1.13.2, the swept query carries the current player box as its exclusion shape (`WorldView.java:158-163`). Because that current box is inside the open basin and above its floor, it does not overlap the Cauldron collision shape, so the candidate is retained; the downward sweep reaches the 4/16 floor and clips to `0.25−0.5 = −0.25`, leaving feet at y=0.25 (`WorldView.java:112-150`; `Entity.java:580-583`; `VoxelShapes.java:194-203`; `VoxelShape.java:184-245`). Under the stated geometry, the old and new post-move feet coordinates differ by exactly 1/16 block.

The local-player move path delegates to the inherited collision mover (1.12.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:846-851`; 1.13.2 `LocalClientPlayerEntity.java:871-876`; living movement calls `Entity.move` at 1.12.2 `LivingEntity.java:1514` and 1.13.2 `:1575`). The falling request is reachable in ordinary dry free fall with loaded chunks and gravity enabled (1.12.2 `LivingEntity.java:1524-1536`; 1.13.2 `:1480-1483,1580-1597`). Preconditions: player is airborne and not on a step path; neutral horizontal input or auto-jump disabled; `abilities.flying=false` (1.12.2 `PlayerEntity.java:1386,1390-1395`; 1.13.2 `:1442,1454-1459`); `noClip=false` (1.12.2 `Entity.java:462-466`; 1.13.2 `:473-477`); not fall-flying, climbing, under Levitation or Slow Falling, or using no-gravity; and no fluid, fire, world-border sentinel, unloaded query area, or other collision interference.

This concrete witness clips against the basin floor and avoids the walls. It does not establish general internal-plane/component clipping equivalence for modern voxel decomposition or runtime parity.
Boundary: 1.13 is the first inspected release with the basin beginning at Y=4/16. This establishes the change between the inspected 1.12.2 and 1.13 releases; it does not locate a change inside that interval.
