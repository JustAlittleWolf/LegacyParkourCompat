# Anvil collision shape becomes component geometry
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/AnvilBlock.java`, `getShape`, lines 31-32 and 92-95; default `Block.getCollisionShape`, `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, lines 387-389.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/AnvilBlock.java`, static shape construction, lines 29-37, and `getShape`, lines 71-73; default `Block.getCollisionShape`, `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, lines 409-410. 1.13.2 has the same `AnvilBlock.java` source hash and geometry.
Difference: For each facing axis, 1.12.2 exposes one full-height cuboid spanning the anvil's horizontal footprint. First verified in 1.13, the shape becomes a union of a narrow base, two narrower intermediate sections, and a top plate, leaving the spaces between those components empty; 1.13.2 retains the same shape code.
Movement meaning: A player moving beside or under an anvil can now pass through regions inside the old bounding cuboid but outside the new component union; `Entity.move` obtains world collisions and clamps Y, X, and Z against them (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).
Boundary: 1.13 is the first inspected release with component geometry. Facing-axis orientation is preserved: the X-axis facing selects the shape whose top plate spans X, and the Z-axis facing selects the shape whose top plate spans Z. This establishes the change between the inspected 1.12.2 and 1.13 releases; it does not locate a change inside that interval.
