# Anvil collision shape becomes component geometry

1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/AnvilBlock.java`, `getShape`, lines 31-32 and 92-95; default `Block.getCollisionShape`, `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, lines 387-389.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/AnvilBlock.java`, static shape construction, lines 29-37, and `getShape`, lines 71-73; default `Block.getCollisionShape`, `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, lines 409-410. 1.13.2 has the same `AnvilBlock.java` source hash and geometry.

Difference: For each facing axis, 1.12.2 exposes one full-height cuboid spanning the anvil's horizontal footprint. First verified in 1.13, the shape becomes a union of a narrow base, two narrower intermediate sections, and a top plate, leaving the spaces between those components empty; 1.13.2 retains the same shape code.

Movement meaning: A player moving beside or under an anvil can now pass through regions inside the old bounding cuboid but outside the new component union; `Entity.move` obtains world collisions and clamps Y, X, and Z against them (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, including the block callback at line 1001, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).

**Concrete conditional ordinary-move witness (1.12.2 and 1.13.2).** In an otherwise empty, loaded, in-border client world, put one east-facing Anvil at `(0,0,0)`. An ordinary `0.6F × 1.8F` local player (`Entity.java:174-179` old; `:186-191` new), centered at `(-0.4,0.3,-0.125)`, starts airborne, not sneaking/riding, with `noClip=false`; request `Entity.move(SELF,+0.5,+0.1,0)`. The starting box is approximately X `[-0.7,-0.1]`, Y `[0.3,2.1]`, Z `[-0.425,0.175]`. The old full Anvil box overlaps the swept player volume in Y/Z, so the scan (`World.java:964-1014`, block callback at `:1001`) supplies a candidate and `Box.intersectX` clips positive-X movement at the Anvil's west face (`Box.java:185-203`), yielding `dx≈0.1`. In 1.13.2 the X-facing Anvil selects `SHAPE_Z`, whose top starts at Z `0.1875`, whose upper intermediate components start farther inward, and whose base ends at Y `0.25`; the swept box has `maxZ≈0.175` and `minY=0.3`, so no component overlaps it. The shape collector (`WorldView.java:112-150,154-164`) emits no Anvil candidate, and `VoxelShapes.calculateMaxOffset` leaves the requested positive-X movement unchanged (`Entity.java:578-599`; `VoxelShapes.java:194-205`), so `dx=0.5`. The predicted difference is about `+0.4` blocks along X for this move. This is a source-derived conditional witness, not a runtime observation; it requires no other collision shapes/entities or border substitution, and stays outside the step, sneak-edge, and auto-jump routes.

Boundary: 1.13 is the first inspected release with component geometry. Facing-axis orientation is preserved: the X-axis facing selects the shape whose top plate spans X, and the Z-axis facing selects the shape whose top plate spans Z. This establishes the change between the inspected 1.12.2 and 1.13 releases; it does not locate a change inside that interval. The added witness covers only its stated ordinary airborne path and does not close general component/internal-plane clipping under modern voxel decomposition or runtime parity.
