# Brewing stand collision base is inset
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/BrewingStandBlock.java`, shape constants and `addCollisions`, lines 36-37 and 69-73.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/BrewingStandBlock.java`, composite `SHAPE`, line 33, and `getShape`, lines 56-57. 1.13.2 has the same `BrewingStandBlock.java` source hash and geometry.
Difference: The central post has the same 2/16-by-2/16 footprint and 14/16 height. First verified in 1.13, the low plate changes from the full 16/16-by-16/16 footprint at 2/16 height to a 14/16-by-14/16 plate inset 1/16 from every side; 1.13.2 retains the same shape code.
Movement meaning: Near a brewing stand's lower outer edge, the player's available collision space changes by the inset strip; both player movement paths consume this block shape through the world collision query and axis resolution (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).
Boundary: 1.13 is the first inspected release with the inset plate. This establishes the change between the inspected 1.12.2 and 1.13 releases; it does not locate a change inside that interval.
