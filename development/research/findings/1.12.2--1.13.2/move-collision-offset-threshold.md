# Move clipping zeroes a tiny remainder before a later collision shape
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `entity/Entity.java:567-600`; `util/math/Box.java:185-243`; `world/World.java:964-1039`.
1.13.1, Ornithe Feather: `entity/Entity.java:578-599`; `block/shape/VoxelShapes.java:194-205`; `block/shape/VoxelShape.java:184-235`; `world/WorldView.java:112-152,154-163`; `world/World.java:1755-1758`; `world/EntityView.java:21-35`.
Difference: The 1.12.2 move loop clips each ordered `Box` candidate to its exact face gap. 1.13.1 folds a `Stream<VoxelShape>` and returns zero before the next shape whenever the current offset is below `1.0E-7`. In 1.13 the block and entity collision shapes were combined into one VoxelShape and clipped once (`WorldView.java:191-218`; `VoxelShapes.java:207-243`); it did not apply this cutoff between the stone and Shulker candidates in the witness. The ordered per-shape stream fold first appears in 1.13.1; 1.13.2 retains the same relevant method bodies.
Movement meaning: With a local player at `x = 1 - 0.3F - 5.0E-8`, airborne (`onGround == false`), moving `+X` by `0.8F`, place a full stone block at `(1,65,0)` and a closed, alive Shulker with box `[1.5,65,1]..[2.5,66,2]`. Let the player's `y` be `65.2` and `z` be `0.8`. The stone is the first X obstruction and leaves the positive face gap (about `5.0E-8`). In 1.12.2 the later Shulker box cannot reduce that already-clipped offset, so the player moves to the stone face. In 1.13.1 the block `IntVoxelShape` first returns the same gap; before the following Shulker shape is evaluated, the `1.0E-7` guard changes the offset to zero, leaving the player at the pre-move position. The on-ground/descending step gate is false, so no step candidate supersedes this result.
