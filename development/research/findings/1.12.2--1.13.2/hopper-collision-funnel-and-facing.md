# Hopper collision geometry becomes facing-dependent
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java`, `DOWN_SHAPE` and side shapes plus `addCollisions`, lines 38-42 and 56-62.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java`, component shapes and `getShape`, lines 33-41 and 54-68; `net/minecraft/inventory/Hopper.java`, `INSIDE`, line 10. 1.13.2 has the same `HopperBlock.java` and `Hopper.java` source hashes and geometry.
Difference: 1.12.2 uses the same bottom slab (Y=0..10/16) and four full-height 2/16 side strips for every hopper facing. First verified in 1.13, the shape selects by facing: it subtracts the hopper interior from a top rim/funnel union and adds a narrow, facing-specific outlet. For a downward-facing hopper, its bottom is a 4/16-square outlet plus an 8/16-square funnel from Y=4/16 to 10/16, rather than the old full-footprint 10/16 slab; 1.13.2 retains the same shape code.
Movement meaning: A player intersecting a hopper's lower interior encounters different collision volume. For horizontal facings, the newer geometry has a side-directed outlet at Y=4/16 to 8/16, but that volume already lies inside the 1.12.2 full-footprint slab; the change removes surrounding lower collision volume and replaces the full-height side strips with an upper rim plus the selected outlet. `Entity.move` consumes the returned block shapes (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).
Boundary: 1.13 is the first inspected release with facing-selected component geometry. This establishes the change between the inspected 1.12.2 and 1.13 releases; it does not locate a change inside that interval.
