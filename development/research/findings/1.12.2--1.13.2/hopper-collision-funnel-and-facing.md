# Hopper collision geometry becomes facing-dependent
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java`, `DOWN_SHAPE` and side shapes plus `addCollisions`, lines 38-42 and 56-62.
1.13, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java`, component shapes and `getShape`, lines 33-41 and 54-68; `net/minecraft/inventory/Hopper.java`, `INSIDE`, line 10. 1.13.2 has the same `HopperBlock.java` and `Hopper.java` source hashes and geometry.
Difference: 1.12.2 uses the same bottom slab (Y=0..10/16) and four full-height 2/16 side strips for every hopper facing. Starting in 1.13, the shape selects by facing: it subtracts the hopper interior from a top rim/funnel union and adds a narrow, facing-specific outlet. The top union starts at Y=10/16, while the interior subtraction starts at 11/16, leaving a full-footprint solid band at Y=10/16..11/16. The 8/16-square funnel at Y=4/16..10/16 also remains solid; that subtraction does not hollow it. For a downward-facing hopper, the lower outlet is 4/16 square, replacing part of the old full-footprint lower slab. Exact 1.13.1 and 1.13.2 retain the same shape code.
Movement meaning: A player intersecting a hopper's lower interior encounters different collision volume, and the new 10/16..11/16 band adds central collision volume above the old slab. For horizontal facings, the newer geometry has a side-directed outlet at Y=4/16 to 8/16, but that volume already lies inside the 1.12.2 full-footprint slab; other lower volume is removed. `Entity.move` consumes the returned block shapes (1.12.2 `net/minecraft/world/World.java`, lines 964-1019, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 567-595; 1.13 `net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java`, lines 113-150 and 154-196, and `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, lines 578-598; 1.13.2, `WorldView.java`, lines 112-150 and 154-196, and `Entity.java`, lines 578-598).
Boundary: The adjacent stable releases 1.12.2 and 1.13 establish the stable cutover at 1.13. The intervening snapshot introduction is not claimed.

## Conditional local-player displacement witness

This adds a concrete conditional displacement case without changing the finding's release-boundary statement.

For one ordinary Hopper at block position (0,0,0) with FACING=DOWN, place an airborne player whose horizontal center is x=z=0.5 and whose feet are at y=1.0, with ordinary width 0.6, in a clear, non-liquid scene. Give its normal SELF movement a downward request (0,-0.5,0). The player's horizontal footprint [0.2,0.8] × [0.2,0.8] fits inside the newer opening [2/16,14/16] × [2/16,14/16]. In the stated setup it starts above, without overlapping either Hopper shape.

In 1.12.2, HopperBlock.DOWN_SHAPE covers the full horizontal block footprint through Y=10/16 (net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java:38). The requested downward movement is clipped at the shape's top, so the player's feet end at Y=10/16. In 1.13.2, HopperBlock.DOWN_SHAPE contains BASE_SHAPE and the interior subtraction leaves the full horizontal slice Y=10/16..11/16; the upper opening begins above Y=11/16 (net/minecraft/block/HopperBlock.java:33-37; net/minecraft/inventory/Hopper.java:10). The same downward movement is clipped at Y=11/16. The resulting vertical displacement is 6/16 downward in 1.12.2 versus 5/16 in 1.13.2, a 1/16-block difference.

The paired local movement paths supply the collision shapes and vertical clip (1.12.2 net/minecraft/world/World.java:964-1010, net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:567-576, and net/minecraft/util/math/Box.java:205-220; 1.13.2 net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java:578-583, net/minecraft/world/WorldView.java:112-151,154-177, net/minecraft/block/state/BlockState.java:205-210, net/minecraft/block/Block.java:393-394,1300,1901,1943-1945, and net/minecraft/block/shape/VoxelShape.java:184-235). The source review also verified the registered Hopper reaches its state shape through the 1.13.2 collision path.

Keep this witness conditional on the down-facing state, centered 0.6-wide player, an airborne vertical-only move large enough to reach both surfaces, and a clear loaded in-border scene with no other colliders or entities. Exclude no-clip/no-gravity, climbing, levitation, fall-flying/creative flight, slow-fall, fluid, and step-up branches. A shorter descent that does not reach both surfaces does not realize the full 1/16 difference. Ordinary falling can provide a sufficient downward request, but this finding does not establish the exact preceding-tick trajectory.

The 11/16 contact is a source-derived vertical clip against the remaining plane in the collected 1.13.2 voxel shape. This witness does not establish behavior for other axes, overlap states, Hopper facings or collision arrangements, and it is not a runtime measurement or movement-loop test. It binds the 1.12.2 and 1.13.2 endpoints; it does not independently revise the existing 1.13 cutover or establish runtime parity.
