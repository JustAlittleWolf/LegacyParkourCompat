# Moving piston head short-arm threshold
1.12.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/entity/MovingBlockEntity.java`, `addCollisions`, lines 323–344; `net/minecraft/block/PistonHeadBlock.java`, `getArmShape`, lines 76–89.
1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/entity/MovingBlockEntity.java`, `getCollisionShape`, lines 324–351; `net/minecraft/block/PistonHeadBlock.java`, `getArmShape`, lines 71–87.
Difference: The moving piston source-head state sets `SHORT` using `1.0F - progress < 0.25F` in 1.12.2 and `1.0F - progress < 4.0F` in 1.13.2. The ordinary constructor/tick path visits `progress` values 0, 0.5 and 1 in both releases, so the 1.13.2 comparison is true throughout that live path. Raw NBT loading assigns progress without a lower clamp: at -3, the 1.13.2 comparison is false. The predicate must therefore retain its arithmetic rather than be replaced by a constant. The `extending != ...` expression therefore selects a different arm shape through most or all of the transition. In 1.12.2, the collision provider adds the head and arm boxes separately; in 1.13.2, `getCollisionShape` returns the moved state’s voxel shape through `MovingBlock.getCollisionShape` and the world collision query.
Movement meaning: When a moving piston head’s arm intersects a player, its collision geometry can differ during the extension/retraction transition and change the player’s collision-resolved movement. This is a direct player response to a piston, even though piston motion is externally produced.
