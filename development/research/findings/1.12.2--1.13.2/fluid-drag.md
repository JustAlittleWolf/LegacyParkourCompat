Difference: 1.12.2 scans a water material over `getShape().grown(0.0, -0.4F, 0.0).contract(0.001)`. `World.applyLiquidDrag` gathers each touched `LiquidBlock.getFlow` result, and `LiquidBlock.getFlow` returns a normalized vector. If the accumulated vector is nonzero and `hasLiquidCollision()` is true, World normalizes the accumulated sum and adds `0.014 * vector` to velocity. From 1.13, `checkWaterState` passes the contracted full player shape to a fluid-tag scan. The scan accepts fluid cells whose surface reaches the box bottom; when `hasLiquidCollision()` is true, it accumulates each fluid state's normalized flow, scales the current contribution by the running maximum sampled depth when that depth is below `0.4`, averages by the count of qualifying cells, and adds the `0.014`-scaled result. It skips the final normalization for `PlayerEntity`. The 1.13 and 1.13.2 scanner and `FlowingFluid.getFlow` bodies are the same in the inspected sources.

Movement meaning: A nonflying local player with the foot of its default box penetrating one flowing-water cell by `0.2` blocks, and no other water cell in the box, is a concrete witness: both sides detect water and the cell supplies a nonzero normalized flow vector `v`; 1.12.2 adds `0.014v`, while 1.13/1.13.2 adds `0.014(0.2v)`. This is a reachable ordinary water-current response. The 1.12.2 and 1.13.2 cited callers both use `PlayerEntity.hasLiquidCollision() == !abilities.flying`, so the default nonflying player reaches the flow application. The exact introduction boundary is unknown within 1.12.2--1.13; 1.13 is the first inspected release with the new path, and the behavior persists in 1.13.2.
