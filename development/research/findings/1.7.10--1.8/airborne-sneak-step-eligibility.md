# Airborne sneaking no longer blocks a landing step
1.7.10, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 383–494, decays `eyeHeightSneakOffset` by `0.4F` at entry and requires `(var20 || eyeHeightSneakOffset < 0.05F)` in the step gate at line 494. The local input path in `net/minecraft/client/entity/living/player/InputClientPlayerEntity.java`, lines 151–153, sets the offset to `0.2F` while sneaking.
1.8, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `move(double,double,double)`, lines 370–459, has the same positive-step-height, support and horizontal-clipping gates but no sneak-offset predicate.
Difference: On a sneaking movement tick, the old input path leaves `eyeHeightSneakOffset=0.2F` before `Entity.move`; entry decay by `0.4F` leaves approximately `0.080000006F` at the old gate, strictly greater than `0.05F`. For an airborne player `var20` is false, so the old gate rejects stepping. The 1.8 gate attempts the same step whenever the shared support and horizontal-clipping conditions hold. This eligibility change is independent of the separate step-height default and step-candidate solver changes.
Movement meaning: Place a two-layer snow block at `(1,1,0)` on a full floor at Y=0. Start a sneaking local player airborne with feet at Y=1.08 and center `(x,z)=(0.69,0.5)` (width `0.6F`), and request `(dx,dy,dz)=(0.10,-0.10,0)`. Downward motion clips at floor top Y=1, making the old post-clip support predicate true; horizontal motion clips to `dx=0.01` at the snow edge. At the old gate `var20` is false and the decayed offset exceeds `0.05F`, so 1.7.10 keeps the clipped box at bottom Y=1 and center X=0.70. In 1.8 the step gate runs: even with `stepHeight=0.5F` held equal for both comparisons, the player clears the snow top at Y=1.125, moves the full `0.10`, and descends to Y=1.125, ending centered at X=0.79. The actual 1.8 default is also sufficient. This source-derived witness isolates eligibility from the height change; it is not a runtime trajectory.
