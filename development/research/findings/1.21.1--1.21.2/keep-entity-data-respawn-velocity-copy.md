# Keep-entity-data respawn copies local-player velocity
1.21.1, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.21.1/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, lines 1076-1151; `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `resetPos`, lines 625-637; `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundRespawnPacket.java`, `KEEP_ENTITY_DATA` and `shouldKeep`, lines 13-14, 34-35. For the ordinary End Portal route, the server sends `dataToKeep=3` during the Overworld-to-End player transfer (`net/minecraft/world/level/block/EndPortalBlock.java`, `getPortalDestination`, lines 65-99; `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `changeDimension`, lines 847-888).
1.21.2, Mojmap: `decompiled_minecraft/1.21.2/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleRespawn`, lines 1132-1202; `net/minecraft/network/protocol/game/ClientboundRespawnPacket.java`, `KEEP_ENTITY_DATA` and `shouldKeep`, lines 13-14, 34-35. The corresponding End-bound transfer sends the same relevant `dataToKeep` byte and targets the same dimension (`net/minecraft/world/level/block/EndPortalBlock.java`, `getPortalDestination`, lines 69-99; `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `teleport`, lines 1029-1079).
Difference: with `dataToKeep=3`, `shouldKeep((byte)2)` is true in both versions. The 1.21.1 handler replaces LocalPlayer, copies entity data but not delta movement, then calls `resetPos`, which sets delta to `Vec3.ZERO`. The 1.21.2 handler copies the old LocalPlayer's delta movement under that same keep-entity-data condition; it calls `resetPos` only in the false branch. This compares the same raw keep byte and equivalent End destination input, not the entire version-specific respawn packet. The later position correction separately preserves the difference: the old server path emits no relative arguments and the client zeros delta, while the new End Portal transition sends a zero relative-delta operand that is added to the copied velocity. Those later flags are server-produced and witness persistence, not the local cause.
Movement meaning: on a successful ordinary Overworld-to-End End Portal transfer with nonzero local-player velocity, the replacement LocalPlayer has zero delta in 1.21.1 and retains the previous delta in 1.21.2 immediately after respawn and its position correction. This is the client `KEEP_ENTITY_DATA` replacement-player movement-state operation; the End Portal path is the concrete reachable example.
