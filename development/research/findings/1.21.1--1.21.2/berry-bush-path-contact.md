# Sweet berry bush path contact assigns the next-move slowdown
1.21.1, Mojmap: net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java, move calls tryCheckInsideBlocks at 705; checkInsideBlocks reads getBoundingBox and iterates only that final AABB at 1001-1020. net/minecraft/world/level/block/SweetBerryBushBlock.java, entityInside and makeStuckInBlock at 80-89; net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java, makeStuckInBlock suppresses the multiplier while flying at 1589-1593.
1.21.2, Mojmap: net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java, movementThisTick recording at 1035; applyEffectsFromBlocks and segment traversal at 736-751, 1038-1061. net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java, travel then applyEffectsFromBlocks at 2771-2775. net/minecraft/world/level/block/state/BlockBehaviour.java, default getEntityInsideCollisionShape returns Shapes.block() at 353-354. net/minecraft/world/level/block/SweetBerryBushBlock.java, entityInside and makeStuckInBlock at 79-92; net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java, flying suppresses makeStuckInBlock at 1582-1586.
Difference: In 1.21.1 the player invokes entityInside for blocks in its resulting bounding box after movement. In 1.21.2 the local living player records move segments; after travel, the block-effect pass traverses blocks along those segments and invokes entityInside for the default full-block inside shape. In both versions the berry handler assigns the same (0.8F, 0.75, 0.8) multiplier to living entities other than foxes and bees without an AGE gate; AGE gates only the excluded server damage branch. Player.makeStuckInBlock skips the assignment while flying. Otherwise makeStuckInBlock stores the multiplier, which Entity.move multiplies into the next requested movement before clearing it (1.21.1 Entity.java:611-614; 1.21.2 Entity.java:633-637 and 2525-2528). A non-flying player segment can leave a bush and finish with no player-box overlap: 1.21.1 makes no new assignment, while 1.21.2 visits the traversed bush and assigns one for the following move. The adjacent stable source bodies pin this movement-trigger change to 1.21.2.
Movement meaning: A non-flying player’s first move after fully exiting a sweet berry bush can still receive one extra 0.8 horizontal and 0.75 vertical movement multiplication in profiles emulating 1.21.2 or later.
