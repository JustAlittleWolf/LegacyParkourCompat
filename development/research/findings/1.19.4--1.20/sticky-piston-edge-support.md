# Sticky piston carries edge-supported entities
1.19.4, Mojmap: `1.19.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/level/block/piston/PistonMovingBlockEntity.java`, `moveStuckEntities` and `matchesStickyCritera`, lines 186–208.
1.20, Mojmap: `1.20/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/level/block/piston/PistonMovingBlockEntity.java`, same methods, lines 186–205; the same method bodies persist in 1.20.1, 1.20.2, 1.20.3 and 1.20.4.
Difference: the sticky-honey branch now accepts an on-ground, normally pushable entity when `isSupportedBy(movingBlockPos)` is true, even if its center X/Z falls outside the translated moving-block AABB. The scan AABB's upper Y bound also changes from `1.5000000999999998` to `1.5000010000000001`. Accepted entities are passed to `moveEntityByPiston` with the existing horizontal movement delta. `Entity.isSupportedBy` checks `mainSupportingBlockPos`; `setOnGroundWithKnownMovement` updates that support position through `checkSupportingBlock`.
Movement meaning: while a horizontal honey block is moving, a player whose bounding-box edge is supported by it can be carried even when the player's center lies just outside its footprint. The old center-coordinate gates excluded that player. The 1.20 support-position selector is a dependency of the new branch.
