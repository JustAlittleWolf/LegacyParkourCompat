# Mayfly double-press flight toggle is gated while riding a non-jumpable vehicle

1.21.10, Mojmap: `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `rideTick` 2234–2240.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tick` 2542–2583 and 2542–2654.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` 751–770, `jumpableVehicle` 522–523, `tick` 195–220, `isControlledCamera` 664–666; `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `aiStep` 455–458; `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getControlledVehicle` 3446–3448.
1.21.11, Mojmap: `1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` 792–811, `jumpableVehicle` 563–564; `1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `aiStep` 452–458; `1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getControlledVehicle` 3462–3464.

Difference: With `mayfly` enabled, outside the always-flying spectator branch, not swimming, and after a fresh jump press initialized `jumpTriggerTime` to 7, a later fresh press while the timer remains positive toggles `Abilities.flying` in 1.21.10 even when the player is a passenger. The post-branch `super.aiStep()` decrements the positive timer in both versions; a rejected second press does not reset it, and the normal per-tick decrement continues. In 1.21.11, the toggle proceeds only if `getVehicle() == null || jumpableVehicle() != null`. `jumpableVehicle()` returns the controlled vehicle only when it implements `PlayerRideableJumping` and `canJump()` is true. The new predicate is an additive gate; the timer and remaining toggle operations are otherwise unchanged in the paired body. The adjacent spectator/always-flying condition is equivalent in both releases: `MultiPlayerGameMode.isAlwaysFlying()` and `isSpectator()` both reduce to `localPlayerMode == GameType.SPECTATOR` (1.21.10 lines 512–514; 1.21.11 lines 520–522), while the automatic flight path and post-super ground reset are unchanged.

A boat witnesses the rejected vehicle case: `AbstractBoat` extends `VehicleEntity` and is not a `PlayerRideableJumping` implementation; its interaction mounts passengers and its controlling-passenger method identifies the passenger (1.21.10 `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/vehicle/AbstractBoat.java` 50, 696–703, 755–756; 1.21.11 `1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/vehicle/boat/AbstractBoat.java` 52, 694–701, 752–753). On foot, `getVehicle() == null` keeps the toggle eligible. A controlled saddled horse is a positive mounted control: `AbstractHorse` implements `PlayerRideableJumping`, its player-mount path is reachable, `canJump()` returns `isSaddled()`, and its controlling-passenger predicate requires a saddled horse with a player passenger (1.21.10 `1.21.10/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/animal/horse/AbstractHorse.java` 78, 492–499, 641–665, 885–887, 956–957; 1.21.11 `1.21.11/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/animal/equine/AbstractHorse.java` 79, 489–496, 637–662, 881–883, 950–951).

Movement meaning: The passenger path is reachable: `ClientLevel.tickEntities` filters out passenger entities, then its `tickPassenger` path calls `rideTick()` for Player entities. `LocalPlayer.rideTick()` delegates through `LivingEntity.rideTick()` to `Entity.rideTick()`, which invokes virtual `tick()`. The local player tick calls the superclass tick after its loaded gate; `LivingEntity.tick()` reaches `aiStep()`. The new guard therefore is not dead code on the passenger path. For a mayfly player riding a boat or another non-jumpable vehicle, a fresh jump press during the double-press window no longer toggles flight; on-foot players and players controlling a jumpable saddled horse remain eligible. This finding does not emulate the vehicle's motion or input handling. `Abilities.flying` is the state written by the toggle and sent through the existing `onUpdateAbilities()` path. The same `aiStep` later adds vertical player delta from jump/shift input when flying and the local player is the controlled camera. The state can remain set across a later dismount until another existing condition changes it, so a dismount before grounding can expose different player-side flight input behavior. Source proves that the 1.21.10/1.21.11 guards produce different local `Abilities.flying` values for the stated precondition and that this value gates the local camera-controlled vertical velocity write. The report predicts that a later airborne dismount can therefore enter different player-side flight-input behavior.
