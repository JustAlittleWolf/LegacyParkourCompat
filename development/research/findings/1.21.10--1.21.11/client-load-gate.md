# Client load gate
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `tick()`, lines 195–220; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, load state and timeout methods, lines 1875–1889; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, level-ready notification, lines 2639–2651.
1.21.11, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `tick()`, lines 211–235; `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, load state and level-ready notification, lines 420, 2654–2666, 2740–2746.
Difference: 1.21.10 decrements `Player.clientLoadedTimeoutTimer` on every local player tick and runs movement once `Player.hasClientLoaded()` is true (`clientLoaded || timeout <= 0`). 1.21.11 removes that player-side timeout gate from `LocalPlayer.tick()` and checks `connection.hasClientLoaded()`, a packet-listener boolean set after `levelLoadTracker.isLevelReady()`; its getter has no timeout fallback. The server listener retains a separate load timeout.
Movement meaning: local `LivingEntity`/movement simulation and movement/input packet updates begin after level readiness in 1.21.11. In 1.21.10 they can begin after 60 local ticks even if the readiness notification has not arrived. This is limited to client load/respawn readiness and its dependency is the client packet listener’s level-load status; it does not change movement math in an already-ready level.
