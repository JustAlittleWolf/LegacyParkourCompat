# Fall-flying gravity with NoGravity

1.19.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel(Vec3)` lines 1983–2058 and `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(double, boolean, Vec3)` lines 2165–2193.
1.20.6, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `getDefaultGravity()` lines 2100–2102, `travel(Vec3)` lines 2104–2178 and `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(double, boolean, Vec3)` lines 2282–2294; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getGravity()` lines 1105–1106.
Difference: In the locally controlled travel branch, 1.19.4 captures `0.08`, replacing it with `0.01` when vertical velocity is non-positive and Slow Falling is active. Its fall-flying branch always adds the captured value multiplied by `(-1.0 + cosSquared * 0.75)`, with no `isNoGravity()` gate. In 1.20.6, travel captures `getGravity()`; `LivingEntity.getDefaultGravity()` reads `Attributes.GRAVITY`, while `Entity.getGravity()` returns `0.0` when `isNoGravity()` is true. The same Slow Falling gate applies `Math.min(value, 0.01)`, leaving that value at zero under NoGravity. The water helper is not evidence of a fluid NoGravity gravity change: 1.19.4 returns the input movement unchanged when `isNoGravity()` is true, while 1.20.6 returns it unchanged when the passed gravity is zero. Lava also has a separate gate: 1.19.4 applies its gravity term only when `!isNoGravity()`, and 1.20.6 only when the captured gravity is nonzero.
Movement meaning: For a locally controlled fall-flying entity with NoGravity, 1.19.4 still applies its captured `0.08` value (or `0.01` under the downward Slow Falling gate) to the fall-flying vertical-velocity update; 1.20.6 applies zero. This finding concerns that fall-flying response.
