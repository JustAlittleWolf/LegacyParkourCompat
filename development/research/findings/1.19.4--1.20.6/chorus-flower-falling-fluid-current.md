# Chorus flower falling-fluid current
1.19.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/ChorusFlowerBlock.java` has no support-shape override; inherited `BlockBehaviour.getBlockSupportShape`, lines 250–252, returns the full-cube collision shape. `FlowingFluid.getFlow`, lines 87–96, and `isSolidFace`, lines 104–114, consume horizontal face sturdiness for falling fluid.
1.20.6, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/ChorusFlowerBlock.java`, `BLOCK_SUPPORT_SHAPE`, line 31, and `getBlockSupportShape`, lines 58–60, return box(1,0,1,15,15,15). `FlowingFluid.getFlow`, lines 88–97, and `isSolidFace`, lines 105–115, retain the face-sturdy current branch. `BlockBehaviour.BlockStateBase.isFaceSturdy` uses SupportType.FULL, lines 836–841.
Difference: Chorus flowers keep their full-cube player collision shape, but the newer support shape no longer fills any horizontal block face. A neighboring chorus flower therefore passes the older horizontal FULL-face sturdy check and fails the newer check.
Movement meaning: Falling water beside a chorus flower, with no other face-sturdy neighbor at the checked positions, takes the older normalize-and-add(0,-6,0) current branch but skips it in the newer source. Entity.updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing reads this current and adds it to player velocity, so this support-shape change is a direct fluid movement response despite unchanged collision geometry and vanilla block states. Source comparison of verified intermediate releases finds no override in 1.20.1 and the override in 1.20.2; the boundary is 1.20.2.
