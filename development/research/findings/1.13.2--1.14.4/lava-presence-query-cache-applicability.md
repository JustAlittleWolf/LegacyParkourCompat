Difference: the inspected older projections (1.8.9, 1.9.0, 1.9.4, 1.10.2, 1.11.2, 1.12.2 and 1.13.2) compute `isInLava` from `World.containsMaterial` on every call. Their query shape is contracted by `(0.1, 0.4, 0.1)`; in 1.13.2 `Box.contract` delegates to the equivalent negative `grown` operation. The 1.14.0–1.14.4 sources instead return a stored boolean. Each `Entity.move` clears it just before the entity block-collision pass; the pass visits block positions from the entity box inset by `0.001` at each minimum and maximum, and a liquid-block callback sets the flag only for lava. This is the ordinary entity-box collision pass, with its `0.001` inset, rather than the older `0.1/0.4/0.1` contracted query.
Movement meaning: in the locally controlled or movement-authoritative `moveRelative` branch, `LivingEntity` uses `isInLava` to choose its lava versus land/fall-flying movement path, except that player flight bypasses the lava branch. The `jumping` gate in `LivingEntity.mobTick` also selects lava jump handling. `PlayerEntity.mobTick` delegates to `LivingEntity`, and its non-flight `moveRelative` path delegates there; `ClientPlayerEntity` extends `PlayerEntity`. A non-flying client player can therefore receive different movement handling when lava intersects the `0.001`-inset entity box but not the older contracted query box; cached presence can also persist after later position or shape writes until another block-collision pass.
