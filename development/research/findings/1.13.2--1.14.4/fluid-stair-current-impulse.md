1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow` and `isSolidFace`, lines 54–99 and 105–123, reject `StairsBlock` before testing its face shape. `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `m_69693160`, lines 2509–2571, accumulates tagged-fluid flow and adds a `0.014` impulse only when the accumulated vector is nonzero.

1.14.4, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow` and `isSolidFace`, lines 54–100 and 106–115, use the collision-face sturdy result for horizontal neighbors; the matching south-facing straight bottom stone-brick stair fills its SOUTH face and is absent from the generated vanilla `LEAVES` recipe (`net/minecraft/data/tag/BlockTagsProvider.java:299–300`). The vanilla WATER recipe includes flowing water (`net/minecraft/data/tag/FluidTagsProvider.java:17–20`). `net/minecraft/entity/Entity.java`, `m_69693160`, lines 2563–2622, accumulates the resulting flow and adds the same `0.014` impulse to player velocity.

Difference: with the same flowing-water state `LEVEL=8, FALLING=true` at `P=(0,0,0)` and `minecraft:stone_brick_stairs[facing=south,half=bottom,shape=straight,waterlogged=false]` at `P+SOUTH`, old `getFlow` returns `(0,0,0)` while new returns `(0,-1,0)`. The other horizontal neighbors are air with empty fluid and have no other collision-face-solid cells along this falling-fluid branch; the stair's fluid is empty. For the local-player body-fluid query at `(0.5,0.48,0.5)` with standing dimensions `0.6 × 1.8`, liquid collision enabled, and an area-loaded client query, the contracted box contains exactly one qualifying tagged-water cell at `P`; the remaining scanned cells are air/empty fluid. This contributor has immersion depth approximately `0.4079`, so it is not depth-scaled. Old writes no impulse and new adds `(0,-0.014,0)` to the player's velocity. `LivingEntity.moveRelative` consumes the velocity in the water branch.

Movement meaning: the direct player velocity write differs for this same-state query. At both endpoints, the default vanilla `FluidTags.WATER` recipe includes flowing water; at 1.13.2 and 1.14.4, the default vanilla `BlockTags.LEAVES` recipe contains the six leaf blocks and omits stairs. The block/fluid snapshot and player position are paired assumptions; this does not prove the state snapshot's producer history, a subsequent position trajectory, or a subsequent runtime trajectory. Custom/runtime tag replacement is outside the default-vanilla claim.

Boundary: exact 1.14 introduces the face-based form and 1.14.1–1.14.4 retain it. ## Additional conditional witness: glass face changes falling-water current

This supplements the existing stair-face witness for the same `FlowingFluid.getFlow` / `isSolidFace` operation. It adds an alternate material and current-vector precondition; it is not a second independent operation.

1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, `getFlow` and `isSolidFace`, lines 54–97 and 105–123. For falling fluid, `getFlow` tests each horizontal neighbor and its upper cell, and applies `normalize().add(0,-6,0)` on the first solid face. `isSolidFace` rejects `Block.isExceptionForAttachment` before `FaceShape.SOLID`; glass is a placement exception through `Block.java:241–257,248`.

1.14.4, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/fluid/FlowingFluid.java`, lines 54–98 and 106–115, uses `BlockState.m_87223014` for the same horizontal-face gate. That method uses cached values or `Block.isFaceSolid` (`BlockState.java:326–327,390–416`); `Block.isFaceSolid` tests a full collision face and excludes the leaves tag (`Block.java:402–408`). Vanilla glass has a full collision face from the base block shape/collision methods (old `Block.java:388–395`; new `:371–378`) and is not leaves.

For the conditional current witness, set P=`(0,5,0)` to flowing water `LEVEL=8,FALLING=true`; set W=`(-1,5,0)` to non-falling flowing water level 7; place glass at `(0,5,-1)` and leave all other horizontal neighbor faces and their upper cells empty. A same-family fluid above P makes `getSpreadFluid` compute `asFlowing(8,true)` (old `FlowingFluid.java:163–196`; new `:156–189`). The level-7 value at W is computed when a horizontal level-8 neighbor and water level drop 1 are supplied (`WaterFluid.java:99–100` / `:103–105`), but this does not prove a completed tick places W: side spreading is gated by `FlowingFluid.spread` and `spreadToSides` (old `FlowingFluid.java:125–159`; new `:118–153`). Treat W's placed level-7 state as stipulated, along with the glass and surrounding block layout. No full preceding fluid-tick history is claimed.

At P, the raw level gradient toward W is `1/9`, so both `getFlow` methods begin from `(-1/9,0,0)`. Old rejects the north glass face and normalizes to `(-1,0,0)`; new sees a full north collision face, applies the falling-fluid `-6` Y term, and normalizes `(-1,-6,0)` to approximately `(-1/√37,-6/√37,0)`.

Stipulate a non-flying, non-riding local player at horizontal center `(x=0.5,z=0.5)`, feet `y=4.2`, with standing dimensions `0.6F × 1.8F` and zero velocity. The position is a static initial condition, not a proved prior trajectory. The contracted contact query scans only x/z column 0 and y rows 4 and 5; the source above P at y=6 and W at x=-1 are outside that scan. P is the sole qualifying water cell. Its immersion depth is greater than `0.4` under both the old raw height `8/9` and new context height `1.0`, so the scan does not depth-scale the flow. Player `hasLiquidCollision()` is true when not flying (`PlayerEntity.java:1826–1828` / `:1769–1771`).

The body-fluid contact scan adds the single-player flow vector times `0.014` to velocity (old `Entity.java:2554–2567`; new `:2608–2618`): old adds `(-0.014,0,0)`; new adds approximately `(-0.014/√37,-0.084/√37,0)`. `Entity.baseTick` refreshes water state before `LivingEntity.tick` calls `mobTick` and its movement consumer (old `Entity.java:394–415`, `LivingEntity.java:1689–1691,1752,1920–1926`; new `Entity.java:357–378`, `LivingEntity.java:1979–1981,2046,2221–2227`). This closes a conditional player velocity-state difference, not the subsequent water-travel position delta.
