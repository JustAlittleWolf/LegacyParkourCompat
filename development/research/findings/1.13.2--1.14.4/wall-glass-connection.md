# Wall collision arm connects to glass
1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/WallBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo`, `isExceptionForConnection`, placement/update predicates and collision-shape lookup, lines 37–63, 75–110; `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, `isExceptionForAttachment`, lines 249–258; `net/minecraft/block/PaneBlock.java`, collision-shape construction and lookup, lines 38–84.
1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/WallBlock.java`, `connectsTo`, placement/update predicates and collision-shape lookup, lines 45–116; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Block.java`, `isExceptionForConnection` and `isFaceSturdy`, lines 259–267, 403–409; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/CrossCollisionBlock.java`, shape construction and collision lookup, lines 35–96.
Difference: With glass directly east of a wall, 1.13.2 rejects the connection through `Block.isExceptionForAttachment`, despite the glass face being solid. In 1.14.4 the glass face is sturdy and the narrower connection-exception list omits glass, so the east connection becomes true. When the wall's post is enabled, the shared collision-shape builder adds an east arm from local X 5/16 to 1, Z 5/16 to 11/16, and Y 0 to 24/16; the old shape retains only its post.
Movement meaning: Put the wall at (0,0,0) and glass at (1,0,0), with the player standing on top of the glass at Y=1, centered at X=1.31 and Z=0.5. The 0.6-wide player box starts clear of the wall-cell boundary (minX≈1.01; source stores width as `0.6F` and halves it when constructing the box: 1.13.2 `Entity.java:190–191,309–315`; 1.14.4 `EntityType.java:340–342`, `Entity.java:310–316`). A 0.1 move west overlaps the new arm and is clipped after about 0.01 in 1.14.4; the old post remains clear and permits the full move in 1.13.2. The glass top supports the player while its collision volume only touches the box vertically.
