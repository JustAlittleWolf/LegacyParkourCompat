# Inner-stair wall-face selection is unchanged in the checked fixture

**Resolved source coverage.** The earlier side-swap claim and its acceptance are superseded. For an ordinary dry cobblestone wall beside a sole naturally reachable dry Oak inner stair, the wall selects the same horizontal arm in exact 1.13, 1.13.1, 1.13.2, 1.14, 1.14.4 and 26.2. Both stair halves have the same horizontal result. The wall's other horizontal neighbors and the cell above are ordinary air in this fixture. There is no implementation task for this clause.

The exact 1.13.2 Ornithe Feather `StairsBlock.getFaceShape` (lines 108–129) compares the stair facing `direction` with `face` and with a rotation of the **queried face**. INNER_LEFT returns SOLID if `direction == face || direction == face.clockwiseY()`; INNER_RIGHT uses `face.counterClockwiseY()`. Inverting those equalities with the direction switches (`Direction.java:126–139,174–187`) gives `{F, CCW(F)}` for INNER_LEFT and `{F, CW(F)}` for INNER_RIGHT. Both stair and wall files are byte-identical across the three exact 1.13 releases.

The wall asks the neighbor's face opposite the wall-to-stair offset (`WallBlock.java:75–84,105–116`). A north-facing stair's west face is solid for INNER_LEFT only. Its east face is solid for INNER_RIGHT only. The wall west of the stair queries WEST and connects to INNER_LEFT. The wall east of the stair queries EAST and connects to INNER_RIGHT. Both are direct occupied-side checks with Minecraft's +X east and +Z south axes.

The exact 1.14 and 1.14.4 Feather wall uses a full collision-face test. Their stair files are byte-identical. `StairsBlock.java:37–69,98–105` defines the NW/NE/SW/SE quadrant bits and maps by shape ordinal and facing ID. For NORTH, inner-left mask 7 fills north and west; inner-right mask 11 fills north and east. The SOUTH/WEST/NORTH/EAST index order gives the same rotated face rule for all facings. The upper/lower shapes mirror Y and preserve those horizontal faces. The provenance gap for intermediate 1.14.1–1.14.3 remains outside this conclusion.

Exact 26.2 unobfuscated `StairBlock.java:37–45,74–88` builds a north/east full-face base inner shape, maps INNER_RIGHT directly to facing and INNER_LEFT to counterclockwise facing. `Shapes.rotateHorizontal` supplies the four rotations. `WallBlock.java:105–143,176–226` checks the opposite neighbor side and chooses a LOW arm if the side connects with air above. The centered post stays raised for zero or one arm. In the bounded fixture, the 1.13.2 and 26.2 occupied wall geometry also agrees: in 1/16 units, the post is X/Z [4,12] and Y [0,24]; the north arm is X [5,11], Z [0,11], Y [0,24], rotated for other sides. This does not address other neighbors, contextual shape or stored-state mutations, or player trajectory parity.
