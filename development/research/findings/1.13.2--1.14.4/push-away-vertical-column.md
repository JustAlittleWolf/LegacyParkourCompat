# Local-player push-away vertical-column probe

Minecraft 1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `pushAwayFrom` and `wouldCollideAt` (`:401–455`); `entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java`, `doesNotSuffocate` and `shouldSuffocate` (`:1479–1485`).

Minecraft 1.14.4, Ornithe Feather: `client/entity/living/player/LocalClientPlayerEntity.java`, `pushAwayFrom` and `wouldCollideAt` (`:389–447`); `entity/living/player/PlayerEntity.java`, `shouldSuffocate` (`:1420–1422`).

Difference: the checked 1.13, 1.13.1, and 1.13.2 `pushAwayFrom`, `wouldCollideAt`, and player suffocation-predicate bodies are identical: the sampled block position at `floor(sampleY)` is checked, with the non-swimming predicate also checking the block above. 1.14.4 instead scans the sampled X/Z column over `floor(box.minY) <= y < ceil(box.maxY)`. The exact stable 1.14 release and 1.14.1–1.14.3 already use this vertical scan, through `ClientPlayerEntity.pushOutOfBlocks` → `cannotFitAt`; their `doesNotSuffocate` uses `BlockState.canSuffocate`, whereas 1.14.4 uses `isViewBlocking` through `shouldSuffocate`.

Separately, for non-swimming `wouldCollideAt`, the tested predicate changes from `Block.isSolid` in 1.13.2, which excludes signal sources, to `BlockState.isViewBlocking` in 1.14.0 and 1.14.4, which checks movement-blocking material and full collision shape without that signal-source exclusion. The equivalent check is present in each inspected 1.14.1–1.14.3 Yarn release as `doesNotSuffocate` / `Block.canSuffocate`. In 1.13.2, the references are `LocalClientPlayerEntity.wouldCollideAt` (`:401–455`), `PlayerEntity.shouldSuffocate` / `doesNotSuffocate` (`:1479–1485`), and `Block.isSolid` (`:264–267`). In 1.14.0 Feather Gen2 `1.14+build.2`, they are `LocalClientPlayerEntity.wouldCollideAt` (`:435–447`), `PlayerEntity.shouldSuffocate` (`:1402–1403`), and `Block.isViewBlocking` (`:264–265`). In 1.14.1 Yarn `1.14.1+build.10`, 1.14.2 Yarn `1.14.2+build.7`, and 1.14.3 Yarn `1.14.3+build.13`, `ClientPlayerEntity.cannotFitAt` / `doesNotSuffocate` and `Block.canSuffocate` are at `ClientPlayerEntity.java:421–433`, 1.14.1 `PlayerEntity.java:1401–1403` / `Block.java:270–271`, 1.14.2 `PlayerEntity.java:1401–1403` / `Block.java:269–270`, and 1.14.3 `PlayerEntity.java:1403–1405` / `Block.java:268–270`. In 1.14.4 Feather Gen2 `1.14.4+build.2`, they are `LocalClientPlayerEntity.wouldCollideAt` (`:435–447`), `PlayerEntity.shouldSuffocate` (`:1420–1422`), and `Block.isViewBlocking` (`:274–276`). For the registered redstone block, the old predicate answer is false because it is a signal source; the new predicate answer is true for its movement-blocking material and full collision shape. The changed answer is already present in 1.14.0 and each inspected release through 1.14.4.

Movement meaning: with a non-swimming local player at box `[0.16,0.76] × [0.8,2.6] × [0.2,0.8]`, center `(0.46,0.5)`, velocity `(0.25,0.3,0.4)`, `noClip=false`, stone at `(0,0,0)`, and air in the horizontal neighbor columns at Y=0,1,2, all four old-version samples floor to `(0,1,0)` and miss the lower stone, preserving velocity. The new scan sees that stone and the ordered four corner responses finish at `(-0.1,0.3,0.1)`. The witness is a conditional raw overlap input, not an asserted collision-free trajectory. The new probe is present in stable release 1.14; the cached exact release catalog lists no stable release between 1.13.2 and 1.14 (snapshots are out of scope), so 1.14 is the first stable release with the probe in this interval.

**Additional redstone predicate witness.** Use the same raw local-player snapshot in both versions: `noClip=false`, `isSwimming=false`, center `(x,z)=(0.5,0.5)`, width `0.6F`, vertical box `[0.25,2.05]`, and velocity `(0,0,0)`. Put the common registered `REDSTONE_BLOCK` at `(0,0,0)` and air in all cardinal neighbor columns and other sampled cells for Y=0,1,2. Every old four-corner sample addresses the redstone block at Y=`floor(0.75)=0`; the old predicate skips the response because redstone is not solid under `Block.isSolid`. The newer vertical scan includes row 0 and its predicate sees the redstone block as view-blocking. West, west, east, east are the ordered direction checks, and their component writes leave velocity `(0.1,0,0)`; the old result remains `(0,0,0)`. Since this redstone is in a row both algorithms inspect and other rows are empty, this case witnesses the predicate difference. It is a conditional direct local-player response on a raw snapshot; it does not establish collision-free placement, state history, trajectory reachability, full-pair coverage, or runtime parity.
