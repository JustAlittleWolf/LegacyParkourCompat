# Fence collision arm connects to glass
1.13.2, Ornithe Feather: `net/minecraft/block/FenceBlock.java`, `shouldConnectTo` and placement/update predicates, lines 46–54, 84–107; `net/minecraft/block/Block.java`, `isExceptionForAttachment`, lines 249–258; `net/minecraft/block/PaneBlock.java`, collision-shape construction and lookup, lines 38–84.
1.14.4, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/level/block/FenceBlock.java`, `connectsTo` and placement/update predicates, lines 43–96; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/Block.java`, `isExceptionForConnection` and `isFaceSturdy`, lines 259–267, 403–409; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/CrossCollisionBlock.java`, shape construction and collision lookup, lines 35–96.
Difference: With glass directly east of a fence, 1.13.2 rejects the connection because glass is an attachment exception, even though its face shape is solid. In 1.14.4 the glass face is sturdy and glass is absent from the connection-exception list, so the east connection becomes true. The shared collision-shape builder then adds the east rail from local X 6/16 to 1, Z 6/16 to 10/16, and Y 0 to 24/16; the old shape retains only the central post.
Movement meaning: Put the fence at (0,0,0) and glass at (1,0,0), with the player standing on top of the glass at Y=1, centered at X=1.31 and Z=0.5. The 0.6-wide player box starts clear of the fence-cell boundary (minX≈1.01; source stores width as `0.6F` and halves it when constructing the box: 1.13.2 `Entity.java:190–191,309–315`; 1.14.4 `EntityType.java:340–342`, `Entity.java:310–316`). A 0.1 move west overlaps the new rail and is clipped after about 0.01 in 1.14.4; the old central post remains clear and permits the full move in 1.13.2. The glass top supports the player while its collision volume only touches the box vertically.
