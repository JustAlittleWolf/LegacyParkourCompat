Difference: In the non-flying water branch, 1.13.2 moves the entity, applies vertical drag `0.8F`, then checks the resulting `velocityY <= 0.0` for the Slow Falling terminal-descent rule. In 1.14 and 1.14.4, `moveRelative` captures `bl = entryVelocityY <= 0.0` before the water branch; after movement and drag, that captured value gates the same threshold test. For a locally controlled player (the local-player override returns `true` in both versions), take a non-sprinting, non-sneaking, non-swimming, non-riding, non-flying, non-fall-flying state with gravity enabled (`isNoGravity()` false) with Slow Falling and water contact. Start at `velocityY = -0.001`; collide vertically with slime during `move`, and have no horizontal collision. In 1.14, a vertical displacement invokes the collided block's `beforeCollision`; SlimeBlock reverses a descending entity's vertical velocity unless it is sneaking, changing `-0.001` to `+0.001`. Water drag then yields `+0.0008`. In 1.13.2 the post-drag terminal predicate is false, so subtracting `d / 16.0 = 0.000625` leaves `+0.000175`. In 1.14 and 1.14.4 the entry-time `bl` is true, and the captured-predicate threshold branch sets `velocityY` to `-0.003`. Requiring no horizontal collision excludes the later horizontal-collision response that can replace vertical velocity with `0.3F`.

Movement meaning: In this reachable local-player case, slime contact during Slow Falling water descent leaves a small upward velocity in 1.13.2, while the 1.14 behavior immediately changes it to downward velocity. The exact 1.14 source already has the captured-entry predicate and water operation order; these are present in the supplied 1.14.4 representative as well. Applicability is evidenced only for the supplied exact representatives 1.13.2, 1.14, and 1.14.4; other profile aliases and mapping snapshots are not established by this finding.
