Difference: 1.13.2 returns a full-footprint collision box from local Y 0 to 9/16 for every bed state. 1.14.4 returns a full-footprint mattress from Y 3/16 to 9/16 plus two 3/16-high corner legs, selected by facing and bed part. The ordinary collision path uses these state shapes in both versions.
Movement meaning: With a stored bed at (0,0,0), a full-cube floor block at `BlockPos(0,-3,0)` (top surface Y=-2), and open space between them, a grounded, non-sprinting player centered at X=Z=0.5 with zero horizontal velocity has a 0.6×1.8 AABB spanning X/Z 0.2–0.8 and Y -2 to -0.2. Its footprint avoids every corner leg. A normal 0.42 vertical jump clips at +0.2 in 1.13.2 when the player reaches the bed underside, but at +0.3875 in 1.14.4 when the player reaches the mattress. There is no horizontal movement to trigger the step-up branch in either version. This conditional witness assumes the map contains the floating bed state; it does not require a particular placement or world-generation path.
