Difference: In 1.13.2, ordinary dry travel moves first, then checks the player’s current `(x, 0, z)`. On the logical client, gravity is applied only when that position’s chunk is loaded and its `WorldChunk.isLoaded()` is true. `EmptyChunk` does not override `isLoaded`; it inherits `WorldChunk.loaded`, initially false. For a missing chunk, the branch sets vertical velocity to `-0.1` above Y 0 or `0` otherwise, then multiplies it by `0.98F`. In 1.14.4, `travel` captures the friction/support `BlockPos` from the player’s pre-move position. After moving, the client missing-chunk branch checks that saved position. `MultiPlayerLevel.hasChunk` always returns true, so the normal branch subtracts `d` (`0.08`, or `0.01` for downward Slow Falling) when `isNoGravity()` is false; the result is then multiplied by `0.98F`. Both versions process Levitation before the chunk/gravity gate, and Levitation clears fall distance. The 1.13.2 absent-chunk fallback also overrides vertical velocity even when no-gravity is set; in 1.14.4 the always-true client chunk predicate reaches the no-gravity condition normally.

Movement meaning: On a clear, dry, unobstructed path crossing from loaded chunk x=0 into absent chunk x=1, move from x=15.9 to x=16.1 during a tick with zero initial vertical velocity and no Levitation or Slow Falling. In 1.13.2 the post-move check sees the absent chunk and produces approximately `-0.098` vertical velocity. In 1.14.4 the saved pre-move position is in the loaded chunk; the client predicate is true, so ordinary gravity produces approximately `-0.0784`. The chunk-crossing tick therefore differs in player vertical motion.
