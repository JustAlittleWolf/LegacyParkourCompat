**Boundary verdict:** The earliest checked stable release where this actual player collision response occurs is `1.14`. Exact `1.13.2` is the last stable release in the preceding line checked and still excludes the overlapping block; exact `1.14` has the direct block collision scan and clips to approximately `0.0375`.
