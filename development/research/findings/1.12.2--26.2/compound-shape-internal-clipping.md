## Conditional player-sized witness: brewing-stand internal plane

This source-level witness uses the default standing player dimensions `.6F × 1.8F`; it is conditional on an already embedded/teleported player state and is not claimed as a natural trajectory. Place a brewing stand at block position `(0,0,0)`, put the player at `(x=.1,y=0,z=.05)`, set `onGround=false`, and request positive X movement `+.2` with Y/Z movement zero (`noClip=false`). Source constructs the AABB from `f=.6F/2.0F` as `[.1-f,0,.05-f]..[.1+f,1.8F,.05+f]` (nominal decimal shorthand `[-.2,0,-.25]..[.4,1.8,.35]`; exact float-derived edges are approximately `maxX=.40000001192`, `maxZ=.35000001192`). Old `PlayerEntity.setSize(0.6F,1.8F)` is at `PlayerEntity.java:399` and the centered AABB construction is `Entity.java:299-305`; newer `Avatar.STANDING_DIMENSIONS = EntityDimensions.scalable(0.6F,1.8F)` is at `Avatar.java:21,25` and `EntityDimensions.makeBoundingBox` centers `width/2.0F` at lines 15-24. Both player versions use float dimensions, so the exact-formula witness has the same width and height in each.

The old 1.12.2 provider emits the post `[.4375,0,.4375]..[.5625,.875,.5625]` and plate `[0,0,0]..[1,.125,1]` independently (`net/minecraft/block/BrewingStandBlock.java:36-37,70-78`). The swept query for this motion has `zMax≈.35000001192 < .4375`, so strict intersection excludes the post (`Box.intersects`, same file `Box.java:237-246`); it includes the plate. The player already overlaps the plate in X, so old `Box.intersectX` does not clip a positive displacement against it: the plate's `minX=0` is below the player's current `maxX≈.40000001192`, while its `maxX=1` is above the player's current minX (`Box.java:185-203`). Old movement therefore retains `+.2`.

The 26.2 provider ORs a stem with a lower plate (`net/minecraft/world/level/block/BrewingStandBlock.java:38,61-63`). `Block.column` makes the newer plate inset to x/z `.0625..9375`, while the stem occupies x/z `.4375..5625` and y `.125.. .875` (`Block.java:177-185`). `Shapes.or` performs `join(..., OR)` followed by `optimize` (`Shapes.java:127-139`); `VoxelShape.optimize` enumerates merged boxes and rejoins them (`VoxelShape.java:96-100,117-139`). For the published provider's union grid, the stem contributes the internal X plane `.4375`; the lower plate occupies the voxel immediately to its right for y `0.. .125` and z `.0625.. .4375`. The player's cross-section intersects that plate cell (`y=0..1.8`, `z≈-.25000001192.. .35000001192`) but does not intersect the stem (`zMax≈.35000001192 < .4375`). For movement X+, `VoxelShape.collide` finds the first occupied X cell after the player's current `maxX≈.40000001192` and clips to its near plane `.4375`, yielding `.4375-(.1+.6F/2.0F)≈.03749998808` (`VoxelShape.java:267-285`; `DiscreteVoxelShape.isFullWide`, `DiscreteVoxelShape.java:62-75`).

To isolate internal-plane decomposition from changed occupied geometry, use a control shape made from the *exact old* post and plate bounds, with no modern-provider geometry substituted: OR post `[7/16,9/16] × [0,14/16] × [7/16,9/16]` with plate `[0,1] × [0,2/16] × [0,1]`, then apply the published modern `Shapes.or`/`optimize` and `VoxelShape.collide` algorithms. The `7/16` post side remains a grid coordinate after box enumeration/rejoin, even though the lower plate fills cells on both sides of it. `collideX` returns the first occupied cell's near plane, so the same `.0375` clip results in this control. By contrast, applying old `Box.intersectX` separately to those exact old components returns `+.2` as described above. This same-geometry control proves a representation/clipping-plane effect independently of the modern plate's 1/16 inset or the newer stem's bottom extent. The actual 26.2 provider also clips at that plane for the stated cross-section; the provider's other occupied-geometry changes are not the basis of the control result.

The witness is conditional on the source collision query being reached for this embedded player state; `noClip` must be false and `onGround=false` avoids the old step-up branch for `deltaY=0`. It establishes a collision-response difference for one player-sized AABB, not ordinary travel or a naturally reachable entry into that state. The modern clip result is `.4375-(.1+.6F/2.0F)≈.03749998808`, conventionally rounded to `.0375`; this is far outside the `1.0E-7` epsilon. Old movement's collision-list and axis-clip path is `Entity.java:567-601`, `World.java:964-1039`, and `Box.java:185-246`. The 26.2 provider and voxel clipper are `BlockBehaviour.java:327-329`, `VoxelShape.java:252-302`, and the block/shape sources above; caller checks are recorded in the parent's separate audit.
