# Flying local players can sprint in shallow water by 1.21.9

1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` lines 722–744, `canStartSprinting` lines 1065–1075, and `shouldStopRunSprinting` lines 837–844; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `isUnderWater` and water checks.
1.21.9 and 1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` lines 726–747, `canStartSprinting` lines 1062–1069, `isSprintingPossible` lines 1055–1060, and sprint stop helpers lines 841–849; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `isInShallowWater` lines 1445–1451 in 1.21.9 and 1450–1456 in 1.21.10; `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `getFlyingSpeed` lines 1867–1871. The relevant `LocalPlayer` sprint methods are identical in 1.21.9 and 1.21.10.
Difference: In 1.21.8, `canStartSprinting` rejects any player in water unless underwater. From 1.21.9, the start check delegates to `isSprintingPossible(abilities.flying)`, whose shallow-water condition is bypassed when the local player's flying ability is true. The run-stop path applies the same flying-sensitive condition, so a flying, non-swimming local player with forward input and other sprint gates satisfied can start and retain sprint while in shallow water. `Entity.isInShallowWater()` is `isInWater() && !isUnderWater()`. In 1.21.10, the relevant start/stop behavior is unchanged. The stable cutover is 1.21.9.
Movement meaning: A flying local player in shallow water can enter and keep the sprint state in 1.21.9 and 1.21.10, while 1.21.8 rejects the start. In 1.21.9/1.21.10, `Player.getFlyingSpeed` doubles the flying speed when sprinting, so the changed eligibility affects flight movement speed.
