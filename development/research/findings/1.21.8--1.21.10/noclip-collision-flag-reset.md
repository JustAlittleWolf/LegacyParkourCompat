# Noclip movement clears stale collision flags
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` noclip branch (663–666); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `tick` (281–285) and `getFlyingSpeed` (2045–2050); `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` sprint update (731–743) and `shouldStopRunSprinting` (837–844).
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` noclip branch (670–677); `net/minecraft/world/entity/player/Player.java`, `tick` (240–244) and `getFlyingSpeed` (1867–1873); `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` sprint update (735–747) and `shouldStopRunSprinting` (841–845).
Difference: Both versions move a `noPhysics` entity directly by its requested vector. 1.21.8 leaves `horizontalCollision`, `verticalCollision`, `verticalCollisionBelow` and `minorHorizontalCollision` unchanged on that branch. 1.21.10 clears all four. Player tick assigns `noPhysics = isSpectator()` in both versions, so this branch is reachable by the local player.
Movement meaning: A player who has a non-minor horizontal collision and then enters spectator mode carries a stale collision into noclip movement in 1.21.8. After one spectator movement call, 1.21.10 has cleared it. With forward and sprint input, no blindness, enough sprint food and no passenger or shallow-water restriction, subsequent `LocalPlayer.aiStep` therefore cancels run sprinting in 1.21.8 but can retain it in 1.21.10. The unchanged flying-speed getter doubles flight acceleration while sprinting. The fixed historical profile remains constant; this witness changes game mode, not movement profile.
