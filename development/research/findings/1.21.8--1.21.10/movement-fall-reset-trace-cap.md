# Movement fall-reset trace is capped at eight blocks
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 663-700; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel` lines 2245-2271 and `aiStep` lines 2721-2822.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 670-713; `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel` lines 2275-2301 and `aiStep` lines 2751-2852; `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` calls `super.aiStep()` at line 834.
Difference: In both versions, the reset trace runs only when fall distance is nonzero, resolved movement squared length is at least `1.0`, and the block clip finds a fall-damage-resetting hit. 1.21.8 clips from the starting position through the full resolved vector. 1.21.10 clips along the normalized resolved vector scaled by `min(resolvedMovement.length(), 8.0)`.
Movement meaning: A local player consumes movement through inherited `LivingEntity.travel` and the shared `Entity.move`. If a resolved client-player move longer than eight blocks reaches a fall-damage-resetting block only beyond the first eight blocks, 1.21.10 does not reset fall distance for that hit while 1.21.8 can. The inspected sources establish the client consumer and source gate, but no concrete client-player trajectory with resolved length greater than eight is established; reachability of that condition remains open.
