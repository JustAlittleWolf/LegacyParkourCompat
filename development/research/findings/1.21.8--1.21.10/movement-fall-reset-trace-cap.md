Difference: In all three versions, the trace runs only when fall distance is nonzero, resolved movement squared length is at least `1.0`, and the clip finds a `FALLDAMAGE_RESETTING` block or water hit. In 1.21.8, the clip ends at the full resolved displacement. In 1.21.9 and 1.21.10, the resolved vector is normalized and scaled by `min(length, 8.0)` before clipping; this eight-block limit is present by 1.21.9 and unchanged in 1.21.10.
Movement meaning: A local player position correction carries a finite delta movement in `PositionMoveRotation`; `ClientPacketListener.handleMovePlayer` writes it directly to the local player's velocity in all three releases. Inherited `LivingEntity.travel` sends that displacement through `Entity.move`. In a clear fall with nonzero fall distance and water or a reset block more than eight blocks down the path, 1.21.8 can reset fall distance while 1.21.9 and 1.21.10 do not. This state has a movement consumer: `Player.maybeBackOffFromEdge` checks fall distance in `isAboveGround` before reducing horizontal motion near an edge, so the difference can change a later player movement response as well as fall handling.
