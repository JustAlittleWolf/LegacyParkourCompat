# Movement-triggered block callback traversal
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 663-700, movement record creation at 696; `applyEffectsFromBlocks` lines 785-834; `checkInsideBlocks` lines 1113-1195.
1.21.9 and 1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` lines 670-713, movement record creation at 709/712; `applyEffectsFromBlocks` lines 798-847 (1.21.9) and 801-850 (1.21.10); `checkInsideBlocks` lines 1134-1246 (1.21.9) and 1137-1251 (1.21.10). `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep` applies block effects at line 2822 (1.21.8), 2852 (1.21.9 and 1.21.10); `net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `aiStep` calls `super.aiStep()` at line 830 (1.21.8) and 834 (1.21.9 and 1.21.10).
Difference: 1.21.8 movement records retain only a boolean indicating axis-independent movement; the callback traversal chooses axes from the resolved displacement. Starting in 1.21.9, records retain the original post-edge-backoff request and traversal chooses axes from that vector. In 1.21.9 and 1.21.10, each record also has a 16-iteration cell budget; traversal stops when it is exhausted and performs a separate one-cell check at the endpoint. The endpoint fallback does not revisit omitted intermediate cells.
Movement meaning: A local player reaches this callback-processing path through `LocalPlayer.aiStep` and inherited `LivingEntity.aiStep`. The different requested-versus-resolved axis order can change which crossed blocks receive callbacks first. The 16-cell budget can omit intermediate cells only if a player's movement record consumes the budget; no concrete client-player trajectory reaching that limit is established by these sources, so that consequence remains conditional and its reachability is open.
