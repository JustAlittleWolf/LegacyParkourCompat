# Movement-triggered block callback traversal

Minecraft 1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` around line 696, `applyEffectsFromBlocks` around 785–834, and `checkInsideBlocks` around 1113–1195; `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `forEachBlockIntersectedBetween` around line 172.

Minecraft 1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` around line 712, `applyEffectsFromBlocks` around 801–856, and `checkInsideBlocks` around 1137–1225; `net/minecraft/world/level/BlockGetter.java`, `forEachBlockIntersectedBetween` around 171–280.

Difference: In 1.21.8, the movement record retains whether movement was axis-independent; callback traversal selects the axis order from the resolved displacement. In 1.21.10, the record retains the vector passed to `collide` after movement transforms in `move` (including piston limiting, the stuck multiplier when applicable, and edge back-off), and traversal selects the order from that vector. The Y-X-Z versus Y-Z-X selector is unchanged. The segment walk also changed: 1.21.10 enumerates endpoints and AABB corners before a DDA walk based on the furthest corner, visits at most 16 steps per segment, and checks the endpoint once when that limit is exhausted. The record-wide pass-index budget is a separate operation documented in [movement-triggered block callback budget](../1.21.8--1.21.9/movement-triggered-block-callback-budget.md).

Movement meaning: A local player reaches this route through `LocalPlayer.aiStep`, inherited `LivingEntity.aiStep`, and `Entity.move`/`applyEffectsFromBlocks`. When diagonal movement collides and slides, the vector passed to `collide` can select a different axis ordering from the resolved displacement, changing the order in which crossed block cells receive callbacks. Segment traversal limits also govern which cells are visited for long segments. This finding covers callback ordering and per-segment enumeration; the adjacent Bubble Column contact-response finding remains separate.
