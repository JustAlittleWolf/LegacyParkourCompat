# Short swept bubble-column contact no longer guarantees a vertical impulse
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks(Vec3, ...)`, lines 1138–1178; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 51–61; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleColumn` and `onInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2623–2668.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks(Vec3, ...)`, lines 1167–1208; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 51–64; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleColumn` and `onInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2696–2741.
Difference: 1.21.8 limits sufficiently short movement to final-box candidates. 1.21.9 lowers the trivial-motion cutoff from `Mth.square(0.99999F)` to `Mth.square(1.0E-5F)` and admits start-side swept candidates. A bubble column has a full-block entity-inside shape sentinel and an empty ordinary collision shape. In 1.21.10 the sentinel still admits the callback without the moving-AABB shape test. The callback receives `isPrecise = movedFar || deflatedTargetBox.intersects(blockPos)` and guards both vertical responses with that flag. The impulse formulas retain their earlier arithmetic.
Movement meaning: In the accepted 1.21.9→1.21.10 vertical exit, feet move from y=0.98 to y=1.075 in one 0.095 displacement. The translated-back deflated box admits the bubble cell, while the final deflated box misses it and the squared displacement of 0.009025 is below the 0.9999800001005053 precise-distance threshold. The post-travel callback gives the upward open-above response in 1.21.9. In 1.21.10 the callback is still invoked and the `isPrecise=false` argument suppresses the response. The numerical trace assumes `abilities.flying == false`, no sprinting or Dolphin's Grace, and default water-movement efficiency.
