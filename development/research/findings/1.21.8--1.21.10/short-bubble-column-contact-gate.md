# Short swept bubble-column contact no longer guarantees a vertical impulse
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks(Vec3, ...)`, lines 1138–1178; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 51–61; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleColumn` and `onInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2623–2668.
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `checkInsideBlocks(Vec3, ...)`, lines 1167–1208; `net/minecraft/world/level/block/BubbleColumnBlock.java`, `entityInside`, lines 51–64; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `onAboveBubbleColumn` and `onInsideBubbleColumn`, lines 2696–2741.
Difference: 1.21.8 calls the bubble-column response whenever the moving entity intersects its inside shape. In 1.21.10 the entity-intersection callback receives a `moved-far-or-final-box-overlaps-cell` flag; `BubbleColumnBlock.entityInside` returns without applying its bubble impulse when the flag is false. The impulse formulas themselves are unchanged.
Movement meaning: A short swept contact that intersects a bubble-column shape but ends with the player's bounding box outside that cell can now pass without the column's upward or downward velocity adjustment. The old client applies the adjustment on that contact.
