Difference: In 1.21.8, `AABB.collidedAlongVector` inflates each candidate box by exactly half the moving box's size on each axis. Starting in 1.21.9, it subtracts `1.0E-7` from each half-size before inflation. Center containment and segment clipping are unchanged. `Entity.checkInsideBlocks` uses this helper for entity-inside block shapes; its fluid path obtains an AABB from `Fluid.getAABB` and tests it through the same helper. The tolerance change remains in 1.21.10.
Movement meaning: `LocalPlayer.aiStep` reaches the inherited `LivingEntity.aiStep` block-effect pass after travel, where both block-shape and fluid-shape sweeps are evaluated for recorded movement. A position correction sets the local player's position and velocity directly; this lets the next client tick place the player's center within `1.0E-7` of the inflated upper face of a source-water AABB while the player's box still overlaps the water block cell. The older helper reports the fluid hit and calls the fluid's `entityInside`; the 1.21.9/1.21.10 helper can miss it. The same tolerance also applies to the entity-inside collision shape of blocks.
