Difference: `AABB.collidedAlongVector` inflates each candidate box by half the moving box's size in 1.21.8. Starting in 1.21.9, it subtracts `1.0E-7` from each half-size before inflation. Center containment and segment clipping otherwise remain the same. `Entity.checkInsideBlocks` uses this helper for block entity-inside shapes and calls `Entity.collidedWithFluid`, which obtains the fluid's AABB through `Fluid.getAABB` and tests it through the same helper.
Movement meaning: The changed tolerance affects both block-shape callback eligibility and the fluid-intersection predicate in the shared entity block-effect traversal. The source change can alter either result when its only candidate intersection lies within the removed margin. `LocalPlayer.aiStep` reaches inherited `LivingEntity.aiStep` block-effect processing (1.21.8 line 830; 1.21.9 and 1.21.10 line 834), which checks blocks and fluids for recorded movement. No concrete client-player trajectory and candidate block/fluid shape exercising the narrow margin is established here, so that trajectory-level effect remains conditional and reachability is open.
