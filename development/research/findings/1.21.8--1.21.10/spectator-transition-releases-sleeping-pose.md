# Spectator transition releases sleeping pose
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tick` sleep check (2546–2548) and `stopSleeping` (3467–3488); `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleGameEvent` game-mode update (1479–1480); `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `setGameMode` (1621–1639).
1.21.10, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `tick` sleep check (2576–2578) and `stopSleeping` (3497–3518); `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `canInteractWithLevel` (324–326); `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleGameEvent` game-mode update (1484–1485); `net/minecraft/server/level/ServerPlayer.java`, `setGameMode` (1684–1702).
Difference: 1.21.8 stops sleeping in this tick path only when `checkBedExists()` is false. 1.21.10 also stops sleeping when `canInteractWithLevel()` is false; that predicate is `isAlive() && !isRemoved() && !isSpectator()`. The normal player game-mode transition to spectator updates the client game mode without itself calling `stopSleeping` in `ServerPlayer.setGameMode` in either release. `stopSleeping` clears the sleeping state and restores the standing pose, position and rotation using the bed stand-up search.
Movement meaning: A player who remains asleep through a transition to spectator exits the sleeping pose on the next living-entity tick in 1.21.10, restoring the standing dimensions and stand-up placement; 1.21.8 retains the sleeping state while the bed remains. This concerns the direct pose/dimension/position response to an external game-mode state change, not eligibility to enter a bed.
