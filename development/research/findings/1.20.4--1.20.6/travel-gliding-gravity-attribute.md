# Player gravity is attribute-backed in travel and gliding

Difference: At default gravity `0.08` and descending with Slow Falling, 1.20.6's `Math.min(0.08, 0.01)` equals 1.20.4's `0.01`. 1.20.6 instead accepts a non-default effective gravity value throughout player travel. In fall-flying travel, the branch adds a vertical term using that same local: `g * (-1.0 + cos(pitch)^2 * min(1.0, lookLength / 0.4) * 0.75)` The corresponding source operation evaluates `cos(pitch)`, squares it, multiplies by the clamped look-length ratio and `0.75`, then adds `g` times the resulting `(-1.0 + ...)` coefficient to current vertical velocity. The remaining fall-flying corrections occur in the same order in 1.20.4 and 1.20.6, then velocity is multiplied by `(0.99F, 0.98F, 0.99F)` and passed to `move`. At default effective gravity this local contribution is the same; when 1.20.6's effective gravity differs from `0.08`, the fall-flying vertical update differs. With descending Slow Falling, the local first becomes 1.20.4's `0.01` or 1.20.6's `Math.min(g, 0.01)`, so the cap condition also applies to fall-flying. 1.20.6's `getGravity()` returns zero when no-gravity is set, while 1.20.4 still begins from `0.08`; no producer for that state or attribute value is inferred here. When descending with Slow Falling and supplied gravity below `0.01`, 1.20.6 uses that lower value; 1.20.4 still uses `0.01`. The fluid helper uses the same effective gravity local and its zero check differs when the effective player gravity is non-default. In normal land travel, 1.20.6 subtracts the effective gravity local in the available-chunk/server branch; 1.20.4 subtracts its fixed local only under `!isNoGravity()`. In water and lava, 1.20.4 gates the quarter-gravity adjustment on `!isNoGravity()` while 1.20.6 gates it on effective gravity being nonzero. The 1.20.6 `FlyingAnimal` vertical-drag alternative is excluded because non-player movement is outside this source campaign's scope.

Movement meaning: `Player.createAttributes()` inherits `LivingEntity.createLivingAttributes()` on 1.20.6. The local-player path reaches virtual `LivingEntity.travel` through `LivingEntity.aiStep()` and `Player.travel`; the jump-held Elytra path can set shared fall-flying flag 7 under the gates above. `LivingEntity.travel` is entered under its `isControlledByLocalInstance()` gate, and its fall-flying branch is selected after the water and lava branches when `isFallFlying()` is true. That branch uses the same gravity local as ordinary and fluid travel. The same travel body routes fluid-falling adjustment through `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement`, and the water/lava branches apply their own gravity adjustment. The 1.20.6 client attribute receiver installs synchronized base values and modifiers. This establishes player-side reachability and the incoming-value consumer; it does not identify a particular server producer or assume a particular configured value. Source-proven: the default base gravity remains `0.08`, while non-default effective gravity affects ordinary, fall-flying, fluid-falling, water and lava player travel; under descending Slow Falling, 1.20.6 caps effective gravity at `0.01`, rather than replacing every descending value with `0.01`. The fall-flying branch's exact caller, state gates, gravity term and subsequent velocity/move order are paired. The finding excludes `FlyingAnimal` and other non-player movement. The bounded method/helper branches and player client receiver are closed for this finding. Other gravity consumers, server-side provenance, full S3-gravity-drag closure and any measured trajectory remain outside this finding.
