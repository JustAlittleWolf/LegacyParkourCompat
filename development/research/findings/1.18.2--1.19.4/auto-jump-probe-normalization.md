# Auto-jump probe uses a different inverse-square-root calculation

1.18.2, Mojmap: `1.18.2/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `LocalPlayer.move` 889–894 and `updateAutoJump` 900–996; `1.18.2/mojmap/net/minecraft/util/Mth.java`, `fastInvSqrt(float)` 485–491.
1.19.4, Mojmap: `1.19.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `LocalPlayer.move` 880–885 and `updateAutoJump` 891–987; `1.19.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/util/Mth.java`, `invSqrt(float)` 387–389; JOML 1.10.5, `org.joml.Math.invsqrt(float)`.
1.19.3, Mojmap: `1.19.3/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`, `updateAutoJump` 911–1007 still calls `Mth.fastInvSqrt(float)` at line 931, confirming the adjacent 1.19.3→1.19.4 boundary.

Difference: 1.18.2 and 1.19.3 normalize the horizontal movement with the one-step float bit-hack in `Mth.fastInvSqrt`; 1.19.4 calls `Mth.invSqrt`, whose float overload delegates to JOML 1.10.5's `1.0F / (float)Math.sqrt((double)value)`. The move caller, auto-jump eligibility gates, direction cutoff and collision-search logic are retained. This changes the normalized probe vector, though `Math.max(speed * 7.0F, 1.0F / inverseSqrt)` can keep the look-ahead length unchanged.

Movement meaning: This changes a reachable automatic-jump collision result. On an ordinary first grounded walking tick from rest, a player with default movement speed `0.1F`, standing on stone (`0.6F` friction), moving forward with yaw `-90°`, can move `0.1F` along +X. At initial center `(0.1005, 0, 0.5)`, with auto-jump enabled, timer clear, on ground after the move, forward input still held, no passenger or shift key, and stone underfoot, the `canAutoJump` gates pass. For this displacement, the old approximation returns `9.982521F`, JOML returns `10.0F`, while the look-ahead remains `0.7F` in both cases. The side-ray endpoint is therefore `x=0.9992765` with the old result and `x=1.0005000` with JOML. A stone cube at `(1,0,0)` with empty space above and around it is inside the inflated collision-search box in both cases; its full-cube side is crossed at `y=0.51` and `z=0.2/0.8` only by the JOML ray. The old scan leaves `autoJumpTime` unchanged, while the new scan records the cube top at `y=1` and sets `autoJumpTime=1`. The player’s actual moved box remains clear of the cube.

Exact witness source: 1.19.4 `Player.createAttributes` sets movement speed to `0.1F` (Player.java 207–213) and `getSpeed` returns that attribute (Player.java 1482–1485); grounded travel applies `speed * (0.21600002F / (friction^3))` and adds input before moving (LivingEntity.java 2088–2111, 2152–2163, 2197–2210; Entity.java 1181–1195). With stone friction `0.6F`, that grounded acceleration rounds to `0.1F`; the direct move clears the wall because the player's leading edge remains below `x=1`. `Entity.getForward` / `Vec3.directionFromRotation` at pitch `0°`, yaw `-90°` faces +X (Entity.java 1885–1887; Vec3.java 213–222). Default block shape is full and its collision shape is retained for colliding blocks (BlockBehaviour.java 290–296); AABB line intersection uses strict bounds (AABB.java 225–233).
