# Nether portal contact returns to the full-cell sentinel

1.21.5, Mojmap. `1.21.5/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/level/block/NetherPortalBlock.java` 49, 61–69 returns its axis-dependent central slab from `getEntityInsideCollisionShape`; `world/entity/Entity.java` 1079–1120 and 1129–1133 applies the swept-shape test.

1.21.6, Mojmap. `1.21.6/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/level/block/NetherPortalBlock.java` 61–70 has no inside-contact override. The default `BlockBehaviour.getEntityInsideCollisionShape` returns `Shapes.block()` (`world/level/block/state/BlockBehaviour.java` 314–316), and `world/entity/Entity.java` 1113–1162 accepts the sentinel without the narrow-shape test. The same operation remains in 1.21.7 and 1.21.8: the portal block has no override, the default returns the sentinel, and the entity scan bypasses the narrow-shape test at the same member locations.

A scan that visits a portal cell while the player remains beside the slab skips `entityInside` in 1.21.5 and invokes it again in 1.21.6–1.21.8. Movement meaning: Keep a normally opened survival `InventoryScreen` active while contact is recorded, with the physical jump key held. An eligible living local player must be awake, not a passenger, without portal cooldown, and affected by blocks; normal block scanning excludes removed or `noPhysics` entities (`Entity.isAffectedByBlocks`, 1.21.6 `Entity.java` 851–852). The inventory screen is non-pausing and closes during the portal transition before the next `input.tick`. The mouse was released when the inventory opened; on a non-macOS system with the game window active, `MouseHandler.grabMouse` repolls key mappings only when mouse capture is currently ungrabbed. A still-physically-held jump key is then sampled before the grounded jump gate; with jump available (on ground and no jump delay), `LivingEntity.aiStep` can call `jumpFromGround` and raise Y velocity. Opening the inventory sends a container-close packet; the inspected server handler only closes the menu.

Evidence: 1.21.6 `Minecraft.java` 1872–1877, `InventoryScreen.java` 19, `AbstractContainerScreen.java` 668–670, `LocalPlayer.java` 682–703 and 870–892, `MouseHandler.java` 398–403, `KeyMapping.java` 55–58, `KeyboardInput.java` 23–32, `LivingEntity.java` 2777–2791. The same local chain applies in 1.21.7 and 1.21.8 at the corresponding members. The consequence describes a reachable local jump path, not server movement acceptance.
