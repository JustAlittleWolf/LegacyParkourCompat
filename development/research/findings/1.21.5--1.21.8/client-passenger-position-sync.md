# Client position-sync passenger write follows interpolation state

1.21.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleEntityPositionSync` (567–586).
1.21.8, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleEntityPositionSync` (616–637).
1.21.6 and 1.21.7, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/client/multiplayer/ClientPacketListener.java`, `handleEntityPositionSync` (632–635).

Difference: Both endpoint handlers update the entity's position codec. For a target that is not locally authoritative, they call `moveOrInterpolateTo` when it is ticking and its squared distance to the target is at most `4096`; otherwise they call `snapTo`. After either position operation, 1.21.5 calls `positionRider(localPlayer)` and `localPlayer.setOldPosAndRot()` when `isInterpolating() && hasIndirectPassenger(localPlayer)`. From 1.21.6 through 1.21.8, that post-operation gate is `!isInterpolating() && hasIndirectPassenger(localPlayer)`. The predicate reads the handler's state after the position operation; it is not a synonym for the interpolation-versus-snap branch. `snapTo` sets raw position and rotation, snapshots old position and rotation, and reapplies position, but does not cancel an already active interpolation handler (`Entity.snapTo`: 1.21.5:1514–1520; 1.21.8:1591–1597). `moveOrInterpolateTo` delegates to an existing handler, and `isInterpolating()` reports whether that handler has active interpolation (1.21.5:2161–2174; 1.21.8:2329–2342).

Movement meaning: A concrete direct-passenger witness is a non-eye-submerged boat with a remote player first and the local player second in its passenger list. The client applies those passenger IDs in packet order and starts each riding the vehicle (1.21.5 `ClientboundSetPassengersPacket.java`:17–35 and `ClientPacketListener.java`:1051–1063; 1.21.8 packet:17–35 and listener:1100–1112). The boat allows two passengers and uses its first living passenger as controller; the boat's local-authority predicate delegates to that controller, while `Player.isLocalClientAuthoritative()` is true for the local player (1.21.5 `AbstractBoat.java`:64, 201, 746–758, `Entity.java`:3162–3169 and `Player.java`:1312–1314; 1.21.8 `AbstractBoat.java`:65, 202, 749–761, `Entity.java`:3335–3342 and `Player.java`:1324–1326). The remote player therefore makes the boat non-locally-authoritative while the local player remains its direct passenger. For a ticking boat and a target within 64 blocks, `moveOrInterpolateTo` starts three interpolation steps; the active handler makes the 1.21.5 post-operation gate call the immediate rider placement and makes the 1.21.8 gate skip it. With no active handler after the position operation, the inverse gate calls that placement in 1.21.8 and skips it in 1.21.5. `hasIndirectPassenger` recurses through vehicle ancestors, but `positionRider` writes the player's position only for a direct passenger (1.21.5 `Entity.java`:3153–3160, 2019–2023; 1.21.8:3326–3333, 2187–2191); for a nested local player, the handler can reset old position and rotation without moving the player. The ordinary client passenger tick still calls `rideTick`, then `vehicle.positionRider(this)` for an attached player in both endpoint versions (1.21.5 `ClientLevel.java`:289–299, `Entity.java`:2011–2017; 1.21.8 `ClientLevel.java`:306–316, `Entity.java`:2179–2185). This difference concerns the immediate position-sync response.

Release boundary: In 1.21.4, the immediate rider write is inside the snap `else` branch (`ClientPacketListener.java`:556–574). In 1.21.5, it moves after both entity-position branches and is gated by `isInterpolating()`. The inverse `!isInterpolating()` gate is present in 1.21.6 and 1.21.7 and persists in 1.21.8. The 1.21.6 and 1.21.7 listener sources are byte-identical to the 1.21.8 listener, and their embedded Mojmap provenance records bind the exact release metadata, client input and per-source hashes. Those two roots have no adjacent campaign ready marker or source-manifest sidecar; the available evidence pins the source boundary to 1.21.6 but does not label those roots campaign-ready. The earlier 1.21.1–1.21.3 packet-era passenger change is a separate transition and is not revised by this later handler-gate finding.

Coverage is limited to the inbound entity-position-sync handler and its direct local-player write and passenger-tick dependency. The regular relative-movement, teleport and player-position packet handlers have unchanged relevant passenger-response gates in the compared 1.21.5 and 1.21.8 endpoints. Server packet production, vehicle physics, other movement operations and runtime reproduction are outside this finding.
