Difference: entering swimming while not already swimming requires sprinting, the virtual `isUnderWater()` predicate and no passenger in 1.16.5 and 1.17. 1.17.1 additionally requires `level.getFluidState(blockPosition).is(FluidTags.WATER)`. Staying in swimming retains sprinting, body-water contact and no passenger. The non-flying Player wrapper delegates to Entity. For the local client player, `isUnderWater()` returns `wasUnderwater`: Player sets that field from the previously stored eye-fluid tag before calling `super.tick()`. The ensuing Entity base tick updates body-water state, then eye-fluid state, then swimming. Entity's own `wasEyeInWater && isInWater()` implementation is overridden on this player path.
Movement meaning: a non-flying, non-spectator sprinting client player with previous eye contact with water can enter swimming in the older sources even when its current feet block has no water. A concrete precondition is a standing player with feet at Y=63.5 beneath water at Y=65: its upper body and eye sample can contact that water while the feet block at Y=63 is air. Keep that eye-fluid tag from the preceding tick, start sprinting during that tick's input processing, and the next base tick reaches the changed entry predicate. Old entry succeeds; 1.17.1 entry fails. With body-water contact retained, swimming changes the Player travel look-direction response and later pose selection. This is a direct player predicate reading vanilla water state; no particular block type is required. Exact 1.17 retains the older gate, establishing 1.17 to 1.17.1 as the stable-release change.
