# Fluid scanning checks a narrower unloaded-chunk margin
1.21.11, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 3509–3513; `touchingUnloadedChunk`, lines 3576–3583; `net/minecraft/world/level/LevelReader.java`, `hasChunksAt`, lines 197–210.
26.1.2, published unobfuscated: `net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityFluidInteraction.java`, `update` and `hasFluidAndLoaded`, lines 32–42 and 91–118; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getFluidInteractionBox`, lines 4015–4023.
Difference: the older path aborts fluid scanning unless every chunk touched by the entity bounding box inflated by `1.0` is loaded. The newer path checks only chunks spanning the deflated interaction box plus one sampled block on each horizontal side, and only proceeds if those loaded sections contain fluid. At the high horizontal edge this is a narrower chunk range: for an entity box ending at `x=14.7`, the old inflated check reaches block `x=16` (chunk 1), while the newer sampled range reaches block `x=15` (chunk 0).
Movement meaning: with water or lava under the entity in chunk 0 and adjacent chunk 1 unavailable, the old guard returns before setting fluid height or applying current; the newer guard can scan the loaded fluid and update height/current. The block-coordinate example follows the source bounds; the resulting player impulse difference is inferred from the paired callers.
