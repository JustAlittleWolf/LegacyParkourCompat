# Small accumulated fluid currents are suppressed
1.21.11, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 3553–3570. The guard applies for any nonzero accumulated current; player currents are averaged by the number of matching fluid cells, then scaled and added.
26.1.2, published unobfuscated: `net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityFluidInteraction.java`, `Tracker.applyCurrentTo`, lines 171–188. The guard returns when the accumulated vector's squared length is below `1.0E-5F` before player averaging and scaling.
Difference: The newer tracker drops nonzero accumulated vectors shorter than `sqrt(1.0E-5)` (about `0.0031623`) before applying them. In the older code, these vectors still reach the player response; when the player already has horizontal speed at least `0.003`, the low-speed minimum-current branch does not mask the difference. Neighboring fluid flows can partially cancel to produce such a vector, so a player moving through that flow retains the small current impulse in 1.21.11 and receives none in 26.1.2. The paired `FlowingFluid.getFlow` implementations retain the same neighbor-height and normalization operations (1.21.11 `FlowingFluid.java:55–100`; 26.1.2 `FlowingFluid.java:55–100`), closing that dependency.
