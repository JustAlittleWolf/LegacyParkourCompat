# Surface-boat passengers use a clipped fluid interaction box
1.21.11, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 1501–1506; `updateInWaterStateAndDoWaterCurrentPushing`, lines 1509–1522; `updateFluidOnEyes`, lines 1524–1535; `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 3509–3573.
26.1.2, published unobfuscated: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidInteraction`, lines 1558–1581 and `getFluidInteractionBox`, lines 4015–4027; `net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityFluidInteraction.java`, `update`, lines 68–79; `net/minecraft/world/entity/vehicle/boat/AbstractBoat.java`, `modifyPassengerFluidInteractionBox`, lines 782–795.
Difference: the older path skips water height/current updates for a passenger of an `AbstractBoat` when it is not underwater, but still scans lava with the ordinary deflated entity bounding box. The newer path delegates the passenger's scan box to the boat for every boat status. When the boat is not underwater—including land, air, and water-surface statuses—it clips the box to start at the boat's `maxY`, or returns `null` when the boat covers the passenger box. The clipped box is used for water and lava height/current sampling and also for `eyesInside`. Fluid tops equal to the clipped `minY` are accepted by the new scanner; only fluid entirely below that boundary is omitted.
Movement meaning: the movement claim is limited to height/current sampling: a passenger whose lower bounding box overlaps lava strictly below a non-underwater boat's top can have lava height/current recognized in the older path and omitted in the newer path. The clipping gate also applies while the boat is on land or in air, not only at a water surface. Although the clipped scan changes `eyesInside` eligibility, a movement consequence for an aboard player is not established: `updateSwimming` requires `!isPassenger`, and air/drowning effects are excluded. Concrete downstream travel effects from changed height/current inputs remain inferred.
