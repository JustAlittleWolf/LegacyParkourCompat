# Fluid current and height use a different vertical origin
1.21.11, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`, lines 3509–3542.
26.1.2, published unobfuscated: `net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityFluidInteraction.java`, `update`, lines 32–76; `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `getFluidInteractionBox`, lines 4015–4023.
Difference: both scanners use the bounding box deflated by `0.001` for their sampled region and accept a fluid top only when it is at or above that box's `minY`. The older scanner measures fluid depth from the deflated box's `minY` (line 3537); the newer scanner measures from the raw entity bounding box `minY` (helper line 43, used at line 72), which is `0.001` lower. For accepted overlaps, stored fluid height and the low-depth current scaling input are therefore `0.001` larger in the newer source. At exact equality with the deflated `minY`, the old scanner sets its overlap Boolean while storing height zero; the new scanner stores `0.001`. A fluid top strictly between the raw and deflated `minY` values is rejected by both source gates.
Movement meaning: reachable shallow water or lava can produce a different `getFluidHeight` value and, below the `0.4` current-scaling gate, a different current impulse. Height-based thresholds may also see the shifted value. No Boolean in-fluid disagreement follows from the strict-between boundary case; downstream trajectory effects from the changed accepted heights are inferred.
