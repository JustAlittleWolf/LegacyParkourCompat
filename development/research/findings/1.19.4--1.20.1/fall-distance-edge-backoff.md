1.19.4, Mojmap: `1.19.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `travel` 2089-2096; `handleRelativeFrictionAndCalculateMovement` 2153-2157. The latter issues the `MoverType.SELF` move before the Levitation branch resets fall distance.
1.20, Mojmap: `1.20/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `aiStep` 2559-2573. It resets fall distance for Levitation before invoking `travel`; the decompile's embedded provenance record is `1.20/mojmap/.decompile-provenance.json`.
Player edge logic, both releases: `world/entity/player/Player.java`, `maybeBackOffFromEdge` 1041-1099 (1.19.4) / 1043-1101 (1.20); `world/entity/Entity.java`, `move` 568-569 (1.19.4) / 612-613 (1.20) invokes `maybeBackOffFromEdge` before collision resolution.

Difference: With Levitation active, 1.19.4 reaches the player's `SELF` move using the previous fall distance, then resets it in `travel`. In 1.20, `aiStep` resets it before `travel` and its `SELF` move. A concrete edge witness is a non-flying, sneaking, airborne player with prior fall distance greater than `maxUpStep`, a negative requested Y, and support within `maxUpStep` beneath the starting footprint, while a full horizontal move would leave that support. Before the old move, `Player.isAboveGround` fails its `fallDistance < maxUpStep` check, so edge backoff is skipped and the full horizontal move can leave the support. Before the new move, fall distance is zero; the support makes `isAboveGround` true, so the 0.05-step horizontal probe backs the player off the edge.

Movement meaning: Levitation can therefore change horizontal player position at a ledge through the fall-distance input to edge backoff. This witness is Levitation-only. Slow Falling has a separate timing/gating difference and is not claimed here.
