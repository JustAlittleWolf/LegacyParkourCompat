# Entity push square-root precision
1.16.5, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `push(Entity)`, lines 1180–1207; `net/minecraft/util/Mth.java`, `sqrt(double)`, lines 37–38. Caller path: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `pushEntities` and `doPush`.
1.17.1, Mojmap: `net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java`, `push(Entity)`, lines 1278–1303; caller path: `net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `pushEntities` and `doPush`.
Difference: Under the unchanged guards (not passengers of the same vehicle, neither entity has `noPhysics`, and `max(abs(dx), abs(dz)) >= 0.01F`), 1.16.5 assigns `Mth.sqrt(double)` to a double; that helper returns `(float)Math.sqrt(d)`, so the divisor is rounded to float before the following divisions and reciprocal. 1.17.1 uses `Math.sqrt(double)` and retains double precision. The later cap, scaling, and two `push` writes keep their order. The old `pushthrough` multiplier is absent in 1.17.1, but the vanilla field has no writes in 1.16.5 and therefore remains its default `0.0F`; its old multiplication by `1.0F` has no effect for vanilla entities.
Movement meaning: During client-side living-entity collision handling, a local player overlapping a pushable entity can receive the horizontal velocity written by `Entity.push`; the changed square-root precision changes the normalization and resulting push for some coordinate differences. `LivingEntity.pushEntities` calls `doPush` without a logical-server-only gate. The exact patch introduction is unknown within 1.16.5–1.17.1.
