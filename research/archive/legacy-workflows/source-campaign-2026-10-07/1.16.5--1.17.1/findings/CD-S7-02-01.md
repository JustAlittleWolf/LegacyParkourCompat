## Source-level difference

Both `Entity.baseTick()` methods call `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing()`, which checks lava and then water and delegates to `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing`. In A, that method deflates the entity bounding box by `0.001`, computes the integer fluid-scan bounds, and returns early only if `level.hasChunksAt` says those scan bounds are unavailable. In B, the method first calls `touchingUnloadedChunk()`, which inflates the full bounding box by `1.0` and checks the resulting horizontal chunk range; failure returns before the scan, fluid-height update, or velocity calculation.

For a player whose deflated scan box intersects only loaded chunks, but whose one-block inflated margin intersects an unloaded chunk, A proceeds to sample the same fluid states while B exits before sampling. If matching fluid flow produces a nonzero vector, A can add the calculated push to the player's delta movement; B does not. The remaining sampled-fluid and push calculation is paired in the inspected method bodies. This is a changed guard on direct player velocity, not a claim about world or chunk generation.

## Reachability and dependencies

The client player tick reaches `Entity.tick()` and `baseTick()` through the paired player/entity tick chain recorded in S1-02. The fluid branch is reached for water or lava overlap; the finding's concrete condition additionally requires the scan bounds to be loaded, an unloaded chunk inside the newer inflated margin, fluid tagged with the checked tag, `isPushedByFluid()` true, and a nonzero accumulated flow. A `Player` is exempt from the non-player flow normalization in both method bodies. `LevelReader.hasChunksAt` in A checks the supplied vertical limits against `[0,256)` and then checks the x/z chunk rectangle. B checks the supplied vertical limits against the dimension build-height range and delegates to the x/z overload; `touchingUnloadedChunk()` uses that x/z overload for the one-block margin.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source proves that the newer guard can suppress fluid-height sampling and the immediate player velocity addition in the stated chunk-margin condition. It does not establish a trajectory, how often the condition occurs in a running world, or any runtime outcome. First changed release within the interval is unresolved.
