When a non-default STEP_HEIGHT value is supplied and synchronized to the client, B uses it as the vertical limit passed into the unchanged entity step-candidate logic. The controlling-player-passenger minimum of `1.0F` remains in both versions. No particular collision result or trajectory is claimed.
