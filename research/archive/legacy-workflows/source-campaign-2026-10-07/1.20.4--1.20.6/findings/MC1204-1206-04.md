## Source-level difference

At default gravity `0.08` and descending with Slow Falling, B's `Math.min(0.08, 0.01)` equals A's `0.01`. B instead accepts a non-default effective gravity value throughout player travel. In fall-flying travel, the branch adds a vertical term using that same local:

`g * (-1.0 + cos(pitch)^2 * min(1.0, lookLength / 0.4) * 0.75)`

The corresponding source operation evaluates `cos(pitch)`, squares it, multiplies by the clamped look-length ratio and `0.75`, then adds `g` times the resulting `(-1.0 + ...)` coefficient to current vertical velocity. The remaining fall-flying corrections occur in the same order in A and B, then velocity is multiplied by `(0.99F, 0.98F, 0.99F)` and passed to `move`. At default effective gravity this local contribution is the same; when B's effective gravity differs from `0.08`, the fall-flying vertical update differs. With descending Slow Falling, the local first becomes A's `0.01` or B's `Math.min(g, 0.01)`, so the cap condition also applies to fall-flying. B's `getGravity()` returns zero when no-gravity is set, while A still begins from `0.08`; no producer for that state or attribute value is inferred here.

When descending with Slow Falling and supplied gravity below `0.01`, B uses that lower value; A still uses `0.01`. The fluid helper uses the same effective gravity local and its zero check differs when the effective player gravity is non-default. In normal land travel, B subtracts the effective gravity local in the available-chunk/server branch; A subtracts its fixed local only under `!isNoGravity()`. In water and lava, A gates the quarter-gravity adjustment on `!isNoGravity()` while B gates it on effective gravity being nonzero. The B `FlyingAnimal` vertical-drag alternative is excluded because non-player movement is outside this source campaign's scope.

## Reachability and dependencies

`Player.createAttributes()` inherits `LivingEntity.createLivingAttributes()` on B. The local-player path reaches virtual `LivingEntity.travel` through `LivingEntity.aiStep()` and `Player.travel`; the jump-held Elytra path can set shared fall-flying flag 7 under the gates above. `LivingEntity.travel` is entered under its `isControlledByLocalInstance()` gate, and its fall-flying branch is selected after the water and lava branches when `isFallFlying()` is true. That branch uses the same gravity local as ordinary and fluid travel. The same travel body routes fluid-falling adjustment through `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement`, and the water/lava branches apply their own gravity adjustment. The 1.20.6 client attribute receiver installs synchronized base values and modifiers. This establishes player-side reachability and the incoming-value consumer; it does not identify a particular server producer or assume a particular configured value.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: the default base gravity remains `0.08`, while non-default effective gravity affects ordinary, fall-flying, fluid-falling, water and lava player travel; under descending Slow Falling, B caps effective gravity at `0.01`, rather than replacing every descending value with `0.01`. The fall-flying branch's exact caller, state gates, gravity term and subsequent velocity/move order are paired. The finding excludes `FlyingAnimal` and other non-player movement. The bounded method/helper branches and player client receiver are closed for this finding. Other gravity consumers, server-side provenance, full S3-gravity-drag closure and any measured trajectory remain outside this finding. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
