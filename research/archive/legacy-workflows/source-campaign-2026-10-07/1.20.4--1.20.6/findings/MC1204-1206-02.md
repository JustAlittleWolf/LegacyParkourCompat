## Source-level difference

For jump power `<= 1.0E-5F`, including equality, A writes that power into vertical velocity and marks an impulse; B skips the velocity write, sprint impulse and impulse flag. For values above the cutoff, both set vertical velocity from the jump helper. On a sprinting jump, A's float `0.2F` multiplication and B's double `0.2` multiplication have different precision/order. The `Vec3` addition path resolves to the same component-wise double addition in both versions.

## Reachability and dependencies

`LivingEntity.aiStep()` reaches the virtual ground-jump routine under the paired on-ground/fluid conditions and `noJumpDelay == 0` (A lines 2571-2587; B lines 2651-2667). `Player.jumpFromGround()` delegates to the LivingEntity method (A lines 1440-1447; B lines 1462-1469). Grounded mayfly double-tap activation additionally reaches the same override in B at `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` line 734; A's paired toggle lines 726-732 contain no such call (MC1204-1206-01). Jump helper output depends on block jump factor, Jump Boost, and in B the generic jump-strength attribute (MC1204-1206-03). These conditions close reachability for the described conditional differences; no specific block, effect or attribute value is assumed.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: the threshold changes whether velocity and impulse state are written; sprinting arithmetic changes at the floating-point operation level. The resulting trajectory has not been runtime-tested. Food/stat effects after the player override and non-player behavior are outside the scope of this finding. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
