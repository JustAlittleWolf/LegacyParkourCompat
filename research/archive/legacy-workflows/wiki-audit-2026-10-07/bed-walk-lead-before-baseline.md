# Wiki claim disposition: walking onto beds predates the audit baseline

## Claim and source

The official [Walking page revision 2757268](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Walking?oldid=2757268) dates the “walking onto a bed without jumping” change to Java snapshot 14w32c, during the 1.8 development cycle. This movement audit begins at released 1.8.9. Therefore this particular Wiki lead describes behavior that predates the audited range; it is not itself a new change at any interval whose left endpoint is 1.8.9 or later.

## Disposition and limit

Disposition: **out of range as a first-introduction claim**. The Wiki lead does not identify any later change to bed collision, player step height, or movement onto beds. This snapshot does not claim those mechanics stayed unchanged after 1.8.9; any later exact-source boundary remains unresolved unless another source lead identifies it. No source trees were inspected for this boundary disposition, and no runtime validation was performed.
