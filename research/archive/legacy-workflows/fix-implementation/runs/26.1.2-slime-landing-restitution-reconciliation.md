## Source behavior and bounded witness

A `Entity.move(MoverType, Vec3)` calls the landing block's `updateEntityMovementAfterFallOn` after fall processing when simulated Y movement was clipped. The slime callback reads the entity's still-negative Y velocity and reflects it; for a `LivingEntity`, the multiplier is `1.0F`.

B replaces that callback with `restituteMovementAfterCollisions`. On downward collision, its low-speed gate selects zero restitution when `-currentMovement.y < getEffectiveGravity()`. The later formula writes Y as `(0.0 - currentMovement.y) * 1.0 * 0.0`, so the result is zero even when the clipped movement has nonzero Y.

The accepted source-only witness is a standing player, without Slow Falling or bounce suppression, walking from a 1/16-high carpet onto adjacent existing slime one carpet-height below. The ordinary falling step gives approximately `currentMovement.y = -0.0784`; clipping to the slime top gives `movement.y = -0.0625`. B compares `0.0784 < 0.08` and writes zero. A's callback sees the negative entity velocity and writes a positive value. This proof does **not** assume clipped Y is zero and does not claim a runtime trajectory.
