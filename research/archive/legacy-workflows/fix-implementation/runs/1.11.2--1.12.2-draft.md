Status: implementation complete and buildable; the `V1_11_2` boundary is verified against the 1.11.1 and 1.12 group-base sources and revalidated against both exact endpoints. Runtime trajectory parity remains for the TAS validation workflow.

The draft contains the three movement deltas currently supported by the 1.11.2 and 1.12.2 source comparison:

- Fall-flying uses the historical head-yaw rotation vector and reproduces the old float sine-table indexing.
- Beds do not apply the later bounce restitution when emulating the older behavior.
- Server-side pig and minecart dismount placement follows the older candidate-box and candidate-order behavior.

Each bridge is gated to `Player`, so the generic 26.2 hooks preserve vanilla behavior for non-player entities.

The exact 1.11.1 and 1.12 Feather sources confirm the transition at `V1_12`, so the old behavior belongs to the last older group, `V1_11_2`. At 1.12, fall-flying uses the inherited body-yaw look vector, `BedBlock.beforeCollision` applies `0.66F` bounce, and `dismountRider` changes the box and checks it with a `0.0` vertical offset.
