## Implementation

- The reusable client bridge is `ClientPacketListenerMixin`; its existing pre-rebuild check stays `vehicle.hasIndirectPassenger(player)`, matching the modern packet handler and retaining nested-to-direct transitions.
- During `handleSetEntityPassengersPacket`, a wrapper records only a successful `Entity#startRiding(Entity, boolean, boolean)` call where the passenger is the local player and the vehicle is the captured boat. The TAIL dispatch now requires both that event and the pre-packet indirect-membership guard. Nested riders that remain nested do not trigger the old yaw reset.
- The TAIL hook remains after the packet's passenger-list rebuild. The handler's main-thread check runs before passenger lookup/ejection; unknown-vehicle handling reaches TAIL without a captured refresh. Initial direct mounts have no pre-packet membership and continue through vanilla's native mount yaw write. The correction writes only current movement-facing `yRot`; interpolation and head-yaw fields remain excluded.
- The `V1_17_1` change stays exact-profile guarded. `ChangeResolver` selects the closest registered change whose `emulates` ordinal is at least the selected profile, and returns no changes for `CURRENT`; therefore the correction is limited to the 1.17.1 profile and native movement is unchanged.
