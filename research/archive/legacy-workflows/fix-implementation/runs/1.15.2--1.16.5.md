## Injection and registration

- `LocalPlayer.aiStep()V`: client mixin intercepts the sprint-trigger reset PUTFIELD at ordinal 1, the water descent fluid-effectability call, and the `suffocatesAt(BlockPos)Z` result. These are client-only operations.
- `Player.maybeBackOffFromEdge(Vec3,MoverType)Vec3`: common mixin dispatches the old edge algorithm and passes the protected surface predicate through a mechanical invoker.
- `LivingEntity.aiStep()V` and `travelInLava(Vec3,double,boolean,double)`: common redirects expose the fluid jump gate and shallow-lava branch decisions.
- `EntityFluidInteraction.applyCurrentTo(TagKey,Entity,double)V` and nested `EntityFluidInteraction$Tracker.applyCurrentTo(Entity,double)V`: common hooks suppress lava currents and conditionally bypass the weak-current minimum.
- `change/v1_15_2/Pre116MovementChanges` implements six independent hook interfaces. `change/v1_16/Pre1162MovementChanges` implements the two 1.16.2-boundary hooks. `MovementChanges` registers both through the existing Fabric entrypoint.
- Mixin configs are registered separately for common and client classes. Mixin bridges leave native/current, disabled, non-player, and unmatched paths on vanilla behavior.
