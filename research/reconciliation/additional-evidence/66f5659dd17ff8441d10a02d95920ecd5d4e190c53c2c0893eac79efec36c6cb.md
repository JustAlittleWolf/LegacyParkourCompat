The `LocalPlayer#isHorizontalCollisionMinor` body converts yaw to float radians, computes double sine/cosine, rotates `xxa`/`zza`, compares squared input and resolved horizontal magnitudes against `1.0E-5F`, and then returns whether the angle from `acos(dot / sqrt(product))` is below `0.13962634F`; either near-zero magnitude returns false.
