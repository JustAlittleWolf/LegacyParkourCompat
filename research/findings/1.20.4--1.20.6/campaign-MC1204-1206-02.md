# MC1204-1206-02: Ground-jump power cutoff and sprint impulse precision change

- **1.20.4**, mojmap: `1.20.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`; lines 2008-2017.
- **1.20.6**, mojmap: `1.20.6/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`; lines 2069-2082.

## Difference

For jump power `<= 1.0E-5F`, including equality, 1.20.4 writes that power into vertical velocity and marks an impulse; 1.20.6 skips the velocity write, sprint impulse and impulse flag.

For values above the cutoff, both set vertical velocity from the jump helper.

On a sprinting jump, 1.20.4's float `0.2F` multiplication and 1.20.6's double `0.2` multiplication have different precision/order.

The `Vec3` addition path resolves to the same component-wise double addition in both versions.

## Movement meaning

`LivingEntity.aiStep()` reaches the virtual ground-jump routine under the paired on-ground/fluid conditions and `noJumpDelay == 0` (1.20.4 lines 2571-2587; 1.20.6 lines 2651-2667). `Player.jumpFromGround()` delegates to the LivingEntity method (1.20.4 lines 1440-1447; 1.20.6 lines 1462-1469).

Grounded mayfly double-tap activation additionally reaches the same override in 1.20.6 at `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` line 734; 1.20.4's paired toggle lines 726-732 contain no such call (MC1204-1206-01).

Jump helper output depends on block jump factor, Jump Boost, and in 1.20.6 the generic jump-strength attribute (MC1204-1206-03).

These conditions close reachability for the described conditional differences; no specific block, effect or attribute value is assumed.

Source-proven: the threshold changes whether velocity and impulse state are written; sprinting arithmetic changes at the floating-point operation level.

Food/stat effects after the player override and non-player behavior are outside the scope of this finding.
