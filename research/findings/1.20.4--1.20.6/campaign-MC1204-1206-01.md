# MC1204-1206-01: Grounded flight activation invokes the jump routine

- **1.20.4**, mojmap: `1.20.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`; lines 726-732.
- **1.20.6**, mojmap: `1.20.6/mojmap/net/minecraft/client/player/LocalPlayer.java`; lines 729-739.

## Difference

With `mayfly`, non-always-flying mode, the second jump-key press, not swimming, and grounded state, 1.20.6 toggles flight on and additionally calls the player jump routine. 1.20.4 toggles flight on without that call.

The actual vertical impulse depends on the jump-power guard documented separately.

## Movement meaning

`KeyboardInput.tick` captures the jump key into local input (the paired input-sampling slice is byte-identical); `LocalPlayer.aiStep` consumes the rising-edge/timer path.

This finding records only the new call and its ground gate.

The downstream conditional jump-power/sprint behavior is paired in MC1204-1206-02 and the attribute input is paired in MC1204-1206-03.

The `Player` override's statistic and food-exhaustion side effects are excluded from this movement finding.

Source-proven: grounded flight activation newly enters the normal player jump call chain in 1.20.6. 1.20.4 resulting velocity change is conditional on the jump-power guard.
