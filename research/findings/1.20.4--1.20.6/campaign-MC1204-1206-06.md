# MC1204-1206-06: Player scale becomes an attribute-backed dimension input

## Difference

The default player scale remains `1.0`, preserving normal player dimensions. 1.20.6 exposes a synchronized non-default scale that changes player pose dimensions; after the attribute value changes, the living tick refreshes dimensions, eye height and bounding box. 1.20.4 has no player scale attribute.

## Movement meaning

The player dimension method feeds the bounding-box and pose-fit checks; `Entity.refreshDimensions()` updates the active box/eye height.

The 1.20.6 attribute packet receiver applies supplied values to the client attribute map, and `LivingEntity.tick()` refreshes dimensions after the attribute becomes dirty.

The exact upstream producer and same-tick effects before the tick-tail refresh are not assumed here.

Source-proven: 1.20.6 can change player dimensions and related collision/eye-height state from a syncable generic scale value; the default remains parity.

No collision result or observed movement trajectory is asserted.
