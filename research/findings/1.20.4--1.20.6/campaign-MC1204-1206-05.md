# MC1204-1206-05: Player step height becomes attribute-backed

- **1.20.4**, mojmap: `1.20.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`; lines 3423-3426.
- **1.20.6**, mojmap: `Attributes.java`; lines 3490-3493.

## Difference

For a player at the default 1.20.6 value, the step limit remains `0.6`, matching 1.20.4's constructor-set field.

When a non-default STEP_HEIGHT value is supplied and synchronized to the client, 1.20.6 uses it as the vertical limit passed into the unchanged entity step-candidate logic.

The controlling-player-passenger minimum of `1.0F` remains in both versions.

No particular collision result or trajectory is claimed.

## Movement meaning

Player inherits `LivingEntity.maxUpStep()` and the new attribute instance.

In 1.20.6, the syncable attribute update handler applies received values; `Entity.move()` calls the override while evaluating step candidates.

These paired links close the finding's input-to-consumer dependency.

Source-proven: 1.20.6 supports a syncable player step-height value that can alter the step-candidate height input, while the default remains `0.6`; 1.20.4 has no corresponding generic player attribute and sets the field to `0.6F` in the living-entity constructor.

The client receiver applies supplied values in 1.20.6.
