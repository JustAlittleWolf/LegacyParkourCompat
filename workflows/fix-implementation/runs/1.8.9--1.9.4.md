## Target verification

The canonical `26.2--unobfuscated.json` marker in the shared read-only staging root has SHA-256 `B7F4F560CB96B8264D558CE976326F6AC7017D2C244710B9394089A8746E674C` and records the original client jar SHA-256 as `40896ee9f1e2bec3c934daac7e93d41e9d3c9f2f8ae0ca366d52ffbfd1afa290`. The marker's `LocalPlayer.java` and `LivingEntity.java` source hashes were checked against the stable source tree. Target owner, descriptors, and call order were checked against the 26.2 unobfuscated client/common jars.

- `LocalPlayer.aiStep()V` calls `PlayerRideableJumping.onPlayerJump(I)V` before `sendRidingJump()`, so the mount bridge can suppress the local update while retaining the charge packet. On 26.2, the target callback also toggles stand-slide state, attempts to stand, and stores the pending jump scale; the V1_8 bridge skips that whole local callback to match the absence of the 1.8 local setter.
- `AbstractBoat.controlBoat()V` uses `0.005F` for left/right-only propulsion, `0.04F` for forward thrust, and `0.005F` for reverse thrust. The side-only constant ordinal and thrust ordinals were checked from target bytecode.
- `Player.aiStep()V` resets fall distance before its movement call. Suppressing that reset matches 1.8, which does not reset `fallDistance` in `PlayerEntity.moveRelative`.
- `FoodData.tick(ServerPlayer)V` has a fast-regen `isHurt` call followed by the slow-regen branch and its exhaustion call. Only those exact operation ordinals are redirected.
- The chunk and local-input call owners/descriptors are recorded in the previous checkpoint and were compile-verified after bridge edits.
