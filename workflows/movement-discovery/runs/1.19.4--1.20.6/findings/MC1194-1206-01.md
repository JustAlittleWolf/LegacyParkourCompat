## Source-level difference

Under the shared preconditions `mayfly == true`, `isAlwaysFlying() == false`, the jump key rising edge entering the second-press branch (`jumpTriggerTime != 0`), and `isSwimming() == false`, both versions toggle flight on. If the player is grounded, only B additionally invokes the player jump routine. When jump power exceeds its helper threshold, that routine writes positive vertical velocity and sets the impulse flag. The B `Player` override also awards the normal jump statistic after the superclass call.
