## Correspondence and call order

- Input implementation: A `KeyboardInput extends client.player.Input`; B `KeyboardInput extends client.player.ClientInput`, which stores a `world.entity.player.Input` record. The physical keyboard reads are paired in `KeyboardInput.tick`; A produces directional impulses while B produces a key record and normalized vector. The B `LocalPlayer.applyInput()` / `modifyInput()` and A input-scaling plus `LivingEntity.aiStep()` friction form the relevant consumer path; the complete numerical closure remains in progress.
- Local player chain: both use `LocalPlayer -> AbstractClientPlayer -> Player -> LivingEntity -> Entity`. Both local-player input paths feed `xxa`, `yya`, `zza`, and `jumping` before jump and travel. B changes the dispatch hook from A's `serverAiStep()` to `applyInput()` for client input; it still proceeds through living-entity jump and travel processing.
- Sprint controller: paired at A `LocalPlayer.aiStep()` and B `LocalPlayer.aiStep()`. Both decrement `sprintTriggerTime` before the sprint-start decision. A assigns literal `7`; B reads `Options.sprintWindow()` (default 7, range 0–10). Eligibility gates, effects, item-use components and sprinting consequences have dependencies still open; see coverage/dependency sections and F-1.
- No inference is made from class names alone. Exact source member anchors and hashes appear in [navigation-1.21.11.md](navigation-1.21.11.md) and findings.
