## Source-level difference

The player exhaustion amounts associated with jumping and movement-distance categories differ. Preserve the distinct swim, sprint, sneak and ordinary movement cases; this finding groups the related stat method but does not claim identical branch conditions beyond the listed expressions.

## Reachability and dependencies

Player movement/jump stats -> `addFatigue(float)` -> HungerManager.addExhaustion. Both `PlayerEntity.addFatigue` implementations only mutate hunger when `!world.isClient`; HungerManager consumes exhaustion above 4.0F. LocalClientPlayerEntity sprint gating reads synchronized food level (`foodLevel > 6.0F` in inspected path); ClientPlayNetworkHandler applies UpdateHealth food values. Thus this is a server-authoritative movement-related state input, not a claim of client-side exhaustion simulation.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Different exhaustion accumulation can change later hunger depletion and therefore the local sprint gate after server updates. Exact timing depends on server tick, food state and synchronization; no gameplay timing was measured. First changed release remains unknown within the pair.
