## One bounded, source-produced movement witness

Use a Player in fall-flying pose, with an intact Elytra, no sneak input, no water, and no ordinary collision shapes along the path. The only non-air blocks in the path are a cobweb at `(1, 19, 0)` and a stone support at `(2, 18, 0)`. Face `+X` and pitch up by 10 degrees (Minecraft pitch `-10°`). Start the ten-step source sequence at approximately `(-12.3309, 16.6581, 0.50)` with zero horizontal and vertical velocity. Nine source-produced boost/travel steps place the player at approximately `(0.69, 19.10, 0.50)` before the qualifying tenth step. Reversing the first nine source-equation displacement sums gives that start; the ninth endpoint box is `x=[0.39,0.99]`, `y=[19.10,19.70]`, `z=[0.20,0.80]` and remains clear of the cobweb cell.

The movement vector is generated by the 1.18.2 firework/Elytra source path, not supplied as an arbitrary `Entity.move` vector:

1. `FireworkRocketItem.use(Level, Player, InteractionHand)` creates the attached `FireworkRocketEntity` for a fall-flying Player and adds it to the level. `FireworkRocketEntity(Level, ItemStack, LivingEntity)` stores that Player as the attached entity. On each attached-rocket tick for which `isFallFlying()` is true, `FireworkRocketEntity.tick()` adds `look*0.1 + (look*1.5 - deltaMovement)*0.5` to that entity's velocity. The rocket's minimum lifetime is ten ticks, so ten boost applications are within the source-defined lifetime.
2. `LivingEntity.travel(Vec3)`'s fall-flying branch computes the glide vector from current velocity/look, applies the source's vertical and pitch-lift corrections plus `0.99F` horizontal damping, and calls `move(MoverType.SELF, getDeltaMovement())`. Applying those exact source equations at pitch `-10°` with zero initial velocity gives a ninth cumulative position near `(0.69,19.10,0.50)` and a tenth movement near `(1.6195,0.3115,0)`. The first nine displacement sums are approximately `(13.0209,2.4419,0)`, which is why the initial point is chosen as `(-12.3309,16.6581,0.50)`. The branch assigns `fallDistance = 1.0F` before every move because its computed vertical velocity is greater than `-0.5`; the tenth move's Y is positive. Thus the final qualifying call is a `SELF` move with nonzero fall distance and resolved length squared greater than `1.0`. No sneak input bypasses Player's ground-surface edge-backoff gate; airborne state also keeps the grounded-only fall/landing branch false.

## Writers after the F-005 reset and later Player consumer

In this final move, the source order is: F-005 clip/reset; `setPos`; collision/on-ground bookkeeping; `checkFallDamage`; then `tryCheckInsideBlocks()` and its final-box callbacks. For the stated upward resolved Y and `onGround == false`, `Entity.checkFallDamage` takes neither branch that changes `fallDistance`: it resets only for grounded movement and accumulates only for negative resolved Y. The final-box callback enumeration excludes the cobweb; the other intersected cells are air. Therefore no later writer in this move changes the zero established by F-005. This conclusion follows from each later writer's gate and the final box, not from F-005's write alone.

For the subsequent consumer, remove the Elytra and hold sneak before the next Player travel/move. `LivingEntity.updateFallFlying()` clears the fall-flying flag when the chest item is no longer an enabled Elytra. The next ordinary travel path has no fall-flying assignment to `fallDistance`; its source-derived positive vertical velocity (about `0.23` before gravity) remains positive after gravity for that next movement, so `checkFallDamage` again sees positive Y and leaves the field alone. At its following `Player.maybeBackOffFromEdge` call, Player is airborne, uses `MoverType.SELF`, and is sneaking, so it evaluates `isAboveGround()` before edge back-off. The predicate first compares `fallDistance < maxUpStep` (default step is `0.6`); the reset path has `0.0 < 0.6`. Its downward probe moves the current box by `-0.6` and intersects stone at `(2,18,0)`: the endpoint feet are about `19.4115`, above the block top at `19`, and the box overlaps the block in X/Z. `noCollision` is therefore false and `isAboveGround()` returns true. The comparison path without F-005 retains the Elytra branch's `1.0F`; `1.0 < 0.6` is false, so it returns false before the probe. This identifies a later direct movement predicate whose result differs because of the F-005 reset, with the web callback and subsequent fall-distance writers closed.

The witness ends at the edge predicate. It does not invoke fall/landing production: the moves are airborne and have nonnegative Y; the stone is only in the later downward probe, not in the player box. No health or damage response is asserted.
