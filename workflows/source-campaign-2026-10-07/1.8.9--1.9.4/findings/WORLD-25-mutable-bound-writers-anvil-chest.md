Chest's method checks neighboring blocks in north, south, west, east precedence, then writes the matching single/double chest bounds to the singleton block. The constructor sets Chest's single-block defaults; Anvil's constructor leaves the inherited default bounds. Neither generic A `Block.addCollisions` nor A `World.getCollisions` invokes either updater: World dispatches each intersecting block to `addCollisions`, which turns the current singleton bounds into a world-space box.
