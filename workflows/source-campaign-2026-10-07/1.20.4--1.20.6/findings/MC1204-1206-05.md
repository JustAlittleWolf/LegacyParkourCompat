## Source-level difference

For a regular player at the default B value, the step limit remains `0.6`, matching A's inherited field. A non-default synchronized B step-height value changes the vertical range used by the unchanged entity step-candidate logic. The controlling-passenger minimum of `1.0F` remains in both versions.

## Reachability and dependencies

Player inherits `LivingEntity.maxUpStep()` and the new inherited attribute. `Entity.move()` calls the override while evaluating step candidates. This finding covers the step-height input to that path; full candidate shape/collision behavior remains an open collision-slice dependency.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: B allows a syncable player step-height value to alter step-candidate height, while default behavior retains `0.6`. Collision outcomes and trajectories are not runtime-tested. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
