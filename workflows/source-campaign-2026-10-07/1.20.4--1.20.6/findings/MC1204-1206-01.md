- Newer version B: 1.20.6
- Mechanic / coverage slice IDs: S1-flight-tick, S2-flight-abilities, S3-ground-jump

- Confidence: source-confirmed
- Applicability: player with flight ability enabled; activates flight from the ground through the jump-key double-tap path
- First changed release: unknown within (1.20.4, 1.20.6]

## Source-level difference

With `mayfly`, non-always-flying mode, the second jump-key press, not swimming, and grounded state, B toggles flight on and additionally calls the player jump routine. A toggles flight on without that call. The actual vertical impulse depends on the jump-power guard documented separately.

## Reachability and dependencies

`KeyboardInput.tick` captures the jump key into local input; `LocalPlayer.aiStep` consumes the rising-edge/timer path. This finding records only the new call and its ground gate. Jump power, sprint impulse and its threshold are separate dependencies. The `Player` override's statistic and food-exhaustion side effects are excluded from this movement finding.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: grounded flight activation newly enters the normal player jump call chain in B. A resulting velocity change is conditional on the jump-power guard. No trajectory or persistence through later flight movement is asserted. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
