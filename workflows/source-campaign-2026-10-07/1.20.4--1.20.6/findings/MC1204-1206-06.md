## Source-level difference

The default player scale remains `1.0`, preserving normal player dimensions. B exposes a synchronized non-default scale that changes player pose dimensions; after the attribute value changes, the living tick refreshes dimensions, eye height and bounding box. A has no player scale attribute.

## Reachability and dependencies

The player dimension method feeds the bounding-box and pose-fit checks; `Entity.refreshDimensions()` updates the active box/eye height. Attribute values are external synchronized inputs. The exact producer and timing of a non-default value are not assumed here; same-tick effects before the tick-tail refresh are not claimed.
