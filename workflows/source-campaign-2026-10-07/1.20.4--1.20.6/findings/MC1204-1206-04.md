# MC1204-1206-04: Player gravity is attribute-backed and Slow Falling is bounded by it

- Older version A: 1.20.4
- Newer version B: 1.20.6
- Mechanic / coverage slice IDs: S3-gravity-drag, S6-attributes, S6-server-boundary
- Classification: changed behavior
- Confidence: source-confirmed
- Applicability: player travel with a non-default gravity attribute; Slow Falling branch also differs when gravity is below its `0.01` cap and vertical velocity is non-positive
- First changed release: unknown within (1.20.4, 1.20.6]
- Runtime validation: not performed

## Paired evidence

- A source: `../../../../build/movement-campaign-2026-10-07/ready/1.20.4/mojmap/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java`, `LivingEntity.travel(Vec3)`, lines 2035-2166, SHA-256 `f7bc53db24c1798f19f9bd6f6356c86d5e560e9c8e8aac60decaf15cc785e07d`. The base gravity local is `0.08`; when delta Y is non-positive and Slow Falling is active, A replaces it with `0.01`.
- B source: corresponding `LivingEntity.travel(Vec3)`, lines 2104-2233, SHA-256 `c66ec8dc3b1856e490e5834a46185589030d9fbc411e3e2ce64c73203cd753b2`. B reads `getGravity()`; in the same descending/Slow Falling condition it applies `Math.min(gravity, 0.01)`.
- A fluid helper: `LivingEntity.getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement(double, boolean, Vec3)`, lines 2215-2231 in the same A file/hash. Its gravity adjustment is gated by `!isNoGravity() && !isSprinting()` and subtracts `gravity / 16.0` from vertical motion, with the existing `-0.003` near-terminal-speed clamp.
- B fluid helper: corresponding method, lines 2282-2298, same B file/hash. Its gate is `gravity != 0.0 && !isSprinting()`; the adjustment formula and clamp are otherwise the same. `travel` reaches this helper in both water and lava branches (A lines 2074, 2085; B lines 2143, 2154).
- B gravity source: `LivingEntity.getDefaultGravity()` lines 2100-2102 returns `getAttributeValue(Attributes.GRAVITY)`; `Entity.getGravity()` lines 1101-1107 returns zero when `isNoGravity()` and otherwise delegates to `getDefaultGravity()`. `LivingEntity.createLivingAttributes()` includes gravity at lines 292-305, and `Player.createAttributes()` inherits that builder. B `Attributes.GRAVITY` is syncable, default `0.08`, range -1 to 1 (`Attributes.java` lines 42-44; SHA-256 `7e30d87c7a58b14d0052d2f9f7319d997b49ae7d025579cd762d28e845b2e82e`). The client applies incoming attribute snapshots to the matching living entity by setting the base value and replacing modifiers (`ClientPacketListener.handleUpdateAttributes`, lines 2059-2082; SHA-256 `e121e998ec25211aaecf91fffd0ea699cebd47eddea9134efd3f2ffbef8eb7cd`). A has no generic gravity attribute in `Attributes.java`; A's `travel` literal is `0.08`.

## Source-level difference

At default gravity `0.08` and descending with Slow Falling, B's `Math.min(0.08, 0.01)` equals A's `0.01`. B instead accepts a non-default gravity value throughout player travel. When descending with Slow Falling and supplied gravity below `0.01`, B uses that lower value; A still uses `0.01`. The fluid helper uses the same effective gravity local and its zero check is equivalent to A's no-gravity gate at default gravity/no-gravity settings, but differs when a player has a non-default gravity value (including zero while `isNoGravity()` is false). In normal land travel, B subtracts the effective gravity local in the available-chunk/server branch; A subtracts its fixed local only under `!isNoGravity()`. In water and lava, A gates the quarter-gravity adjustment on `!isNoGravity()` while B gates it on effective gravity being nonzero. The B `FlyingAnimal` vertical-drag alternative is excluded because non-player movement is outside this source campaign's scope.

## Reachability and dependencies

`Player.createAttributes()` inherits `LivingEntity.createLivingAttributes()` on B. Local player travel delegates into `LivingEntity.travel`; the same travel body routes fluid-falling adjustment through `getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement`, and the water/lava branches apply their own gravity adjustment. The 1.20.6 client attribute receiver installs synchronized base values and modifiers. This establishes player-side reachability and the incoming-value consumer; it does not identify a particular server producer or assume a particular configured value.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: the default base gravity remains `0.08`, while non-default synced gravity affects ordinary, fluid-falling, water and lava travel; under Slow Falling B caps effective gravity above by `0.01`, rather than replacing any descending gravity with `0.01`. The bounded method/helper branches and player client receiver are closed for this finding. Other gravity consumers, server-side provenance, full S3-gravity-drag closure and any measured trajectory remain outside this finding. First introduction is unknown within the endpoint interval.
