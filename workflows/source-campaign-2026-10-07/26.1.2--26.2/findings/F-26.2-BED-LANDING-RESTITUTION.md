## Source-level difference

For a local player landing downward on an ordinary bed, with bounce unsuppressed and downward speed at least the effective-gravity threshold, A's bed callback uses the living-entity factor `1.0` and writes positive Y velocity equal to `-currentMovement.y * 0.66F`. B's generic collision restitution uses the bed's registered `0.75F`; with clipped vertical movement zero, the formula writes `-currentMovement.y * 0.75`. These are different vertical responses for the same qualifying input. B also writes zero for bed landings below the effective-gravity threshold, while A's bed callback bounces for any negative Y velocity; the separate slime finding already records the bounded low-speed slime case.

This finding assumes the ordinary local-player BOUNCINESS default (`0.0`) or a value no greater than the bed restitution; the synced attribute, if externally modified above `0.75`, raises B's restitution through the stated `max` formula. Crouch/shift suppression and B's explicit suppression tag bypass the bed bounce condition.

## Reachability and dependencies

The client `LocalPlayer` reaches `Player`/`Avatar`/`LivingEntity` movement and its `Entity.move` collision handling. `Player.canSimulateMovement()` returns true for the local client player. A bed support is selected as the effect block for the clipped landing. The rewritten state is player Y velocity, consumed by subsequent local travel/ticks. B's new BOUNCINESS attribute is part of living attributes and syncable; its base default is zero. The paired bed registration is source evidence for the `0.75F` block value. No damage calculation is emulated or compared; A's bed `fallOn` damage multiplier is outside this finding.

## Consequence and uncertainty

The paired sources establish a different player Y-velocity write for the stated bed-landing inputs. No trajectory or gameplay reproduction was run. The source pair establishes the change somewhere between these endpoints only; its first release is unknown within the interval. Other block and entity restitution paths remain open under `S-MOVE-RESTITUTE`.
