# Candidate: Nether portal callback uses a broader entity-inside shape

- Finding ID: `F-NETHER-PORTAL-INSIDE-SHAPE-1.21.5-1.21.8`
- Status: candidate; exact block-inside shape difference and native Player portal callback path are verified; provider/callback closure and independent source review remain pending.
- Slice routes: S4.4, S5.2, S7.3; inventories INV-COLLISION, INV-WORLD-MOVEMENT, INV-STATE, INV-EXTERNAL.
- A evidence: `NetherPortalBlock#getShape` returns the axis-specific thin portal shape at lines 61-64, and its `getEntityInsideCollisionShape` override returns that shape at lines 66-69 (`NetherPortalBlock.java`, SHA-256 `1cb1b0a796f491deb2317d8a2706442741a627582ca86e3cc7adb6345f975d1b`). The inherited default in `BlockBehaviour#getEntityInsideCollisionShape` is `Shapes.block()` at lines 314-316 (shared A/B SHA-256 `15418b0026919ef5ab97780a6ce8844677dc82d36cc4433a58f379b837d5cc50`). `Entity#checkInsideBlocks` invokes `entityInside` only if the shape is the sentinel `Shapes.block()` or the movement path intersects the shape (A `Entity.java:1092-1098`, SHA-256 `fc177733e9cc4b2d5cf2562e5529d0f4e0b9c3690f081fbd58aa9119eb173dc4`).
- B evidence: `NetherPortalBlock` no longer overrides `getEntityInsideCollisionShape`; its source SHA-256 is `883beec01685148971b2b284be2267b5a6a1f258c49e2da4dd28fb4155d49967`. It therefore inherits the unchanged full-block sentinel default. B `Entity#checkInsideBlocks` retains the same sentinel-or-path-intersection test and calls the callback (`Entity.java:1155-1161`, SHA-256 `c403e6176d27b5bfd6aaa0dea3735ffa4fcf80dbae58766661dd84453b7e9704`).
- Reachable Player callback: `NetherPortalBlock#entityInside` is identical A/B and calls `Entity#setAsInsidePortal` when `canUsePortal(false)` succeeds (A lines 107-110; B lines 102-105). The unchanged `Entity#setAsInsidePortal` stores or updates `PortalProcessor` unless the entity is on portal cooldown (A lines 2210-2221; B lines 2378-2389). `Entity#handlePortal` later processes it and applies the returned `TeleportTransition` (A lines 2223-2244; B lines 2391-2413). These methods are on the direct player path; portals and player dimension transitions predate both releases.
- Exact precondition: a movement callback scan reaches a NetherPortal block while the player is eligible for portal use and not on portal cooldown; the player's movement path intersects the block volume but misses the axis-specific thin portal shape. A then does not call the portal callback for that block; B's inherited `Shapes.block()` sentinel causes the callback to run and can start or refresh `PortalProcessor`, which can lead to a portal transition.
- Analysis: the default full-block sentinel intentionally bypasses shape intersection in `Entity#checkInsideBlocks`, while the removed override constrained the 1.21.5 callback to the portal plane. This changes when direct player movement starts portal processing and may change later position/dimension movement. The candidate does not claim that ordinary collision shapes or travel math changed. Candidate remains pending the complete provider/callback and direct player transition inventory plus blind review.
