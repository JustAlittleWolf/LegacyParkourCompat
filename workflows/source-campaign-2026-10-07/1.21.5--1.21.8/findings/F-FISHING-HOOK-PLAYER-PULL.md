- Player reachability: `FishingHook#canHitEntity()` delegates to `Projectile#canHitEntity()` for non-item entities; an eligible non-owner Player can be hooked. Server `onHitEntity()` stores that entity in `hookedIn` (A lines 266-270; B lines 277-281) while the current state remains `FLYING`. This callback runs from `checkCollision()` before the non-water gravity branch in the same `tick()`. A therefore subtracts gravity and moves the hook after capture; B sees `hookedIn != null`, skips gravity, and moves it with its prior velocity. On a later tick the hook first changes to `HOOKED_IN_ENTITY` and returns; a subsequent tick places it on the hooked entity. Rod use invokes `retrieve()` (A lines 442-448; B lines 453-459), which calls `pullEntity(hookedIn)`; the unchanged `pullEntity()` adds `0.1 * (owner position - hook position)` to the target's delta movement (A lines 499-505; B lines 510-516; body SHA-256 `55cc5f961d8b18d603fb6ad74cb0888e6d0d08ee617d7f3858390a22c50f3f06`).
- Exact candidate precondition: a server-side, non-water FishingHook hits an eligible Player while in `FLYING`, and its owner reels during the short interval before a later hook tick places the hook at the captured Player. The A/B hook positions differ because A's post-hit gravity-and-move phase runs while B skips gravity after `hookedIn` is set; `retrieve()` then supplies the differing hook position to the unchanged Player delta-movement writer. If the owner waits until after the hook has been positioned on the Player, this candidate path converges. It does not claim `pullEntity()` or Player travel math changed.
- Analysis: this is a post-hit position delta from the same changed gravity guard, not a changed hit-registration rule. The direct authoritative Player pull formula is unchanged, but its hook-position input can differ in the bounded pre-attachment reel window. Reachable timing and materiality remain pending source review, so this stays a candidate.
