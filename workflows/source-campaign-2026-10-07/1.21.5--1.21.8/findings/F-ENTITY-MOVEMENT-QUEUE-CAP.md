# Candidate: bounded player movement history can change block-contact path

- Finding ID: `F-ENTITY-MOVEMENT-QUEUE-CAP-1.21.5-1.21.8`
- Status: candidate; source pair and threshold are verified, independent source review and full provider-impact closure are pending.
- Slice routes: S4.4, S5.2, S7.2; inventories INV-COLLISION, INV-WORLD-MOVEMENT, INV-EXTERNAL.

- Player reachability: A/B `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl#handleMovePlayer` calls `player.move(MoverType.PLAYER, ...)` for accepted, non-passenger position packets (A lines 958-1028; B lines 977-1047). Its per-tick packet counter logs after five packets and changes the movement-check multiplier, but does not reject solely for packet frequency (A lines 995-1004; B lines 1015-1024). The next player tick drains recorded samples through inherited `LivingEntity#aiStep`/`applyEffectsFromBlocks` (A `LivingEntity.java:2765-2770`; B `LivingEntity.java:2817-2822`). By contrast, piston movement immediately calls `applyEffectsFromBlocks(from,to)` and removes its own recorded sample, so it is a Player movement path but does not accumulate toward this cap; A/B call sequence is unchanged (A `PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:186-192`, SHA-256 `bd7bcc95fb811b6ed4e50fe0679fcc72e86cbf2445ef72c35ed8e25c80da361e`; B `PistonMovingBlockEntity.java:185-191`, SHA-256 `5965f9f6adb4fe10222208a411a8cde72dd6e1821de06416330e17203db0e2fd`).
- Concrete Player movement consumer: A/B `BlockBehaviour#getEntityInsideCollisionShape()` defaults to `Shapes.block()`, so a replay segment crossing a WebBlock invokes its unchanged `WebBlock#entityInside()` (A/B source SHA-256 `ba24400cf87b00c88c5bd441c781a128b6973a46bff3df62c6144e9bfc5abb43`). That callback calls `Entity#makeStuckInBlock()` with `(0.25, 0.05, 0.25)`; the unchanged method stores `stuckSpeedMultiplier` and resets fall distance. On the next `Entity#move`, the stored multiplier scales the movement delta and is then cleared with delta movement zeroed (A lines 647-653; B lines 674-680). Thus an omitted or newly introduced WebBlock contact in the merged replay can change a later Player movement step through a concrete, unchanged movement consumer.
- Concrete reachability: A/B `Pig#registerGoals()` includes `WaterAvoidingRandomStrollGoal` (A lines 77-78; B lines 78-79), while `Pig#getControllingPassenger()` returns its first Player only when the pig is saddled and that Player holds a carrot on a stick (A lines 87-90; B lines 88-91; whole-source SHA-256 `55eced2d4b51adf8d301e020e6c3b1bba75b05df0a964dfb7e9d28f3a3c7d801` / `718df89ae3dabd249fc815d69d34e992b9712ae89db0c08027c74181a4fc4a24`). If the player remains aboard after no longer meeting that steering condition, the pig is non-local-authoritative on the client while `hasIndirectPassenger(localPlayer)` is true. A/B `ServerEntity` produces and broadcasts `ClientboundEntityPositionSyncPacket` when `teleportDelay > 400` (A lines 149-153; B lines 151-159, then broadcast at 194-195); whole-source hashes are `7bfafc72617c21d3618eeba9f58389d0b653f641d6986a3bc9ff608902b7b4c0` / `2406502b40ff09f5796c93fbcfeef72cd4e7bc9a695481c8e070125c3f860a47`. This provides a vanilla Player-passenger route to the changed client guard; it does not establish how often the state difference changes visible or later movement, and callback-provider/scope review remains pending.
- Exact precondition: at least 101 accepted non-passenger player movement packets invoke `Entity.move` before that player's block-effect drain, and the first two queued samples' axis-ordered paths differ from the straight segment joining the first sample's start to the second sample's end. If that path difference intersects a non-air block or fluid shape whose inside callback changes player movement state, the callback set/order may differ. The source proves a native packet path to the threshold, but does not establish that ordinary client ticks generate that packet burst.
- Analysis: absent the cap, the B replay reconstructs each individual movement's axis order, matching A's recorded sequence. At the threshold B collapses history, so its later block/fluid contact scan can omit or add crossed candidates relative to A. This is a server-authoritative player-contact path difference, not a claim that the client's collision displacement, knockback calculation, combat damage, or vehicle physics changed. Marked candidate until the complete A/B callback-provider inventory and blind source review establish impact and disposition.
