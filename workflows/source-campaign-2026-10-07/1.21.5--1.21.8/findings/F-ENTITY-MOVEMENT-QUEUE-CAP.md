- Exact precondition: at least 101 accepted non-passenger player movement packets invoke `Entity.move` before that player's block-effect drain, and the first two queued samples' axis-ordered paths differ from the straight segment joining the first sample's start to the second sample's end. If that path difference intersects a non-air block or fluid shape whose inside callback changes player movement state, the callback set/order may differ. The source proves a native packet path to the threshold, but does not establish that ordinary client ticks generate that packet burst.
- Analysis: absent the cap, the B replay reconstructs each individual movement's axis order, matching A's recorded sequence. At the threshold B collapses history, so its later block/fluid contact scan can omit or add crossed candidates relative to A. This is a server-authoritative player-contact path difference, not a claim that the client's collision displacement, knockback calculation, combat damage, or vehicle physics changed. Marked candidate until the complete A/B callback-provider inventory and blind source review establish impact and disposition.
