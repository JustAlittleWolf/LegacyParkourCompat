## Source-level difference

For a grounded landing with positive accumulated fall distance on a state in `OCCLUDES_VIBRATION_SIGNALS`, A calls the block's `fallOn()` but does not dispatch `HIT_GROUND`; B calls `fallOn()` and dispatches the event with explicit entity, position and current-foot-block context. On other landing states, A also dispatches the event, but through `gameEvent(GameEvent.HIT_GROUND)` without the explicit context arguments shown in B. The collision caller and `checkFallDamage()` branch preserve the same grounded/fall-distance guards and fall-distance reset order.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: B dispatches a `HIT_GROUND` event for grounded landings with positive fall distance even when the support state is tagged to occlude vibration signals; A suppresses that event for those states. The dispatch itself does not write player position, velocity or pose. A later event listener could change world state, but no such response or movement consequence is claimed here.
