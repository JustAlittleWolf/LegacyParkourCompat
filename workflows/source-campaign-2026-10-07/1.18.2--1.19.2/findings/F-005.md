## Source-level difference

The fall-distance and grounded guards are the same at the checked method boundary. A sends `HIT_GROUND` only if the block state passed to `checkFallDamage()` does not occlude vibration signals. B removes that condition and always dispatches for a grounded landing with positive `fallDistance`. A routes the event through the entity wrapper at its block position; B supplies the entity, exact entity position, and current feet block state in an explicit context.

## Reachability and dependencies

Player collision-resolved movement -> `Entity.move()` -> `checkFallDamage()` -> block `fallOn()` -> `HIT_GROUND` dispatch. A fall with positive accumulated fall distance landing on a state tagged `OCCLUDES_VIBRATION_SIGNALS` reaches the changed gate. `HIT_GROUND` is vibration-tagged and mapped by Sculk Sensors in both endpoint sources, but the listener's line-of-sight and event-context checks, external tag replacement, and resulting redstone/world state are separate downstream conditions.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source-proven: B dispatches a player `HIT_GROUND` event in the above landing case where A suppresses it, and changes event position/context. Neither checked branch directly writes player position or velocity. A listener may respond differently and change world state; no listener trajectory or later movement consequence was simulated. Runtime validation was not performed.
