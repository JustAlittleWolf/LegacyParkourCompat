# F-002: ascending player movement bypasses crouch edge restraint in 1.19.2

- Status: source-confirmed
- Scope: player edge restraint before entity collision; 1.18.2 is A and 1.19.2 is B.
- Preconditions / reachability: a non-flying player makes a `SELF` or `PLAYER` move, is staying on a ground surface (the Player predicate is shift-key-down), is above ground, and the requested movement has positive Y while horizontal motion extends beyond supported ground.
- A behavior: `Player.maybeBackOffFromEdge(Vec3,MoverType)` applies horizontal X/Z backoff in 0.05 increments under the above guards, preserving the requested Y component. The guard has no condition on requested Y.
- B behavior: the same guard adds `requestedDelta.y <= 0.0`. Positive-Y movement bypasses the backoff loops, leaving X/Z unchanged for the subsequent collision resolver.
- Call order / state path: `Entity.move()` applies stuck multipliers, calls `maybeBackOffFromEdge()`, then calls `collide()` with the resulting vector. Thus the added Y guard changes the vector entering collision resolution for reachable ascending self/player moves.
