## Source-level difference

For an exact current whose scanned overlap is shallow, A scales the one current by overlap measured from the deflated box minimum, averages it, and reaches the Player minimum-impulse branch. B scales the current by tracker height measured from the entity's un-deflated minimum, then rejects the accumulated vector by squared length before averaging or applying the minimum impulse.

The conditions are reachable with one flowing-water cell at `(100,64,100)` in LEVEL 1, its east neighbor at `(101,64,100)` in LEVEL 2, and a supported, side-walled horizontal channel extending east through LEVEL 7 to a water source at `(107,64,100)`. The water update path produces each flowing level by subtracting the water drop-off of 1 from the higher neighbor amount. The target's north, south and west neighbors are solid channel walls, so their empty fluid states contribute no fallback flow; the east LEVEL 2 neighbor contributes `1/9F - 2/9F = -1/9F` in X. The final `getFlow()` is therefore `(-1, 0, 0)`. This uses actual vanilla fluid states and the paired vanilla flow calculation, rather than an arbitrary input vector.

Place a standing, unmounted Player at X/Z `(100.5,100.5)` with its minimum Y `δ = fluidTop - playerY = 0.002` above the LEVEL 1 cell, where `fluidTop = 64 + (1.0F/9.0F)`. Its `0.6F × 1.8F` box, deflated by `0.001`, scans only X/Z cell 100 and Y cells 64-65; only Y=64 contains fluid. The source cell at X=107 and level-2 neighbor at X=101 remain outside this scan. The Player is not flying, does not ride a vehicle, and has zero horizontal delta movement.

In A the water overlap measured from the deflated box is approximately `δ - 0.001 = 0.001`. The source expression `int blockY + float fluidHeight` is evaluated as float before assignment to its double temporary, while B's `double fluidBottom + float fluidHeight` is evaluated as double. For the chosen height this makes A's fluid top about `3.39E-6` higher than B's; A's overlap is consequently about `0.00100339`, which remains inside the same strict-guard interval. The one current therefore becomes approximately `(-0.00100339,0,0)`; after water strength `0.014`, the impulse length is approximately `0.0000140475`, which is not below Vec3's strict `< 1.0E-5F` normalization floor. With old X/Z movement zero, it is below the strict minimum-impulse threshold and normalizes to the `0.0045000000000000005` horizontal impulse before being added to delta movement.

In B tracker height is approximately `δ = 0.002`, so the one accumulated current is `(-0.002,0,0)` with squared length approximately `4.0E-6`. The strict `< 1.0E-5F` cutoff returns before averaging, strength scaling, minimum-impulse logic, or `addDeltaMovement`. Both versions' player fluid gates match (`!abilities.flying`); the difference comes from the changed shallow-current scaling/cutoff order and reference height.

The load preconditions are concrete: choose coordinates 100/101 in one fully loaded chunk and keep the one-block scan border in that chunk. A's `touchingUnloadedChunk()` then passes for the inflated box. B's `hasFluidAndLoaded()` sees that loaded FULL chunk and a section containing water across its expanded X/Z range. The water tag is present in both exact client resources. Level-1 current tracking is reached because B excludes a fluid top only when it is strictly below `box.minY`; here the top is about `0.001` above that minimum. Its positive tracker height also makes `isInFluid(WATER)` true, reaching the current application call.

## Reachability and dependencies

The per-tick path is `Player.tick()` / inherited `Entity.tick()` -> A `updateInWaterStateAndDoFluidPushing()` -> water helper -> `updateFluidHeightAndDoFluidPushing(WATER, 0.014)`, versus B `Entity.tick()` -> `updateFluidInteraction()` -> `EntityFluidInteraction.update()` -> `applyCurrentTo(WATER, this, 0.014)` -> tracker cutoff. Both exact Player overrides permit the path when not flying. Fluid LEVEL states are constructed through the same `FlowingFluid.getFlowing(amount, false)` / state-definition mechanism; the horizontal source-to-level chain is rooted in the exact WaterFluid amount and drop-off methods. The verified player dimensions and box scans give one counted state, while the adjacent level-2 state supplies its nonzero current.

The caller and relevant state writes are closed for this case: B reports in-water from positive tracker height, both paths write only the player's delta movement for the current response, A also updates its per-tag fluid-height map, and B updates per-tag height/eye trackers. Player horizontal velocity cutoff reads X/Z only; the vanilla movement producer may supply zero X/Z while the player crosses the fluid surface. No health/food/combat or non-player producer is needed. Exact loaded-chunk/section guards and the water resource tag are included above.

## Consequence and uncertainty

Source proves a reachable conditional player delta-velocity difference: A adds a nonzero minimum horizontal impulse in the stated shallow-overlap state, while B does not add the water current. No trajectory was run or inferred. The A/B pair does not establish the first release that introduced the cutoff. The wider S4.6 inventory remains open for lava, general fluid contact/eye consumers, mounted-player box reachability and the separate resources/dependencies those paths read. This exact non-mounted Player WATER branch is source-evidenced; its immutable snapshot awaits a separately assigned blind reviewer.
