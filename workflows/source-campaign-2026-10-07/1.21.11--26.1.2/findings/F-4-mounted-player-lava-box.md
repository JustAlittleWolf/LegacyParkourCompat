## Source-level difference

In A, the mounted non-underwater boat guard skips only WATER-current scanning. The separate LAVA scan still receives the complete deflated Player box. In B, the boat clips the passenger interaction box's minimum Y to the boat's `maxY` and the shared WATER/LAVA tracker discards any lava cell whose top is strictly below that clipped minimum. This can remove an otherwise overlapping lava cell from the Player's LAVA height/current input before Player current application.

## Reachability and dependencies

Use a fixed source state in a vanilla Nether, without simulating vehicle motion. Place a normal `OAK_BOAT` at `(100.5D, 65.5D, 100.5D)` with yaw `0`, no passengers initially, and the vanilla STONE floor at Y=64. The boat has dimensions `1.375F x 0.5625F`, so its box is `[99.8125,65.5,99.8125]` to `[101.1875,66.0625,101.1875]`. There is no WATER. The paired boat status scan therefore does not return a water status; no collision lies in the boat's thin ground-friction probe below Y=65.5, so `getStatus()` yields `IN_AIR` and `isUnderWater()` is false. The existing paired `AbstractBoat.interact()` path can mount a non-flying Player; the boat has no passengers and the Player width `0.6F` is below the boat width `1.375F`.

For this single-passenger, yaw-zero boat, `rideHeight = 0.5625F / 3.0F = 0.1875F`, the boat-side Z offset is zero, and the Player vehicle attachment is `(0,0.6,0)`. Applying the paired `positionRider()` formula to the fixed boat position yields Player position `(100.5D, 65.5D + 0.1875D - 0.6D, 100.5D)`, approximately `(100.5, 65.0875, 100.5)`. The Player is standing: it is a passenger, is not sleeping/swimming/fall-flying/spin-attacking or crouching, and its `0.6F x 1.8F` standing box clears the Y=64 floor. Its box is approximately X/Z `[100.2,100.8]`, Y `[65.0875,66.8875]`; deflating by `0.001` gives minimum Y `65.0885`. A queries Y cells 65-66 and only X/Z cell 100. B's box modifier clips minimum Y to boat.maxY `66.0625`, so its cell scan covers Y=66 and only X/Z cell 100.

Put flowing LAVA LEVEL 1 at `(100,65,100)`, LEVEL 2 east at `(101,65,100)`, LEVELS 3 through 7 at X=102 through 106, and a LAVA source at `(107,65,100)`. STONE at Y=64 supports the row. Keep the north/south/west cells at Y=65 empty; their cells below are STONE, so they provide no below-fluid fallback in `getFlow()`. The east LEVEL 2 cell alone contributes `1.0F/9.0F - 2.0F/9.0F`, yielding normalized flow `(-1,0,0)`. Under the Nether's `FAST_LAVA=true` configuration, drop-off is 1 and the source-to-level chain is produced by the paired `FlowingFluid.getNewLiquid()` logic. These are source-produced fluid states, not an arbitrary supplied flow.

The target top is `65 + (1.0F/9.0F)`: A's int-plus-float operation yields `65.11111450195312`; B's double-plus-float result is `65.11111111193895`. Both are below boat.maxY `66.0625`. B's interaction box starts at Y `66.0625`, so its integer cell scan begins at Y=66 and never enumerates the target cell at Y=65; the per-cell `fluidTop < box.minY` gate is therefore not evaluated for that omitted cell. The target remains inside B's loaded source-fluid section. B's loaded-region gate passes: the scan plus its horizontal margin stays in loaded chunk `(6,6)`, and the intersected section `[64,80)` contains the target lava. A's expanded-box chunk check also passes in that loaded chunk. A's Player scan overlap is approximately `65.11111450195312 - 65.0885 = 0.0226145`. This is below its `0.4` height scale threshold, so A keeps a nonzero westward per-cell vector; Nether scale `0.007` gives magnitude about `0.0001583`, above Vec3's strict `1.0E-5F` normalization floor. With old horizontal velocity zero, A reaches its Player minimum impulse branch and writes a nonzero westward delta. B records no LAVA height/current from the target, so its LAVA tracker has no current to apply. The empty cell at Y=66 contributes nothing. Both exact Player predicates allow fluid push because `abilities.flying` is false.
