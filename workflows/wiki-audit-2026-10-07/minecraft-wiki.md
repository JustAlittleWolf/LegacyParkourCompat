# Minecraft Wiki audit (2026-10-07)

## Scope and evidence rule

This independent Wiki track records candidate claims for the movement-difference campaign. The Minecraft Wiki is a lead source, not a source of release truth. Every historical Wiki claim below is labeled **wiki-only claim** until both compared exact-version source trees or bytecode have been inspected. Do not infer code from Wiki phrasing, rounded numbers, current behavior, or snapshot notes.

Scope: player movement, collision shapes for blocks present in the target version, player dimensions and poses, movement math/input state, fluids, effects, and enchantments. Exclude hunger, exhaustion, health, damage, regeneration, combat, non-player physics, visual-only changes, and features or blocks absent from the target version. The audit boundary is native 26.2; 26.3/26.4 are outside scope.

## Source handoff checked

The coordinated source owner has published and validated the requested exact-version trees through 1.21.5, including the narrow 1.16.1/1.16.2 corroboration, plus 26.2/unobfuscated. I read each readiness JSON, checked its exact ID and namespace, and recomputed the three published SHA-256 hashes for the source manifest, artifact manifest, and movement diagnostics; all matched. For 26.2, Entity.java, LivingEntity.java, Player.java, and LocalPlayer.java hashes also matched the source manifest. Every source file cited below was checked against its corresponding manifest entry. The requested 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, and 26.1.2 endpoints are still pending publication.

The 26.2 endpoint's LivingEntity.java has MIN_MOVEMENT_DISTANCE = 0.003 at line 163; getFluidFallingAdjustedMovement at lines 2685-2697 uses 0.003 comparisons and retains the 0.005 constant in a separate condition; the movement clamping branch at lines 3068-3079 zeros components below 0.003, while player horizontal motion is treated separately at horizontalDistanceSqr() < 9.0E-6. Player.updatePlayerPose at lines 343-355 checks SWIMMING, desired pose, CROUCHING, then SWIMMING fallback. LocalPlayer.aiStep at lines 784-829 handles crouch-fit, sprint triggers and sprint stop branches.

These are source-verified facts about the 26.2 endpoint only, not proof of when any behavior changed. Other requested endpoints remain queued. Pairwise historical conclusions remain **unresolved** until both exact releases are ready and their manifests and method bodies are checked.

## Explicit exclusions from Wiki leads

- Hunger/exhaustion costs, hunger-based sprint restrictions, health, damage, healing/regen, drowning, and combat knockback.
- Camera interpolation, FOV-only changes, arm animation, pane texture/model/culling history unless source shows collision changed.
- Sculk sensing or other detection consequences.
- Creative flight unless the selected mode includes it.
- Bedrock and console rows.
- Modern-only blocks/features (for example blue ice, honey, powder snow, soul speed) when absent from the target release.
- Any release after native 26.2.
